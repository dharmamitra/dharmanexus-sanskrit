Source: Luo 2010 (chs. 9-14)

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi caryāpaṭalaṃ sudurlabham | 
yena yogī kurute sarvacaryā atyantavarāṅganā || 9.1 ||
aṣṭasiddhisamāyuktā paścāc caryāṃ samārabhet | 
yad yat sattvāḥ siddhyarthino tāṃ tāṃ dāpayet sadā || 9.2 ||
kare kapālaṃ gṛhītvā caryā bhramate vratī | 
nagno muktaśikho bhūtvā yatra tatra yadā tadā | 
evaṃ caryā sadā kartavyam || 9.3 ||
sarvāvaraṇavinirmuktam ākāśatulyatāṃ gataḥ | 
bhikṣām aṭate prājñaḥ gṛhe gṛhe niḥsaṃśayaḥ |
manonukūla sarvaṃ gṛhṇan tu dhyānālayaḥ niḥsaṃśayaḥ || 9.4 ||
bhikṣāṃ yadārthayed yogī hṛṣṭacittena mānasaḥ | 
na iti śūnyatābhāvaṃ dadāti iti bhāvanām || 9.5 ||
jā ity anuttaraṃ yānaṃ mahāmudrā sa kathyate | 
jāha iti śrutamātreṇa yogī dharmaparāyaṇaḥ || 9.6 ||
paścād bhakṣayed dravyaṃ kapāle tu vyavasthitam | 
dravyaṃ bhakṣitamātreṇa patitaḥ supto mahāmatiḥ || 9.7 ||
gṛhe 'ṅgaṇe caiva cchārakūṭe vṛkṣamūle | 
yahi tahi yadā tadā yena tena hṛṣṭacetasā |
īdṛśaś caryāyogayuktasya mahāmudrā labhyate dhruvam || 9.8 ||
tathā coktaṃ tantre—
jatha pi tatha pi jaha pi taha pi jena tena hua buddha | 
saiṃ viappe ṇāsiā saala sahāve śuddha || 9.9 ||

iti buddhakapālatantre yoginīniruttare guhyātiguhyatare caryāpaṭalo navamaḥ ||   ||

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi sattvārthaikasuniścayam | 
paṭalaṃ hemakaṃ dhruvam || 10.1 || 
muktikā śuktikā caiva rājapaṭa pravālasya ca |
yena sattvā dhanavato yānti |
ācārye prasanne san || 10.2 || 
na ta auṣadha bāhyam ity uktam— 
na cāpi cchalla pallavaḥ sa ca |
na cāpi pāradaṃ rasam |
na gandhakaṃ nābhrakam |
na te kāntavaṅgatāmrakam |
na cāpi aparalohasyāpi siddham auṣadham uttamam || 10.3 ||
īdṛśaṃ vacanaṃ śrutvā vajrapāṇi mahābalaḥ |
praṇipatya pādarajāṃsi ca |
sattvaikadṛḍhabhaktena sādhu devī iti bruvan || 10.4 ||
ke te auṣadhi divyā siddhiś ca kīdṛśī bhavet | 
kathayāhi tvaṃ mahādevī śrotum icchāmi ādarāt || 10.5 || 
kathayāhi tvaṃ mahādevi |
ke te siddhauṣadhi varāḥ |
kena sattvo dhanavanto yānti āyurvardhanatatparam || 10.6 ||
ke te paṭhitasiddha mahauṣadhī |
pūrvasevā nāpekṣanti |
kena dravyaṃ bahavo yānti vandhyā sutavatī bhavet |
mṛtavān saṃjīvate na mriyate punaḥ punaḥ || 10.7 ||
āha— 
atraiva gṛhe sthite |
sidhyante mahauṣadhī |
sa ca sidhyante subhājanaṃ divyaṃ 
na jānanti bāla mohitāḥ || 10.8 || 
ajñānenāvṛtā bālā ātmārthaikatatparāḥ |
kathaṃ sattvārtha sādhyante hā hā kaṣṭaikatatparam || 10.9 || 
subhājane tiṣṭhate rasam |
karṇikāgūḍhagocare |
nāḍi madhye rasanī nāma
tiṣṭhanti divyamahauṣadhī || 10.10 ||
lohaṃ yena hematāṃ yānty ekenāsaṅkhyaṃ vidhyati |
īdṛśī mahauṣadhī varām |
tiṣṭhate rasanī tathā || 10.11 ||
saṃyogavidhipūrveṇa sidhyate auṣadhī varā |
saṃyogaṃ tatra pravakṣyāmi śṛṇvantu dṛḍhabhaktitaḥ || 10.12 ||
sūryavatī yadā nārī | 
tāruṇya kṛṣṇavarṇā |
śrutavatī iha tantre ca || 10.13 ||
nirlajī nirapekṣī syāt nirlobhī sattvavatsalī |
tathāgatān sevayantī nityaṃ 
suguptā nirghṛṇaḥ sa ca || 10.14 ||
imāṃ nārī samāgṛhya divyamahauṣadhi samārabhet | 
atyantarāgiṇaḥ puṃsaḥ |
śīghradrutir bhaved yasya sa na sidhyati || 10.15 ||
īdṛśī mahauṣadhī |
buddhānām api sudurlabhā |
tām aṅganāṃ sevayet prājñaḥ atyantavīracetasam || 10.16 ||
tathā coktam—
dhairyaṃ hi parama nāyakapadam |
pravadanti siddhāḥ |
ata eva hi vīracetasā kāryaṃ subhājane pracodayet || 10.17 || 
tatra sūryavatī nārī hemakaraṇasudurlabhī |
svahṛdi cintayec candraṃ | 
siddhaṃrasenālipitam |
tatra hrīṃkārabījaṃ sarvadravyaika 
pūrvaṃ merurājena bhūṣitam || 10.18 ||
paścāt kāryaṃ samārabhet prājñaḥ sattvārthaikatatparaḥ | 
subhājane nikṣipayed vastu 
vāme dakṣiṇākuṃcitam || 10.19 ||
sahapalatāmraṃ pāśe sthāpayet vartulam |
abhāve loham eva ca || 10.20 ||
paścāt karma samārabhet prājño vīrayogena cetasā |
sumerucandrabījarājasya dravībhūya niḥsaṃśayaḥ || 10.21 ||
subhājane viśramate tatra cyutiyogāt kharaṇam eva ca |
śīghraṃ divyaṃ samāgṛhya guḍā lepayed budhaḥ || 10.22 || 
lepitaguḍāmātreṇa |
agnimadhye nikṣipayet |
paścād rakṣate prājñaḥ hemo bhavati niḥsaṃśayaḥ || 10.23 || 
tasya māsa-m-ekena pala śatāni vidhyati |
evaṃ hemakaraṇaṃ divyaṃ 
trailokye-m-api durlabham || 10.24 ||
apramāṇaṃ yadi mahauṣadhi |
tadā buddho 'py apramāṇaḥ |
idaṃ tantraṃ mahārāja |
yad yad vastu nigaditam |
sarve te satyatāṃ yānti buddhakāpālayogataḥ || 10.25 || 
ayam apramāṇaṃ yaḥ karoti prājñaḥ 
śrutavanto mahāmatiḥ |
te sarve narakaṃ yānti yāvad buddhair na hīyate || 10.26 || 
caturāśītidharmaskandhasāhasraṃ 
yoginīnāṃ niruttaram |
sarve te micchatāṃ yānti auṣadhyasiddhamātrataḥ || 10.27 || 
pañcānantaryakāriṇo 'pi ye sattvā 
buddhadharmaviheṭhakāḥ |
sidhyante mahauṣadhiṃ divyaṃ 
tatkṣaṇād eva niḥsaṃśayaḥ |
kiṃ punar bhāvanāyuktasya sattvārthaikatatparaḥ || 10.28 || 
sidhyante auṣadhīṃ varām |
hema tāro 'pi jāyate |
jīvate kalpakoṭiṃ naraḥ yāvad buddhajñānam eva ca || 10.29 || 
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi vandhyānāṃ putrakāraṇam | 
saṃyogena snāpitamātreṇa tatkṣaṇāt sutavatī bhavet || 10.30 || 
snānavidhiṃ pravakṣyāmi yathoktaṃ pūrvanāyakaiḥ | 
pañcavarṇarajaḥ kṛtya atha vā śuklam eva ca |
vartayen maṇḍalaṃ ghoraṃ grahaṇatrāsakārakam || 10.31 || 
kalaśaṃ catvāras tatra |
vartulaṃ bṛhadudaram |
udakena pūrayet prājñaḥ vastreṇāpi veṣṭayet || 10.32 || 
likhitaṃ cihnam ātmānaiva rudhireṇa tu |
sā strī yā vandhyā tasyā eva | 
pradhānadhātu saṃgṛhya 
kalaśamukhaṃ lepayed viduḥ || 10.33 ||
paścād bhāvanāparayuktasya śrīherukapade sthitaḥ | 
kṛṣṇaraktamahāghoraṃ vajrahūṃkārasambhavam | 
pralayānalabhīṣaṇaṃ ghorahūṃkāram 
uccāraṇan viduḥ || 10.34 || 
caturbhujam ekavaktraṃ ca evam uccāraṇan viduh | 
kalaśamadhye bhāvayet prājñaḥ 
nṛtyantaṃ gurubhīṣaṇam || 10.35 ||
paścāc catvāraḥ puṃsāḥ khaḍgadhṛtā mahāyaśāḥ | 
atyantakrodhacetasā || 10.36 ||
tatkṣaṇāt snānamātreṇa te puṃsā gurubhīṣaṇāḥ | 
ākāśa hanyante khaḍgena mārayanti ca || 10.37 ||
īdṛśo yogayuktasya snāpitā pramadā tathā | 
sutavatī bhaved vandhyā buddhakāpālayogataḥ || 10.38 ||

iti buddhakapālatantre yoginīniruttare guhyātiguhyatare divyamahauṣadhivandhyāsnānavidhipaṭalo daśamaḥ ||   ||

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi paṭalaṃ gāruḍayogajam | 
pūrvoktavidhānena maṇḍalaṃ vartayed budhaḥ || 11.1 || 
herukaṃ niṣpādayet prājño 'ṣṭayoginī samantataḥ | 
pūrvoktacihnabhujādyaiḥ paraṃ hi varṇa śuklam eva ca |
digyoginī bhavet śuklaṃ vidig raktam eva ca || 11.2 || 
pūrvoktavidhānaiś ca maṇḍalaṃ pratiṣṭhate budhaḥ | 
kalaśaṃ catvāri caiva pūrvoktacihnaṃ likhet || 11.3 ||
paścāc chiṣyam anugrahet |
vidyādharakaraṇayogataḥ |
pūrvā vidhānena-v-adhyeṣaṇā dānaṃ gaṇacakram || 11.4 ||
paścān maṇḍalaṃ pūjāpayet |
anenaiva mantreṇa punaḥ śiṣyam anugrahet || 11.5 ||
daṣṭaka bhūtale na kiṃcij jīvāpayituṃ śakyate |
tyaktaṃ gāruḍikena kāladaṣṭaṃ vadanti ca || 11.6 ||
paścāt karma samārabhet prājño buddhakāpālayoginaḥ | 
ātmānaṃ heruko bhūto 'ṣṭayoginī samantataḥ |
sarve te śukladehā tu |
vidhivad yogaṃ tu || 11.7 ||
śālipiṣṭakamayaṃ gṛhītvā vidyādharadvayaṃ kārayet |
tasya haste dāpayet khaḍgam atyantasamāhitaḥ || 11.8 ||
tasya hṛdaye likhen mantraṃ yatra tatra patre tathā | 
mahātailena kṛtvā masīṃ mantraṃ likhed viduḥ || 11.9 || 
mantraḥ—
oṃ buddhakapālinī hrīḥ | oṃ kāpālavajriṇī āḥ | vidhvaṃsaya sarvanāgān | utthāpaya vidyādharān | jīvāpaya kāladaṣṭān | bho bho bhagavati buddhakāpālinī sarvanāgakṣayaṃkarī | haḥ ha haḥ svāhā || vidyādharasya hṛdayamantraḥ || 11.10 ||
evaṃ paripūrṇaṃ kṛtvā patra hṛdaya sthāpayet | 
anena mantreṇa pallavena saptavārān avāhayet || 11.11 ||
oṃ buddhakāpālinī | ghaṭ ghaṭ ghaḍa ghaḍa hrīḥ hrīḥ haḥ haḥ he he haṃ haḥ || 11.12 ||
saptavārāvāhitamātreṇa vidyādharadvayam | 
antarīkṣaṃ bhūtvā yudhyanti | 
ekasmin khaṇḍakhaṇḍaṃ bhūtvā patati 
daṣṭakotthati nātra saṃśayaḥ || 11.13 ||
aparo 'pi karaṇa | 
ye durlabhāḥ padmotsthālādi | 
pañcadaśa pustake na likhitaṃ 
gamyate guruprasādataḥ || 11.14 ||

iti buddhakapālatantre yoginīniruttare guhyātiguhyatare vidyādharakaraṇopadeśaniścayapaṭala ekādaśaḥ ||   ||

atha vajrapāṇiś citrasenā yoginī muhur muhuḥ praṇipatyaivam uvāca— || 12.1 ||
sādhu devī mahāprājñā pradhānabhūtayoginī |
uktaṃ tvayā yat prayogaṃ trailokyeṣv api durlabham || 12.2 ||
dṛṣṭā mayā sarvatantrāṇi ye te prayogāḥ sudurlabhāḥ |
iha tantre tvayā yad uktaṃ sarvabuddhair na bhāṣitāḥ || 12.3 ||
aho hī sattvārthatatpari |
aho hī sarvabuddhasaṃjananī 
aho hī mahāmudrātatpari || 12.4 ||
bhāṣasva devi sarvamantrāṇāṃ 
ye te prayogāḥ sudurlabhāḥ |
yogino yena citraṃ kurvanti nānāścaryam eva ca | 
prathamapaṭale yad yad uktaṃ devi 
tatprayogaṃ kathayāhi me || 12.5 ||
īdṛg vacanaṃ śrutvā citrasenā tu yoginī |
hasitāṭṭaṭṭahāsena |
trāsitāḥ sarvadevāś ca || 12.6 ||
mārāḥ sarve prapalāyanti vighnāś ca pralayaṃ gatāḥ | 
āścaryaṃ jinavaraiḥ sarvaiḥ aho hāsasya niścayaḥ || 12.7 ||
āha—
dhvajam ālambitaṃ gṛhītvā | 
māṃsaṃ tasyāpi gṛhṇāti |
tasya kaṭibhāgāsthiṃ tu gṛhītvā kuṇḍa kārayet || 12.8 ||
tasya kāpālam anu gṛhya tenaiva śirasā varti dāpayet |
tasya tailenāpūrayet kuṇḍam upari sthāpayet || 12.9 ||
aparo 'pi kajjala pātayet | 
yāvat kajjala patati |
yogī buddhakāpāladharo bhūtvā 
imaṃ mantram uccārayan || 12.10 ||
oṃ buddhakapālinī dhara 2 dhadhara 2 dharāpaya 2 śoṣaya 2 tāpaya 2 drāṃ 2 āṃ māṃ sāṃ ra vāṃ kuru 2 bho bhagavati buddhakapālasyājñā mā laṃghaya śīghraṃ kajjala pata pata pātaya 2 oṃ 2 āḥ hūṃ sumbhaya bho bhagavati hūṃ srāṃ phaṭ || 12.11 ||
īdṛśaṃ mantrarājasya trailokyam api śoṣaṇam |
bhūtavetāḍaḍākinyaḥ rākṣasāḥ piśācādayaḥ || 12.12 ||
śrutamātreṇa pralayaṃ yānti | 
anye 'pi ca vighnāḥ sarva |
tāvad uccārayen mantraṃ |
yāvat kajjalaṃ na patati ca || 12.13 ||
kajjalapatitamātreṇa | 
dvayakareṇa gṛhītvā ca |
paścād yogī mahāprājñaḥ kelimālinī pivetaṃ punaḥ || 12.14 || 
ṣoḍaśābdikāṃ kanyā rūpayauvanakṛpāvatīm |
tāṃ kāmayed yogī sattvārthaikatatparaḥ || 12.15 ||
nagnī muktaśikhī bhūtvā 
hāḥhoḥmantram uccārayed viduḥ |
kelimālinī pivāpayet tatra paścāt krīḍā samārabhet || 12.16 || 
aṭṭaṭṭahāsino bhūtvā phetkāraṃ tu mahākṛpā |
kajjalaṃ tu mahādivyaṃ vāmahaste tu sthāpayet || 12.17 ||
paścāt śanaiḥ kāryaiḥ kuryād yogaṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ |
tadudbhavaṃ yad dravyaṃ padmakeśarasambhavān || 12.18 || 
tenaiva kajjalena malanam anāmikāṅguṣṭhe 'sya ca |
kusumaṃ dāpayed vaḍikāṃ kārayed aṣṭottaraśatāni || 12.19 || 
ekaikavaṭikāṃ aṣṭottaramantraśatenābhimantrya japet—
oṃ buddhakāpālinī hrī hūṃ phaṭ || 12.20 ||
anena mantreṇa kajjalaṃ tu 
mahābāho trailokyeṣv api durlabham |
durlabhaṃ sarvatantreṣu durlabhaṃ yoginīparam || 12.21 || 
añjitakajjalamātreṇa anāmikāṅguṣṭhena tu |
paśyate sarvabuddhāṃś ca trisāhasrarajopamān || 12.22 ||
aśītilakṣayojana |
adhorddhena paśyati |
vāmahastamalitamātreṇa 
tatkṣaṇād eva bhadrako sa jāyate || 12.23 || 
tarjjanyāñjitamātreṇa paśyate sacarācaram | 
pretabhūtapiśācānām anyāni ca siddhāni ca || 12.24 ||
yojanakoṭisahasraṃ tu adhorddhena paśyati | 
vāmahaste malitamātreṇa 
svastho bhavati na saṃśayaḥ || 12.25 || 
madhyamenāñjitamātreṇa | 
atyantāścaryakārako bhavati |
saptatāḍapramāṇāni kṛṣṇajvālāntaśekharaḥ |
sphuṭayet sarvameghānām eṣa kajjala durlabhaḥ || 12.26 || 
vāmahastena malitamātreṇa manuṣyatāṃ yānti tatkṣaṇāt | 
vṛddhāṅguṣṭhenāñjitamātreṇa kajjalaṃ tu mahātmanaḥ || 12.27 ||
pratimāṃ tu liṅgaparvatavṛkṣaṃ tu anyāni yāni tāni ca | 
krodhadṛṣṭyāvalokitamātreṇa khaṇḍaṃ sahasraṃ tu jāyate || 12.28 ||
punar vāmena malitamātreṇa svastho bhavati na saṃśayaḥ | 
kaniṣṭhāṅguṣṭhenāñjitamātreṇa 
cakṣuradṛśyo bhavati tatkṣaṇāt |
buddhair api na dṛśyate || 12.29 ||
tridaśeṣu bhramet prājñaḥ yad yad vastum icchati |
bhāṇḍārapriyadravyam eva ca |
harate tatkṣaṇād eva kajjalasya mahātmanaḥ || 12.30 ||
īdṛśaṃ kajjalaṃ prayogaṃ nānāprayogasudurlabham |
karoty añjanamātreṇa svarūpaparivartanaṃ sa ca || 12.31 || 
anena kajjalenaiva |
vidyādharo bhavati |
kākolūkaśakunasarpaśvānajambukamahāvyāghrabhallū otukaḥ |
tathā makṣikāś ca tathā hasti gardabham eva ca || 12.32 ||
evaṃ citraṃ karoti kajjalena tu |
sarvatra sarva eva ca |
prayogaḥ param atra durlabhāḥ |
prayogāḥ pustake 'likhitā jñātavyā guruprasādataḥ || 12.33 ||

bhaginī bhāginī jananī duhitā bhāryā-m-eva ca | 
evaṃ kāmayet prājña mahāmudrāṃ yad iṣyate || 13.2 ||
ḍombinī nartakī caiva |
dhorviṇī caṇḍālinī carmakāriṇī smṛtā |
evaṃ kāmayed dhīmān pañcaite yoginī varām || 13.3 || 
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi aśuddhacittaśodhanāt || 13.4 || 
bhaginī bhavet cakṣur bhāginī tu śrotram eva ca |
jananī bhaved ghrāṇaṃ rasaṃ duhitā tu eva ca | 
mano bhaved bhāryā |
pañcaite varā divyāṃ mahāmudrāpradāyikī || 13.5 || 
nartakī bhaven mohaṃ dveṣaṃ ḍombinīṣu ca |
rāga bhaved dhorvī |
mānaṃ caṇḍālinī khyātā |
maherṣyā carmakārī ca pañcaite varāṅganā || 13.6 ||
cakṣuṣā dṛśyate mohaṃ śabdaṃ dveṣo 'pi jāyate |
ghrāṇena bhujyate rāgaṃ rasaṃ mānaṃ tu eva ca | 
sparśena jāyate īrṣyā cittasyāśuddham eva ca || 13.7 ||
tasmād yogī mahāprājña atyantayogacetasaḥ |
siddhir na syād bhavet śuddhyā 
punarjanmabhavāntare || 13.8 ||
abhāvena kathaṃ bhāvo | 
bhāvaḥ kīdṛśaṃ abhāvo 
bhāvo bhāvyaḥ kathaṃ bhavet || 13.9 ||
bhaginī kathaṃ rūpaṃ gaurī śyāmā kṛṣṇavarṇajām |
īdṛśaṃ rūpa saṃyuktaṃ śuddhacitto na drakṣyati || 13.10 || 
bhāginī kathaṃ śrūyate śabdaḥ dhvanis trividham eva ca | 
īdṛśo yogayuktasya śuddhacitto na śṛṇoti || 13.11 || 
ghrāṇaṃ trividhaṃ dṛṣṭvā kā yā sā jananī suguhyakā | 
adṛṣṭamānasaṃ yuktaṃ kathaṃ rāgo 'pi jāyate || 13.12 || 
svajihvena bhuñjate dravyaṃ ṣaḍrasaṃ svāda yādṛśī |
svādena bhuñjate yuktaṃ svādaṃ rūpasya kīdṛśī || 13.13 || 
manena sukhaṃ harṣam eva ca |
manaḥ sukham ubhayavicāraṇāt |
vicāra tatra na vidyate || 13.14 ||
bhāve na abhāvo asti abhāvam eva ca | 
bhāvābhāvam ubhayaśūnyam 
ete cittasya śuddhatā || 13.15 ||
ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi bāhyakāyaśodhanāt || 13.16 ||
dṛṣṭiyuktaṃ bhavet karuṇā maitrī āliṅganasya ca || 
saṃgupte yogātmā muditā varāṅganā tathā |
ubhayamelāpakaṃ tat kṣaraṇam 
upekṣā rasā smṛtā mayā || 13.17 || 
evaṃbhāvayogayuktasya kathaṃ rāgeṇa bādhyate |
darśanāj jāyate duḥkham āliṅganāt kiṃcit tatphalam | 
saṃgharṣaṇāj jāyate harṣo viṣādaṃ sarvaśeṣataḥ || 13.18 || 
evaṃ traidhātuka utpannaṃ harṣaviṣāda etat phalam | 
saṃśuddhacittayogātmā trayavastu na bādhyate || 13.19 ||
yat kiṃcit khāna pānaṃ ca sukhāvahabhojanaṃ tathā | 
tat sarvaṃ viṇmūtrādi prakalpayet yogavit sadā || 13.20 ||
yat kiṃcid ratikrīḍā āliṅganacumbanasya ca |
malanaḥ mardanaś caiva mahāmudrā sa kathyate |
īdṛśo bhāvayuktasya sidhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 13.21 ||
pañca yoginī buddhasya lakṣmīmahāmudrāpradāyikā | 
tāṃ pūjayed yogī kāmate na ca lipyate || 13.22 ||
lepa īrṣyādisaṃyuktaṃ tasya doṣair na bādhyate |
sa yogī paramaṃ tattvaṃ bhavadoṣair na lipyate || 13.23 ||
yāvan na viṣaya unmuliaï
tāma budhataṇu kema | 
searahianavaaṃkuraha
tarusampatti na jema || 13.24 ||

iti buddhakapālatantre yoginīniruttare guhyātiguhyatare cittaviśuddhipaṭalaḥ trayodaśamaḥ ||   ||

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi |
citrasenāpraśasyapaṭalam || 14.1 ||
tathā—
atha vajrapāṇiprabhṛtayo bodhisattvāḥ sumeruparamāṇurajasamā yoginyaḥ, utphullalocanā hṛṣṭacittā citrasenā praṇipatti stūyanta || 14.2 ||
aho hī bhagavatī devī |
sarvabuddhānāṃ tu jananī lakṣmī ghorā mahāyaśā |
sattvārtheṣu vatsalī || 14.3 || 
mahākṛpāvatī devī |
mahāprajñā mahāmudrā mahārthaphalapradāyikā || 14.4 || 
buddhakāpāle mahātantre buddhair yeṣāṃ na prakāśitā |
gopitā vajradhareṇaiva bodhisattvāś ca agocarāḥ | 
tāṃ bhāṣitā devī ekarutena prakaṭīkṛtāḥ || 14.5 ||
aho hī mahābhājanā devī sarvayoginīpiṇḍīkṛtāu | 
ye te siddhaṃ sudurlabham |
sarve tathāgateṣv agocarāḥ | 
tāṃ prakaṭīkṛtā devī paṭhimātreṇa sidhyati || 14.6 ||
aho śṛṇvantu bhagavatī |
yoginyo bodhisattvāḥ śṛṇvantu |
vītarāgā maharddhikāḥ śṛṇvantu |
tathāgatāś ca sarvabuddhāḥ śṛṇvantu nāgāś ca sadevamānuṣāḥ || 14.7 ||
ayaṃ tantrarājasya pāṭhamātreṇa yat puṇyaṃ tat sarvapuṇyam anumodayantu pariṇāmayantu mahāmudrārthe niryātayantu buddhaguṇākarān || 14.8 ||
kṣamantu māṃ yat prakaṭīkṛtam | 
gūḍhārthaṃ mantrāś ca prayogaṃ sudurlabham |
suguptagocarasya ca |
prakaṭīkṛtya sattvārthena jinavaraiḥ kṣamantu mām || 14.9 ||
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi mantrajāpaṃ mahābalam | 
yad icchet siddhiṃ varāṃ divyāṃ 
japed buddhakāpālayogataḥ || 14.10 ||
oṃ hrīṃ buddha hūṃ kāpālinī āḥ || 
praveśe oṃ | niṣkāśe hrīṃ | viśrāme buddhaḥ | sukhe hūṃ | astamaye kāpālinī | utthāne āḥ || 14.11 ||
evaṃjāpaṃ sudurlabham |
evaṃ buddhakāpālayogataḥ |
evaṃjāpaṃ mānayuktaṃ siddhir bhavati na saṃśayaḥ || 14.12 || 
hṛdayamantraḥ—
oṃ buddhakāpālinī āḥ hūḥ haiḥ hūṃ phaṭ || 14.13 ||
upahṛdayamantraḥ—
oṃ buddhakāpālinavajriṇi hau durjaya durlakṣe hūṃ phaṭ || 14.14 ||
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi sekakāle yathā vidhim | 
prajñā adhyeṣate yena śiṣyeṇa dṛḍhabhaktitaḥ || 14.15 || 
maṇḍalapañcakaṃ kṛtvā sugandhena tu vastunā | 
adhyeṣayati mahādevī sarvabuddhair alaṃkṛtā || 14.16 ||
pivāvayati divyadravyaṃ khādya mukha dāpayet |
pīvitā dravya hṛṣṭacittena mānasaḥ || 14.17 ||
harṣitā devī dṛṣṭvā idam vacanam abravīt | 
saṃsārapaṅkalagno 'haṃ trātā bhavāhi sundarī || 14.18 || 
channavāsanāsaṃyuktaṃ vikalpaṃ bahulena ca |
tān upaśamayasva devī mahāyaśā kṛpāvatī || 14.19 || 
niryātayed alaṃkāra tat sarvābharaṇamaṇḍitām |
paścāt 
ātmānaṃ niryātayet prājña dṛḍhabhaktena cetasā || 14.20 || 
nānākhādyaṃ nānāpeyaṃ tasya purato niryātayet |
prajñā toṣitā yena sarvabuddhaiḥ prapūjitāḥ || 14.21 || 
atraiva sthite buddhā aparo 'pi siddhanāyakāḥ |
tatraiva tiṣṭhanti sarvaḍākinyaḥ anye ye ca kṛpākulāḥ || 14.22 || 
indī yattha vilayagaü naṭṭa vi appasahāva |
so hale paramāṇandagaï phuḍa pucchaha gurupāa || 14.23 || 
tasmai namo yadudayena samānakālam 
astaṅgatāni viṣayaiḥ samam indriyāṇi |
yaś cābhinandajinasundaramūrtir eko
jāgarti ko 'pi nibhṛtaṃ hṛdi paṇḍitānām || 14.24 ||

Title: Viśeṣastava
Author: Rādhasvāmin (a.k.a. Bhaṭṭarādhasvāmin)
Source: Schneider, Johannes. 2023. Viśeṣastava. Der Sanskrittext nach den Handschriften bearbeitet und übersetzt. Indica et Tibetica 61. Marburg: Indica et Tibetica Verlag. [bilingual Skt.-Tib. ed. on pp. 54-85] 
Tibetan: D 1109, D 4562
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó, Budapest 2023.12.23-24

namo buddhāya |

śāstṝn anyān samutsṛjya tvām ahaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ |
bhagavaṃs tatra ko hetur nirdoṣo guṇavān asi || 1 ||
doṣabhakto 'thavā loko doṣān vā guṇato gataḥ | 
guṇagrahaṇamando vā yenānyāñ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ || 2 ||
vāsudeveśverādīnāṃ harṣāt tadbhaktibhir janaiḥ | 
ye guṇāḥ parikīrtyante doṣās te tava śāsane || 3 ||
rudreṇa tripuraṃ dagdhaṃ kruddhenaikeṣuṇā kila | 
krodhāgnir eva nirdagdhas tvayā jñānamaheṣuṇā || 4 ||
durdagdhena hi kāmena punar ākarṣito haraḥ | 
tvajjñānāgnau hutaḥ kāmaḥ samūlo vilayaṃ gataḥ || 5 ||
tilottamāyāṃ jātāsthaḥ śūlī jātaś caturmukhaḥ | 
mārakanyāvilāsais te romāgram api no 'ñcitam || 6 ||
cakravākān api jayaty ardhena vanitāṃ vahan | 
śaṃkaro 'śaṃkareṇa tvaṃ naiva kāmena mohitaḥ || 7 ||
na tan mārabalaṃ jetum alaṃ cakritriśūlinau | 
vinā cakratriśūlābhyāṃ yan maitryastreṇa te jitam || 8 ||
nīlakaṇṭhīkṛtaḥ śarvo jarobāṇair hato hariḥ | 
sopakramau tu tau devau tvaṃ nātha nirupakramaḥ || 9 ||
vāmanaṃ rūpam āsthāya hariṇā māyayā baliḥ | 
vañcitas tvaṃ tu māyājño na ca vañcayase parān || 10 ||
taiḥ paṅgugativad dṛṣṭaṃ viṣṇos trailokyalaṅghanam | 
dṛṣṭaṃ yair adbhutaṃ nātha tvaddṛddhīnāṃ vijṛmbhitam || 11 ||
kumāraḥ sahitaḥ ṣaṣṭhyā brahmacaryaṃ carann api | 
tvayā tu maithunāḥ saptasaṃyogād bhasmasāt kṛtaḥ || 12 ||
sraṣṭāro hi vayaṃ lokasyoktaṃ brahmādibhiḥ kila | 
karmajās te ca lokaś ca tvayoktaṃ tattvadarśinā || 13 ||
ahalyādarśanād vajrī kāmena kila mohitaḥ | 
vīra dārās tvayā tyaktāḥ kāma eva vimohitaḥ || 14 ||
somaḥ kavalatāṃ yāti vivaśo rāhuhastinaḥ | 
tan nāsti bhūtaṃ yasya tvaṃ vivaśo vaśam āpnuyāḥ || 15 ||
nṛpā rāmāmbarīṣāḍyā vanebhyaḥ punar āgatāḥ | 
vivekāmṛtatṛptas tvaṃ punaḥ sugata nāgataḥ || 16 ||
ālabhyāḥ paśavo yajñe prāhuḥ svargaṃ jigīṣavaḥ | 
pipīlakasyāpi vadho nānujñātas tvayānagha || 17 ||
pareṣāṃ jīvitājyena svārtham anyair makhe hutam | 
parārthaṃ tu tvayaikena svajīvāhutayo hutāḥ || 18 ||
ṛṣibhir bahavo dagdhāḥ kruddhaiḥ śāpāgnibhir janāḥ | 
tvayā jñānāgninā dagdhā janānāṃ janmahetavaḥ || 19 ||
ṛṣīṇāṃ ca tapaḥsiddhiḥ śāpasiddhyānumīyate | 
tava nātha tapaḥsiddhiḥ kṛpāsiddhyānumīyate || 20 ||
ṛṣibhiḥ pātitaṃ liṅgaṃ śambhoḥ śāpena kāminaḥ | 
tvayā mūlam anarthānāṃ kāma eva nipātitaḥ || 21 ||
bahutvāt smṛtidaurbalyāt pūrvottaraparāhatam | 
śrūyate vyāsavacanaṃ sarvatra na ca te mune || 21* ||
prāyaḥ paroktāny uktāni yāni dvaipāyanādibhiḥ | 
tvayāsādhāraṇo dharmaḥ svayaṃ jñātvā prakāśitaḥ || 22 ||
vivikteṣv eva kathyante vedās tīrthyavacāṃsi ca | 
āghoṣya tava saiṃhena dharmo nādena kathyate || 23 ||
dṛṣṭatattvo 'pi sāṃkhyānāṃ bhāradvājo vasantayā | 
saṃgataḥ saṃgatā naiva strībhiḥ śiṣyās tava prabho || 24 ||
viśiṣṭe deyam ity evam ulūkaḥ prāha netvare | 
śvabhyo 'pi nātha dātavyaṃ kāruṇyād bhāṣitaṃ tvayā || 25 ||
śarīratāpair icchanti nirgranthā granthiśātanam | 
tvaṃ punar mānasais tāpair granthiśātanam icchasi || 26 ||
anapekṣyaiva bhāvānāṃ siddhim icchanti tārkikāḥ | 
na kiṃcid anapekṣyāstīty uktaṃ vācaspate tvayā || 27 ||
mokṣaṃ kila gataḥ prāhuḥ śuko bhittvārkamaṇḍalam | 
tvaṃ tu prāptaś ca nirvāṇaṃ na gataś cāsi kutracit || 28 ||
mokṣam anyena janakaḥ śuko 'nyena pathā gataḥ | 
tvaṃ tu yena pathā yātaḥ śiṣyās tenaiva te gatāḥ || 29 ||
ṣoḍaśa strīsahasrāṇi viṣṇor āsīt parigrahaḥ | 
tvaṃ tu lokottamaḥ śāstā nirmamo niṣparigrahaḥ || 30 ||
saṃraktaḥ kāśisundaryāṃ vyāso vikṛtim āgataḥ | 
gṛhasaṃstho 'py anāryais tvam indriyārthair na mohitaḥ || 31 ||
kāmārto vikalībhūto vanitāpādatāḍitaḥ | 
vyāso na punar eva tvaṃ gṛhibhūto 'pi kampitaḥ || 32 ||
pramadārthī gato rāmaḥ pāraṃ kila mahodadheḥ | 
ṣaṣṭiṃ ca strīsahasrāṇi tvaṃ vihāyāśramaṃ gataḥ || 33 ||
karṇaduryodhanādīnāṃ hariṇā cintito vadhaḥ | 
asavo 'pi tvayā tyaktāḥ sattvānāṃ hitakāmyayā || 34 ||
viṣṇunā kapaṭaṃ kṛtvā yā hṛtā vikramais tribhiḥ | 
sā kṣitis tṛṇavat tyaktā bhavatā nirmumukṣuṇā || 35 ||
kṛtvā niḥkṣatriyaṃ lokaṃ prāṇakṣayakaraiḥ śaraiḥ | 
yad yaśaḥ prāptavān rāmaḥ śāstre tad ayaśas tava || 36 ||
yasyāḥ kṛte ca saṃgrāmaḥ kṛtaḥ kauravapāṇḍavaiḥ | 
tāṃ latām iva savyāḍāṃ śriyaṃ tyaktvāsi nirgataḥ || 37 ||
devadāruvane śambhor yad yogeśvaraceṣṭitam | 
tvadīyaṃ śāsanaṃ prāpya tad ajñānaviceṣṭitam || 38 ||
dvijair adyāpi maghavān ahalyājāra gīyate | 
tvayāgraphalalābhena śakrasyodghoṣitaṃ yaśaḥ || 39 ||
kṛṣṇo hi kṛṣṇayavanatrāsārto 'dhaḥ praviṣṭavān | 
khaṭvāyā mucukundasya nirbhīs tvaṃ sarvataḥ prabho || 40 ||
prajāpatir duhitaraṃ svām akāmayata śrutiḥ | 
akalmaṣaṃ brahmacaryaṃ śiṣyāṇām api te mune || 41 ||
dānagrahaṇavat proktā saṃkrāntiḥ puṇyapāpayoḥ | 
dvijais tvayoktam evaṃ hi kṛtanāśo 'kṛtāgamaḥ || 42 ||
cetanālakṣaṇo 'stv ātmā kāpilaiḥ parikalpyate | 
evaṃ hi saty anityatvam ātmanaḥ kathitaṃ tvayā || 43 ||
jñānād anyo vibhuś cātmā kaṇādaḥ parigītavān | 
tvayoktam evam ajño 'sau kāṣṭhatulyaḥ prasajyate || 44 ||
saṃkocī ca vikāsī ca jīvo dehapramāṇavān | 
prāha nagnaḥ pradeśitvāt tasyoktā te vināśitā || 45 ||
puruṣaḥ kāraṇaṃ nityaṃ jagataḥ kalpyate dvijaiḥ | 
nityaṃ ca kāraṇaṃ ceti tvayoktaṃ nāsti kiṃcana || 46 ||
ṣaṣṭiṃ putrasahasrāṇi sagarasya mahātmanaḥ | 
dṛṣṭyā dadāha kapilas tatrāpi karuṇā tava || 47 ||
pradhānāj jagad utpannaṃ kapilaḥ prāha tattvavit | 
karmakleśodbhavaṃ janma jagataḥ kathitaṃ tvayā || 48 ||
mokṣe 'pi kapilādīnām ātmano bhavahetavaḥ | 
tvaṃ tu śāntiṃ gataḥ saṃkhyāṃ naiṣi śānta ivānalaḥ || 49 ||
kaiścit tīrthakarair mokṣo gatimān ity udāhṛtaḥ | 
gatir yatra sa saṃsāras tvayā jñātvā prakāśitaḥ || 50 ||
ārāmādyāḥ samārambhā nagnānāṃ pāpahetavaḥ | 
ta eva khalu sarvajña tvayoktāḥ puṇyahetavaḥ || 51 ||
śiśupālasya saṃgrāme jīvaḥ kṛṣṇena ghātitaḥ | 
na śarīrāt pṛthagbhūto jīvo 'stīty uditaṃ tvayā || 51* ||
dehād dehāntaraṃ yāti jīvaḥ kila nirañjanaḥ | 
na śarīrāt pṛthagbhūto jīvo 'sti kathitaṃ tvayā || 52 ||
krodhān mṛtasya saṃgrāme vyāsenoktā śubhā gatiḥ | 
tadavasthena cittena tvayoktā nātha durgatiḥ || 53 ||
kīrtanāt pāṇḍuputrāṇāṃ puṇyam uktaṃ pṛthak pṛthak | 
agamyāṃ ye 'bhigacchanti nindyās te tava śāsane || 54 ||
skandenopendrarudrādyā dhārayanti sadāyudham | 
bhayād vā bhīṣaṇārthaṃ vā nirbhīs tvaṃ na ca bhīṣaṇaḥ || 55 ||
na bhīṣayasi kaṃcit tvaṃ na ca kaiścid vibhīṣitaḥ | 
nyastaśastras tvam eko hi tenāsi jagato 'dhikaḥ || 56 ||
vedādivacanaṃ prāyaḥ pūrvottaraparāhatam | 
vaktuḥ saṃkleśatāṃ vakti vacaḥ sarvajñatāṃ tava || 57 ||
pūrvāparaṃ virodhīni śāstrāṇy anyāni prāyaśaḥ | 
ādimadhyāvasāne tu tava vākalmaṣaṃ matam || 58 ||
na śūdrāya matiṃ dadyād ity uktaṃ dharmavādibhiḥ | 
caṇḍālebhyo 'pi kāruṇyāt saddharmo deśitas tvayā || 59 ||
drumaṃ bhadraṃ vinirmucya durvanaṃ kaḥ samācaret | 
tvāṃ vītarāgam āsādya ko 'nyāñ śāstṝn named budhaḥ || 60 ||
viṣayān api bhuñjānā jñānān mucyanti kāpilāḥ | 
saṃkliṣṭasya kuto mokṣas tvayoktaṃ vītakalmaṣa || 61 ||
anyaśāstreṣu tan noktaṃ yad uktaṃ bhagavaṃs tvayā | 
kṛtvā tu puṇyasaṃbhāraḥ sattvebhyaḥ pariṇāmyate || 62 ||
devāyatanayajñeṣu kṛtvā bhūtābhimardanam | 
laukikā dharmam icchanti tac chāstre garhitaṃ tvayā || 63 ||
dharma eva kutas tatra yatra prāṇivadho dhruvam | 
svakair api tvayā prāṇaiḥ sattvāḥ saṃrakṣitā vibho || 64 ||
yatra satyam ahiṃsā ca brahmacaryaṃ damo dayā | 
śākyarājakulodbhūta saddharmas tava saṃmataḥ || 65 ||
ātatāyinam āyāntam api vedāntagaṃ raṇe | 
uktaṃ loke tvayāpy uktaṃ kāruṇyaṃ vadhakeṣv api || 66 ||
svo mātulaḥ kumāreṇa śaktyā bhinno 'bhigīyate | 
kleśajālaṃ tvayā vīra prajñāśaktyā vidāritam || 67 ||
svam apy antaḥpuraṃ naiva paritrātaṃ hi viṣṇunā | 
trāṇāya sarvasattvānāṃ tvaṃ vibho nityam udyataḥ || 68 ||
jarāsandhabhayaṃ jñātvā mathurāṃ tyaktavān hariḥ | 
sattvo naivāsty asau kaścid bibhiyās tvaṃ yato mune || 69 ||
anayād vilayaṃ yātā vāsudevasya bāndhavāaḥ | 
tvayā tu bāndhavāḥ sarve saṃsārād vipramocitāḥ || 70 ||
matāni paratīrthyānāṃ vimṛśāmi yathā yathā | 
tathā tathā me tvayy eva nātha cittaṃ prasīdati || 71 ||
ity asarvajñasiddhānte doṣavigrāhitāśayāḥ | 
nirdoṣam api śāstāraṃ tvāṃ na paśyanty abuddhayaḥ || 72 ||
loko 'bravīd amī śākyā vedadevadvijadviṣaḥ | 
svakam evāparādhaṃ tu lokaḥ kiṃ nāvabudhyate || 73 ||
yataḥ sukhaṃ sukhenaiva labhyate tava śāsane | 
atas te vādiśārdūla jano 'yaṃ ramate mate || 74 ||
bhūtān prabhāṣamāṇasya vidhinānena te guṇān | 
yat puṇyam asti me tena jagad astu tvayā samam || 75 ||
viśeṣastavam uccārya yan mayāsāditaṃ śubham | 
tena sarvaṃ jagan nātha bhūyād bodhiparāyaṇam || 76 ||

iti viśeṣastavaḥ samāptaḥ || bhaṭṭarādhasvāminaḥ ||

Title: Devātiśayastotra (a.k.a. Devatāvimarśastuti)
Author: Śaṃkarasvāmin (var. )
Source: Schneider, Johannes. 2014. Eine buddhistische Kritik der indischen Götter: Śaṃkarasvāmins Devātiśayastotra mit Prajñāvarmans Kommentar, Nach dem tibetischen Tanjur herausgegeben und übersetzt. Wiener Studien zur Tibetologie und Buddhismuskunde Heft 81. Wien: Arbeitskreis für tibetische und buddhistische Studien Universität Wien. [Skt. updated ed. on pp. 163-167] 
Tibetan: D 1112 
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó, Budapest 2023.12.26

oṃ namaḥ śrīsamyaksaṃbuddhasvāmine || 

pratyakṣato na bhagavān sugato na viṣṇur 
ālokyate na ca haro na hiraṇyagarbhaḥ | 
teṣāṃ tu rūpacaritātiśayaprabhāvaṃ 
śrutvā vicārayati ko guṇavān na veti || 1 ||
viṣṇuḥ samudyatagadāyudharaudrapāṇiḥ 
śaṃbhur vilagnanṛśirograkapālamālī | 
ekāntaśāntacaritātiśayas tu buddhaḥ 
kaṃ pūjayāma upaśāntam aśāntarūpam || 2 ||
duryodhanādikulanāśakaro babhūva 
viṣṇur haras tripuranāśakaraḥ kilāsīt | 
krauñcaṃ guho 'pi dṛḍhaśaktihataṃ cakāra 
buddhas tu kevalam ayaṃ jagato hitaiṣī || 3 ||
pīḍyo mamāyam ayam eva ca rakṣaṇīyo 
vadhyo 'yam ity api surottamanītir eṣā | 
niḥśreyasasābhyudayasaukhyahitaikabuddheḥ 
buddhasya naiva ripavo na ca vañcanīyāḥ || 4 ||
rāgādidoṣajanakāni vacāṃsi viṣṇor 
unmattaceṣṭitakarāṇi ca yāni śambhoḥ | 
niḥśeṣadoṣaśamakāni tathāgatasya 
vandyatvam arhati na ko 'tra vicārayadhvam || 5 ||
yaś codyataḥ paravadhāya ghṛṇāṃ vihāya 
trāṇāya yaś ca kṛpayā vidhiṣu pravṛttaḥ | 
rāgī ca yo bhavati yaś ca vimuktarāgaḥ 
pūjyas tayoḥ ka iha taṃ vadatānucintya || 6 || 
śakraṃ vajradharaṃ balaṃ haladharaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ sacakrāyudhaṃ 
skandaṃ śaktiharaṃ śmaśānanilayaṃ rudraṃ triśūlāyudham | 
etān duḥkhabhayārtitān gataghṛṇān bālān vicitrāyudhān 
nityaṃ prāṇivadhodyatapraharān kas tān namasyed budhaḥ || 7 ||
na yaḥ śūlaṃ dhatte na ca yuvatim aṅke suvadanāṃ 
na cakraṃ śaktiṃ vā na ca kuliśam ugraṃ na ca halam | 
vinirmuktaṃ kleśaiḥ parahitavidhānodyatadhiyaṃ 
śaraṇyaṃ lokānāṃ tam ṛṣim upayāto 'smi śaraṇam || 8 ||
rudro rāgavaśāt striyaṃ vahati yo – – hriyā varjito 
viṣṇuḥ krūrataraḥ kṛtaghnacaritaḥ skandaḥ svayaṃ jñātihā | 
krūromā mahiṣāntakṛn naravasāmāṃsāśanā mātaraḥ 
pānepsī ca vināyako daśabale svalpo 'pi doṣo 'sti kaḥ || 9 ||
bandhur na me sa bhagavān ripavo na cānye 
sākṣān na dṛṣṭacara ekataro 'pi caiṣām | 
śrutvā vacaḥsucaritātiśayaprabhāvaṃ 
buddhaṃ guṇātiśayalolatayā śritāḥ smaḥ || 10 ||
	% NB also in *Saddharmaparikathā! 
nāsmākaṃ sugataḥ pitā na ripavas tīrthyā dhanaṃ naiva no 
dattaṃ tena tathāgatena na hṛtaṃ kiṃcit kaṇādādibhiḥ | 
kiṃ tv ekāntajagaddhitaḥ sa bhagavān buddho yataś cāmalaṃ 
vākyaṃ sarvamalāpahāri ca yatas tadbhaktimanto vayam || 11 ||
hitaiṣī yo nityaṃ satatam upakārī ca jagataḥ 
kṛtaṃ yena svāsthyaṃ bahuvidharujārtasya jagataḥ | 
sphuṭaṃ yasya jñeyaṃ karatalam ivāvaiti sakalaṃ 
prapadyadhvaṃ santaṃ tam ṛṣim asamaṃ bhaktimanasaḥ || 12 ||
asarvabhāvena yadṛcchayā vā 
parānuvṛttyā vicikitsayā vā | 
ye taṃ namasyanti munīndracandraṃ 
te 'py āmarīṃ saṃpadam āpnuvanti || 13 ||
	% NB parallel to śāṭhyotpāditasyāpi etc. 
paurāṇī śrutir eṣa lokamahito buddhaḥ kilāyaṃ harir 
dṛṣṭvā janmajarāvināśavaśagaṃ lokaṃ kṛpābhyudyataḥ | 
jātaḥ śākyakule vare 'dbhutamatis trātā nṛṇāṃ gautamaḥ 
śāstāraṃ hitam eva kas tam adhunā nāvaiti mūḍho janaḥ || 14 ||
	% NB ref to being Viṣṇu's avatāra 
yadā rāgadveṣād asurasuraratnāpaharaṇaṃ 
kṛtaṃ māyānvītaṃ dharaṇiharaṇāsaktamatinā | 
tadā pūjyo vandyo harir api na mukto 'budhatayā 
vinirmuktaṃ buddhaṃ na namati jano mohabahulaḥ || 15 ||
caturjaladhimekhalākulanitambabhārālasāṃ 
visṛjya haraye mahīṃ sa balir āpa kṛṣṭāṃ daśām | 
pradāya munaye tu pāṃśulavam apy aśoko nṛpaḥ 
kṣitiṃ sakalacandramaṇḍalakarāmbarāṃ prāptavān || 16 ||
pakṣapāto na me buddhe na dveṣaḥ kapilādiṣu | 
yuktimac ca vaco yasya śāstus tasya parigrahaḥ || 17 ||
avaśyam evaṃ katamo 'pi sarvavij 
jagaddhitaikāntaviśālaśāsanaḥ | 
sa eva mṛgyo matisūkṣmacakṣuṣāṃ 
sa cānusevyaḥ kim anarthapaṇḍitaiḥ || 18 ||
yasya ca na santi nikhilā doṣāḥ sarve ca guṇāś ca vidyante | 
brahmā vā viṣṇur vā maheśvaro vā sa me śāstā || 19 ||
yasya doṣā na vidyante vidyante cāmitā guṇāḥ | 
sarvajñaś ca kṛpāluś ca tam ahaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ || 20 ||
evaṃ stute stutisahasranadīsamudre 
buddha vibuddhakamalāyatapattranetre | 
yat kiṃcid asti kuśalaṃ mama vāksamutthaṃ 
sattvā bhavantu sukhinaḥ . . tena sarve || 21 ||

ācāryaśaṃkarasvāmipādakṛtā devatāvimarśastutiḥ samāptā ||

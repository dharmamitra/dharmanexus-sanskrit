Title: Caṇḍamahāroṣaṇatantra
Author: canonical
Source: NGMPP A 136/5
Notes: the only ms. thus far to have additional chapters 26-32. However, there is almost nothing new here inasmuch as Ch. 26 = Raktayamāritantra = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 8 (the homa section also in a Kurukullāsādhana); Ch. 27 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 9; Ch. 28 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 10 \& series of mantras; Ch. 29 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 11; Ch. 30 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 12; Ch. 31 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 13; Ch. 32 = Kṛṣṇayamāritantra 14.

atha bhagavān āha || 

śṛṇu vajradharo rājā rakṣācakraṃ prayogataḥ | 
vighnān vināyakān duṣṭān nintikūtiṃ nāyacābravīt || 
ahaṃ khaḍgadharo rājā rakṣācakraṃ prayogataḥ | 
khaḍgenādīptavappuṣyā sphālayāmi trikāyajām ||
tvaṃ devī sākṣibhūtāsi sarvabuddhānutāyinām | 
yamaghno maṇḍalācāryo maṇḍalaṃ lekhayāmy aham ||
kācchikena mukhaṃ baddhvā maṇḍalāgāradvārataḥ | 
kas tvaṃ bho iti brūyāt subhago 'ham iti bruvīt || 
yamanikābhyantaraṃ dattvā prārthayed gurave sudhīḥ |
abhiṣekārtham ābhaktyā trir uccāryānuprārthayet ||
yathā buddhair mahādharmair vajrasattvābhiṣekataḥ | 
mamāpi trāṇanārthāya varaṃ cāse prayacchatu ||
gītavādyaṃ tathā pūjām arghapādyaṃ tathaiva ca |
ḍhaukayed gauravāc chiṣyaḥ stutiṃ vā tatra kārayet ||
atha caṇḍamahāroṣaṃ śūnyamantram udāharet | 
oṃ śūnyatājñānavajrasvabhāvātmako 'ham |
oṃ sarvatathāgatakāyavākcittasvabhāvātmako 'ham |
oṃ suviśuddhadharmadhātusvabhāvātmako 'ham | 
oṃ namo 'stu kṛṣṇācalāya sarvamāraṃ jambhaya hūṃ phaṭ ||

bhagavān āha ||

atha homaṃ pravakṣyāmi śāntikādiprabhedataḥ | 
yena homena siddhiḥ syāt sarvakarmaprasādhinī ||
śāntike śānticittena pauṣṭike puṣṭicetasā | 
vaśye cotkaṇṭhacittena udvignena tu māraṇe ||
śāntike maṇḍalākāraṃ bāhyākāraṃ tu pauṣṭike | 
vaśyake cārdhacandrākhyaṃ khadhātum iva māraṇe ||
hastāyāmaṃ bhavec chāntau dvihastaṃ pauṣṭike tathā | 
yathā puṣṭau tathā vaśye māraṇe viṃśadaṅgulam || 
hastārdhaṃ vedhayec chāntau hastamātraṃ tu pauṣṭike | 
dvipañca māraṇaṃ guṣṭhaṃ yathā puṣṭau tathā vaśe ||
pratipat syantikaṃ homaṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ tu pauṣṭikam | 
abhicāre caturdaśyām aṣṭamyā vaśyakarmaṇi || 

ity ekallavīrākhye śrīcaṇḍamahāroṣaṇatantre homapaṭalaḥ ṣaḍviṃśatitamaḥ ||   ||

atha bhagavān āha || 

samudramṛttikāṃ gṛhya nāgam ekaṃ tu kārayet | 
oṃphuṃkāraṃ hṛdi nyasyaḥ hrīṃḥṣṭyādimantram ājapet ||
anāvṛṣṭisamaya 'yutajāpena pātayet | 
kṛṣṇasarpasya māṃsāni nimbapattreṇa miśrayet ||
tenaiva varttikāṃ kṛtvā samudre prakṣiped vratī | 
ayutamātrajāpena ūrmistambhanam uttamam ||
caṇḍabījaśataṃ gṛhya māṣataṇḍulamiśritam | 
aṣṭalakṣaprajaptena homād eva tu dṛśyatām ||
añjanena śmaśānasya puttalīṃ kārayed vratī | 
unmattarasalepenāyutamātreṇa kuṣyate || 
catvāri lakṣajāpena siddhārthabījasaṃcayaiḥ | 
suptakotthāpanaṃ kāryaṃ jīvase na ca saṃśayaḥ ||

oṃ vighnāntakṛt vajrakrodha hayagrīva hūru 2 hūṃ phaṭ |
oṃ caṇḍamahāroṣaṇāya trāṃ hāṃ māṃ hāṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
oṃ namaḥ kṛṣṇācalāya sarvamāra jambhaya 2 hūṃ phaṭ | 
oṃ namaḥ śvetācalāya sarvaduṣṭa mohaya 2 hūṃ phaṭ | 
oṃ namaḥ pītācalāya sarvabhaya nāśaya 2 hūṃ phaṭ | 
oṃ namo raktācalāya sarvaśatrumukha bandhaya 2 hūṃ phaṭ |
oṃ namaḥ śyāmācalāya sarvabhūtabhaya nāśaya 2 hūṃ phaṭ |
oṃ namaḥ śrīcaṇḍamahāroṣaṇāya hana 2 daha 2 paca 2 matha 2 sarvabhrāṭa amogha caṇḍamahāroṣaṇa hūṃ phaṭ ||   ||

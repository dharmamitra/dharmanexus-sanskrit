Title: Sattvārādhanastava
Author: Nāgārjuna 
Source: cobbled together from Lévi 1929, pp. 264–265 (based on NAK 3-715 = NGMPP B 23/8) and a ms. fragment identified by Szántó (NAK 5-135 = NGMPP B 24/56); new edition by Cheung forthcoming, renumeration according to this 
Tibetan: D 1125, 4516 
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó, 17/07/2025 Budapest

sattvārtha eva mama bhaktir abhaktir anyā 
sattveṣu tasya mayi yo na kṛpāṃ jahāti | 
tyaktvā kṛpāṃ vicarataḥ skhalitāni yāni 
proddhartum arhati kṛpaiva tu tāni nānyaḥ || 1

tenārcito 'smi sa ca śāsanadhūrdaro me 
sattveṣu yasya karuṇānugatā pravṛttiḥ 
śīlaṃ śrutaṃ ca karuṇā ca matiś ca paṭvī 
yasyāsti tena sugato 'rcita eva nityam || 2

sattvopakāram adhikṛtya gato 'smi siddhiṃ
sattvārtham eva tanum eṣa samudvahāmi |
sattvān hinasti manasāpi hi yaḥ sa kasmān 
mām eva saṃśrayati yo mayi nirvyapekṣaḥ || 3 
	% nirvipekṣaḥ in ms. frag.

pūjā tu sā bhavati sattvahitekṣaṇāpi
pūjyasya yā manasi tuṣṭim upādadāti |
hiṃsātmikā paraviheṭhanasambhavā vā
pūjā na pūjyam anugacchati saṃskṛtāpi || 4 

dārāḥ sutāś ca vibhavāś ca mahattvarājyaṃ
māṃsāni śoṇitavaśe nayane śarīram |
yeṣāṃ priyatvam adhikṛtya mayojjhitāni
yas tān viheṭhayati tena viheṭhito 'ham || 5 

sattvopakāraparamā hi mamāgrapūjā
sattvāpakāraparamaś ca parābhavaḥ syāt |
duḥkhaṃ sukhaṃ ca samasattvasamānam iṣṭaṃ 
sattveṣu yaḥ praharate + + + + + + || 6 
	% 6cd om. Lévi 

sattvān prāpya mayā kṛtāni kuśalāny ārādhitās tāyinaḥ
prāptāḥ pāramitāś ca sattvasamiter evārtham ātiṣṭhatā |
sattvārthena samudyatena manasā mārasya bhagnaṃ balam
sattvair eva tathā tathā viracitaṃ yenāsmi buddhaḥ kṛtaḥ || 7

kasmin vastuni sidhyatām iha kṛpā maitrī ca kvālambyatāṃ 
kvopekṣāmuditādivastuviṣayāḥ kasmin vimokṣadayaḥ |
kasyārthe karuṇāpareṇa manasā kṣāntiś ciram bhāvitā
na syur janmani janmani priyavidhau mitraṃ yadi prāṇinaḥ || 8

sattvā eva gajādibhāvagatayo dattā mayānekaśaḥ
sattvā eva ca pātratām upagatā deyaṃ mayā grāhitāḥ |
sattvair eva vicitrabhāvagamanād asmatkṛpā vardhitā
sattvān eva na pālayāmi yadi cet kasyārtham arthaḥ kṛtaḥ || 9 

saṃsāre vyasanābhipātabahule na syur yadi prāṇino 
janmāvartaviḍambanena yamalokaṃ prāpya sātmīkṛtāḥ |
saṃsārāt taraṇaṃ ca saugatam idaṃ māhātmyam atyadbhutaṃ
kasyārthena samīhitaṃ yadi na me sattvā bhaveyuḥ priyāḥ || 10

yāvac cedaṃ jvalati jagataḥ śāsanaṃ śāsanaṃ me
tāvat stheyaṃ parahitaparair ātmavadbhir bhavadbhiḥ |
śrutvā śrutvā ca mama vicaritaṃ sattvahetor akhinnaiḥ
khedaḥ kāryo na ca tanum imām uktasārāṃ bhavadbhiḥ || 11 
	% anne: vahadbhiḥ

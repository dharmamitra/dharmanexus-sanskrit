Title: *Trikāyastava
Author: Nāgārjuna 
Source: unpublished transcript with notes by John Newman posted on academia.edu 
Tibetan: D 1123 

yo naiko nāpy anekaḥ svaparahitamahāsampadādhārabhūto
naivābhāvo na bhāvaḥ kham iva samaraso durvibhāvyasvabhāvaḥ |
nirlepaṃ nirvikāraṃ śivam asamasamaṃ vyāpinaṃ niṣprapañcaṃ 
vande pratyātmavedyaṃ tam aham anupamaṃ dharmakāyaṃ jinānām || 1

lokātītam acintyaṃ sukṛtaśataphalām ātmano yo vibhūtiṃ 
parṣanmadhye vicitrāṃ prathayati mahatīṃ dhīmatāṃ prītahetoḥ |
buddhānāṃ sarvalokaprasṛtam aviratodārasaddharmaghoṣaṃ
vande saṃbogakāyaṃ tam aham iha mahādharmarājyapratiṣṭham || 2

sattvānāṃ pākahetoḥ kvacid anala ivābhāti yo dīpyamānaḥ 
sambodhau dharmacakre kvacid api ca punar dṛśyate yaḥ praśāntaḥ |
naikākārapravṛttaṃ tribhavabhayaharaṃ viśvarūpair upāyair
vande nirmāṇakāyaṃ daśadiganugataṃ tan mahārthaṃ munīnām || 3

sattvārthaikakriyāṇām aparimitamahājñānapuṇyanayānāṃ 
kāyānāṃ saugatānāṃ prativigatamanovākpathānāṃ trayāṇām | 
kṛtvā bhaktyā praṇāmaṃ kuśalam upacitaṃ yan mayā bodhibījaṃ 
trīn kāyāṃs tena labdhvā jagad idam akhilaṃ bodhimārge niyuñje || 4

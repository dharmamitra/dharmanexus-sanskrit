*bhāvanāvivekaḥ (Edited by Sri V.Aṛamaswami Sastri and Sri K.Aṣivaramakrishna Sastri)

*1ab saṃsargamohitadhiyo viviktaṃ dhātugocarāt/
*1cd bhāvātmānaṃ na paśyanti ye tebhyas sa vivicyate//
	tathāhy āhuḥ --

*2ab sāmānādhikaraṇyāt tadarthabhedābhidhānumā/
*2cd na tādarthyānumā nāha vṛkṣatāṃ śiṃśapāśrutiḥ//
	iha khalu gaganaśaśaviṣāṇayor akriyamāṇatvād abhavatoḥ bhavataś ca ghaṭādes tattvād bhavatyarthasya kartuḥ karotikarmatā/ tathā ca bhavituḥ karotyarthasya kartāṃ prayojaka iti bhavateḥ prayojakavyāpāreṇijutpadyamānaḥ karotyartham avalambate/ tena sā bhāvanā/ karotisamānādhikaraṇāś ca pacatyādaya iti teṣāṃ tādarthyānumānam/
	tadasat/ ekādhikaraṇatā hi tadarthabhedābhidhānanimittaṃ naikārthyam avagamayati/ tādṛśos sahaprayogānupapatteḥ/ na khalu vṛkṣatā śiṃśapādiśabdagocaraḥ, api tv abhidheyapariprāprtatayā gamyamānā/ tad evam aikādhikaraṇyaṃ sphuṭaṃ viparyayasādhanam/ viśeṣābhidhānonnayanam evettham iti cet -- na, tasyāḥ pratyayārthatvenābhyupagamāt/ pratyayasya caikatve vācakabhedādṛte vācyanānātvāfyogāt/ dhātavas tu nānātmānaḥ karotisamānādhikaraṇatayā tadarthaviśeṣābhidhāyina iti sāmpratam/
	api ca --

*3ab bhāvābhāve prayogasya dvidhāpi khalu darśanāt/
*3cd dhātvarthavartisāmānyaṃ karotyartham upāgamat//
	kiṃ karoti ghaṇṭā? dhvanatīti na dhvanijanyamanyat/ sad api vā na vivakṣitam, dhātvarthamātrajijñāsanāt, tathā ca nābhūtaprādurbhāvanam/ ato vastutas tadabhāve 'pi prayujyamānasya karoter iha kutas tadarthaḥ/
	tathā kā kriyā? pākaḥ iti dhātvarthamātrābhidhānāt bhāvanāyā aparāmarśān na bhāvanāviśeṣanimittam aikādhikaraṇyam/ gamyamānāpekṣam iti cet -- na, tiṅanteṣv api prasaṅgāt/ prādhānyāt tiṅanteṣv abhidheyatvam iti cet -- na, ihāpi tadviśeṣapraśnād dhātvarthasya tallakṣaṇārthatvāt prādhānyam abhinnam/ ataḥ sakaladhātvarthānuyāyinaḥ karoteḥ tatsāmānyam artho nābhūtaprādurbhāvanam iti śabdapravṛttividaḥ pratipedire/ tathā cocuḥ -- "kriyāvacanā dhātavaḥ/ kathaṃ jñāyate kriyāvacanāḥ pacatyādayaḥ iti? yadeṣāṃ karotinā sāmānādhikaraṇyam/ kṛbhvastayaḥ kriyāsāmānyavacanāḥ, kriyāviśeṣavacanāś ca pacatyādayaḥ"//
	atha mataṃ na bhāvarahito dhātvarthaḥ/ sarveṇa tena kiṃ cid asad upajanyate iti/ tan na/

*4ab sarvatra yadi dhātvarthebhyāvyaṃ saivāstu bhāvanā/
*4cd svargabhūtisamartho 'nyo vyāpāraḥ ko 'nupāgataḥ//
	bhāvyabhavanasamartho hi vyāpāro bhāvanā/ dhātvartha eva cet kasya cid ātmalābhānukūlātmā, sa eva bhāvanā astu, na tadatiricyamānaśarīrā/
	evaṃ caitat; na khalu svargādiphalodayānukūlaṃ yāgādidhātvarthabhedaprabhāvyaṃ vyāpārāntaram upalabhāmahe yadbhāvanāśabdo 'bhiniviśeta/ yāgādaya eva tu svargādiphalodayāya coditās tatsamarthatayā tathāvyapadeśam arhati/ avyāpārarūpatvād dhātvarthasya naivam, iti yadi manyeta, vaktavyo vyāpāraḥ -- yadi samīhitakāryopajananānuguṇaḥ sādhyasvabhāvo dharmabhedaḥ tadapekṣayā tathā vyapadeśyaḥ, na dhātvarthas tathāvidhatvam ativartate iti/ atha parispandaḥ, tadā tadātmanāṃ tāvat dhātvarthānām avihataṃṃ bhāvanātvam/
	aspandamānakartṛkeṣu "āste" "yajate" "jānāti" iti cātmakartṛkeṣu tadabhāvaprasaṅgaḥ/

*5ab ātmakartṛkam evāha vyāpāraṃ yo 'pi bhāvanām/
*5cd kartrantare 'sya nākhyātaṃ dhīsāmyān nopacārataḥ//
	yo 'pi manyate sakalapuruṣapratyātmavedanīyaḥ cetanasyātmana audāsīnyapracyutyupalakṣyamāṇātmā prayatnaśabdasaṃvedanīyo vyāpāro bhāvanā, kartrantare 'sya nākhyātaṃ prayujyeta -- "kūlaṃ patati", "ratho gacchati", "agnir jvalati" iti/ na ca "śṛṇota grāvāṇaḥ" itivatsamāropeṇa prayogaḥ, pratyasyāvailakṣaṇyāt/ na hi "ratho gacchati" "karī gacchati" iti pratyayo viśiṣyate/
	api ca --

*6ab dhātugocaranirvṛttiparyantatvāc ca karmatā/
*6cd sarvatra tasya na paraḥ karaṇābhimate 'py asau//
	puruṣaprayatno hi dhātvarthanirvṛttimātrāpavargī; sarvatra kāmye naimittike ca tatkarmatāprasaṅgaḥ, vyāpāraviśeṣānupalambhāt/ na hi kāmye 'pi prayoge karaṇābhimatasya dhātvarthasya parastāt tatsādhyasvargādiphalaviśeṣasādhanaḥ prayatnabhedaḥ samasti/
	apūrvannāma na prayatnabhedaḥ, na kartṛvyāpāraḥ, karaṇavyāpāratvābhyupagamāt/ anyathā vyāpārasaṅkarāt kārakabhedābhāvaprasaṅgād ity alam anena//

*7ab karmaṇo bhāvabhedaṃ yaḥ phalāvadhitayā vadet/
*7cd tasyādṛṣṭaphaleṣv iṣṭam anuṣṭhānaṃ na yuktimat//
	dhātur hi karma svarūpamātreṇopādatte/ ākhyātopasaṃdhānāt tu bhāvanātmanā avagamyate/ tathā hi -- yāvatā karmakṣaṇotpādanicayeneṣṭam arthaṃ sādhayati tāvatyākhyātaṃ prayujyate/ atas tena tena phalenāvacchidyamānaḥ sa sa karmakṣaṇotpādanicayaḥ tena tenākhyātenābhidhīyate/ tad idaṃ phalāvadhikaṃ karmaṃ bhāvyatayā avasīyate, nābhāvyamānaṃ phalabhāvanāyālam iti/ phalotpādikā avasthā ākhyātārthaḥ/ "tathā yateta yathā yatkiñcid bhavati" iti darśitam/ evaṃ avasthābhedena bhāvanākarmaṇor bhedaḥ/ uktaṃ ca -- "pūrvāparībhūtaṃ bhāvam ākhyātenācaṣṭe vrajati pacatīty upakramaprabhṛtyapavargaparyantam" iti// tatra pratikṣaṇam apavargavataḥ karmaṇaḥ kāv upakramāpavargau? phalāpekṣayā tu kalpyete iti yo vadet, tasya pratyakṣaphaleṣu karmasu kāmam ārambhavirāmau kalpyete, puraḥ parastāc ca tatparyantakarmakṣaṇapracayaparijñānāt/ adṛṣṭaphaleṣu tv anirūpitaphalaparyantaparimāṇabhedeṣu kathaṃ pravartate? na hi niyukto 'py aprasiddhaviṣayaviśeṣaḥ pravartituṃ prabhavati/ pravṛttau vā puraḥ phalādarśanāt akṛtaśāstrārtham ātmānam avabudhyamāno na virontum arhati/ śāstrārthasya phalotpatteḥ, phalotpattyā ca kṛtaśāstrārthatvād itaretarāśrayaprasaṅgād ity alaṃ prasaṅgena/
	prakutam anusarāmaḥ --

*8ab dhātupratyayayor vācye pratīyete na hi kriye/
*8cd saṃbandho nāvakalpeta dvayoḥ sādhyābhidhāyinoḥ//
	"yeṣān tūtpattau" ity ākhyātavartī dhātvarthaḥ sādhyaḥ kriyātmopeyate/ yadi ca pratyayaḥ kriyāntaram ācakṣīta, kriyādvayaṃ pratīyeta -- odanaṃ pacati, karoti ghaṭam iti yathā/ na ca tathā pratīyate/ tasmān na dhātvarthavyatirekiṇī ākhyāteṣu kriyā asti/
	sādhyasya ca sādhyāntareṇa samatvād asaṃbandhatā/ tataś ca tadabhidhāyinoḥ prakṛtipratyayayoḥ samabhivyāhārānupapattiḥ/ karaṇatvād dhātvarthasyānupālambha iti cet -- na; "yeṣān tūtpattau" iti virodhāt/ na hi tadbhāvanābhiprāyam; nāmapadārthasya dhātvarthasaṃbandhe dṛṣṭārthatā, apekṣitasaṃbandhapratipādanārthatvāt/
	api ca --

*9ab na bhāve nāpi dhātvarthe kāṣṭhair iti samāgamaḥ/
*9cd akriyātvād anyarodhāc chābdatvāt karaṇasya ca//
	na tāvad dhātvarthasaṃbandhitā kāṣṭhānām, karaṇānāṃ kriyāpekṣiṇāṃ atadātmanā karaṇāntareṣṇa saṃgamāyogāt/ nāpi bhāvanāsaṃbandhitā, tasyāḥ karaṇāntaroparuddhatvāt/ nāpy aṃśāntaratayā saṃbandhaḥ, śābdatvāt karaṇatvasya/ tad evaṃ yatra karaṇabhāvaḥ kriyāyāṃ tasyāḥ avaruddhatvāt, dhātvarthe ca akriyātmanyakaraṇatvāt kāṣṭhaiḥ pacatīti saṃbandhābhāvaḥ/ arthakṣiptabhāvanādvārakaḥ saṃbandha iti cet -- na; avyavadhānena saṃbandhabodhanāt/ na hi mṛdā ghaṭam, kāṣṭhaiḥ pacatīti samaḥ saṃbandhāvagamaḥ, ekatra nairākāṅkṣyāt, anyatrānyāpekṣaṇāt//

*10ab viklidyanti pacantīti na viśeṣaḥ prakalpyate/
*10cd kaḥ pratyayārtho dhātūktavyāpāro yadi kartṛtā//
	api ca -- "viklidyanti pacanti" ity ubhayatra prayojakavyāpārasya pratyayenābhidhānam, dhātubhyāṃ ca viklitter iti nārthabhedaḥ syāt/ nanv asti tāvat kartṛbhedena vyāpārabhedaḥ tadabhidhānān na doṣaḥ/ tathā hi -- viklidyantīti taṇḍulāḥ kartāraḥ, pacantīti devadattādayaḥ/ kiṃ punarbhavān kartṛlakṣaṇaṃ matvaivam āha -- yadi sādhananiyogādikam, kathaṃ taṇḍulānāṃ kartṛtvam? devadattādaya eva kartāra ity aviśeṣaprasaṅgaḥ/ athāguṇato dhātunā abhidhīyamānavyāpāratā, ka idānīṃ pratyayārthaḥ? kartṛvyāpārasya bhāvanāyā dhātunaivābhidhānāt/ phaladhātvarthārthabhāvanānātvād dhātvarthārtho vyāpāro dhātuvācyaḥ, phalārthas tu dhātvarthakaraṇakaḥ pratyayavācyaḥ/ naitat sāram/

*11ab na hi dhātvarthakaraṇaphalabhedaprabhāvanaḥ/
*11cd dhātvarthaṃ phalabhedaṃ ca vyāpāro dṛśyate 'ntarā//
	yo 'pi vyavahitaphaleṣu dṛśyate, so 'pi dhātvarthāntarātmakaḥ prayatnāntarajanmā, na prācīnadhātugocaraprabhāvanīya iti/

*12ab karmaṇā ca karotyartho vyāpto 'tas tadayogataḥ/
*12cd tadabhāvaḥ pratyayārthasādhane 'pi vibhaktayaḥ//
	karoteḥ sadā sakarmakatvāt tadyogaviraheṇa karotyarthābhāvaṃ pratīmaḥ jvalanaviraheṇeva dhūmābhāvam/ tathā hi -- na jātu "yajeta svargam", "śayīta svāsthyam" "āsīta sukham" iti dṛṣṭacaraḥ sasargaḥ/
	yadi manvīta saṅgatisamarthavibhaktibhedābhāvād evam/ tathā hi -- dhātvarthasādhanāvagāhinyaḥ subvibhaktayaḥ, pratyayārthakarmaṇi niyojye svargakāmādau tadadarśanāt/
	tac ca vārtam; evaṃ sati śeṣasaṃbandhahetoḥ ṣaṣṭyā avyāhataprasaratvāt/ api ca pratyayārthabhāvanākaraṇadhātvarthanāmadheyebhyo viśvajidādibhyaḥ karaṇavibhaktimīkṣamāṇo nedam adhyavasātum arhati/ asty eva karmasaṃbandho "yajeta svargakāmaḥ" iti cet -- na; puruṣaviśeṣaṇatvena(tvāt) sākṣādasaṃbandhāt/ "svargāya yajeta" iti sākṣātsaṅgatir iti cet -- na; akarmarūpatvāt, karmarūpasaṃbandhābhāvasya ca bhāvanābhidhānābhāvahetutvāt/ akarmatā ca caturthīnirdeśāt/ "svargāya yāgaḥ" iti ca dhātvarthenāpi saṃbandhāt/

*13ab tulyārthatvaṃ bhāvayati bhavatīti prasajyate/
*13cd kaḥ pratyayārtho dhātūktavyāpāre khalu kartari//
	"bhāvayati ghaṭaṃ", "kumbhakāraḥ", "bhavati ghaṭam" ity api prasajyeta, tulyārthatvāt/ tathā hi -- ekasya(tra) kartuḥ kumbhakārasya vyāpāraṃ ṇijācaṣṭe, anyatrākhyātapratyaya iti na bhidyate 'rthaḥ/ prayojakavyāpāro ṇijarthaḥ, kartṛvyāpāra ākhyātārthaḥ iti nānārthatvaṃ yo manyate, vaktavyas teneha kartṛprayojakabhedaḥ/ yadi sādhanānāṃ pravartayitā nivartayitā ca kartā, sa caikaḥ kumbhakāra eveti tadvyāpārābhidhānāt tulyārthatvam/ ghaṭaḥ kartā, prayojakaḥ kumbhakāraḥ iti cet -- katham anīśituḥ sādhanapravṛttinivṛttyor ghaṭasya kartṛtvaṃ tādṛśaṃ kulālam atikramya/ athāguṇato dhātunā abhidhīyamānavyāpāratvam -- kas tarhi idānīm ākhyātārthaḥ? dhātunā abhihitatvāt tadvyāpārasya/ na ca vyāpārabhedadarśanam, yena dhātupratyayayor viṣayavibhāgaḥ syāt/ karaṇatvaṃ ca śabdato 'nyato vā dhātvarthasyobhayatrāpy aviśiṣṭam iti durvāraṃ tulyārthatvam/ ato na pratyayo bhāvanāvacana iti sūktam -- "ṇyantasya bhavater arthaḥ kenāṃśenābhidhīyate" iti//
	kiñ ca --

*14ab kartṛvyāpāra iṣṭaś cet pacyādāv api bhāvanā/
*14cd dhātvarthāntararūpaḥ syāt so 'py adhiśrayaṇādikaḥ//
	yadi ca kartṛvyāpāro bhāvanā; sa ca pacyādiṣv adhiśrayaṇādikaḥ, taṃ pacir evopādatte, anyathā sidhyativiklidyatibhyām aviśeṣaḥ syāt/
	athāpi nopādadīta, tathāpy ādhiśrayatyādidhātvantarārtha iti nānyo dhātvarthād bhāvanāpadārthaḥ/ atha mataṃ -- karotīty anvitābhāsapratyayaviṣayatayā anuvṛttātmā vyāvṛttāvagrahagrahaṇagocarebhyo dhātvarthebhyo 'nyo bhāvanāpadārtha iti -- tad asat/ evaṃ hi sāmānyalakṣaṇānupātitayā dhātvarthasāmānyam eva syāt, na padārthāntaraṃ bhāvanāpadārthaḥ/
	atha mataṃ -- satyaṃ sādhyatayā sāmānyam, na gotvādivattadrūpatayā/ tasyāḥ pratipuruṣaṃ śīghūṃ karoti, mandaṃ karotīti svasāmānyaviśeṣābhyāṃ yogāt/ dhātvarthavarti tu karaṇarūpaṃ sāmānyam anyad eva/
	uktaṃ hi --
	"anyad eva hi dhātvarthasāmānyaṃ karaṇātmakam/
	anyac ca bhāvanā nāma sādhyatvena vyavasthitam//" iti//
	tad etadapeśalam/ tathā hi --

*15ab na kriyākaraṇākāre dve jñāne anuyāyinī/
*15cd yataḥ pratīmaḥ sāmānye dve sādhyasamavāyinī//
	na hi karaṇaṃ karaṇam iti kriyākārakavyatirikte 'rthe pacyādiṣu pratyayaḥ, yataḥ kriyārūpavyatiriktaṃ karaṇātmakaṃ dhātvarthasāmānyaṃ kalpyeta/ śīghramandādiṣu tu dhātvarthasāmānyasyaiva karotivācyasyāvāntarasāmānyabhedaḥ śoṇakarkādir ivāśvatvasya/
	uktaṃ ca --
	"na hi dhātvarthakaraṇaḥ phalabhedaprabhāvanaḥ/
	dhātvarthaṃ phalabhedaṃ ca vyāpāro dṛśyate 'ntarā//" (iti)//

*16ab vācyo lakṣaṇabhedaś ca nanu dhātvarthabhāvayoḥ/
*16cd bhāvaḥ parispanda iti yajatyādāv asambhavaḥ//
	avaśyaṃ ca bhāvadhātvarthabhedavādinā tayoḥ spaṣṭo rūpabhedo darśayitavyaḥ/ aspandātmā dhātvarthaḥ, spandātmako bhāva iti cet, aspandamānakartṛkeṣu "yajate" "jānāti" ity ākhyātārtheṣu tadabhāvaprasaṅgaḥ/ gacchatyādau ca dhātvarthasya bhāvanātvaprasaṅgaḥ/ cetanavyāpāras tu purastātpratyuktaḥ/
	kaś caiṣa parispandaḥ? na khalv ayam āśrayābhimatād bhedena gamyate/ kathaṃ na gamyate? yadā saty apy āśraye pratyakṣe kadā cic calatīti pratyayaprādurbhāvaḥ, (ataḥ) tadatiriktārthālambanaḥ/
	naitat sāram --

*17ab kāryaṃ vibhāgasaṃyogalakṣaṇaṃ calatīti dhīḥ/
*17cd ālambatāṃ tato nānyo viśeṣo gamyate sthitāt//
	sa khalu parispando' kṣagocaraḥ pūrvottaradeśavibhāgasaṃyogaphalo 'bhyupagamyate/ tathā ca tāv eva gocarayatveṣa pratyayaḥ/ na khalu pūrvottaradeśahānopādāne apratipadyamānaś calatīti budhyate/ na ca sthitān nipuṇato 'pi nirūpayadbhir anyo viśeṣaḥ calatīti lakṣyate/
	nanu calitvā sthito 'pi saṃyogavibhāgavān eva, na calatīti gamyate/ anyatarakarmajanmanoś ca saṃyogavibhāgayor dviṣṭhatvenobhayatrakriyāpratyayaprasaṅgaḥ/ sthāṇuśyenayor vibhāgamātram, ekatra samuditayos taddhetutvam iti cet -- anyatarakarmajanmanor ubhayor api prasaṅgaḥ; yadā sthāṇur ekena śyenena saṃyujyate 'nyataś ca viyujyate/ na ca kramaviśeṣavantau tadālambanam/ puraḥsaṃyuktaśyenāntaravibhāgapurassare 'pi śyenāntara saṃyoge 'bhāvāt/
	ucyate --

*18ab acchinnotpattayo deśaviśeṣatyāgasaṅgamāḥ/
*18cd tadgocarāḥ padārthānām ākāśo deśa iṣyate//
	na khalu saṃyogavibhāgamātre calatīti pratyayaḥ/ api tv avicchinnotpādasaṃyogavibhāgaprabandhe/ sthite ca tadabhāvaḥ/ na ca sthāṇau deśaviśeṣaviṣayau saṃyogavibhāgau; saṃyogimātrasyādeśatvāt/ na hi śyenau sthāṇor deśāv iti pratiyanti laukikāḥ/ ākāśa eva tu sakaladeśipadārthānāṃ deśa iti pratīmaḥ/ atas tatpradeśasaṃyogavibhāgāv eva tadālambanam iti sthāṇau na prasaṅgaḥ//
	nanu vyomno 'pratyakṣatvād vāyuvanaspatisaṃyogavibhāgavattatsaṃyogavibhāgānāṃ pratyakṣāvṛttitvenānaindriyakatvād indriyajanmanaḥ pratyakṣasya viṣayatā na yuktā/
	na na yuktā/ anyathā --

*19ab kathaṃ gamyeta vayaso niyatā dhāradeśatā/
*19cd calato 'kṣātivṛttāś ced vihāyodeśasaṅgamāḥ//
	apratyakṣatve hi nabhovibhāgasaṃyogādīnāṃ kathaṃ patati patatriṇi deśabhedādhikaraṇajñānam? kriyayā kāraṇena kāryānumānād iti ced -- na; niyatadeśādhikaraṇapratīteḥ/ iha prāptaḥ, neha iti niyatapratyayo na deśaviśeṣasaṃyogāpratyakṣatāyām avakalpate/ viyadvitatālokanivahabhedād upapanna iti cet -- sa eva tarhy ākāśadeśa iti tadvartisaṃyogavibhāgau pratyakṣau/
	api ca -- pratyakṣāpratyakṣavṛttyor api saṃyogavibhāgayoḥ siddhayoḥ pratyakṣatvakalpanā yuktā, na tv asiddhasya karmaṇaḥ/ tābhyām eva tarhi saṃyogavibhāgābhyāṃ kriyām anumimīmahe, kādācitkayor ahetutvānupapatteḥ/ kādācitkatvād eva cāśrayamātrahetutvāyogāt hetvantarāpekṣaṇāt/
	naitat sāram; yataḥ --

*20ab nityatve sarvadā janma syāt saṃyogavibhāgayoḥ/
*20cd anityatve 'sya yo hetus tayor evāstu tena kim//

*23ab saṃyogāntaṃ vartamānaṃ tata unnīyate katham/

*24ab siddher guṇaviśeṣeṇa pacatīty api saṃvidaḥ/

*25ab yāgena bhāvayet svargam ity arthaḥ parikalpitaḥ/
*25cd svargakāmasya yāge tu niyogaḥ sampratīyate//

	tasmiṃs tv anuṣṭhite svargādiphalaṃ svato 'nyato vā kutaś cid bhaviṣyati, naiva vā; na tv ayam arthaḥ śakyo 'bhyupagantum/

	syāt tāvad eṣa nityeṣu parihāro nānityeṣu/ api cānarthakyaṃ syād ity anartho nāvagantuṃ śakyata ity uktam/ punarupadeśād vā svargakāmaḥ punaḥ prayokṣyata iti nānarthakyam/

*26ab buddhvaitad evaṃ bhagavāndadarśaṃ khalu bādariḥ/

	atrocyate --

*27ab anekārthatvam anyāyyaṃ tenaikādhikaraṇyataḥ/
*27cd karotinā tiṅanteṣu bhāvabhedo 'numīyate//

	yady api pratyayo 'bhedān na viśeṣāvagamāyālam, padantuprakṛtibhedabhinnaṃ prakṛtyarthānuraktapratyayārthapradhānam/ pratyayo 'pi paurvāparyaniyamāt padavan na svatantraḥ sāmānyamātre dhiyamādadhāti, api tu prakṛtyapekṣayā ācakṣāṇaḥ svārtham eva samarpayati/ sarvathā tāvat padaṃ viśeṣe vartate/ tena tāvat svato bhedavatyā api dhātvarthopadhānavyajyamānasphuṭaviśeṣāyās tatra viśeṣābhidhānam aviruddham/
	nanu pratyayasya sāmānyam abhidheyam iṣṭam, tat katham ubhayos sāmānyābhidhāyinor aikādhikaraṇyam?
	ucyate --

*28ab bhedopahitasāmānyavacano 'py apṛthakśrutiḥ/
*28cd ko rājā yāti? pāñcālarāja ity abhivīkṣyate//
	praśnavartinā hi sāmānyaśabdena vyavacchinnasāmānyaviṣayāḥ śrutayaḥ paridṛṣṭasamānādhikaraṇaprayogāḥ; ko rājā yāti? pāñcālarāja iti yathā/
	yukta evottare sāmānyanirdeśaḥ; anyathā praśnagataṃ tadapekṣaṇīyaṃ syāt, viśeṣatas tu bhāvanāyā asaṃvijñānapadabhedatvāt paropadhānavyajyamānasphuṭaviśeṣāyāḥ/ tasmān na siddhasādhanam, na viparyayasādhanam, na dṛṣṭāntābhāvaḥ/

*29ab bhāvābhāve prayogas tu dvidhāpi na nirīkṣyate/
*29cd antatas so 'sti dhātvarthagocaro lakṣyate tathā//
	ghaṇṭā dhvanatīti bhāvyābhāvena bhāvanāyā abhāva ucyate/ sarvaś ca dhātvarthaḥ kiñ cid asad utpādayati/ dhvananam api duḥkhaṃ jñānaṃ vā/ antato dhātvartha eva bhāvyaḥ/ na hi sarvatra dhātvarthasya karaṇataiva, karmatāpi tu dva cit tanmātranirvṛttivivakṣāyām/ kā kriyā? pākaḥ iti ca, dvividhatvāc chabdārthasya śrautalakṣyamāṇabhedena/ ato nābhāvo bhāvanāyāḥ śabdataḥ, tadviśeṣalakṣaṇārthatvāt pākaśabdasya/
	tiṅante 'pi tarhi lakṣyamāṇa eva bhāvo 'stu/ naitat sāram/
	yataḥ --

*30ab kaḥ pākaḥ pacatīty atra buddhibhedam apahnute/
*30cd na bhāvanāpārāmarśam antareṇa ca bhāvyatā//
	idaṃ bhavān pṛṣṭo vyācaṣṭām -- pākaḥ pacatīty anayor dhātvartho bhidyate, na vā bhidyate, iti/ ko hi sphuṭaṃ pratyayabhedam avajānīte/ tathā hi -- tiṅante bhāvyaḥ sa gamyate na tathābhūto(rūpo) ghañante, bhāve tadvidhānāt, śuddhasya ca dhātvarthasya tattvavyākhyānāt -- "dhātvarthaḥ kevalaḥ śuddho bhāva ity abhidhīyate" iti//
	tat tarhi bhāvyatvaṃ bhāvanāsaṃsparśakṛtam iti tiṅantānāṃ sā arthaḥ/ gamyamānatve hi tiṅantaghañantayoḥ naiva dhīr bhidyeta, aviśeṣāt/
	na ca sādhanasambandhād bhedaḥ, "gamyate" "sthīyate" iti prasaṅgāt/
	kiñ ca --

*31ab pākaṃ karoti pacatīty ākhyātārtho nidarśyate/
*31cd bhedena śabdavṛttijñaiḥ pākādau na tv ayaṃ kramaḥ//
	pacati, pākaṃ karotīti tiṅantapadārthaṃ bhedena nidarśayati nāpratipadya bhedam, pākādiṣu tu na tathā, bhedapratipatter abhāvād iti gamyate/
	parikalpya bhedaṃ tathopadarśanam, pākasyābhinirvartanaṃ karotīti yatheti cet -- na, pākādāv anidarśanāt/ śabdopakalpitas tiṅanteṣv api bheda iti cet -- astu tāvac chabdārthabhedaḥ, vastuny upapādayiṣyate/
	nanv evaṃ bhāvanāyā api sādhyarūpāyāḥ pratīter bhāvanāntarāpātāḥ, tad api tathaivety anavasthā/ yad api mataṃ vyāpāro hi sā, rūpam eva tasyāḥ tādṛśam aparādhīnopajananam, ato rūpāt tathātvasiddhiḥ nānyasaṃsparśavyapekṣiteti, dhātvartho 'pi tarhi tathā astu/ tathā hi -- so 'pi vastutas tathābhavan na vā āpāditatathābhāvaḥ svaśabdaiḥ vyāpārātmopādīyate; ghañādiṣv api kārakasambandhopapatteḥ, anyathā avyāpāre kārakasambandhāyogāt, avyāpārasambandhinaḥ kārakatvānupapatteḥ, kriyāsambandhaprabhāvitatvāt kārakabhāvasya/ buddhibhedas tu pākādiṣu pratyayopāttena siddhabhāvenābhibhavāt, anabhibhavāc ca pacatyādau/ tac cānabhibhūtaṃ rūpam anyathāśakyanidarśanaṃ vyāpārāntarasaṃsparśenaiva nidarśayanti bhāvanāyā iva/ na ca siddhasādhyatayor aikaśabdyaṃ viruddham bhedābhedayor iva gotvādiṣu, bhinnaprakṛtipratyayopādānatvāt/ bhāvyarūpapratītinidarśanābhyāñ ca dhātvarthasya tiṅanteṣu bhāvanām icchato dhātvarthasya karmatvaprasaṅgaḥ, karaṇatvaṃ na syāt/ tasmād dhātvartha eva vyāpārātmā svaśabdopādānaḥ phalabhavanasamartho bhāvanā kriyā ceti sāmpratam//
	tad etad asamañjasam --

*32ab dṛśyamāne vastubhede neṣṭā śabdārthamātratā/
*32cd pratyayārthenābhibhavaḥ siddhe tadbhāvaniścaye//
	darśayiṣyate hy avyāpārātmano dhātvarthād bhedavatī kriyā/ ato na śabdasāmarthyavilasitam evaitad iti yuktam/ asati vastuni tathā nāma bhavet/ sambandhagrahaṇāpekṣatvāc ca śabdasya na vastusvarūpakÂptyai vibhavaḥ/ niścite ca dhātubhiḥ bhāvyatāyā ihopādāne abhibhavābhidhānaṃ yuktam/ na ca tathā, pramāṇābhāvāt, sādhanasambandhasya gamyamānabhāvanāviṣayatvāt, tathāpi nidarśanāt "śaṅkulayā khaṇḍaḥ" iti/

*33ab viklidyanti pacantīti na viśeṣaḥ prakalpate/
*33cd ṛte vyāpāranānātvāt kartṛbhedo 'pi vai kutaḥ//
	na tāvat tiṅanteṣu dhātvartha eva vyāpārātmopādīyate, viklidyanti pacantīti dhātvarthābhedāt pratyayābhedaprasaṅgāt/ kartṛbhedān naivam iti cet -- na, vyāpārabhedam antareṇa tadanupapatteḥ/ yathā sādhanānāṃd niyoktṛtve kartṛlakṣaṇe yajñadattādaya eva kartāro na taṇḍulāḥ, abhidhīyamānavyāpāratve tu taṇḍulā eva/ anena bhinatti, dalati, chinatti, dvidhābhavatītyādayo vyākhyātāḥ/ tasmād dhātvarthātirikto vyāpāra upeyaḥ, yatkṛto 'rthabhedaḥ kartṛbhedaś ca//
	aparaḥ kalpaḥ -- syān mataṃ karmasamavāyikriyeṣv eva dhātvarthātirikto vyāpāro gamyate/ kartṛsthakriyeṣu katham? / ucyate --
*33cd ṛte vyāpāranānātvāt kartṛbhedo 'pi vai kutaḥ//

	naitat sāram --

*34ab viklittimātrasaṅkalpaviśeṣaikāvalambanau/
*34cd jñāyamānatadarthatvān neṣṭāv anyābhidhāyinau//
	"pākaḥ" "yāgaḥ" iti viklittimātraṃ saṅkalpamātraṃ cācakṣāte paciyajī, na tadatiriktaṃ kartṛvyāpāram, pākasya kriyety anidarśanāt/
	tathā "pacyante tañḍulāḥ svayam eva" "yajate yajamānaḥ" iti, tanmātrasyaiva tadvyāpāratvābhyupagamāt/ tathā ca "pacati devadattaḥ", "yajanti yājakāḥ" iti prajñāyamānatadarthasya nārthāntaraviṣayatvaṃ yuktam/
	avyabhicārād dhi tāvanmātram arthaḥ, nādhikam, vyabhicārāt, śabdāntaranibandhanatvāc ca, pratyayāgame hi pratīyamānaḥ tadartha eva kartṛvyāpāraḥ/ pradhānānāñ ca prāyeṇa pratyayārthatvāt prādhānyād api pratyayārthatvam/

	sattāyā eva sādhyarūpeṇa dhātunopādānāt sādhyatāpratītiḥ, na punaḥ pratyayena bhāvanāyā abhidhānāt/ sattā bhāva iti ca sattāyā dhātunaiva siddharūpeṇa upādānāt siddharūpapratītiḥ, na punaḥ pratyayena bhānāyā anabhidhānāt/ vyāpārasaṃsparśakṛtasya sādhyatvasya pacyādau niścayāt/ ihaiva tarhi vyabhicāraḥ "yajate yajamānaḥ", "pacyante svayam eva taṇḍulāḥ" iti/ vyāpārāntaropagame vā dhātor avyabhicāraḥ/ na, abhyupagamāt lenābhidhānasya, tannibandhanatvāt tasyānyathā(pi) siddheḥ pākādiṣu vyabhicārāt, bhedenānidarśanād apratīteḥ, itaratra tair niṣkarṣaṇāt, atulyārthatvāt tanniṣkarṣaḥ, sāmānyaviśeṣayoḥ sādhyasādhanabhāvāyogāt, sāmānyasyāsādhyatvāt, anabhidheyākhyānāpatteḥ/
	kiñ ca --

*35ab tad eva tasya karaṇaṃ na vai karma ca kalpate/
*35cd bhāvanāṃ saṅgirante 'to dhātvarthavyatirekiṇīm//
	"yajetāśvamedhena", "īje bahubhir yajñaiḥ", "svaṃ yajñaṃ yajati" ity ekatve kriyākārakabhāvānupapattiḥ/ sāmānyaviśeṣabhedo 'pi na kriyākārakasambandham upapādayituṃ kṣamaḥ, sāmānyasyāsādhyatvenākriyātvāt, viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyasambandhe tu kārakavibhaktivirodhaḥ/ nāpy ekadeśayoḥ kriyākārakabhāvaḥ, ubhayatrāpi samudāyasya śabdopādānatvāt/ nāpi dhātūpādānāyām apy antarbhūtasāmānyakarmakaraṇikāyāṃ viśeṣakarmakaraṇasaṃbandhaḥ -- yathā "muṇḍayati māṇavakam", "mālinyopaślokayati" iti; "svaṃ yajñam", "bahubhir yajñaiḥ" ity api dhātunā bhāvanopādānāt viśeṣabhāvanābhyupagamāt viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyasambandhaprasaṅgāt/ anupādāne ca siddhavyabhicāro 'sau na tiṅante 'pi tām upādātum arhati/ etena "odanapākaṃ pacati" ityādayo vyākhyātāḥ/ tasmād yatraiṣa kārakabhāvo nāmapadopādānadhātvarthasya vibhaktibhir vyajyate, sa tadatirekī tiṅanteṣu pratyayopādānaḥ karotyarthaḥ pratijñāyate vṛddhaiḥ/ "iṣṭavān yajñaiḥ" ity api dravyapradhānabhāvanādhigamān niṣṭhādiṣu tathā pratīter avirodhaḥ//
	yat tūktaṃ bhāvanāviṣayatvapratīteḥ karaṇatvahāniḥ, tadanirūpitakaraṇa(ta)ttvasya/
	tathā hi --

*36ab karaṇaṃ khalu sarvatra kartṛvyāpāragocaraḥ/
*36cd tirodadhāti kartāraṃ pradhānaṃ tannibandhanam//
	na khalu kartṛvyāpāralakṣaṇabhāvanāptyā dhātvarthasya karmatvaprasaṅgaḥ karaṇatvabādho vā, paraśvādiṣu tathā prasaṅgāt/ na chidādiṣu sākṣādvyāpriyate kartā, kin tu paraśvādikaraṇe/ tadvyāpāraḥ tadudyamananipātanādilakṣaṇaḥ paraśugocara eva/ ataḥ tasyāvyavahitavyāpāratayā kriyāsiddheḥ sādhakatamatvam/ tadvyāpārāyattapravṛttitayā ca karaṇasya kartuḥ svātantryamārād upakāriṇo 'pi; anyathā karaṇataiva na syāt/ 	api ca --

*37ab sādhanānām abhāvas syāt kartṛkarmātirekiṇām/
*37cd sādhanānāṃ niyogo hi kartṛvyāpāra iṣyate//
	pralīnedānīṃ kartṛkarmātiriktasādhanāntarasaṅkathā, sādhananiyogaḥ kartṛvyāpāra iti tanniyogaviṣayatāpattyā sarvatra karmatvāpātāt/ kas tarhi karmetaravibhāgaḥ? kathañ ca karaṇaparikṣayiṇi kartṛvyāpāre tadāptivirahiṇaḥ karmatā/ ucyate --

*38ab anyārthāptivivakṣāyām akarmātiśayād vinā/
*38cd uddeśād anyavṛttyāpi phalam eveṣyatetarām//
	yat khalūddiṣṭavastvantarāyā nāntarīyakatvena viṣayatām upayadabhisaṃhitaṃ tadakarma atiśayābhāvāt/ uddeśyaṃ tatkarmānyagocarayāpi kriyayā tasyaivāptum iṣyamāṇatvāt tadabhisandhipravartitāyā itarasaṃbandhasya nāntarīyakatvāt/
	ataḥ karaṇasyāpi dhātvarthasya sādhyatvapratītinidarśane aviruddhe/ yathā paraśunā chinattīti paraśor udyamananipātyamānatvapratītiḥ paraśum udyacchati nipātayati dvidhābhavanāyeti nidarśanaṃ prayuṅkte ceti/
	yat tu kartṛvyāpāro 'dhiśrayaṇādiḥ dhātvarthāntarātmaka iti, atrāhuḥ --

*39ab kathyamānād rūpabhedād dvidhā dhātvarthasaṅgatiḥ/
*39cd anyotpādānukūlātmā bhāvanā kiṃ praduṣyati//
	sarvathā pacati yajatīti prākprayujyamānagocaro na bhavatīti pratipāditam, dhātvantarais tv abhidhānaṃ karotibhāvayatibhyām iva na pakṣakṣatimāvahati/

*40ab dhātūnām abhidhānīyān kartṛkarmasamāśrayān/
*40cd labdhātmano 'kriyārūpānviśeṣān pratijānate//
	yathā śrayateścullīsthālīsaṃyogaviśeṣaḥ āsiñcater āsicyamānādhārasaṃyogaviśeṣaḥ/ evaṃ sarvatra yāvatpaces taṇḍulāvayavānāṃ vibhāgāḥ praśithilāvayavasaṃyogavibhāgāḥ/

*41ab udāsīnatvavicchedalakṣyaṃ dhātvarthakāraṇam/
*41cd spandavyāpāraparyāyaṃ karotyarthaṃ pracakṣate//
	tathā hi -- adhiśrayati udakam āsiñcati pacati paṭhatīti vā adhiśrayaṇādisaṃyogabhedebhyo vyāpārātmako bhedena karoti spandate ceṣṭate vyāpriyate iti vyāpārarūpamadhyakṣyate/ na caiṣa tajjanyatvābhimataviśeṣālambanaḥ pratyayaḥ, yataḥ --

*42ab vyāpārabuddhyupajano na saṃyogavibhāgayoḥ/
*42cd sator api hi ceṣṭitvā virāmaṃ samupāgate//
	na calitvā sthite kriyāprabhavapūrvottaradeśavibhāgasaṃyogābhāvaḥ, tadālambano vyāpārapratyayo na jātu viramet/

*43ab vibhāgena samucchedāt saṃyogapracayaḥ kutaḥ/
*43cd bhaviṣyadvṛttisaṃyogavibhāgādhyakṣatā katham//
	yad apy avicchinnotpādadeśasaṃyogavibhāgapracayālambana iti, tad api na samyak/ na hy uttarasaṃyogaḥ pūrvavibhāgād ṛte/ na vibhāgaḥ saṃyogena sahaikatra/ tadvibhāgocchedāt kutas tatpracayaḥ?
	acchinnotpādaś ca niyogabhāvyuttarasamavetatā vartamānānāṃ/ teṣu vartamānānāgateṣu tadaviṣayaṃ katham indriyaṃ dhiyaṃ janayet? mano 'pi cāgaṇitakuśalasya naivam artham/
	api ca -- sthāṇau pūrvottaraśyenābhyāṃ saṃyogavibhāgābhyāṃ vyāpāradhīḥ syāt/
	ādhāradeśasaṃyogavibhāgābhyāṃ seti ca na yuktam/ tathā hi --

*44ab jalāvayavasaṃyogavibhāgau sthiramūrtiṣu/
*44cd jalādhāreṣu matsyeṣu vyāpārapratyayād vinā//
	pratītaṃ hi jalaṃ deśo matsyānām, na sthāṇor iva śyeno na deśaḥ/ tadavayavasaṃyogavibhāgasadbhāve 'pi teṣu mahattvādakampanīyeṣu balavadvidhārakaprayatnavidhṛtātmasu na kriyāvattvābhyupagamaḥ/ tasmān na tannibandhanaḥ/
	nabhasastvapratyakṣatvāt tadvṛttyoḥ saṃyogavibhāgayor durlabhā pratyakṣatā/
	na ca viyadvitatālokanibandhanaḥ, santamase jhaṭiti vidyuti kriyāpratyayāt/
	tasmād asti dhātūpādānatadāhitaviśeṣātiriktātmā kriyāpadārtho bhāvanāparyāyaḥ/
	nanv evaṃ bhāvanāto dhātvarthaniṣpattiḥ, tataḥ phalasyeti kathaṃ tanniṣpādyo dhātvarthaḥ tasyāḥ karaṇaṃ? svargādi vā tatsādhyaṃ karma?
	tasmāt sa eva dhātvarthas tathā bhāvanā astu, svargaṃ dhātvarthañ cāntarāvyāpārāntarānupalabdher ity uktam/
	uktottaram etat/ tathā hi --

*45ab phale na sākṣāt karaṇe kartuś ceṣṭā phalārthinaḥ/
*45cd karaṇatvaṃ pradhānāṃśe tādrūpyaṃ vā yatas tataḥ//
	neme anyakarmakaraṇatvavilakṣaṇe phaladhātvarthakarmakaraṇatve/ darśitaṃ paraśunā chinattīty api na dvidhābhavane kartṛvyāpṛtiḥ, api tu karaṇe sādhananiyogādilakṣaṇatvāt kartuḥ
	tatraiva caiṣa vyāpāraḥ tadabhisandhānāt, ananyaprakāratvāt/
	iyāṃs tu viśeṣaḥ -- dhātvarthasyotpādena viniyogaḥ, paraśvādes tu siddhasyodyamananipātanādinā/ vṛkṣādes tu karmatvaṃ dvidhābhavane kartṛtvāt tadarthaṃ paraśunipātanodyamanalakṣaṇam/
	dvidhābhavanaṃ prati karaṇatvaṃ ca paraśoḥ chinattau pradhānāpekṣam, tādrūpyasya vā tadadhīnatvāt/
	na hy udyamana nipātanamātram asambhavaddvidhābhavanaṃ chinattīti nirūpyate/
	tasmād ihāpi dhātvarthasyotpādako 'nugrāhako vā kartṛvyāpāraḥ samuddiṣṭaphalabhavanatayā pratilabdhabhāvanāvyapadeśo dhātvarthopanibaddhapradhānāṃśa tādrūpyaphalakarmako dhātvarthakaraṇakaś ceti na doṣaḥ/
	na hi dvidhābhavati (bhāvayati) paraśuneti paraśuṃ dvidhābhavanaṃ cāntareṇa kartṛvyāpāra eko 'parasādhyo 'parasādhana upalabhyate/ na ca karaṇatvaṃ viruddham, pradhānāṃśaniṣpattes tadadhīnatvāt/ tathehāpi phalabhūtakriyāṃśāpekṣatvāt kārakavibhaktivibhāgavyavasthāyāḥ/
	tathā hi --

*46ab pradhānāṃśam upādāya sādhanāny ekavākyatām/
*46cd vrajanti nānyavyāpāro rūpato hy anyasādhanaḥ//
	tasmād anupālambho 'yaṃ vyāpārānupalambha iti/ yat tv ātmakartṛkeṣu yajatyādiṣv asaṃbhava iti, tatra ke cit --

*47ab bhāṣye yateteti girā prayatnaṃ pratipedire/
*47cd acetane prayogasya prācuryān nopacāradhīḥ//
	tathā yateta yathā yatkiñcidbhavatīti phalabhā(bha)vanānukūlaḥ prayatna evākhyātārtho darśitaḥ/ acetane tv ākhyātaprayoga upacārāt/ prayogaprācuryān naupacārikaḥ (kapra) pratyayaḥ/ idan tu samādhānaṃ siddhe tadbhāve syāt/ na ca pratyaye samāne hetvantare cāsati tatprasiddhir asti, viparyayāvagamasyāśakyavāraṇatvāt/

*48ab udāsīnatvavicchedasāmānyātmā tato mataḥ/
*48cd karotyartho dvayoś ceha tadrūpapratyayodbhavaḥ//
	udāsīnatvavicchedo hi karotyarthaḥ, udāsīne kriyājñānābhāvāt/
	udāsīnatāpracyutidhiyaś ca dvaidham, ātmani prayatnāt, anyatra parispandāt/
	tathā hi --
	prayatnavantam ātmānam anudāsīnaṃ kurvāṇaṃ vyāpāravantamavaiti, parispandamānaṃ cānyat/ ataḥ prayatnaparispandayor audasīnyavicchedasāmānyayos tadābhāsadhī viṣayatvena kriyāpadārthatvān nātmakartṛkeṣu tadabhāva iti/
	tad uktam -- "na parispanda evaikaḥ kriyā naḥ kaṇabhojivat" iti/
	athāpi parispandena prayojanam, asau --

*49ab manobhūtaparispanda ātmanas tatprayatnajaḥ/
*49cd viklittyādir yathā tasmin sādhanopanipātataḥ//
	ātmamanassaṃyogaviśeṣād vijñānasaṅkalpādyutpattiḥ/ tannibandhanam ātmasamavetādṛṣṭaprayatnahetumenasi karma parispandarūpam upeyate/ ātmaprayatnāpekṣaś ca bhūteṣu dravyopādānādilakṣaṇaḥ parispandaḥ/ ubhayo 'py ayam ātmanas tadadhīnatvāt, viklittyādivat/ viklitticchidābhidās taṇḍulādisamavāyinyo 'samavetā api devadatte tatkriyāḥ, tāsu tasya kartṛtvāt, pradhānakriyāyāṃ sarvakārakopanipātāt, ekavākyatvāt/ na hi svavyāpāraviṣayaḥ karaṇādibhāvaḥ kārakāṇām, teṣāṃ bhedād ekavākyatvānupapatteḥ/
	evañ ca kartṛviniyojyatayā sarvasādhanānāṃ tadvyāpāraḥ kartur iti teṣāṃ bhāvanāntargatiḥ tanmukhena sādhanatāpy upapannā/

*50ab paropādhikriyārūpo jñāyate na kriyāntaram/
*50cd sambandhād eva sādhyatvam ataḥ karaṇasaṅgatiḥ//
	dhātvarthasya bhāvanām anurañjayataḥ tadviṣayatāpratītyā tatparāmarśakṛtaṃ tadantargatasya kriyātvam iti kutaḥ kriyādvayapratyayaḥ? na hi jātiguṇoparakteṣu tadrūpabhedaḥ/ bhāvanāsambandhitayaiva tasya sādhyatā na tatsambandhaṃ vihanti, tadabhāve tasyāpy abhāvaprasaṅgāt/ ataś ca karaṇasambandho 'py upapannaḥ, bhāvanāviṣayatvam anupayataḥ karaṇatāyogāt sādhyatāpratīteḥ kriyārūpāpatteḥ/

*51ab pradhānavyāpṛtir yasya dhātvarthaṃ praty apīṣyate/
*51cd sa kartātaḥ pacantīti viklidyantīti bhidyate//
	na vai khalv aguṇataḥ dhātunā abhidhīyamānavyāpāraḥ kartā, yena pratyayārthaḥ paryanuyujyeta, api tu dhātvarthaṃ pratyabhisaṃhitapradhānavyāpāraḥ, sarvatra dhātvarthasya kartṛvyāpāraṃ lakṣyam abhidheyaṃ vā prati viśeṣaṇatvāt/ tena viklidyantīti taṇḍulavyāpārasya jvalanāvayavābhighātajanmano 'vayavāśritasya sthālyudakādyanusaraṇātmakasya dhātvarthaṃ prati pradhānasyābhidhānam, pacatīty atra tu devadattavyāpārasyādhiśrayaṇādeḥ pratyayenābhidhānād arthabhedaḥ kartṛbhedaś ca siddhyatīti na pratyayārthaparyanuyogāvakāśaḥ/ kathaṃ punas tulyārthatvāt pacinā viklideḥ pareṇa pratyayena taṇḍulavyāpāra eva prayoktṛbhiḥ vivakṣyate, na devadattavyāpāro 'pi paceḥ pareṇai(ṇe)va? prakṛtīnāṃ rūpabhedāt tulyārthatve 'pi svārthe pratyayasya śaktibhedāt/

*52ab bhāryā dārā jalānyāpa ekārthatve 'pi dṛśyate/
*52cd prakṛte rūpabhedena pratyayayārtho viśeṣavān//
	jalānīti prakṛtyarthasamavāyi tadekārthasamavāyi vā bahutvaṃ bahuvacanārthaḥ, tathā bhāryā iti/ āpo dārā iti tu prakṛtyarthāvayavasamavāyi tadāśrayaikārthasamavāyi (vā), prakṛtirūpabhedāt tadapekṣatvāt pratyayasyābhidhānaśakteḥ/
	tathehāpi paciparo dhātvarthaikārthasamavāyinaṃ vyāpāraṃ karmakartṛviṣayaḥ pratyaya āha; bhinnāśrayaṃ vā anyatra/ viklidiparas tu prakṛtyai(tyarthai)kārthasamavāyinam ity anavadyam/

*53ab agniṃ cinuta ity atra svaṃ yajñaṃ yajatīti ca/
*53cd darśanāt karmayogasya nābhāvas tadabhāvataḥ//
	dhātvarthasya hi cayanasyeṣṭakāḥ sthalaṃ vā avyavadhānāt karma, na vyāpārāntaravyavadhīyamāno vyabhicāryagniḥ/ sa tu bhāvanākarma/ agniśabdaś ca na sthalavacanaḥ api tu jvalanavacanaḥ/ evaṃ hy uktam -- "atra hy eṣa prasiddhaḥ" iti/ "svaṃ yajñaṃ yajati" iti tu na bhāvanāto 'nyatra karmatvam iti varṇitam/ tathā mahābhāṣye karmadvayasambandho viklitter bhāvanābhedenaiva darśataḥ -- "iha tāvat taṇḍulān odanam pacatīti, dvyarthaḥ paciḥ, taṇḍulān vikledayan odanaṃ nirvartayati" iti/

*54ab prayojakakriyām āhuḥ kartṛvyāpāragocarām/
*54cd kartṛvyāpāraviṣayo dhātor artho vivakṣyate//
	svavyāpāre sarvasya (kartṛtvāt anyathā kārakatvāyogāt) niyogāt kartṛvyāpāraviṣayaḥ preraṇādiḥ prayojakakartṛvyāpāro ṇijarthaḥ, dhātvarthaviṣayaḥ kartṛvyāpāras tiṅartha iti na bhavati bhāvayatyos tulyārthateti/

*55ab tad evaṃ bhāvanārthatvād ākhyātapratyayasya hi/
*55cd yāgena bhāvayet svargam ity arthaḥ śabdanirmitaḥ//
	tat katham?

*56ab apekṣitatvād bhāvyasya kāmaśabdā hi tatparāḥ/
*56cd viśeṣaṇapradhānatvaṃ daṇḍītyādiṣu darśitam//
	yady api svargādayaḥ puruṣaviśeṣaṇatvena śrūyante, tathāpi kāmaśrutayaḥ tatpradhānāḥ, "daṇḍī praiṣānanvāha", "lohitoṣṇīṣā ṛtvijaḥ pracaranti" iti darśanāt/ kutaḥ? bhāvanā(na)yā bhāvyasyāpekṣitatvāt svargādīnāṃ ca kāmya(māna)tayā bhāvyatvapratīteḥ dvitīyāntaprayoga iva tayāpi iṣṭatvapratipādanāt/ tena puruṣapratītiḥ svargakāmapade 'nuvādaḥ/ svargam ity etāvad vidhitsitam, bhāṣyaviśeṣaparatvāt, dhātvarthasya śrautasyāpi vidhijñānavirodhād akarmatvāt/ karaṇatve 'pi śrutyanugrahād avirodhāt, śāstrādhīnatvād yogyāyogyatvayor ajñānāt, (yogyatvasya ayogyatvājñānāt) sarvaphalabhāvanānāṃ ca dhātvarthaviśeṣasādhanatvāt/
	syān matam -- svargādiviśeṣaṇaparatve 'pi guṇabhāvaḥ svargādeḥ syāt/ na, bhāvyasya prathamam apekṣaṇāt, sādhaneṣu copacārād icchāyāḥ/
	nanu na bhāvyapratipattipradhānāḥ kāmaśrutayaḥ, api tu niyojyaviśeṣapratipādanaparāḥ/ anyathā kasyāyam upadeśaḥ, ko niyujyate, kasyedaṃ kartavyam, ity ajhānāt sāpekṣam asamāptam apratipattikaram eva śāstraṃ syāt/
	naitat sāram, yataḥ --

*57ab pratīyate niyogo 'rthāt samarthasya phalārthinaḥ/
*57cd anyathā hy asamarthānāṃ durvārādhikṛtir bhavet//
	yo 'pi hi śabdān niyojyaviśeṣāvasāyaṃ manyate so 'pi niyojyaviśeṣapratipattau sāmarthyam upaiti/ anyathā śabdavaśena śaktikalpanāyām andhādīnām apy atrārthe 'dhikāraḥ syāt/ saty api cec chabde sāmarthyam upāśrīyate niyojyāvagame tadasaty evāstu/ tasmād yo niyogārthaṃ pratipattuṃ tadviṣayaṃ cānuṣṭhātuṃ samarthaḥ phalārthī ca sa niyojyaḥ, asamarthasyānarthino 'śakye 'nabhilaṣite (vā) yāge pravartanāyogāt/
	kiñ ca --

*58ab atatparatve phalatā (svargāde)paśvāder nāsti śabdataḥ/
*58cd na sāmarthyād anuṣṭhānaṃ niyogaikanibandhanam//
	yadi svargakāmādiśrutayo na phalabhedādhigamanaparāḥ na śabdavatī phalatā svargādīnām; nāpi sāmarthyataḥ, anupapadyamānābhāvāt/ anyathā(pi) niyujyamānā (na) bhāvāt/ anuṣṭhānam ayuktam iti cet -- na, tasya niyogamātrāyattatvābhyupagamāt/ ekavākyatvam iti cet -- ko virodhaḥ? anyad icchati anyat karotīti svargakāmo yāgaṃ kuryād ity ayam eva virodhaḥ (iti cet) ata eva tarhi na puruṣaviśeṣaparāḥ svargakāmādiśrutayaḥ, phalaparās santu/
	api ca --

*59ab niyogaparatantrasya puruṣasyānyad icchataḥ/
*59cd kriyānyasya viruddheti na niyogapradhānatā//
	anyad icchati anyat karoti iti virodhaṃ vadato niyogaprādhānyaṃ hīyate, phalārthitāhetutvān niyogasya/ dṛśyate ca niyogasāmarthyād anyad apīcchatām anyakriyā paratantrāṇām/ bhāvyasyaiva sato viśeṣaṇatvam iti cet -- na, niyogaviṣayāsaṃbandhāt, tataḥ kriyāntarabhāvyasy api viśeṣaṇatvāt/ icchāmātrapravṛttidarśanāc cāyuktam evaitat/
	nanv evaṃ naimittikatve cāgnihotrādīnāṃ nityatvavyāghātaḥ/ ayam apy aparo doṣo 'stu, na tu niyojyaviśeṣaparāt phalapratipattiḥ/ nanu svargakāmasya yāgaḥ kartavyo 'vagamyate/ kathaṃ ca sa tasya kartavyaḥ yadi taṃ na niṣpādayati? na, gṛhadāhavata ivecchāvataḥ kiñ cid asampādayato 'pi kartavyatā syāt/ yadi tarhy asampādayati svargaṃ yāge puruṣaḥ pravartate na tarhi svargakāmaḥ/ na, nimeṣādiṣv atathābhūteṣv api pravartamānasya svargakāmatvāvighātāt/ hetvantarāt tatra pravṛttir iti cet -- ihāpi niyogād iti na doṣaḥ/
	syād etat -- svargasiddhāvicchāpagame niyoganiṣpattiḥ/ anyathā kṛtayāgo 'pi niyojya eva syāt, na kṛtaniyogaḥ/ ato niyogārthaniṣpattisāmarthyāt svargādisiddhi[niṣpattiḥ]/ na, viṣayasiddhyā niyoganiṣpatteḥ, svargādeś ca niyojyaviśeṣaṇatvenāviṣayatvāt/ anyathā gṛhadāhāder apy apagamaprasaṅgaḥ/ tasmāt phalaparāḥ svargakāmādiśrutaya iti manoharam/

	ity āryamaṇḍanasya kṛtir bhāvanāviveko 'vasitaḥ//

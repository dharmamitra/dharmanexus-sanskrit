Śivasvarodaya (Kaivalyadhama ed., 2015) transcribed by Lubomir Ondrackha

śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ
maheśvaraṃ namaskṛtya śailajāṃ gaṇanāyakam |
guruṃ ca paramātmānaṃ bhaje saṃsāratāraṇam ‖1‖

śrī devy uvāca
devadeva mahādeva kṛpāṃ kṛtvā mamopari |
sarvasiddhikaraṃ jñānaṃ kathayasva mama prabho ‖2‖
katham brahmāṇḍam utpannaṃ kathaṃ vā parivartate |
kathaṃ vilīyate deva vada brahmāṇḍanirṇayam ‖3‖

īśvara uvāca
tattvād brahmāṇḍam utpannaṃ tattvena parivartate |
tattve vilīyate devi tattvād brahmāṇḍanirṇayaḥ ‖4‖

devy uvāca
tattvam eva paraṃ mūlaṃ niścitaṃ tattvavādibhiḥ |
tattvasvarūpaṃ kiṃ deva tattvam eva prakāśaya ‖5‖

īśvara uvāca
nirañjano nirākara eko devo maheśvaraḥ |
tasmād ākāśam utpannam ākāśād vāyusambhavaḥ ‖6‖
vayos tejas tataś cāpas tataḥ pṛthvī samudbhavaḥ |
etāni pañcatattvāni vistīrṇāni ca pañcadhā ‖7‖
tebhyo brahmāṇḍam utpannaṃ tair eva parivartate |
vilīyate ca tatraiva tatraiva ramate punaḥ ‖8‖
pañcatattvamaye dehe pañcatattvāni sundari |
sūkṣmarūpeṇa vartante jñāyante tattvayogibhiḥ ‖9‖
atha svaraṃ pravakṣyāmi śarīrasya svarodayam |
haṃsacārasvarūpeṇa bhavej jñānaṃ trikālajam ‖10‖
guhyād guhyataraṃ sāram upakāraprakāśanam |
idaṃ svarodayaṃ jñānaṃ jñānānāṃ mastake maṇiḥ ‖11‖
sūkṣmāt sūkṣmataraṃ jñānaṃ subodhaṃ satyapratyayam |
āścaryaṃ nāstike loke ādhāraṃ tv āstike jane ‖12‖

atha śiṣya lakṣaṇam
śānte śuddhe sadācāre gurubhaktaikamānase |
dṛḍhacitte kṛtajñe ca deyaṃ caiva svarodayam ‖13‖
duṣṭe ca durjane kruddhe nāstike gurutalpage |
hīnasattve durācāre svarajñānaṃ na dīyate ‖14‖
śṛṇu tvaṃ kathitaṃ devi dehasthaṃ jñānam uttamam |
yena vijñānamātreṇa sarvajñatvaṃ praṇīyate ‖15‖
svare vedāś ca śāstrāṇi svare gāndharvam uttamam |
svare ca sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ svaram ātmasvarūpakam ‖16‖
svarahīnaś ca daivajño nāthahīnaṃ yathā gṛham |
śāstrahīnaṃ yathā vaktraṃ śirohīnaṃ ca yadvapuḥ ‖17‖
nāḍībhedaṃ tathā prāṇa tattvabhedaṃ tathaiva ca |
suṣumnāmiśrabhedaṃ ca yo jānāti sa muktigaḥ ‖18‖
sākāre vā nirākāre śubhaṃ vāyubalāt kṛtam |
kathayanti śubhaṃ kecit svarajñānaṃ varānane ‖19‖
brahmāṇḍakhaṇḍapiṇḍādyāḥ svareṇaiva hi nirmitāḥ |
sṛṣṭisaṃhārakarttā ca svaraḥ sākṣān maheśvaraḥ ‖20‖
svarajñānāt paraṃ guhyaṃ svarajñānāt paraṃ dhanam |
svarajñānāt paraṃ jñānaṃ na vā dṛṣṭaṃ na vā śrutam ‖21‖
śatruṃ hanyāt svarabale tathā mitrasamāgamaḥ |
lakṣmīprāptiḥ svarabale kīrtiḥ svarabale sukham ‖22‖
kanyāprāptiḥ svarabale svarato rājadarśanam |
svareṇa devatāsiddhiḥ svareṇa kṣitipo vaśaḥ ‖23‖
svareṇa gamyate deśo bhojyaṃ svarabale tathā |
laghudīrghaṃ svarabale malaṃ caiva nivārayet ‖24‖
sarvaśāstrapurāṇādi smṛtivedāṅgapūrvakam |
svarajñānāt paraṃ tattvaṃ nāsti kiñcid varānane ‖25‖
nāmarūpādikāḥ sarve mithyā sarveṣu vibhramaḥ |
ajñānamohitā mūḍhā yāvat tattvam na vidyate ‖26‖
idaṃ svarodayaṃ śāstraṃ sarvaśāstrottamottamam |
ātmāghaṭaprakāśārthaṃ pradīpakalikopamam ‖27‖
yasmai kasmai parasmai vā na proktaṃ praśnahetave |
tasmāt etat svayaṃ jñeyam ātmano vā ''tmanātmani ‖28‖
na tithir na ca nakṣatraṃ na varo grahadevatā |
na ca viṣṭir vyatīpāto vaidhṛtyādyas tathaiva ca ‖29‖
kuyogo nāsty ato devi bhavitā vā kadācana |
prāpte svarabale śuddhe sarvam eva śubhaṃ phalam ‖30‖
dehamadhye sthitā nāḍyo bahurūpāḥ suvistarāḥ |
jñātavyāś ca budhair nityaṃ svadehajñānahetave ‖31‖
nābhisthānagakandordhvam aṅkurād eva nirgatāḥ |
dvisaptati sahasrāṇi dehamadhye vyavasthitāḥ ‖32‖
nāḍīsthā kuṇḍalīśaktir bhujaṅgākāraśāyinī |
tato daśordhvagā nāḍyo daśaivādhaḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ‖33‖
dve dve tiryag gate nāḍyau caturviṃśati saṃkhyayā |
pradhānā daśanāḍyas tu daśavāyupravāhakāḥ ‖34‖
tiryag ūrdhvas tathā nāḍyo vāyudehasamanvitāḥ |
cakravatsaṃsthitā dehe sarvāḥ prāṇasamāśritāḥ ‖35‖
tāsāṃ madhye daśa śreṣṭhā daśānāṃ tisra uttamāḥ |
iḍā ca piṅgalā caiva suṣumnā ca tṛtīyakā ‖36‖
gāndhārī hastijihvā ca pūṣā caiva yaśasvinī |
alambuṣā kuhūś caiva śaṅkhinī daśamī tathā ‖37‖
iḍā vāme sthitā bhāge piṅgalā dakṣiṇe smṛtā |
suṣumnā madhyadeśe tu gāndhārī vāmacakṣuṣi ‖38‖
dakṣiṇe hastijihvā ca pūṣā karṇe ca dakṣiṇe |
yaśasvinī vāmakarṇe ānane cāpy alaṃbuṣā ‖39‖
kuhūś ca liṅgadeśe tu mūlasthāne tu śaṅkhinī |
evaṃ dvāraṃ samāśritya tiṣṭhanti daśa nāḍikāḥ ‖40‖
piṅgaleḍā suṣumnā ca prāṇamārge samāśritāḥ |
etāhi daśanāḍyas tu dehamadhye vyavasthitāḥ ‖41‖
nāmāni nāḍikānāṃ tu vātānāṃ tu vadāmy aham |
prāṇo 'pānaḥ samānaś ca udāno vyāna eva ca ‖42‖
nāgaḥ kurmo 'tha kṛkalo devadatto dhanañjayaḥ |
hṛdi prāṇo vasen nityam apāno gudamaṇḍale ‖43‖
samāno nābhideśe tu udānaḥ kaṇṭhamadhyagaḥ |
vyāno vyāpī śarīreṣu pradhānāḥ daśa vāyavaḥ ‖44‖
prāṇādyāḥ pañca vikhyātā nāgādyāḥ pañcavāyavaḥ |
teṣām api ca pañcānāṃ sthānāni ca vadāmy aham ‖45‖
udgāre nāga ākhyātaḥ kūrma unmīlane smṛtaḥ |
kṛkalaḥ kṣutakṛj jñeyo devadatto vijṛmbhane ‖46‖
na jahāti mṛtaṃ vāpi sarvavyāpī dhanañjayaḥ |
ete nāḍīṣu sarvāsu bhramante jīvarūpiṇaḥ ‖47‖
prakaṭaṃ prāṇasañcāraṃ lakṣayed dehamadhyataḥ |
iḍāpiṅgalāsuṣumnābhir nāḍībhis tisṛbhir budhaḥ ‖48‖
iḍā vāme ca vijñeyā piṅgalā dakṣiṇe smṛtā |
iḍā nāḍī sthitā vāmā tato vyastā ca piṅgalā ‖49‖
iḍāyāṃ tu sthitaś candraḥ piṅgalāyāṃ ca bhāskaraḥ |
suṣumnā śambhurūpeṇa śambhur haṃsasvarūpataḥ ‖50‖
hakāro nirgame proktaḥ sakāreṇa praveśanam |
hakāraḥ śivarūpeṇa sakāraḥ śaktir ucyate ‖51‖
śaktirūpaḥ sthitaś candro vāmanāḍī pravāhakaḥ |
dakṣanāḍī pravāhaś ca śambhurūpo divākaraḥ ‖52‖
śvāse sakārasaṃsthe tu yad dānaṃ dīyate budhaiḥ |
tad dānaṃ jīvaloke 'smin koṭikoṭiguṇaṃ bhavet ‖53‖
anena lakṣayed yogī caikacittaḥ samāhitaḥ |
sarvam eva vijānīyān mārge vai candrasūryayoḥ ‖54‖
dhyāyet tattvaṃ sthire jīve asthire na kadācana |
iṣṭasiddhir bhavet tasya mahālābho jayas tathā ‖55‖
candrasūryasamabhyāsaṃ ye kurvanti sadā narāḥ |
atītānāgatajñānaṃ teṣāṃ hastagataṃ bhavet ‖56‖
vāme cāmṛtarūpā syāj jagad āpyāyanaṃ param |
dakṣiṇe carabhāgena jagad utpādayet sadā ‖57‖
madhyamā bhavati krūrā duṣṭā sarvatra karmasu |
sarvatra śubhakāryeṣu vāmā bhavati siddhidā ‖58‖
nirgame tu śubhā vāmā praveśe dakṣiṇā śubhā |
candraḥ samas tu vijñeyo ravis tu viṣamaḥ sadā ‖59‖
candraḥ strī puruṣaḥ sūryaś candro gauro 'sito raviḥ |
candranāḍī pravāhena saumyakāryāṇi kārayet ‖60‖
sūryanāḍī pravāhena raudrakarmāṇi kārayet |
suṣumnāyāḥ pravāhena bhuktimuktiphalāni ca ‖61‖
ādau candraḥ site pakṣe bhāskaras tu sitetare |
pratipatto dināny āhus trīṇi trīṇi kṛtodayau ‖62‖
sārdhadvighaṭike jñeyaḥ śukle kṛṣṇe śaśī raviḥ |
vahaty ekadinenaiva yathā ṣaṣṭighaṭīḥ kramāt ‖63‖
vaheyus tadghaṭīmadhye pañcatattvāni nirdiśet |
pratipatto dināny āhur viparīte vivarjayet ‖64‖
śuklapakṣe bhaved vāmā kṛṣṇapakṣe ca dakṣiṇā |
jānīyāt pratipat pūrvaṃ yogī tadgatamānasaḥ ‖65‖
śaśāṅkaṃ vārayed rātrau divā vāraya bhāskaram |
ity abhyāsarato nityaṃ sa yogī nātra saṃśayaḥ ‖66‖
sūryeṇa badhyate sūryaś candraś candreṇa badhyate |
yo jānāti kriyām etām trailokyaṃ vaśagaṃ kṣaṇāt ‖67‖ corr. atām trelokyam
udayaṃ candramārgeṇa sūryeṇāstamanaṃ yadi |
tadā te guṇasaṅghātā viparītaṃ vivarjayet ‖68‖
guruśukrabudhendūnāṃ vāsare vāmanāḍikā |
siddhidā sarvakāryeṣu śuklapakṣe viśeṣataḥ ‖69‖
arkāṅgārakasaurīṇāṃ vāsare dakṣanāḍikā |
smarttavyā carakāryeṣu kṛṣṇapakṣe viśeṣataḥ ‖70‖
prathamaṃ vahate vāyur dvitīyaṃ ca tathā 'nalaḥ |
tṛtīyaṃ vahate bhūmiś caturthaṃ vāruṇo vahet ‖71‖
sārdhadvighaṭike pañca krameṇaivodayanti ca |
kramād ekaikanāḍyāṃ ca tattvānāṃ pṛthag udbhavaḥ ‖72‖
ahorātrasya madhye tu jñeyā dvādaśa saṃkramāḥ |
vṛṣakarkaṭakanyālimṛgamīnāniśākare ‖73‖
meṣasiṃhau ca kumbhaś ca tulā ca mithunaṃ dhanum |
udaye dakṣiṇe jñeyaḥ śubhāśubhavinirṇayaḥ ‖74‖
tiṣṭhet pūrvottare candro bhānuḥ paścimadakṣiṇe |
dakṣanāḍyāḥ prasāre tu na gacched yāmyapaścime ‖75‖
vāmācārapravāhe tu na gacchet pūrva uttare |
paripanthibhayaṃ tasya gato 'sau na nivarttate ‖76‖ corr. nivattate 
tasmāt tatra na gantavyaṃ budhaiḥ sarvahitaiṣibhiḥ |
tadā tatra tu samyāte mṛtyur eva na saṃśayaḥ ‖77‖
śuklapakṣe dvitīyāyām arke vahati candramāḥ |
dṛśyate lābhadaḥ puṃsāṃ saumye saukhyaṃ prajāyate ‖78‖
sūryodaye yadā sūryaś candraś candrodaye bhavet |
siddhayanti sarvakāryāṇi divārātrigatāny api ‖79‖
candrakāle yadā sūryaś candraḥ sūryodaye bhavet |
udvegaḥ kalaho hāniḥ śubhaṃ sarvaṃ nivārayet ‖80‖
sūryasya vāhe pravadanti vijñā jñānaṃ hy agamyasya tu niścayet |
śvāsena yuktasya tu śītaraśmeḥ pravāhakāle phalam anyathā syāt ‖81‖

atha viparīta lakṣaṇam
yadā pratyūṣakālena viparītodayo bhavet |
candrasthāne vahaty arko ravisthāne ca candramāḥ ‖82‖
prathame manasodvegaṃ dhanahānir dvitīyake |
tṛtīye gamanaṃ proktam iṣṭanāśaṃ caturthake ‖83‖
pañcame rājyavidhvaṃsaṃ ṣaṣṭhe sarvārthanāśanam |
saptame vyādhiduḥkhāni aṣṭame mṛtyum ādiśet ‖84‖
kālatraye dināny aṣṭau viparītaṃ yadā vahet |
tadā duṣṭaphalaṃ proktaṃ kiñcin nyūnaṃ tu śobhanam ‖85‖
prātarmadhyāhnayoś candraḥ sāyaṃkāle divākaraḥ |
tadā nityaṃ jayo labho viparītaṃ vivarjayet ‖86‖
vāme vā dakṣiṇe vāpi yatra saṃkramate śivaḥ |
kṛtvā tatpādam ādau ca yātrā bhavati siddhidā ‖87‖
candraḥ samapadaḥ kāryo ravis tu viṣamaḥ sadā |
pūrṇapādaṃ puraskṛtya yātrā bhavati siddhidā ‖88‖
candravāre catuṣpādaṃ pañcapādāś ca bhāskare |
evaṃ ca gamanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ sādhayed bhuvanatrayam ‖89‖
yatrāṅge vahate vāyus tad aṅgakarasantalāt |
suptotthito mukhaṃ spṛṣṭvā labhate vāñchitaṃ phalam ‖90‖
paradatte tathā grāhye gṛhān nirgamane 'pi ca |
yad aṅge vahate nāḍī grāhyaṃ tena karāṅghriṇā ‖91‖
na hāniḥ kalaho naiva kaṇṭakair nāpi bhidyate |
nivartate sukhī caiva sarvopadravavarjitaḥ ‖92‖
gurubandhunṛpāmātyeṣv anyeṣu śubhadāyinī |
pūrṇāṅge khalu kartavyā kāryasiddhir manaḥsthitā ‖93‖
aricaurādharmadharmā anyeṣāṃ vādinigrahaḥ |
kartavyāḥ khalu riktāyāṃ jayalābha sukhārthibhiḥ ‖94‖
dūradeśe vidhātavyaṃ gamanaṃ tu himadyutau |
abhyarṇadeśe dīpte tu taraṇāv iti kecana |95‖
yat kiñcit pūrvam uddiṣṭaṃ lābhādi samarāgamaḥ |
tat sarvaṃ pūṛṇanāḍīṣu jāyate nirvikalpakam ‖96‖
śūnyanāḍyā viparyastaṃ yat pūrvaṃ pratipāditam |
jāyate nānyathā caiva yathā sarvajñabhāṣitam ‖97‖
vyavahāre khaloccāṭe dveṣividyādi vañcake |
kupitasvāmicaurādhe pūrṇasthāḥ syur bhayaṅkarāḥ ‖98‖
dūrādhvani śubhaś candro nirvighno 'bhīṣṭasiddhidaḥ |
praveśakāryahetau ca sūryanāḍī praśasyate ‖99‖
ayogye yogyā nāḍyā yogyasthānepy ayogyatā |
kāryānubandhano jīvo yathā rudras tathā caret ‖100‖
candracāre viṣahate sūryo balivaśaṃ nayet |
suṣumnāyāṃ bhaven mokṣa eko devas tridhā sthitaḥ ‖101‖
śubhāny aśubhakāryāṇi kriyante 'harniśaṃ yadā |
tadā kāryānurodhena kāryaṃ nāḍī pracālanam ‖102‖

atha iḍā
sthirakarmaṇy alaṅkāre dūrādhvagamane tathā |
āśrame dharmaprāsāde vastūnāṃ saṃgrahe 'pi ca ‖103‖
vāpīkūpataḍāgādi pratiṣṭhā stambhadevayoḥ |
yātrā dāne vivāhe ca vastrālaṅkārabhūṣaṇe ‖104‖
śāntike pauṣṭike caiva divyauṣadhi rasāyane |
svasvāmidarśane mitre vāṇijye kaṇasaṃgrahe ‖105‖
gṛhapraveśe sevāyāṃ kṛṣau ca bījavāpane |
śubhakarmaṇi saṃdhau ca nirgame ca śubhaḥ śaśī ‖106‖
vidyārambhādi kāryeṣu bāndhavānāṃ ca darśane |
dharmamokṣe ca dharme ca dīkṣāyāṃ mantrasādhane ‖107‖
kālavijñāne sūtre tu catuṣpādagṛhāgame |
kālavyādhicikitsāyāṃ svāmisambodhane tathā ‖108‖
gajāśvārohane dhanvigajāśvānāṃ ca bandhane |
paropakaraṇe caiva nidhīnāṃ sthāpane tathā ‖109‖
gītavādyādi nṛtyādau nṛtyaśāstravicāraṇe |
puragrāmaniveśe ca tilakachatradhāraṇe ‖110‖
ārttiśokaviṣāde ca jvarite mūrchite 'pi vā |
svajanasvāmisambandhe annāderdārusaṃgrahe ‖111‖
strīṇām dantādi bhūṣāyāṃ vṛṣṭer āgamane tathā |
gurupūjā viṣādīnāṃ cālane ca varānane ‖112‖
iḍāyāṃ siddhidaṃ proktaṃ yogābhyāsādi karma ca |
tratrāpi varjayed vāyuṃ teja ākāśam eva ca ‖113‖
sarvakāryāṇi siddhayanti divārātri gatāny api |
sarveṣu śubhakāryeṣu candracāraḥ praśasyate ‖114‖

atha piṅgalā
kaṭhinakrūravidyānāṃ paṭhane pāṭhane tathā |
strīsaṃgaveśyāgamane mahānaukādhirohaṇe ‖115‖
bhraṣṭakārye surāpāne vīramantrādyupāsane |
vihvalodhvaṃsadeśādau viṣadānādi vairiṇām ‖116‖
śāstrābhyāse ca gamane mṛgayā paśuvikraye |
iṣṭikākāṣṭhapāṣāṇaratnagharṣaṇadāraṇe ‖117‖
gatyabhyāse yantratantre durgaparvatarohaṇe |
dyūte caurye gajāśvādi rathasādhanavāhane ‖118‖
vyāyāme māraṇoccāte ṣaṭkarmādikasādhane |
yakṣiṇīyakṣavetālaviṣabhūtādi nigrahe ‖119‖
kharoṣṭramahiṣādīnāṃ gajāśvārohaṇe tathā |
nadījalaughataraṇe bheṣaje lipilekhane ‖120‖
māraṇe mohane stambhe vidveṣoccaṭane vaśe |
preraṇe karṣaṇe kṣobhe dāne ca krayavikraye ‖121‖
pretākarṣaṇavidveṣe śatrunigrahaṇe 'pi ca |
khaḍgahaste vairiyuddhe bhoge vā rājadarśane |
bhojye snāne vyavahāre dīptakārye raviḥ śubhaḥ ‖122‖
bhuktamārgeṇa mandāgnau strīṇāṃ vaśyādi karmaṇi |
śayanaṃ sūryavāhena kartavyaṃ sarvadā budhaiḥ ‖123‖
krūrāṇi sarvakarmāṇi carāṇi vividhāni ca |
tāni siddhayanti sūryeṇa nātrakāryā vicāraṇā ‖124‖

atha suṣumnā
kṣaṇaṃ vāme kṣaṇaṃ dakṣe yadā vahati mārutaḥ |
suṣumnā sā ca vijñeyā sarvakāryaharā smṛtā ‖125‖
tasyāṃ nāḍyāṃ sthito vahnir jvalate kālarūpakaḥ |
viṣavat taṃ vijānīyāt sarvakāryavināśanam ‖126‖
yadā 'nukramam ullaṅghya yasya nāḍīdvayaṃ vahet |
tadā tasya vijānīyād aśubhaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ‖127‖

kumbhako yuddhakāle ca tisro nāḍyas trayī gatiḥ ‖249‖ corr. °kāla
hakārasya sakārasya vinā bhedaṃ svaraḥ katham |
so 'ham haṃsapadenaiva jīvo jayati sarvadā ‖250‖
śūnyāṅgaṃ pūritaṃ kṛtvā jīvāṅge gopayej jayam |
jīvāṅge ghātam āpnoti śūnyāṅgaṃ rakṣate sadā ‖251‖
vāme vā yadi vā dakṣe yadi pṛcchati pṛcchakaḥ |
pūrṇe ghāto na jāyeta śūnye ghātaṃ vinirdiśet ‖252‖
bhūtatattvenodare ghātaḥ padasthāne 'mbunā bhavet |
ūrusthāne 'gnitattvena karasthāne ca vāyunā ‖253‖
śirasi vyomatattve ca jñātavyo ghātanirṇayaḥ |
evaṃ pañcavidho ghātaḥ svaraśāstre prakāśitaḥ ‖254‖
yuddhakāle yadā candraḥ sthāyī jayati niścitam | corr. °kāla
yadā sūryapravāhas tu yāyī vijayate tadā ‖255‖
jayamadhye tu sandehe nāḍīmadhyaṃ tu lakṣayet |
suṣumnāyāṃ gate prāṇe samare śatru saṃkaṭam ‖256‖
yasyā nāḍyā bhavec cāras tāṃ diśaṃ yudhi saṃśrayet |
tadā 'sau jayam āpnoti nātra kāryā vicāraṇā ‖257‖
yadi saṅgrāmakāle tu vāmanāḍī sadā vahet | corr. °kāla
sthāyino vijayaṃ vidyād ripuvaśyodayo 'pi ca ‖258‖
yadi saṃgrāmakāle tu sūryas tu vyāvṛto vahet | corr. °kāla
tadā yāyi jayaṃ vidyāt devāsuramānave ‖259‖
raṇe harati śatrus taṃ vāmāyāṃ praviśen naraḥ |
sthānaṃ viṣuvacāreṇa jayaḥ sūryeṇa dhāvatā ‖260‖
yuddhadvaye kṛte praśne pūrṇasya prathame jayaḥ |
rikte caiva dvitīyas tu jayī bhavati nānyathā ‖261‖
pūrṇanāḍīgataḥ pṛṣṭhe śūnyāṅgaṃ ca tadāgrataḥ |
śūnyasthāne kṛtaḥ śatrur mriyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ‖262‖
vāmacāre samaṃ nāma yasya tasya jayo bhavet |
pṛcchako dakṣiṇe bhāge vijayī viṣamākṣaraḥ ‖263‖
yadā pṛcchati candrasya tadā sandhānam ādiśēt |
pṛcched yadā tu sūryasya tadā jānīhi vigraham ‖264‖
pārthive ca samaṃ yuddhaṃ siddhir bhavati vāruṇe |
yuddhe hi taijaso bhaṅgo mṛtyur vāyau nabhasyāpi ‖265‖
nimittikapramādād vā yadā na jñāyate 'nilaḥ |
pṛcchākāle tadā kuryād idaṃ yatnena buddhimān ‖266‖ corr. °kāla
niścalāṃ dhāraṇāṃ kṛtvā puṣpaṃ hastān nipātayet |
pūrṇāṅge puṣpapatanaṃ śūnyaṃ vā tatparaṃ bhavet ‖267‖
tiṣṭhann upaviśañ cāpi prāṇam ākarṣayan nijam |
manobhaṅgam akurvāṇaḥ sarvakāryeṣu jīvati ‖268‖
na kālo vividhaṃ ghoraṃ na śastraṃ na ca pannagāḥ |
na śatrur vyādhicaurādyāḥ śūnyasthānāśituṃ kṣamāḥ ‖269‖
jīvena sthāpayed vāyuṃ jīvenārambhayet punaḥ |
jīvena krīḍate nityaṃ dyūte jayati sarvathā ‖270‖
svarajñānabalād agre niṣphalaṃ koṭidhā bhavet |
ihaloke paratrāpi svarajñānī balī sadā ‖271‖
daśaśatāyutaṃ lakṣaṃ deśādhipabalaṃ kvacit |
śatakratusurendrāṇāṃ balaṃ koṭiguṇaṃ bhavet ‖272‖

śrī devy uvāca
parasparaṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ yuddhe prokto jayas tvayā |

īśvara uvāca

īśvara uvāca
candraṃ sūryeṇa cākṛṣya sthāpayej jīvamaṇḍale |
ājanmavaśagā rāmā kathito 'yaṃ tapodhanaiḥ ‖277‖

jīvasthāne gato jīvo bālā jīvāntakārakaḥ ‖278‖
rātryantayāmavelāyāṃ prasupte kāminījane |
brahmajīvaṃ pibed yas tu bālāprāṇaharo naraḥ ‖279‖
aṣṭākṣaraṃ japitvā tu tasmin kāle gate sati | corr. °kāle
tatkṣaṇaṃ dīyate candro moham āyāti kāminī ‖280‖
śayane vā prasaṅge vā yuvatyāliṅgane 'pi vā |
yaḥ sūryeṇa pibec candraṃ sa bhaven makaradhvajaḥ ‖281‖
śiva āliṅgyate śāktyā prasaṅge dakṣiṇe 'pi vā |
tatkṣaṇād dāpayed yas tu mohayet kāminīśatam ‖282‖
nava sapta trayaḥ pañca vārān saṅgas tu sūryabhe |
cander dvituryaṣaṭ kṛtvā vaśyā bhavati kāminī ‖283‖
sūryacandrau samākṛṣya sarpākrāntyā 'dharoṣṭhayoḥ |
mahāpadme sukhaṃ spṛṣṭvā vāramvāram idam caret ‖284‖
āprāṇam iti padmasya yāvan nidrāvaśaṃ gatā |
paścāj jāgarti velāyāṃ coṣyete galacakṣuṣī ‖285‖
anena vidhinā kāmī vaśayet sarvakāminīḥ |
idaṃ na vācyam anyasmin nityājñā parameśvarī ‖286‖
iti vaśīkaraṇam ‖

atha garbhaprakaraṇam
ṛtukālabhavānārī pañcame 'hni yadā bhavet |
sūryacandramasor yoge sevanāt putrasambhavaḥ ‖287‖
śaṅkhavallīṃ gavāṃ dugdhe pṛthvyāpo vahate yadā |
bharttur eva vaded vākyaṃ darpaṃ dehi tribhir vacaḥ ‖288‖
ṛtusnātā piben nārī ṛtudānaṃ tu yojayet |
rūpalāvaṇyasampanno narasiṃhaḥ prasūyate ‖289‖
suṣumnā sūryavāhena ṛtudānaṃ tu yojayet |
aṅgahīnaḥ pumān yas tu jāyate 'tra kuvigrahaḥ ‖290‖
viṣamāṅke divārātrau viṣamāṅke dinādhipaḥ |
candranetrāgnitattveṣu vandhyā putram avāpnuyāt ‖291‖
ṛtvārambhe raviḥ puṃsāṃ strīṇāṃ caiva sudhākaraḥ |
ubhayoḥ saṅgame prāpte vandhyā putram avāpnuyāt ‖292‖
ṛtvārambhe raviḥ puṃsāṃ śukrānte ca sudhākaraḥ |
anena kramayogena nādatte daivadārukam ‖293‖
candranāḍī yadā praśne garbhe kanyā tadā bhavet |
sūryo bhavet tadā putro dvayor garbho vihanyate ‖294‖
pṛthivyāṃ putrī jale putraḥ kanyakā tu prabhañjane |
tejasi garbhapātaḥ syān nabhasy api napuṃsakaḥ ‖295‖
candre strī puruṣaḥ sūrye madhyamārge napuṃsakaḥ |
garbhapraśne yadā dūtaḥ pūrṇe putraḥ prajāyate ‖296‖
śūnye śūnyaṃ yuge yugmaṃ garbhapātaś ca saṃkrame |
tattvavit sa vijānīyāt kathitaṃ tat tu sundari ‖297‖
garbhādhānaṃ mārute syāc ca duḥkhī
dikṣu khyāto vāruṇe saukhyayuktaḥ |
garbhasrāvaḥ svalpajīvaś ca vahnau
bhogī bhavyaḥ pārthivenārthayuktaḥ ‖298‖
dhanavān saukhyayuktaś ca bhogavān arthasaṃsthitiḥ |
syān nityaṃ vāruṇe tattve vyomni garbho vinaśyati ‖299‖
māhendre susutotpattir vāruṇe duhitā bhavet |
śeṣeṣu garbhahāniḥ syāj jātamātrasya vā mṛtiḥ ‖300‖
ravimadhyagataś candraś candramadhyagato raviḥ |
jñātavyaṃ gurutaḥ śīghraṃ na vedai śāstrakoṭibhiḥ ‖301‖
iti garbhaprakaraṇam ‖

atha samvatsaraphalam
caitraśuklapratipadi prātastattvavibhedataḥ |
paśyed vicakṣaṇo yogī dakṣiṇe cottarāyaṇe ‖302‖
candrodayasya velāyāṃ vahamāno 'tra tattvataḥ |
pṛthivy āpaūs tathā vāyuḥ subhikṣaṃ sarvasasyajam ‖303‖
tejovyomnor bhayaṃ ghoraṃ durbhikṣaṃ kālatattvataḥ |
evaṃ tattvaphalaṃ jñeyaṃ varṣe māse dineṣv api ‖304‖
madhyamā bhavati krūrā duṣṭā sarveṣu karmasu |
deśabhaṅgamahārogakleśakaṣṭādi duḥkhadā ‖305‖
meṣasaṃkrāntivelāyāṃ svarabhedaṃ vicārayet |
samvatsaraphalaṃ brūyāl lokānāṃ tattvacintakaḥ ‖306‖
pṛthivyādikatattvena dinamāsābdājaṃ phalam |
śobhanaṃ ca yathā duṣṭaṃ vyomamārutavahnibhiḥ ‖307‖
subhikṣaṃ rāṣṭravṛddhiḥ syād bahusasyā vasundharā |
bahuvṛṣṭis tathā saukhyaṃ pṛthvītattvaṃ vahed yadi ‖308‖
ativṛṣṭiḥ subhikṣaṃ syād ārogyaṃ saukhyam eva ca |
bahusasyā tathā pṛthvī aptattvaṃ vai vahed yadi ‖309‖
durbhikṣaṃ rāṣṭrabhaṅgaḥ syād utpattiś ca vinaśyati |
alpād alpatarā vṛṣṭir agnitattvaṃ vahed yadi ‖310‖
utpātopadravā bhītir alpā vṛṣṭiḥ syur ītayaḥ |
meṣasaṃkrānti velāyāṃ vāyutattvaṃ vahed yadi ‖311‖
meṣasaṃkrānti velāyāṃ vyomatattvaṃ vahed yadi |
tatrāpi śūnyatā jñeyā sasyādīnāṃ sukhasya ca ‖312‖
pūrṇapraveśane śvāse sasyaṃ tattvena sidhyati |
sūryacandre 'nyathābhūte saṃgrahaḥ sarvasiddhidaḥ ‖313‖
viṣame vahnitattvaṃ syāj jñāyate kevalaṃ nabhaḥ | corr. kevala
tatkuryād vastusaṃgraho dvimāse ca maharghatā ‖314‖
ravau saṃkramate nāḍī candram ante prasarpitā |
khānile vahniyogena rauravaṃ jagatītala ‖315‖
iti samvatsaraprakaraṇam ‖

atha rogaprakaraṇam
mahītattve svarogaś ca jale ca jalamātṛtaḥ |
tejasi kheṭavāṭīsthā śākinī pitṛdoṣataḥ ‖316‖
ādau śūnyagato dūtaḥ paścāt pūrṇe viśed yadi |
mūrcchito 'pi dhruvaṃ jīved yadarthaṃ pratipṛcchati ‖317‖
yasmin aṅge sthito jīvas tatrasthaḥ paripṛcchati |
tadā jīvati jīvo 'sau yadi rogair upadrutaḥ ‖318‖
dakṣiṇena yadā vāyur dūto raudrākṣaro vadet |
tadā jīvati jīvo 'sau candre samaphalaṃ bhavet ‖319‖
jīvākāraṃ ca vā dhṛtvā jīvākāraṃ vilokya ca |
jīvastho jīvitapraśne tasya syāj jīvitaṃ phalam ‖320‖
vāmācāre tathā dakṣapraveśe yatra vāhane |
tatrasthaḥ pṛcchate dūtas tasya siddhir na saṃśayaḥ ‖321‖
praśne cādhaḥ sthito jīvo nūnaṃ jīvo hi jīvati |
ūrdhvacārasthito jīvo jīvo yāti yamālayam ‖322‖
viparītākṣarapraśne riktāyāṃ pṛcchako yadi |
viparyayaṃ ca vijñeyaṃ viṣamasyodaye sati ‖323‖
candrasthāne sthito jīvo sūryasthāne tu pṛcchakaḥ |
tadā prāṇaviyukto 'sau yadi vaidyaśatair vṛtaḥ ‖324‖
piṅgalāyāṃ sthito jīvo vāme dūtas tu pṛcchati |
tadā 'pi mriyate rogī yadi trātā maheśvaraḥ ‖325‖
ekasya bhūtasya viparyayeṇa
rogābhibhūtir bhavatīha puṃsām |
tayor dvayor bandhusuhṛdvipattiḥ pakṣadvaye
vyatyayato mṛtiḥ syāt ‖326‖
iti rogaprakaraṇam ‖

atha kālaprakaraṇam
māsādau caiva pakṣādau vatsarādau yathā kramam |
kṣayakālaṃ parīkṣeta vāyucāravaśāt sudhīḥ ‖327‖
pañcabhūtātmakaṃ dīpaṃ śīvasnehena siñcitam |
rakṣayet sūryavātena prāṇī jīvaḥ sthiro bhavet ‖328‖
mārutaṃ bandhayitvā tu sūryaṃ bandhayate yadi |
abhyāsāj jīvate jīvaḥ sūryakāle 'pi vañcite ‖329‖
gaganāt sravate candraḥ kāyapadmāni siñcayet |
karmayogasadābhyāsair amaraḥ śaśisaṃśrayāt ‖330‖
śaśāṅkaṃ vārayed rātrau divāvāryo divākaraḥ |
ity abhyāsarato nityaṃ sa yogī nātra saṃśayaḥ ‖331‖
ahorātre yadaikatra vahate yasya mārutaḥ |
tadā tasya bhaven mṛtyuḥ sampūrṇe vatsaratraye ‖332‖
ahorātradvayaṃ yasya piṅgalāyāṃ sadā gatiḥ |
tasya varṣadvayaṃ proktaṃ jīvitaṃ tattvavedibhiḥ ‖333‖
trirātraṃ vahate yasya vāyur ekapuṭe sthitaḥ |
tadā saṃvatsarāyus taṃ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ ‖334‖
rātrau candro divā sūryo vahed yasya nirantaram |
jānīyāt tasya vai mṛtyuḥ ṣaṇmāsābhyantare bhavet ‖335‖
lakṣyaṃ lakṣita lakṣaṇena salile bhānur yadā dṛśyate |
kśīṇo dakṣiṇapaścimottarapuraḥ ṣaṭ tridvimāsaikataḥ ‖
madhye chidram idaṃ bhaved daśadinaṃ dhūmākulaṃ taddine |
sarvajñair api bhāṣitaṃ munivarair āyuḥ pramāṇaṃ sphuṭam ‖336‖
dūtaḥraktakaṣāyakṛṣṇavasano dantakṣato muṇḍitaḥ
tailābhyaktaśarīrarajjukakaro dīno 'śrupūrṇottaraḥ|
bhasmāṅgārakapālapāśamusalīsūryās tam āyāti yaḥ
kālīśūnyapadasthito gadayutaḥ kālānalasyādṛtaḥ ‖337‖
akasmāc cittavikṛtir akasmāt puruṣottamaḥ |
akasmād indriyotpātaḥ san nipātāgralakṣaṇam ‖338‖
śarīraṃ śītalam yasya prakṛtir vikṛtā bhavet |
tad ariṣṭaṃ samāsena vyāsatas tu nibodha me ‖339‖
duṣṭaśabdeṣu ramate 'śuddhaśabdeṣu cāpyati |
paścāt tapo bhaved yasya tasya mṛtyur na saṃśayaḥ ‖340‖
huṃkāraḥ śītalo yasya phūtkāro vahnisannibhaḥ |
mahāvaidyo bhaved yasya tasya mṛtyur bhaved dhruvam ‖341‖
jihvāṃ viṣṇupadaṃ dhruvaṃ surapadaṃ san mātṛkāmaṇḍalam
etāny evam arundhatīm amṛtaguṃ śukraṃ dhruvaṃ vā kṣaṇam |
eteṣv ekam api sphuṭaṃ na puruṣaḥ paśyet puraḥ preṣitaḥ
so 'vaśyaṃ viśatīha kālavadanaṃ samvatsarād ūrdhvataḥ ‖342‖
araśmibimbaṃ sūryasya vahneḥ śītāṃśumālinaḥ |
dṛṣṭvaikādaśamāsāyur naraś cordhvaṃ na jīvati ‖343‖
vāpyāṃ purīṣamūtrāṇi suvarṇaṃ rajataṃ tathā |
pratyakṣam athavā svapne daśamāsān na jīvati ‖344‖
kvacit paśyati yo dvīpaṃ suvarṇaṃ ca kaṣānvitam |
virūpāṇi ca bhūtāni navamāsān na jīvati ‖345‖
sthūlāṅgo 'pi kṛśaḥ kṛśo 'pi sahasā sthūlatvam ālabhyate
prāpto vā kanakaprabhāṃ yadi bhavet krūro 'pi kṛṣṇāc chaviḥ |
śūro bhīru sudhīr adharmanipuṇaḥ śānto vikārī pumān
ity evaṃ prakṛtiḥ prayāti calanaṃ māsāṣṭakaṃ jīvati ‖346‖
pīḍā bhavet pāṇitale ca jihvāmūle
tathā syād rudhiraṃ ca kṛṣṇam |
viddhaṃ na ca glāyati yatra dṛṣṭyā
jīven manuṣyaḥ sa hi saptamāsam ‖347‖
madhyāṅgulīnāṃ tritayaṃ na vakraṃ
rogaṃ vinā śuṣyati yasya kaṇṭhaḥ |
muhurmuhuḥ praśnavaśena jāḍyāt ṣaḍbhiḥ
sa māsaiḥ pralayaṃ prayāti ‖348‖ corr. pratayaṃ
na yasya smaraṇaṃ kiñcid vidyate stanacarmaṇi |
so 'vaśyaṃ pañcame māsi skandhārūḍho bhaviṣyati ‖349‖
yasya na sphurati jyotiḥ pīḍyate nayanadvayam |
maraṇaṃ tasya nirdiṣṭaṃ caturthe māsi niścitam ‖350‖
dantāś ca vṛṣaṇau yasya na kiñcid api pīḍyate |
tṛtīya māsato 'vaśyaṃ kālājñāyāṃ bhaven naraḥ ‖351‖
kālo dūrasthito vāpi yenopayena lakṣyate |
taṃ vadāmi samāsena yathā 'diṣṭaṃ śivāgame ‖352‖
ekāntaṃ vijanaṃ gatvā kṛtvādityaṃ ca pṛṣṭhataḥ |
nirīkṣayen nijacchāyāṃ kaṇṭhadeśe samāhitaḥ ‖353‖
tataś cākāśam īkṣeta hrīṃ parabrahmaṇe namaḥ |
aṣṭottaraśataṃ japtvā tataḥ paśyati śaṅkaram ‖354‖
śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaṃ nānārūpadharaṃ haram |
ṣaṇmāsābhyāsayogena bhūcarāṇāṃ patir bhavet
varṣadvayena te nātha kartā hartā svayaṃ prabhuḥ ‖355‖ tenātha?
trikālajñatvam āpnoti paramānandam eva ca|
satatābhyāsayogena nāsti kiñcit sudurlabham ‖356‖
tadrūpaṃ kṛṣṇavarṇaṃ yaḥ paśyati vyomni nirmale | corr. nirmala
ṣaṇmāsān mṛtyum āpnoti sa yogī nātra saṃśayaḥ ‖357‖
pīte vyādhir bhayaṃ rakte nīle hāniṃ vinirdiśet | corr. nīla
nānāvarṇo 'tha cet tasmin siddhiś ca gīyate mahān ‖358‖
pade gulphe ca jaṭhare tu vināśaḥ kramaśo bhavet |
vinaśyate yadā bāhū svayaṃ tu mriyate dhruvam ‖359‖
vāmabāhus tadā bhāryā naśyed iti na saṃśayaḥ |
dakṣiṇe bandhunāśo hi mṛtyur māse vinirdiśet ‖360‖
aśiro māsamaraṇam vinā jaṅghe dināṣṭakam |
aṣṭabhiḥ skandanāśena chāyālopena tatkṣaṇāt ‖361‖
prātaḥ pṛṣṭhagate ravau ca nimiṣāc chāyā 'ṅgulīś cādharaṃ
dṛṣṭvā 'rddhena mṛtis tv anantaramahocchāyāṃ naraḥ paśyati |
tatkarṇāṃ sakarāsya pārśvahṛdayābhāve kṣaṇārdhāt svayaṃ
diṅmūḍho hi naraḥ śirovagamato māsāṃs tu ṣaḍ jīvati ‖362‖
ekādi ṣoḍaśāhāni yadi bhānur nirantaram |
vahed yasya ca vai mṛtyuḥ śeṣāhena ca māsike ‖363‖
sampūrṇaṃ vahate sūryaś candramā naiva dṛśyate |
pakṣeṇa jāyate mṛtyuḥ kālajñenānubhāṣitam ‖364‖
mūtraṃ purīṣaṃ vāyuś ca samakālaṃ pravartate | corr. °kāla
tadā 'sau calito jñeyo daśāhe mriyate dhruvam ‖365‖
sampūrṇaṃ vahate candraḥ sūryo naiva ca dṛśyate |
māsena jāyate mṛtyuḥ kālajñenānubhāṣitam ‖366‖
arundhatīṃ dhruvaṃ caiva viṣṇos trīṇi padāni ca |
āyurhīnā na paśyanti caturthaṃ mātṛmaṇḍalam ‖367‖
arundhatī bhavej jihvā dhruvo nāsāgram eva ca |
bhruvau viṣṇupadaṃ jñeyaṃ tārakaṃ mātṛmaṇḍalam ‖368‖
navabhruvaṃ saptaghoṣaṃ pañcatārāṃ trināsikām |
jihvām ekadinaṃ proktaṃ mriyate mānavo dhruvam ‖369‖
koṇāvakṣṇoraṅgulibhyāṃ kiñcit pīḍya nirīkṣayet |
yadā na dṛśyate bindur daśāhena bhaven mṛtaḥ ‖370‖
tīrthasthānena dānena tapasā sukṛtena ca |
japair dhyānena yogena jāyate kālavañcanā ‖371‖
śarīraṃ nāśayanty ete doṣā dhātumalās tathā |
samas tu vāyur vijñeyo balatejovivardhanaḥ ‖372‖ corr. valatejo°
rakṣaṇīyas tato deho yato dharmādi sādhanam |
yogābhyāsāt samāyānti sādhuyāpyās tu sādhyatām |
asādhyā jīvitaṃ ghnanti na tatrāsti pratikriyā ‖373‖
yeṣāṃ hṛdi sphurati śaśvatam advitīyaṃ
tejas tamo nivahanāśakaraṃ rahasyam |
teṣām akhaṇḍaśaśiramyasukāntibhājāṃ 
svapne 'pi no bhavati kālabhayaṃ narāṇām ‖374‖
iḍā gaṅgeti vijñeyā piṅgalā yamunā nadī |
madhye sarasvatī vidyāt prayāgādi samas tathā ‖375‖
ādau sādhanam ākhyātaṃ sadyaḥ pratyayakārakam |
baddhapadmāsano yogī bandhayed uḍḍiyānakam ‖376‖
pūrakaḥ kumbhakaś caiva recakaś ca tṛtīyakaḥ |
jñātavyo yogibhir nityaṃ dehasaṃśuddhihetave ‖377‖
pūrakaḥ kurute vṛddhiṃ dhātusāmyaṃ tathaiva ca |
kumbhakaḥ staṃbhanaṃ kuryāj jīvarakṣāvivardhanam ‖378‖
recako harate pāpaṃ kuryād yogapadaṃ vrajet |
paścāt saṃgrāmavat tiṣṭhel layabandhaṃ ca kārayet ‖379‖
kumbhayet sahajaṃ vāyuṃ yathāśakti prakalpayet |
recayec candramārgeṇa sūryeṇāpūrayet sudhīḥ ‖380‖
candraṃ pibati sūryaś ca sūryaṃ pibati candramāḥ |
anyonya kālabhāvena jīved ācandratārakam ‖381‖
svīyāṅge vahate nāḍī tan nāḍīrodhanaṃ kuru |
mukhabandham amuñcan vai pavanaṃ jāyate yuvā ‖382‖
mukhanāsākṣikarṇāntānaṅgulībhir nirodhayet |
tattvodayam iti jñeyam ṣaṇmukhīkaraṇaṃ priyam ‖383‖
tasya rūpaṃ gatiḥ svādo maṇḍalalakṣaṇaṃ tv idam |
sa vetti mānavo loke saṃsargād api mārgavit ‖384‖
nirāśo niṣkalo yogī na kiñcid api cintayet |
vāsanām unmanāṃ kṛtvā kālaṃ jayati līlayā ‖385‖
viśvasya vedikā śaktir netrābhyāṃ paridṛśyate |
tatrasthaṃ tu mano yasya yāmamātraṃ bhaved iha ‖386‖
tasyāyur vardhate nityaṃ ghaṭikātrayamānataḥ |
śivenoktaṃ purā tantre siddhasya guṇagahvare ‖387‖
baddhvā padmāsanaṃ ye gudagatapavanaṃ san niruddhyāptam uccaiḥ
taṃ tasyāpānarandhre kramajitam anilaṃ prāṇaśāktyā niruddhya |
ekībhūtaṃ suṣumnā vivaram upagataṃ brahmarandhre ca nītvā
nikṣipyākāśamārge śivacaraṇaratā yānti te kepi dhanyāḥ ‖388‖
etaj jānāti yogī ya etat paṭhati nityaśaḥ |
sarvaduḥkhavinirmukto labhate vāñchitaṃ phalam ‖389‖
svarajñānaṃ nare yatra lakṣmīḥ padatale bhavet | corr. °tala
sarvatra ca śarīre 'pi sukhaṃ tasya sadā bhavet ‖390‖
praṇavaḥ sarvavedānāṃ brāhmaṇe bhāskaro yathā |
mṛtyuloke tathā pūjyaḥ svarajñānī pumān api ‖391‖
nāḍītrayaṃ vijānāti tattvajñānaṃ tathaiva ca |
naiva tena bhavet tulyaṃ lakṣe koṭirasāyanam ‖392‖
ekākṣarapradātāraṃ nāḍībhedavivecakam |
pṛthivyāṃ nāsti tad dravyaṃ yad datvā cānṛṇo bhavet ‖393‖
svaratattvam tathā yuddhaṃ devi vaśyaṃ striyas tathā |
garbhādhānaṃ ca rogaś ca kalārddhenaivam ucyate ‖394‖
evam pravartitaṃ loke prasiddhaṃ siddhayogibhiḥ |
candrārkagrahaṇe jāpyaṃ paṭhatāṃ siddhidāyakam ‖395‖
svarasthāne tu samāsīno nidrāṃ cāhāram alpakam |

atha devādidevasya śivasyāmitatejasaḥ|

sarvanugrāhikā śaktiramoghā balaśālinī|| 1||

ekānekavibhāgena saṃsthitāsau śivecchayā|

vidyāvidyeśvareśānī bhāti sādibhave pade|| 2||

tayodbalitasāmarthyāḥ sarvasya prabhaviṣṇavaḥ|

ananteśādividyeśā babhūvuḥ kāraṇecchayā|| 3||

tebhyo .amitabalāścānye tebhyaścānye niyoginaḥ|

bhavanti vimalātmānaḥ sitāsitaphalapradāḥ|| 4||

tebhyo .api sāñjanāścānye patīcchānuvidhāyinaḥ|

yebhyo nikhilamutpannaṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamam|| 5||

prabhuśakteḥ samutpannā mantrāścāmoghaśaktayaḥ|

śivecchayaiva ceṣṭante śivānte vitate .adhvani|| 6||

adhvā yātavyamārgo .atra sa ca patyurna vidyate|

na ca yāturgītaḥ puṃso vibhutvamanayoryataḥ|| 7||

vibhutve gatimattvaṃ tu vibhutā gatimattvataḥ|

na jātu yujyate yasmādvastuto .adhvā kutastayoḥ|| 8||

evaṃ saṃpraśnite .arthe .asminmayā purvaṃ dvijottamāḥ|

prāha gambhīrayā vācā prabhurlokeśvareśvaraḥ|| 9||

patipuṃsorvibhutve .api drāghiṣṭho .adhvā tayoryathā|

tathocyate śṛṇu brahmannayanaṃ puruṣasya ca|| 10||

dīkṣākarma krameṇoktaṃ māyāpariṇatau yataḥ|

so .adhvā pariṇatistasmāddeśātiśayavartinī|| 11||

puṃbandhabhāvamāpannā kramaśaḥ patyuricchayā|

vyutkrameṇāpaneyāsau śivāya gamanaṃ prati|| 12||

antaraṅgatarā śodhyā bahiraṅgatarāpi ca|

bahiraṅge .apanīte syādantaraṅgakriyākramaḥ|| 13||

evamadhvā śivasyokta udbandhakramayuktitaḥ|

loke cādhvavyavastheyaṃ dṛṣṭā vastunibandhane|| 14||

dagdhasaṃsārabījasya yā puṃso .apaścimā sthitiḥ|

sā gatistasya vijñeyā na deśāntarasaṃsthitiḥ|| 15||

dharmāddharmāntaravyaktau satyāṃ loke gataṃ yathā|

procyate paṭaparṇādi vastu tadvatpumānapi|| 16||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe tṛtīya anugrahaśaktipaṭalaḥ||

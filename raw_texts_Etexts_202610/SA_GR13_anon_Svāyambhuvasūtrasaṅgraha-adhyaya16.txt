athācāryo .abhiṣekārthamanantavijayaṃ yajet|

kāmyānāṃ ca prasūtyarthaṃ nidhibhūtamidaṃ yataḥ|| 1||

aiśānyāṃ diśi tasyādau iṣṭakābhiḥ prakalpite|

dvihaste bhadrapīṭhe tu sukṛte .aṣṭāṅgulocchrite|| 2||

vitānopari saṃcchinne puṣpamālāvilambite|

parito vaijayantībhiranekābhiralaṃkṛte|| 3||

puṣpākṣatatilākīrṇe dhūpāmodamanorame|

vicitrapatravallībhiḥ svastikādyaiśca śobhite|| 4||

darbhaviṣṭaravinyaste śivapīṭhaprakalpite|

gandhapuṣpākṣataiḥ śiṣyaṃ tatrasthamabhiṣecayet|| 5||

kalaśairambhusampūrṇaiḥ sraksūtrakṛtakañcukaiḥ|

śaṅkhasvastikapadmāṅkaiścandanena sucarcitaiḥ|| 6||

nalinīdalavinyastaiścūtapallavaśobhitaiḥ|

sarvaratnauṣadhīgandhakuśadūrvāṅkurodakaiḥ|| 7||

sauvarṇai rājataistāmraiḥ mṛnmayairvā navairdṛḍhaiḥ|

prācyādidikṣu vinyastaiḥ paripūtaiḥ śivāmbhasā|| 8||

ananteśādividyeśamantritaiḥ pūrvavatkramāt|

aṣṭābhirmūlavidyābhiḥ mantrakośaiśca secayet|| 9||

śaṅkhadundubhinirghoṣairvīṇāveṇusamanvitaiḥ|

gītaiśca jayaśabdaiśca tadā tamabhinandayet|| 10||

maṇḍalasya tataḥ pārśve dakṣiṇe paścime .api vā|

nītvā saṃsthāpayetpīṭhe sitavastrāvakuṇṭhite|| 11||

mantrapīṭhatvamāpanne kuśapuṣpākṣatottare|

adhikārapade tasminnupaveśya tamarcayet|| 12||

sakalīkṛtya vidhinā śivahastaṃ prakalpya ca|

gandhapuṣpapavitrādyairyathāsambhavato guruḥ|| 13||

yogapaṭṭākṣasūtraṃ ca saṃhitāpustakaṃ tathā|

kartarīṃ karaṇīsūtramācāryāyopapādayet|| 14||

evaṃ sampūjya vidhivadadhikārapade sthitam|

tato .abhivādayecchiṣyamācāryapadato guruḥ|| 15||

praveśya maṇḍalaṃ paścāddakṣiṇāmūrtimāśritam|

prabrūyāddevadeveśamāsanasthamidaṃ vacaḥ|| 16||

śivārcanajapadhyānahomakarmāṇi śaktitaḥ|

anugrahapadasthena kartavyāni dine dine|| 17||

guruparamparāyātaṃ tantrārthakathanaṃ sakṛt|

kartavyaṃ hi tvayā vipra śivajñānānurāgiṇām|| 18||

śucīnāṃ suvinītānāmācārāhitacetasām|

śivabhaktiprapannānāṃ manovākkāyakarmabhiḥ|| 19||

itthaṃ guṇavatāmevaṃ mumukṣūṇāmanugrahaḥ|

kartavyo .aṅgāvikalpena śivājñānuvidhāyinām|| 20||

adya saṅkrāmito yo .ayamadhikāro mayā tvayi|

kartavyaṃ hi tvayā yāvannaiva saṅkrāmito .anyataḥ|| 21||

praṇamya śirasā devaṃ pratipadya yathoditam|

brūyādavighnato .asmākamidamastu śivekṣaṇam|| 22||

tato homaṃ prakurvīta yathoktena padādhvanā|

śivapūrṇāhutiṃ hutvā brahmabhirhomamācaret|| 23||

śivāṅgaiścaiva vidyāṅgairvidyeśairmūlavidyayā|

trirāhutirato homaḥ kartavyaḥ padavartinā|| 24||

guruparvaprarohātmā prāpyaitāṃ padavīṃ dvijaḥ|

ācāryavratamācāryaścaretsarvavratottamam|| 25||

sādhakasyābhiṣekaṃ tu mantrahomādanantaram|

kuryātsiddhividhānajñaḥ siddhikāmasya deśikaḥ|| 26||

prāguktamaṇḍale devamiṣṭvā pūrvavadeva hi|

śaktimūle tataḥ sādhya mantraṃ sarvatra pūjayet|| 27||

puṣpaiḥ sugandhibhiścaiva gandhairgandhodakaistathā|

dhūpairāmodabahulaiḥ suśvetaśca patatrakaiḥ|| 28||

balibhirvividhaiḥ pūtairbhakṣyabhojyaiḥ susaṃskṛtaiḥ|

arghyairaṣṭāṅgasampannaiḥ dīpairdiśi diśi sthitaiḥ|| 29||

āpaḥ kṣīrakuśāstrāṇi taṇḍulāḥ sumanāstilāḥ|

arghyo .aṣṭāṅga iti prokto yavaiḥ siddhārthakaiḥ saha|| 30||

aṣṭottaraśataṃ homaṃ tadardhaṃ pādameva vā|

āhutīnāṃ tu vijñeyo mantrāṇāmanurūpataḥ|| 31||

ājyakṣīraṃ tilaṃ vātha ghṛtena madhunā plutam|

śrīphalāni ghṛtāktāni samidho brahmavṛkṣajāḥ|| 32||

āmalīphalamātraśca mahiṣākṣasya gugguloḥ|

ghṛtāktā gulikā śreṣṭhā homayenmantrasiddhaye|| 33||

tataḥ pūrvoktavidhinā sādhyamantrāsane sthitam|

tantrārthajñaṃ jitakrodhaṃ sādhakaṃ hyabhiṣecayet|| 34||

vidyāṅgaiśca susannaddhaṃ sādhyaikagatacetasam|

śaucopavāsasampannaṃ gurubhaktaṃ dṛḍhavratam|| 35||

prāguktakalaśāmbhobhiḥ sādhyamantrābhimantritaiḥ|

nīrājanakapūrvaiśca kaiścitpītaiḥ sthitaistathā|| 36||

dakṣiṇāyāṃ sthito mūrteruttarābhimukho guruḥ|
tikamūrteruttarā°
        ; mūrtau uttarā°

pūjayetpūrvavaddhīmān sakalīkṛtya sādhakam|| 37||

pradakṣiṇamathāvṛtya praviśya bhavanaṃ guroḥ|

jānubhyāmavanīṃ gatvā saśiṣyo deśikottamaḥ|| 38||

dhyātvā śivaṃ tathā mantramañjalau sādhakasya hi|

dadyānmantraṃ samudgīrya kuśavāritilākṣataiḥ|| 39||

tato vijñāpayeddevaṃ siddhirastvasya śaṅkara|

avighnena yathāśāstraṃ pravṛttasya śivecchayā|| 40||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe .abhiṣekapaṭalaḥ ṣoḍaśaḥ||

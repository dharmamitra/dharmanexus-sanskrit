atha kālasaṃkhyānam || 154 ||
4258
ṣaḍbhiḥ prāṇaiḥ palaṃ proktaṃ ṣaṣṭibhir ghaṭikā ca taiḥ |
ṣaṣṭibhir ghaṭakābhiḥ syād ahorātraṃ tu mānavam || 1 ||
4259
dinaiḥ pañcadaśaiḥ pakṣaḥ śruklaḥ kṛṣṇaś ca gīyate |
pakṣadvayena māsaḥ syāt pitṝṇāṃ tad aharniśam || 2 ||
4260
ṛtur māsadvayenaiva ṣaṇmāsair ayanaṃ smṛtam |
ayanadvitayaṃ varṣo devānāṃ vāsaro niśā || 3 ||
4261
taddvātriṃśatsahasrais tu caturlakṣaiḥ kaliḥ smṛtaḥ |
śeṣaṃ yugatrayaṃ jñeyaṃ kaler dvitricaturguṇam || 4 ||
4262
khacatuṣkaṃ radā vedāś caturyugam udāhṛtam |
cāturyugaikasaptatyā manvantaram udāhṛtam || 5 ||
4263
caturbhir arvudaiḥ kalpaṃ dvātriṃśadbhiś ca koṭibhiḥ |
caturdaśa surendrāś ca patanti brahmaṇohani || 6 ||
4264
caturyugasahasreṇa kalpākhyaṃ brahmaṇo dinam |
tatpramāṇā tathā rātriḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārakāriṇī || 7 ||
4265
atha kālāgnirudrasya tṛtīyanayanotthitā |
jvālā dahati tat sarvaṃ nirvāṇaṃ brahmaṇo yataḥ ||8 ||
4266

4267

brahmādīnāṃ tathā śakteḥ kṣayo janma punaḥ punaḥ ||10 ||
4268

īśaḥ sadāśive sopi śaktau śaktiḥ pare śive || 11 ||
4269

4270

atha śarīram ||255||
4271
asti kāraṇam avyaktaṃ sarvavyāpi parāparam |
sāṃnidhyād api durgrāhyaṃ viśvamūrtyopalakṣitam||1||
4272
nākāraṃ na nirākāraṃ na ca strīpuṃnapuṃsakam|
nāpas tejo marud vyoma bhūmidūre na cāntike||2||
4273
tato 'nicchasya dehasya śaktir icchety ajāyata|
cidrūpiṇyā tayā krīḍāṃ kartum aicchat sa līlayā||3||
4274
kriyārūpā guṇamayī tasyā buddhir ajāyata|
jñānaśaktir iti khyātā kriyāśaktiś ca sā smṛtā||4||
4275
buddher ahaṃ tataś cittaṃ śaktis tasyāḥ sadāśivaḥ|
nabhorūpī tato vāyur īśvaraḥ samajāyata||5||
4276
īśvarāt samabhūd rudro jyotirmaya umāpatiḥ|
rudrād viṣṇur abhūd ādyas trailokyaparipālakaḥ||6||
4277
viṣṇunāpy akṣayo jāto brahmāyaṃ pṛthivīmayaḥ|
tadartham abhavat pūrvaṃ brahmāṇḍaṃ kanakaprabham||7||
4278
tena sṛṣṭaṃ svaśaktyedaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram|
pañcabhiḥ saha saṃbhūya pañcabhūtamayātmakaiḥ||8||
4279
tato brahmā dvidhā bhūtaḥ śivaśaktivibhāgataḥ|
strīpuṃrūpeṇa saukhyārthaṃ suratenāsṛjat prajāḥ||9||
4280
tataś caturmukhasyāsan daśa putrā manobhavāḥ|
teṣāṃ daśa dadau kanyā manojātāḥ prajāpatiḥ||10||
4281
te sarve jñānaniratāḥ susaṃtuṣṭās tapodhanāḥ|
teṣāṃ putraiś ca pautraiś ca vyāptaṃ sarvam idaṃ jagat||11||
4282

daśaputreṣu vikhyātas tasmāj jātaś ca kaśyapaḥ||12||
4283

utpattihetur viśvasya bhūtagrāmāṃś caturdaśa||13||
4284

ekadhā mānuṣī yonir ime bhūtāś caturdaśa||14||
4285

anyeṣām api kāryeṣu pārthivāḥ sapta dhātavaḥ||15||
4286

maithunānandaniratā viṣayāsaktacetasaḥ||16||
4287

ubhayor yogato bījaṃ brahmarūpaṃ pravartate||17||
4288

saumyaṃ reto brahmarūpaṃ tasmiṃs tatra vilambate||18||
4289

tasyāṃ prajāḥ prajāyante yadīcchā pārameśvarī||19||
4290

tadā saṃjāyate jīvo vahniḥ sūryamaṇer iva||20||
4291

napusakaṃ samatvena karmaṇo niyatir yathā||21||
4292

carmaṇācchāditaḥ sapta dhātavaḥ syur anukramāt||22||
4293
piṇḍaṃ majjāsupūrṇānām asnthāṃ [? sic] ṣaṣṭiśatais tribhiḥ|
dvāsaptatisahasrābhir nāḍibhiḥ pañjaraṃ kṛtam||23||
4294

tenedaṃ jñānaśaktyarthaṃ kṛtaṃ veśma kriyāmayam||24||
4295

jñānaśaktyā yutā vāpi lakṣyate cetanāvatī||25||
4296

nātmānaṃ na paraṃ vetti viceṣṭati tadicchayā||26||
4297
prāṇāpānamayī śaktiḥ ṣaṭcakrādhārasaṃyutam|
śāntaṃ śivaṃ vaśīkṛtya vartate kāmakārataḥ||27||
4298
ādhāraḥ kandam ity uktaṃ svādhiṣṭhānaṃ ca janmabhūḥ|
nābhis tu maṇipūrākhyaṃ hṛdayaṃ viddhy anāhatam||28||
4299
viśuddhiḥ kaṇṭha ity uktam ājñācakraṃ bhruvor antare|
ādhāro hi caturbhinnaḥ svādhiṣṭhānaṃ ca ṣaḍdalaiḥ||29||
4300
daśabhir maṇipūrākhyaṃ dvādaśais tu anāhatam|
viśuddhiḥ ṣoḍaśair bhinnam ājñācakraṃ dvayena tu||30||
4301
kālāgnirudra ādhāre śaktiḥ kuṇḍalinī tathā|
ānandākhyā sva[sic]dhiṣṭhāne śaktyā kāmākhyayā saha||31||
4302
nābhau brahmā hṛdi śrīśaḥ kaṇṭhe rudro vyavasthitaḥ|
īśvaro lambikāyāṃ ca tadūrdhve ca sadāśivaḥ||32||
4303
jyotiḥsthānāni caitāni ṣaṭcakrāṇi jagur budhāḥ|
nāsāmūlaṃ viduḥ sthānaṃ nādasyotpattikāraṇam||33||
4304
tasyordhvaṃ yoginīcakraṃ tadūrdhve śaktir āsthitā|
śaktyūrdhve tu śikhā proktā śikhānte dhūmavattamaḥ||34||
4305
tamaso 'nte paraṃ jyotir alakṣyaḥ parameśvaraḥ|
yaṃ dṛṣṭvaiva vilīyante yoginaḥ sthiracetasaḥ||35||
4306
carācareṣu piṇḍasthaṃ padasthaṃ khānilādiṣu|
bhāskarādiṣu rūpasthaṃ rūpātītaṃ paraṃ viduḥ||36||
4307
dvāsaptatisahasrāṇi kandān nāḍyo vinirgatāḥ|
praviśya nābhideśeṣu sarvāṅgeṣu tato gatāḥ||37||
4308
iḍā ca piṅgalā caiva suṣuṃṇā ca sarasvatī|
gāndhārī hastijihvā ca pūṣā caiva nigadyate||38||
4309
alambuṣā kuhūś caiva śaṅkhinī daśamī matā|%4308a-4309b = GS 27a-28b, except GS has tṛtīyakā for sarasvatī and yaśasvinī for nigadyate
etāḥ prāṇavahā jñeyāḥ pradhānā daśa nāḍikāḥ||39||
4310
iḍā nāsāpuṭe vāme piṅgalā dakṣiṇe bhavet|
suṣumṇā tālu bhittvaiva brahmadvāraṃ pravartitā||40||
4311
sarasvatī ca jihvāyāṃ gāndhārī vāmakarṇake|
dakṣiṇe hastijihvākhyā pūṣā vāme ca locane||41||
4312
alambuṣā dakṣiṇe syād bhujadvāre kuhūr bhavet|
ā brahmarandhrān mārgeṇa brahmadaṇḍasya śaṅkhinī|
sadā binduvahā jñeyā liṅgadvāre ca śaṅkhinī||42||
4313

4314

dvāsaptatisahasrāsu nāḍīṣu vicaraty alam||44||
4315
hṛdi prāṇo gude 'pānaḥ samāno nābhisaṃsthitaḥ|
udānaḥ kaṇṭhadeśe ca vyānaḥ sarvaśarīragaḥ||45||% = ŚS 3.7
4316

kṛkaḥ kṣutodgārakaro devadattas tu jṛmbhakaḥ|
dhanaṃjayo nādakaraḥ syād evaṃ daśadhā marut||46||
4317

4318

hṛdgataṃ jīvam āśvāsya bahir gatvā nivartate||48||
4319

4320

4321

tiṣṭhan karoti caitanyadyotaṃ dīpo ghaṭe yathā||51||
4322

4323

4324

4325

4326

4327

4328

4330

4331

4337

4338

4350

apāne mūlakandākhye kāmarūpaṃ ca taj jaguḥ||5||
4352
tad eva vahnikuṇḍaṃ syāc chaktiḥ kuṇḍalinī tathā|
tāṃ jīvarūpiṇīṃ dhyāyej jyotiṣkāṃ muktihetave||6||
4353
svādhiṣṭhānaṃ dvitīyaṃ syāc cakraṃ tanmadhyagaṃ viduḥ|
paścimābhimukhaṃ liṅgaṃ pravālāṅkurasaṃnibham|
tatroḍḍīyāṇapīṭhe tu tad dhyātvā karṣayej jagat||7||
4354
tṛtīyaṃ nābhicakraṃ syāt tanmadhye bhujagī sthitā|

tāṃ dhyātvā sarvasiddhīnāṃ bhājanaṃ jāyate budhaḥ||8||
4355
caturthaṃ hṛdaye cakraṃ  vijñeyaṃ tadadhomukham|
jyotīrūpaṃ ca tanmadhye haṃsaṃ dhyāyet prayatnataḥ|
taṃ dhyāyato jagat sarvaṃ vaśyaṃ syān nātra saṃśayaḥ||9||
4356
pañcamaṃ kaṇṭhacakraṃ syāt tatra vāme iḍā bhavet|
dakṣiṇā piṅgalā jñeyā suṣumṇā madhyataḥ sthitā|
tatra dhyātvā śucir jyotiḥ siddhīnāṃ bhājanaṃ bhavet||10||
4357
ṣaṣṭhaṃ tu tālukācakraṃ ghaṇtṭikāsthānam ucyate|

tatra śūnye layaṃ kṛtvā mukto bhavati niścitam||11||
4358
bhrūcakraṃ saptamaṃ vidyād bindusthānaṃ ca tad viduḥ|
bhruvor madhye vartulaṃ ca dhyātvā jyotiḥ pramucyate||12||
4359
aṣṭamaṃ brahmarandhraṃ syāt paraṃ nirvāṇasūcakaṃ|
tad dhyātvā sūcikāgrābhaṃ dhūmākāraṃ vimucyate|
tac ca jālandharaṃ jñeyaṃ mokṣadaṃ līnacetasām||13||
4360

saṃvidrūpā ca tanmadhye śaktir ūrdhvā sthitā parā|
tatra pūrṇagirau pīṭhe śaktiṃ dhyātvā vimucyate||14||%4351-4360 is quoted in Yogacintāmaṇi under "śāstrāntare"; it is very similar to SSP 2.1-9 (check also Netratantra)
4361

4362

4363

atha rājayogaḥ

4364

4365

4366

4367

4368

4369

4370

4371

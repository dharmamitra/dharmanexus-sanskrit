śivaṃ praṇamya paramaṃ nidhaneśamataḥ param|

jñānadīkṣe pravakṣyāmi te śṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥ|| 1||

athātmamalamāyākhyakarmabandhavimuktaye|

vyaktaye ca śivatvasya śivājjñānaṃ pravartate|| 2||

tadekamapyanekatvaṃ śivavaktrāmbujodbhavam|

parāpareṇa bhedena gacchatyarthapratiśrayāt|| 3||

evametatsureśānaḥ śāstrasadbhāvamuttamam|

sūtradvayena saṃgṛhya prāha vistaraśaḥ punaḥ|| 4||

athātmāvimalo baddhaḥ punarmuktaśca dīkṣayā|

vijñeyaḥ sa tridhāvasthaḥ kevalaḥ sakalo .amalaḥ|| 5||

acetano vibhurnityo guṇahīno .akriyo .aprabhuḥ|

vyāghātabhāgaśaktaśca śodhyo bodhyo .akalaḥ paśuḥ|| 6||

pūrvaṃ māyātmakābandhāditthamātmā sthito mayā|

prokto gurumukhoktena ślokabandhena hi dvijāḥ|| 7||

bhoktā bhojakabhāvena nānābhogavisarpiṇā|

sāmprataṃ procyate baddho bandhenātmā kalādinā|| 8||

atyantamalinasya prāguktasyāvikāriṇaḥ|

kalā saṃśleṣamāyāti śivecchānuvidhāyinī|| 9||

kalodbalitacaitanyo vidyādarśitagocaraḥ|

rāgeṇa rañjitaścāpi buddhyādikaraṇaistataḥ|| 10||

māyādyavaniparyantatattvabhūtātmavartmani|

bhuṅkte tatra sthito bhogānbhogaikarasikaḥ pumān|| 11||

bhogo .asya vedanā puṃsaḥ sukhaduḥkhādilakṣaṇā|

tāṃ samarthitacaitanyaḥ pumānabhyeti karmataḥ|| 12||

karmataśca śarīrāṇi viṣayāḥ karaṇāni ca|

bhogasaṃsiddhaye bhokturbhavanti na bhavanti ca|| 13||

evaṃ māyāñjanastho .aṇurnijadoṣatiraskṛtaḥ|

yāti tanmayatāṃ teṣu māyābhogeṣu rañjitaḥ|| 14||

saṃsārī viṣayī bhoktā kṣetrī kṣetrajña eva ca|

śarīro ceti baddhātmā sakalaścocyate budhaiḥ|| 15||

śivecchayā purānantā śaivī śaivārthadāyikā|

sā śaktirāpatatyādyā puṃso janmanyapaścime|| 16||

tannipātātkṣaratyasya malaṃ saṃsārakāraṇam|

kṣīṇe tasminyiyāsā syātparaṃ niḥśreyasam prati|| 17||

sa deśikamanuprāpya dīkṣāvicchinnabandhanaḥ|

prayāti śivasāyujyaṃ nirmalo niranuplavaḥ|| 18||

anena kramayogena parāṃ kevalatāṃ gataḥ|

anādyaśuddhiśūnyatvātprāpnoti na bhavāntaram|| 19||

itthamātmā samākhyātaḥ sarvāvasthāsvavasthitaḥ|

sāmprataṃ procyate brahman pāśajālamanantakam|| 20||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe paśuvicārapaṭalaḥ prathamaḥ||

sthito dhyāyannantaḥ kimapi caritaṃ svena bhavata- ściraṃ vārtāmenāmatha madupalabhya pracalitaḥ/

jaṭī cīrī rāmetyamṛtavibhavaṃ nāma rasayan muhurharṣodbhrāntaḥ prakṛtisahito'bhyeti bharataḥ//30//

rāmaḥ/ 
(sollāsam/) aho cirāyāyuṣmatsauhārdamupalabhāmaha iti sarvānandānāmupari vartāmahe//

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ 
(sautsukyam/) sakhe mārute kutrāryaḥ//

hanūmān/ ya ete sainyasya purataḥ pañcaṣāstanmadhye puraḥsaraḥ sānujaḥ sa mahātmā bharataḥ//

(lakṣmaṇo nirvarṇayati//)

sītā/ 
(nirūpya/) kahaṃ aṇṇāriso jjevva dīsaï//

vibhīṣaṇaḥ/ haṃho vimānarāja/ cirāya bandhujanadarśanāliṅganasambhāvanādinā mithaḥ pramodamanubhavantvete mahānubhāvāḥ/ tat kṣaṇaṃ virama//

(sarve vimānāvataraṇaṃ nāṭayanti//)

(tataḥ praviśataḥ katicanapradhānapuruṣaparivṛtau bharataśatrughnau//)

rāmaḥ/ 
(sarabhasaṃ pādapatitaṃ bharatamutthāpya/) ehyehi vatsa/

anubhāvayati brahmānandasākṣātkriyāmiva/

sparśaste jaṭharāmbhojaprasphurannālakarkaśaḥ//31//

(iti nirbharamāliṅgya visṛjati/ lakṣmaṇaḥ sapādapatanaṃ bharatamāliṅgati/ śatrughno rāmalakṣmaṇāvabhivādayati//)

ubhau/ kulasthitimanuvartasva//

(bharataśatrughnau daṇḍavat sītāṃ praṇamataḥ//)

sītā/ kumārā jeṭṭhāṇaṃ bhāduāṇaṃ abhimadā hoha//

rāmaḥ/ vatsau bharataśatrughnau/

asmākaṃ vyasanāmbhodhāvayaṃ potatvamāgataḥ/

kapīndro'yaṃ ca laṅkendro mitraṃ dharmahite rataḥ//32//

tat pariṣvajatam//

(iti sugrīvavibhīṣaṇau darśayati//)

(bharataśatrughnau tau pariṣvajya mitho yathocitamupacarataḥ//)

bharataḥ/ ārya kulagururno bhagavān maitrāvaruṇiḥ siṃhāsanagṛhe sampāditasakalābhiṣekasambhāro bhavantaṃ pratīkṣate/ yathājñāpayatyāryaḥ//

rāmaḥ/ 
(svagatam/) kauśikapādāḥ pratīkṣaṇīyāḥ/ sa ca bhagavān maitrāvaruṇirevamājñāpayati/ bhavatu samayocitaṃ pratikariṣyate// 
(prakāśam/) yathājñāpayati kulaguruḥ//

(sarve parikrāmanti//)

(tataḥ praviśati vasiṣṭho daśarathakalatraiḥ saha caryamāṇārundhatī ca//)

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ 
(svagatam/)

kṣamāyāḥ sukṣetraṃ guṇamaṇigaṇānāmapi khaniḥ prapannānāṃ mūrtaḥ sukṛtaparipāko janimatām/

kṛpārāmo rāmo bahiriha dṛśā pāsyata iti pramodādvai tasyāpyupari parivartāmaha ime//33//

bhavatu/ tathāpi lokayātrānuvartanīyā// 
(prakāśam/) vadhvau kausalyāsumitre//

ubhe/ āṇavedu kulagurū//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ diṣṭyākṣatapratinivṛttavatse sthaḥ//

ubhe/ tumhāṇaṃ āsisāṇaṃ pahāvo//

arundhatī/ 
(kaikeyīṃ vilokya/) vatse kaikeyi/ kimevamatidurmanāyase//

kaikeyī/ amba maha mandabhāiṇīe adhaṇṇadāe saalo vi loā evvaṃ kolīṇaṃ bhaṇadi jaṃ vacchāṇaṃ pavāsajaṇaṇī majjhamajaṇaṇī mantharāmuhe āsi/ tā kahaṃ vacchāṇaṃ mae muhaṃ pekkhidavvam//

arundhatī/ vatse alaṃ vṛthā kaulīnaśaṅkayā/ āryamiśrairayamarthastadaivāntareṇa cakṣuṣā sākṣātkṛtaḥ//

sarvāḥ/ kahaṃ via//

arundhatī/ mantharārūpadhāriṇyā śūrpaṇakhyā mālyavadvacanādetad vihitamiti//

sarvāḥ/ aho rakkhasāṇaṃ duṭṭhatābhiāā jo gihaṭṭhidaṃ avalājaṇaṃ pi vādhedi//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ huṃ maṅgalasamaye'lamalaṃ yatkiñciduktaiḥ/ kā punaradyāpi rākṣasābhiyogavārtā//

rāmaḥ/ 
(vasiṣṭhaṃ vilokya/ sollāsam/) sa eṣa bhagavān maitrāvaruṇiḥ/

yaddarśanāt kimapyetad dravībhavati me manaḥ/

rākāsudhākarālokādindukāntopalo yathā//34//

(lakṣmaṇaṃ prati/) vatsa ita itaḥ//

ubhau/ 
(upasṛtya/) bhagavan kulaguro/ rāmalakṣmaṇāvabhivādayete//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/

cakṣuṣāṃ svasvasamaye saṃskāratrayamāpnutām/

vatsau nayena dharmeṇa jñānena ca puraskṛtam//35//

(ubhāvarundhatīmabhivandete//)

arundhatī/ iṣṭairyujyethām//

(ubhau krameṇa sarvā mātaṝrabhivandete//)

sarvāḥ/ 
(tau nirbharaṃ pariṣvajya mūrdhnyupāghrāya/) jaṃ amhe cintemo taṃ tumhāṇaṃ hodu//

(sītā upasṛtya vasiṣṭhaṃ praṇamati//)

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ vatse vīraprasavinī bhava//

(sītārundhatīṃ praṇamati//)

arundhatī/ 
(sītāṃ nirbharamāliṅgya/)

lopāmudrānusūyāhamiti tisrastvayā saha/

pativratāścatasro'tra santu jānaki sāmpratam//36//

(sītā śvaśrūrabhivandate//)

sarvāḥ/ jāde kulapaḍiṭṭhāvaadāraappasaviṇī hohi//

(nepathye/)

pravartantāṃ paurāḥ pratisadanamadyotsavavidhau ciraṃ sve sve karmaṇyatha samavadhattāpyadhikṛtāḥ/

yathoktaṃ sambhāraṃ punariha vidhatta dvijavarāḥ kṛśāśvāntevāsī kuśikapatirājñāpayati vaḥ//37//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ 
(ākarṇya/) aho bhāgyamahimā vatsasya yad bhagavān kauśikaḥ svayaṃ siṃhāsane samabhiṣektuṃ samprāptaḥ//

itare/ priyaṃ priyaṃ naḥ//

(tataḥ praviśati saśiṣyo viśvāmitraḥ//)

viśvāmitraḥ/

satrapratyūhaśāntyai daśarathakarataḥ karṣatainaṃ mayā yad yatsvānte saṃvimṛṣṭaṃ tadanuguṇavidhau yacca vaiyagryamāsīt/

taddaivasyānuguṇyāt prayatanavibhavaiścādya rājye'bhiṣicya śrīrāmaṃ nirvṛtānāṃ phalitamiti muhuḥ sampramodāmahe'ntaḥ//38//

(iti parikrāmati//)

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ sa eṣa kauśikaḥ/

kṣātraṃ prākṛtikaṃ tejo brāhmaṃ yasya viśiṣyate/

lokottaracamatkāranidhestasyādbhutaṃ na kim//39//

(vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrāvupasṛtyānyonyamupacarataḥ//)

viśvāmitraḥ/ bhagavan maitrāvaruṇe kimadyāpi pratīkṣyate//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ yathocitamādriyatām//

viśvāmitraḥ/ 
(divyarṣigaṇamuddiśya/) nirvartyatāṃ rāmabhadrasyābhiṣekaḥ// 
(munayaḥ yathocitamācaranti/ nepathye dundubhidhvaniḥ/ sarve savismayamākarṇayanti//)

(punarnepathye/)

nṛtyantvapsaraso meghaiḥ sicyatāṃ medinītalam/

vitarantvatra puṣpāṇāṃ varṣaṃ kalpadrumāḥ svayam//40//

(sarve savismayaṃ puṣpavṛṣṭiṃ nirūpayanti//)

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ kathaṃ salokapālo bhagavān pākaśāsano rāmabhadrasyābhiṣekamanumodate//

(kṛtābhiṣekamaṅgalo rāmaḥ/ vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrāvupasṛtya/) gurū abhivādaye//

ubhau/

rāmabhadra guṇārāma bhrātṛbhistvaṃ puraskṛtaḥ/

ikṣvākumukhyairbhūpālaiściramūḍhāṃ dhuraṃ vaha//41//

itare/ tathāstu// 
(ityanumodante//)

viśvāmitraḥ/ vatsa rāmabhadra//

rāmaḥ/ ājñāpayantu guravaḥ//

viśvāmitraḥ/

visṛjyetāmetāvanubhūtotsavapramodau sugrīvavibhīṣaṇau/

puṣpakaṃ ca saṅkalpasamayasulabhaṃ rājarājamevāśrayatām//

(rāmastathā karoti//)

viśvāmitraḥ/ vatsa rāmabhadra/

nirvyūḍhaṃ guruśāsanaṃ gurutaraṃ dharmo'pi saṃrakṣito rakṣaḥsaṃharaṇāccikitsitamanorogā trilokī kṛtā/

siddhārthāśca surāḥ sahānujasuhṛddāreṇa rājyaṃ puna- rlabdhaṃ kiṃ karaṇīyametadadhikaṃ śreyastadapyucyatām//42//

rāmaḥ/ ito'dhikamapi śreyo'sti/ tathāpīdamastu bhagavatpādaprasādāt/ 
(bharatavākyam/)

kṣmāpālāḥ kṣīṇatandrāḥ kṣitivalayamidaṃ pāntu te kāmavarṣā vārvāhāḥ santu rāṣṭraṃ punarakhilamapāsteti saṃpannasasyam/

loko nityaṃ pramodaṃ vidadhatu kavayaḥ ślokamāptaprasādaṃ saṅkhyāvanto'pi bhūmnā parakṛtiṣu mudaṃ sampradhārya prayāntu//43//

viśvāmitraḥ/ evamastu//

(iti niṣkrāntāḥ sarve//)

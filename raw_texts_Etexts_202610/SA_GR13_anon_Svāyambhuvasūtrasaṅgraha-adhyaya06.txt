padapramāṇaṃ tatsaṃkhyā vibhāgaṃ ca pracakṣmahe|

dvādaśārasya cakrasya vibhinnasya dvidhā punaḥ|| 1||

ekaikamarakardhaṃ tu kuryātpañcadaśāṃśakam|

śivamoṅkārarūpaṃ ca śivāyoṅkārarūpiṇe|| 2||

pūrvārakārdhamūle tu vyopikārasya tu dakṣiṇe|

evampradakṣiṇe nāsti nyāso .abhīkṣṇaṃ padātpadam|| 3||

sarvāntikapadaṃ yāvatkartavyaṃ siddhimicchatā|

sthāpyasthīsthiśca parataḥ savisargo ma eva ca|| 4||

mūcayāye nipātāśca piṃścākārastathaiva ca|

namāyatamakāraśca vaścave ca tatoñca ca|| 5||

bhakāro na tathā tīca kṣijñārvāśca padatraye|

makāro .anaikathā tā ca tāśidhnāyāstato .akṣaraḥ|| 6||

dhanayaprāśca rūtā ca  nipaścaprāśca protataḥ|

dhyaścacciśca tatastuśca tasau ca va padadvaye|| 7||

vyāśiyāyaśca vāyau ca vātatāpāstathaiva ca|

vyośca thaśca pakāraśca nādharāśca ramau tataḥ|| 8||

bhakāroparataḥ kaśca tarujñarvāstato .antataḥ|

pibādhrūni yatatmāśca mocayaścara eva ca|| 9||

mīñcamaśca svaraścādyo nāmānāmatha eva ca|

varāvaścaiva tu tadā neyavāśca tataḥ punaḥ|| 10||

saptākṣarādvirabhyaste nānte tu caturakṣare|

ṣaḍakṣare pade dve .anye tataśca caturakṣare|| 11||

tryakṣarāṇi tataḥ pañca dve .anye varṇacatuṣṭaye|

śivottamamato vidyādombhūriti padadvaye|| 12||

ekanetraṃ tataścānyadekavarṇaṃ dvivarṇakam|

oṃkārādipadavyūhe catustriṃśatpadātmake|| 13||

jñeyo .asāvekarudrastu trimūrtistripadastataḥ|

śrīkaṇṭhaḥ ṣaṭpado bhūyaḥ padamanyaṃ śikhaṇḍinaḥ|| 14||

evaṃ vibhāgo mantrāṇāṃ śivakāyātmake sthitaḥ|

atha pañcāśato jñeyamekavarṇaṃ padadvayam|| 15||

caturvarṇaṃ trivarṇaṃ ca punaranyatpadadvayam|

pañcākṣarapadaṃ bhūyo dvyakṣaraṃ dvyakṣaraṃ tataḥ|| 16||

caturvarṇāni trīṇyasmātpañcavarṇaṃ padaṃ tataḥ|

catvāri pañca catvāri ṣaṭ catvāryakṣarāṇi tu|| 17||

padāni pañcatānyatra vidyāṅgāstraṃ caturthakam|

caturvarṇaṃ caturvarṇaṃ aṣṭavarṇaṃ tataḥ padam|| 18||

sapta cāṣṭau ca ṣaḍ varṇaṃ vijñeyaṃ tatpadatrayam|

pañcavarṇaṃ tatastebhyaścaturvarṇaṃ padatrayam|| 19||

caturvarṇāni catvāri ṣaḍvarṇātparatastataḥ|

dvyakṣaraṃ dvyakṣaraṃ bhūyaḥ tryakṣaraṃ ca padaṃ tataḥ|| 20||

ekavarṇaṃ dvivarṇaṃ ca trivarṇaṃ caikavarṇakam|

dvyakṣare dvipade jñeye tryakṣaraṃ tu padaṃ punaḥ|| 21||

dvyakṣare ca pade dve .anye padasaṃkhyā prakīrtitā|

sarvāntapadamoṃkāraḥ śivasya parikīrtitam|| 22||

asmivarṇagaṇaṃ jñātvā mantrodayapadodayam|

yajetsaṃvatsarātmānamiṣṭvā yāti śivaṃ padam|| 23||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe śivamantralakṣaṇapaṭalaḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ||

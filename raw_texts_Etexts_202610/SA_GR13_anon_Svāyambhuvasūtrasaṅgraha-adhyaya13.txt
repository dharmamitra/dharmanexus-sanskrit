         $  svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe

atha prabhāte susvapnadarśane kṛtyamācaret|

duḥsvapnadarśane snānaṃ japahomamapaścimam|| 1||

udadheḥ pāragamanaṃ parvatārohaṇādikam|

evaṃvidhaṃ śubhaṃ svapnaṃ dṛṣṭvā siddhimavāpnuyāt|| 2||

kharoṣṭrayānagartādipatanaṃ cāśubhaṃ smṛtam|

tatroktāṃ niṣkṛtiṃ kuryāddarśanasyānurūpataḥ|| 3||

caturhastaṃ dvihastaṃ vā śvetamālikhya paṅkajam|

pūjayettatra deveśaṃ vidyāṅgāstrāntamīśvaram|| 4||

agnāvapi samabhyarcya tṛkahome kṛte sati|

śeṣe ca karmaṇi kṛte śivahastaṃ prakalpayet|| 5||

gandhadigdhakaraṃ kṛtvā vāmahastena dakṣiṇam|

kaniṣṭhādyāsu sadyādīn pañca pañcasu vinyaset|| 6||

śivaṃ karatale sāṃśaṃ sampūjya ca yathākramam|

tena hastena taṃ śiṣyamālabhenmastake hṛdi|| 7||

hṛdayena tataḥ paścātsaklīkṛtya pujya ca|

ānayecchivasāmīpyaṃ vastreṇācchāditānanam|| 8||

brūyāttaṃ prakṣipasvaitāṃ srajamaṅga śivopari|

tatpātāvoditaṃ nāma śrāvyaṃ śivapadottaram|| 9||

tatrāstrāntamakārpaṇyamācāryaḥ prayataḥ paśoḥ|

vastutrayādhikārārthaṃ pañcakaṃ homamācaret|| 10||

homānte tu śikhāṃ chittvā dadyātpūrṇāhutiṃ kramāt|

śivahastamanuprāptaḥ samayyevamihocyate|| 11||

athottarapadaṃ prepsuṃ gāyatryāḥ sanniyojayet|

sarvātmanā samākṛṣya hṛdayācchāntamudrayā|| 12||

jāte rukmarṣasambandhe rudrāṃśāpātakarmaṇi|

garbhādhānavidheḥ pūrvaṃ dadyātpūrṇāhutidvayam|| 13||

garbhādhānaṃ tataḥ kuryāt padabrahmāhutikramāt|

śivena ca śivāṅgaiśca padapañcakasaṃsthitaiḥ|| 14||

evaṃ puṃsavanaṃ kuryātsīmantakaraṇaṃ tataḥ|

jātakarma tataścaiva nāmno .athakaraṇaṃ tathā|| 15||

niṣkrama.aṃ tathānnasya prāśanaṃ cauLakarma ca|

aupanāyikaṃ karma mekhalājinayojanam|| 16||

tato vratasamāptyarthaṃ ekāṃ daśaguṇāhutāḥ|

kṛtvā snānaṃ tatodvāhaṃ mahāyajñāṃśca kārayet|| 17||

pūrvoktāhutihomena samyagekaikaśaḥ kramāt|

pūrṇāhutiṃ prayogeṇa dadyāduttarakarmakṛt|| 18||

pākayajñaṃ haviryajñaṃ somasaṃsthāṃ tataḥ śanaiḥ|

ekadvitriguṇenaiva kārayeddhomakarmaṇā|| 19||

tatastvaudumbare pātre mantrasampūtasaṃskṛtam|

pāyayetsomamācāryo ghṛtagandhodakena vā|| 20||

āsahasrasamāptyarthaṃ homaṃ kṛtvā śivādibhiḥ|

pūrṇāhutiṃ tato dadyānnayānayavikalpitām|| 21||

tato .anenaiva vidhinā kratuprastāvadakṣiṇām|

nirhṛtya nirharedenaṃ vānaprathāśramāntaram|| 22||

pārivrājyavidhānaṃ ca kṛtvā devaṃ praṇamya ca|

kṣamasva devadeveśa giramuktvā visarjayet|| 23||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe samayasaṃskārapaṭalastrayodaśaḥ||

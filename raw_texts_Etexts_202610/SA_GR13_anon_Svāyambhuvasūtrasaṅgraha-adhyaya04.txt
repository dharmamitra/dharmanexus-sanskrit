athātmabandhasantānapaśutvavinivṛttaye|

vyaktaye ca śivatvasya ṣaḍvidho .adhveha kīrtyate|| 1||

tattvādhvā ca padādhvā ca varṇādhvā bhuvanātmakaḥ|

mantrātmakaḥ kalādhvā ca viśatyekaṃ śivaṃ padam|| 2||

aprameyamanirdeśyamanaupamyamanāmayam|

sūkṣmaṃ sarvagataṃ nityaṃ dhruvamavyayamīśvaram|| 3||

aprameyamanantatvādanirdeśyamalakṣyataḥ|

anaupamyamasādṛśyādvimalatvādanāmayam|| 4||

sūkṣmaṃ cānupalabhyatvādvyāpakatvācca sarvagam|

nityaṃ kāraṇaśūnyatvādacalatvācca taddhruvam|| 5||

avyayaṃ paripūrṇatvātsvāmibhāvāttatheśvaram|

śivatattvamidaṃ proktaṃ sarvādhvopari saṃsthitam|| 6||

oṃkārātmatayā bhāti śāntyatītaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ|

bījapaṅkajamadhyasthaḥ samastādhvamukhaḥ prabhuḥ|| 7||

tataḥ sadāśivaṃ tattvaṃ sthitaṃ padacatuṣṭaye|

kṛtsnasaṃhārasaṃsthena vṛttaṃ bījena pūrvavat|| 8||

vyomavyāpipadasthaṃ tu pañcamantratanuḥ śivaḥ|

śāntyadhvā mantrasaṃjño .adhvā navavyūho .akṣarāditaḥ|| 9||

vyomādirūpaparyantaḥ saptākṣarapade sthitam|

jñānaśaktiḥ kriyāśaktiḥ śaktidvayamanantakam|| 10||

vāmā jyeṣṭhā ca raudrī ca śaktitrayamataḥ param|

sarvādiśivaparyante saṃsthitaṃ tatpadadvaye|| 11||

aiśvaraṃ tadadhastattvamanantādyaṣṭake sthitam|

dhyānānte veṣṭitaṃ bījairyonyantasamavasthitaḥ|| 12||

ananteśaśca sūkṣmaśca tṛtīyaśca śivottamaḥ|

ekanetraikarudrau ca trimūrtiraparastataḥ|| 13||

śrīkaṇṭhaśca śikhaṇḍī ca vidyāvidyeśvareśvarāḥ|

śāntyante kramaśaste .aṣṭau padeṣvaṣṭasu saṃsthitāḥ|| 14||

oṃkārādi śivāyāntapadapaṅkajasaṃsthitam|

vidyātattvaṃ tato vidyādvidyādhvādau vyavasthitam|| 15||

vidyāvigraharājñāṅgaṃ rudravyūhakramākṣitam|

sayonipadasaṃsthena veṣṭitataṃ bījavartmanā|| 16||

vidyādvyūho .akṣare jñeyā vāmadevadikaḥ paraḥ|

navakalpasthitaścāsau muktimārgāvalambanaḥ|| 17||

vigraheśvarasaṃjño .api vyūho .aghorādiraṣṭadā|

saṃsthito rūpavānantonatīsaṃjñe pare pade|| 18||

ambakā brahmavetāLī bhṛguṇī sthāṇumatyapi|

rūpiṇī ca mahājvālā mālinī prathitā parā|| 19||

suvetālī ca gaurī ca nandinī ca pare pade|

aghorā ceti vidyānāṃ rājño vyūhena saṃsthitāḥ|| 20||

vidyāṅgāni tataḥ pañca rudrakrodhāsthiśṛṅkhalāḥ|

astrasaṃhārajāśca vyūho .aṅgānāṃ caturthake|| 21||

halāhalaḥ samātaṅgo nīlalohitasaṃjñakaḥ|

icchumbhaśābaraścaṇḍo vaṭarudro mahābalāḥ|| 22||

ete rudrā vṛtā rudrairanekairbalaśālibhiḥ|

vidyātattve juṣantīha vyūhasthāḥ pañcamaṃ padam|| 23||

īśānāṃ natiparyantaṃ padaṃ vidyeśasaṃsthitam|

māyātattvamadhaḥ ṣasstre bījaiḥ ṣoḍaśabhirvṛtam|| 24||

tatrāṣṭau maṇḍaleśānāḥ kramaśaḥ pūrvataḥ sthitāḥ|

teṣāmaṅguṣṭhamātro .agre bhuvaneśaḥ sthitaḥ paraḥ|| 25||

īśānaścaikapiṅgaśca tatastūdbhava eva ca|

bhavaśca vāmadevaśca mahātejāstataḥ paraḥ|| 26||

māyādhaḥ kālatattvaṃ tu saṃsthitaṃ tatpadadvaye|

niyatiścāpare .anyasminkalā vidyā tataḥ punaḥ|| 27||

catvāryetāni tattvāni veṣṭitāni pṛthakpṛthak|

śādirāntākṣarasthena bījavyūhena pūrvavat|| 28||

atra rudrāḥ śikhedādyāḥ krodheśāntāḥ pṛthakpṛthak|

guhyādivyomaparyantādapaṅktiṃ samāśritāḥ|| 29||

śikhedaścaikavīraśca pañcāntaḥ sūragastathā|

jyotiḥ piṅgaśca saṃvartaścaṇḍaḥ krodheśvaraḥ punaḥ|| 30||

tebhyo .adho .adhvā pratiṣṭhāyāḥ dvātriṃśatpadake sthitaḥ|

nivṛttiraparaḥ śeṣaḥ kalādhvaivaṃ prakīrtitaḥ|| 31||

rāgatattvaṃ pratiṣṭhādau saṃsthitaṃ padapañcake|

yadyonipadasaṃsthena vṛtaṃ tat ṣoḍaśātmanā|| 32||

evaṃ śeṣāṇi tattvāni veṣṭitāni pṛthakpṛthak|

ādikhāntākṣarasthena bījavyūhena pūrvavat|| 33||

rāgatattvādadhastasmāttattvatrayasamanvitam|

pauruṣaṃ ṣaṭpadasthaṃ tu tattvaṃ tattvavido viduḥ|| 34||

tayorvyānapyādināntapadapaṅktigatā daśa|

ekaścaiva śivasteṣāṃ rudrāṇāmāditaḥ sthitaḥ|| 35||

ananto .aja umākāntaḥ pracaṇḍaścaikavīrakaḥ|

sarveśāneśvarabhavā ugro bhīmaśca vāmakaḥ|| 36||

ekaikaśaḥ krameṇaite saṃsthitā vimale .adhvani|

yacchantyabhimatānbhogānbhogināṃ bhogatṛptaye|| 37||

prādhānikaṃ tatastattvaṃ dhūpadādyaṣṭake sthitam|

sthānāni yogināmaṣṭau padasthānyeva tatra tu|| 38||

śraikaṇṭhamaumaṃ kaumāraṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ brāhmameva ca|

raibhavaṃ kṛtaṃ caiva tathā cākṛtameva ca|| 39||

devayonyaṣṭakaṃ tebhyo nidhanādipadāṣṭake|

saṃsthitaṃ kramaśastatra brāhmyantasyādita sthitam|| 40||

prājeśaṃ saumyamaindraṃ ca gāndharvaṃ yākṣameva ca|

rākṣasaṃ ca tataḥ paścāt paiśācaṃ cāṣṭamaṃ tataḥ|| 41||

pradhānādbuddhitattvaṃ tu nidhanākhye pade sthitam|

ahaṅkārastathānyasminmanastasmātpadadvaye|| 42||

tayorevendriyāṇyatra śive buddhīndriyāṇi ca|

karmendriyāṇi garvākhye tattvānyevaṃ padadvaye|| 43||

śrotratvak caiva cakṣuśca jihvā ca ghrāṇameva ca|

vākpāṇī ca tathopasthaṃ pāyupādau tato .antataḥ|| 44||

śabdatanmātrapūrvāṇi pañcatattvāni pañcasu|

padeṣu saṃsthitānyeṣu paramātmādiṣu kramāt|| 45||

ākāśādīni bhūtāni tataḥ padacatuṣṭayam|

saṃsthitāni tu catvāri mahātejaḥpadātpunaḥ|| 46||

pañcāṣṭakāḥ sthitāsteṣu pañcavyūhāstu pañcasu|

sthāṇvaṣṭakamukhāste tu procyate śṛṇutādhunā|| 47||

chagalaṇḍaṃ duraṇḍaṃ ca mākoṭam maṇḍaleśvaram|

kālaṃ caraṃ tathā tatra śaṅkukarṇaṃ sthaleśvaram|| 48||

sthūleśvaraṃ ca prathitaṃ sthānaṃ sthāṇvaṣṭakaṃ tataḥ|

vastrapādaṃ rudrakoṭiravimuktaṃ mahālayam|| 49||

gokarṇaṃ bhadrakarṇaṃ ca suvarṇāsthāṇuraṣṭakam|

pavitrāṣṭakamityatra sthānaṃ kṣetropasevanī|| 50||

gayā caiva kurukṣetraṃ nakhalaṃ kakhalaṃ tathā|

vimalaṃ cāṭṭahāsaṃ ca māhendraṃ bhīmakeśvaram|| 51||

guhyametattṛtīyaṃ tu caturthamadhunocyate|

hariścandaṃ suśailaṃ ca jalpamāmrātikeśvaram|| 52||

madhyamaṃ ca mahākālaṃ kedāraṃ bhairavaṃ tathā|

guhyātiguhyametattu pratyātmikamathocyate|| 53||

amareśaṃ prabhāsaṃ ca naimiśaṃ puṣkaraṃ tathā|

āṣāḍhīśaṃ ca caṇḍīśaṃ bhārabhūtiṃ salākulam|| 54||

prayātmikamidaṃ hyetatpañcamaṃ parikīrtitam|

tebhyo .adhaḥ pārthivaṃ tattvaṃ brahmanomiti śrutam|| 55||

bhavādyakṣaraparyantapadasaṃsthaṃ ca paścimam|

veṣṭitaṃ kākṣarasthena bījavyūhena pūrvavat|| 56||

brahmādīśānaparyantasarvalokeśvareśvaraiḥ|

sphuratkiraṇasaṃkāśavapuścandrārdhamūrdhajaiḥ|| 57||

vṛtaṃ tacchatarudraistu daśadikṣu padasthitaiḥ|

śambhurvibhugaṇādhyakṣastryakṣaḥ tridaśavanditaḥ|| 58||

saṃvāhaśca vivājaśca nabholipsu vicakṣaṇaḥ|

śambhorbalaṃ samākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 59||

vicaranti mahādevāstaddigīśasupūjitāḥ|

anantaḥ pālako vīraḥ pātālādhipatirvṛṣaḥ|| 60||

vṛṣabho grasahaḥ śubhro lohitaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ|

anantabalamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 61||

vicaranti mahādevāḥ vāyurājasupūjitāḥ|

vidyādhipo tānabhujaḥ sarvajño vedapāragaḥ|| 62||

mātravṛtāḥ piṅgalākṣo bhūtapālo balipriyaḥ|

sarvavidyādhipo dhātā sukhaduḥkhakarastathā|| 63||

aiśānyāṃ saṃsthitā rudrā daśaite balaśālinaḥ|

īśānabalamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 64||

vicaranti mahādevāstaddigīśasupūjitāḥ|

jaṭāmakuṭadhārī ca nānāratnadharastathā|| 65||

nidhīśo rūpavāndhanyaḥ saumyadehapramardanaḥ|

suprasādaprakāśaśca kāmarūpī ca supriyaḥ|| 66||

someśānāḥ samākhyātāḥ sāṃnaidhyapadasaṃsthitāḥ|

somasya balamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 67||

vicaranti mahādevāḥ somarājasupūjitāḥ|

śīghro laghurvāyuvegaḥ sūkṣmāstīkṣṇaḥ kṣayāntakaḥ|| 68||

pañcātakaḥ pañcaśikhaḥ kapardī meghanādavān|

vāyorbalaṃ samākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 69||

vicaranti mahādevā vāyurājasupūjitāḥ|

balaścātibalaścaiva pāśahasto mahābalaḥ|| 70||

tataśca jayabhadraśca dīrghabāhurbalāntakaḥ|

vaḍavāmukhabhīmau ca vāruṇāṃ diśi saṃsthitāḥ|| 71||

devadānavamartyānāṃ yathābhimatabhogadaḥ|

vāruṇaṃ balamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 72||

vicaranti mahādevā vāruṇeśasupūjitāḥ|

nairṛto māraṇo hantā kruradṛṣṭibhayānakaḥ|| 73||

ūrdhvaśepho virūpākṣo dhūmro lohitadṛṣṭimān|

nirṛterbalamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 74||

vicaranti mahādevā rakṣorājasupūjitāḥ|

yāmyo mṛtyurharo dhātā vidhāta kartṛsaṃjñakaḥ|| 75||

kālo dharmapatikhyātaḥ saṃyoktā ca viyogakṛt|

yamasya balamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 76||

vicaranti mahādevā dharmarājasupūjitāḥ|

agnī rudro hlatāśī ca piṅgaṇḍaḥ svādako haraḥ|| 77||

jvalano dahano babhruḥ bhasmāntoghakṣayāntakaḥ|

agnerbalaṃ samākramya vahniśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 78||

vicaranti mahādevāḥ tejorājasupūjitāḥ|

kapālīśo hyajo buddho vajradehaḥ pramardanaḥ|| 79||

vibhūtiravyayaḥ śāstā pinākī tridaśādhipaḥ|

indrasya balamākramya prabhuśaktisamanvitāḥ|| 80||

vicaranti mahādevāḥ śakrarājasupūjitāḥ|

nikāyāḥ śatarudrāṇāṃ bahiraṇḍe vyavasthitāḥ|| 81||

idānīmantarasthasya procyate .adhvā yathāsthitam|

satyaṃ tapo janastasmānmahaśca svarbhuvastataḥ|| 82||

bhūrlokaścaiva saptaite padeṣu kramaśaḥ sthitāḥ|

brahmābrahmasutātvīśāḥ vinivṛttā vimānagāḥ|| 83||

aniketā manuṣyāśca saṃsthitāḥ sapta saptasu|

pātālāni tathā sapta sthitāni padasaptake|| 84||

balaṃ vitalasaṃjñaṃ ca nitalaṃ sutalaṃ tataḥ|

talātalaṃ tathā cānyadrasātalamiti śrutam|| 85||

mahātalaṃ ca vikhyātaṃ saptamaṃ natisaṃśite|

nāgāḥ sadā navāsteṣu nivasanti sahasraśaḥ|| 86||

sadā bhogabhujo vīrā balenāpratimaujasaḥ|

narako rauravastebhyaḥ śivāyeti pade sthitaḥ|| 87||

kumbhīpākastathāvīcīrnatiyugme yathākramam|

tebhyaḥ kālānalo rudraḥ spokṣare saṃsthito .antataḥ|

virāmastvanayenokto na yenādirasāviha|| 88||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe .adhvapaṭalaścaturthaḥ||

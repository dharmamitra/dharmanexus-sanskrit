athātmano vadhaṃ mantrī na kuryātphalavāñchayā|

na ca duḥkhasamutpattau kāmato dehamutsṛjet|| 1||

yadā nirvedamāpannaḥ prāptabhogo .athavā punaḥ|

dhyānadhāraṇayogena śivalīnamanāstyajet|| 2||

parisaṃkhyāya śabdādīn svayaṃ tatsādhanāni ca|

śarīramindriyagrāmaṃ bhoktuḥ sthānaṃ nirañjanam|| 3||

tataḥ śaivīṃ tanuṃ kṛtvā śivaṃ sampūjya bhaktitaḥ|

āsanaṃ kuśasaṃkḷptamāśrayedastrapūjitam|| 4||

tataḥ paroditaṃ sarvamīśānīdhāraṇāśritaḥ|

kṛtvākṛṣya kapālasthaṃ vāyumūrdhvapathā nayet|| 5||

tena yāyātpathā nityaṃ somamagniṃ paraṃ śivam|

acalaṃ sarvagaṃ devaṃ sarvāntaṃ sarvatomukham|| 6||

tatsthālīnasvatattvena tanmayo na vibhāvyate|

nādeyamiva samprāptaṃ salilaṃ lavaṇāmbhasi|| 7||

anena kramayogena kāmāddehamimaṃ tyajet|

adhruvaṃ sarvaduḥkhānāmāśrayaṃ ca jugupsitam|| 8||

antyeṣṭiṃ vidhivattasya saṃsthitasya dvijanmanaḥ|

kuryādvipaścidācāryo bahirāvasathācchuciḥ|| 9||

śucirdeśaṃ samāsādya gomayenopalepayet|

śivamabhyarcayettatra śivāgniṃ dakṣiṇe tataḥ|| 10||

mṛdbhasmagomayoddhṛṣṭaṃ pracurodakaśaucitam|

śavamāhṛtya tasyāgnerdakṣiṇasyāṃ niveśayet|| 11||

prokṣitoddhūlitasyātha cintayetpraṇavaṃ hṛdi|

sakalīkṛtya sampūjya tato homaṃ samārabhet|| 12||

padādhvanā śivaṃ dhyātvā taṃ devāya nivedayet|

pratigṛhṇīṣva bhagavan śāvyāmenāṃ mahāhutim|| 13||

evamuktvā śivaṃ devamubhayasthaṃ visarjya ca|

tataḥ sadindhanenāgniṃ prasaktena citiṃ nayet|| 14||

snātvodakaṃ tato datvā punaḥ snātvā śivaṃ japet|

sadyādi netraparyantaṃ śāvakarmaviśuddhaye|| 15||

śiṣyaikagurusambandhādguroḥ sambandhinaśca ye|

tyajeyuste tryahaṃ karma nityādanyajjapādikam|| 16||

tebhyo .anye śivamārgasthā gṛhiṇo .agṛhiṇo .api vā|

dinamekaṃ kṣapeyuste śāvāśaucāya dīkṣitāḥ|| 17||

gṛhiṇo lokamārgasthā laṅgayeyurna laukikam|

ācāraṃ dīkṣitāstasmātkurvīran lokasaṃvṛteḥ|| 18||

na ca yadbādhakaṃ vastu śivaśāstroktavartmanaḥ|

na kuryātsūtakānnādi praiṣiddhodanaṃ yathā|| 19||

evamantyeṣṭikaṃ karma samāsātkathitaṃ mayā|

idānīṃ mānasaṃ yāgaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ saṃśitavratāḥ|| 20||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe .antyeṣṭipaṭalaḥ dvāviṃśatitamaḥ||

prathamo ‘dhyāyaḥ

1.1ab yājñavalkyaṃ muniśreṣṭhaṃ sarvajñaṃ jñānanirmalam
1.1cd sarvaśāstrārthatattvajñaṃ sadā dhyānaparāyaṇam
1.2ab vedavedāṅgatattvajñaṃ yogeṣu pariniṣṭhitam
1.2cd jitendriyaṃ jitakrodhaṃ jitāhāraṃ jitāmayam
1.3ab tapasvinaṃ jitāmitraṃ brahmaṇyaṃ brāhmaṇapriyam
1.3cd tapovanagataṃ saumyaṃ saṃdhyopāsanatatparam
1.4ab brahmavidbhir mahābhāgair brāhmaṇaiś ca samāvṛtam
1.4cd sarvabhūtasamaṃ śāntaṃ satyasandhaṃ gataklamam
1.5ab guṇajñaṃ sarvabhūteṣu parārthaikaprayojanam
1.5cd bruvantaṃ paramātmānam ṛṣīṇām ugratejasām
1.6ab tam evaṃ guṇasaṃpannaṃ nārīṇām uttamā vadhūḥ
1.6cd maitreyī ca mahābhāgā gārgī ca brahmavidvarā
1.7ab sabhāmadhyagatā ceyam ṛṣīṇām ugratejasām
1.7cd praṇamya daṇḍavad bhūmau gārgy etadvākyam abravīt

gārgy uvāca

1.8ab bhagavan sarvaśāstrajña sarvabhūtahite rata
1.8cd yogatattvaṃ mama bruhi sāṅgopāṅgaṃ vidhānataḥ
1.9ab evaṃ pṛṣṭaḥ sa bhagavān sabhāmadhye striyā tayā
1.9cd ṛṣīn ālokya netrābhyāṃ vākyam etad abhāṣata

yājñvalkya uvāca

1.10ab uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhadraṃ te gārgi brahmavidāṃ vare
1.10cd vakṣyāmi yogasarvasvaṃ brahmaṇā kīrtitaṃ purā
1.11ab samāhitamanā gārgi śṛṇu tvaṃ gadato mama
1.11cd ity uktvā brahmavic chreṣṭho yājñavalkyas taponidhiḥ
1.12ab nārāyaṇaṃ jagannāthaṃ sarvabhūtahṛdi sthitam
1.12cd vāsudevaṃ jagadyoniṃ yogidhyeyaṃ nirañjanam
1.13ab ānandam amṛtaṃ nityaṃ paramātmānam īśvaram
1.13cd dhyāyan hṛdi hṛṣīkeśaṃ manasā susamāhitaḥ
1.14ab netrābhyāṃ tāṃ samālokya kṛpayā vākyam abravīt
1.14cd ehyehi gārgi sarvajñe sarvaśāstraviśārade
1.15ab yogaṃ vakṣyāmi vidhivad dhātroktaṃ parameṣṭhinā
1.15cd munayaḥ śrūyatām atra gārgyā saha samāhitāḥ
1.16ab padmāsane samāsīnaṃ caturānanam avyayam
1.16cd carācarāṇāṃ sraṣṭāraṃ brahmāṇaṃ parameṣṭhinam
1.17ab kadā cit tatra gatvāhaṃ stutvā stotraiḥ praṇamya ca
1.17cd pṛṣṭavān imam evārthaṃ yan māṃ tvaṃ paripṛcchasi
1.18ab devadeva jagannātha caturmukha pitāmaha
1.18cd yenāhaṃ yāmi nirvāṇaṃ karmaṇā mokṣam avyayam
1.19ab jñānaṃ ca paramaṃ guhyaṃ yathāvad abrūhi me prabho
1.19cd mayaivam ukto druhiṇaḥ svayaṃbhūr lokanāyakaḥ
1.20ab māmālokya prasannātmā jñānakarmāṇy abhāṣata
1.20cd jñānasya dvividhau jñeyau panthānau vedacoditau
1.21ab anuṣṭhitau tau vidvadbhiḥ pravartakanivartakau
1.21cd varṇāśramoktaṃ yatkarma kāmasaṃkalpapūrvakam
1.22ab pravartakaṃ bhaved etat punarāvṛttihetukam
1.22cd kartavyamiti vidhyuktaṃ karma kāmavivarjitam
1.23ab yena yatkriyate samyak jñānayuktaṃ nivartakam
1.23cd nivartakaṃ hi puruṣaṃ nivartayati janmataḥ
1.24ab pravartakaṃ hi sarvatra punarāvṛttihetukam
1.24cd varṇāśramoktaṃ karmaiva vidhyuktaṃ kāmavarjitam
1.25ab vidhivatkurvatas tasya muktir gargi kare sthitā
1.25cd varṇāśramoktaṃ karmaiva vidhivatkāmapūrvakam
1.26ab yena yatkriyate tasya garbhavāsaḥ kare sthitaḥ
1.26cd saṃsārabhīrubhis tasmād vidhyuktaṃ kāmavarjitam
1.27ab vidhivatkarma kartavyaṃ jñānena saha sarvadā
1.27cd jātāśca triṣu lokeṣu ānulomyena mānavāḥ
1.28ab te devānām ṛṣīṇāṃ ca pitṛṇām ṛṇinas tathā
1.28cd ṛṣibhyo brahmacaryeṇa pitṛbhyaś ca sutais tathā
1.29ab kuryād yajñena devebhyaḥ svāśramaṃ dharmam ācaran
1.29cd catvāro brāhmaṇasyoktā āsramāḥ śruticoditāḥ
1.30ab kṣatriyasya trayaḥ proktā dvāvekau vaiśyaśūdrayoḥ
1.30cd adhītya vedaṃ vedārthaṃ sāṅgopāṅgaṃ vidhānataḥ
1.31ab snāyād vidhyuktamārgeṇa brahmacaryavrataṃ caran
1.31cd saṃskṛtāyāṃ savarṇāyāṃ putram utpādayet tataḥ
1.32ab yajed agnau tu vidhivat bhāryayā saha vā vinā
1.32cd kāntāre vijane deśe phalamūlodakānvite
1.33ab tapaś caran vasen nityaṃ sāgnihotraḥ samāhitaḥ
1.33cd ātmany agnīn samāropya saṃnyased vidhinā tataḥ
1.34ab saṃnyāsāśramasaṃyukto nityaṃ karma samācaran
1.34cd yāvat kṣetrī bhavet tāvad yajed ātmānam ātmani
1.35ab kṣatriyaś ca cared evam āsaṃnyāsāśramāt sadā
1.35cd vānaprasthāśramād evaṃ cared vaiśyaḥ samāhitaḥ
1.36ab śūdraḥ śuśrūṣayā nityaṃ gṛhasthāśramam ācaret
1.36cd śūdrasya brahmacaryaṃ ca munibhiḥ kaiścid iṣyate
1.37ab ānulomyaprasūtānāṃ trayāṇām āśramās trayaḥ
1.37cd śūdravacchūdrajātānām ācāraḥ kīrtito budhaiḥ
1.38ab caturṇām āśramasthāmām ahanyahani nityaśaḥ
1.38cd vidhyuktaṃ karma kartavyaṃ kāmasaṅkalpavarjitaṃ
1.39ab tasmāt tvamapi yogīndra svāśramaṃ dharmam ācaren
1.39cd śraddhayā vidhivatsamyak jñānakarma samācara
1.40ab iti me karmasarvasvaṃ yogarūpaṃ ca tattvataḥ
1.40cd upadiśya tato brahmā yoganiṣṭho ‘bhavat svayam
1.41ab śrutvaitad yājñavalkyoktaṃ vākyaṃ gārgī mudānvitā
1.41cd punaḥ prāha muniśreṣṭham ṛṣimadhye varānanā

gārgyuvāca

1.42ab jñānena saha yogīndra vidhyuktaṃ karma kurvataḥ
1.42cd tvayoktaṃ muktir astīti tayor jñānaṃ vada prabho
1.43ab bhāryayā tv evam uktas tu yājñavalkyas taponidhiḥ
143cd tāṃ samālokya kṛpayā jñānarūpam abhāṣata

yājñavalkya uvāca

1.44ab jñānaṃ yogātmakaṃ viddhi yogaś cāṣṭāṅgasaṃyutaḥ
1.44cd saṃyogo yoga utyukto jīvātmaparamātmanoḥ   (ucyate, ityukto??)
1.45ab vakṣyāmy aṅgāni te samyagyathā pūrvaṃ mayā śrutam
1.45cd samāhitamanā gārgi ṛṣibhiḥ saha saṃśṛṇu
1.46ab yamaś ca niyamaś caiva āsanaṃ ca tathaiva ca
1.46cd prāṇāyāmas tathā gārgi pratyāhāraś ca dhāraṇā
1.47ab dhyānaṃ samādhir etāni yogāṅgāni varānane
1.47cd yamaś ca niyamaś caiva daśadhā saṃprakīrtitaḥ
1.48ab āsanāny uttamāny aṣṭau trayaṃ teṣūttamottamam
1.48cd prāṇāyāmas tridhā proktaḥ pratyāhāraś ca pañcadhā
1.49ab dhāraṇā pañcadhā proktā dhyānaṃ ṣoḍhā prakīrtitam
1.49cd trayaṃ teṣūttamaṃ proktaṃ samādhis tv ekarūpakaḥ
1.50ab bahudhā kecid icchanti vistareṇa pṛthak śṛnu
1.50cd ahiṃsā satyam asteyaṃ brahmacaryaṃ dayārjavam
1.51ab kṣamādhṛtir mitāhāraḥ śaucaṃ tv ete yamā daśa
1.51cd karmaṇā manasā vācā sarvabhūteṣu sarvadā
1.52ab akleśajananaṃ proktam ahiṃsātvena yogibhiḥ
1.52cd vidhyuktaṃ ced ahiṃsā syāt kleśajanmaiva jantuṣu
1.53ab vedenokte ‘pi hiṃsā syād abhicārādi karma yat
1.53cd satyaṃ bhūtahitaṃ proktaṃ na yathārthābhibhāṣaṇam
1.54ab karmaṇā manasā vācā paradravyeṣu niḥspṛhā
1.54cd asteyam iti sā proktā ṛṣibhis tattvadarśibhiḥ
1.55ab karmaṇā manasā vācā sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā
1.55cd sarvatra maithunatyāgo brahmacaryaṃ pracakṣate
1.56ab brahmacaryāśramasthānāṃ yatīnāṃ naiṣṭakasya ca
1.56cd brahmacaryaṃ tu tatproktaṃ tathaivāraṇyavāsinām
1.57ab ṛtāvṛtau svadāreṣu saṅgatir yā vidhānataḥ
1.57cd brahmacaryaṃ tu tatproktaṃ gṛhasthāśramavāsinām
1.58ab rājñaś caiva gṛhasthasya brahmacaryaṃ prakīrtitam
1.58cd viśāṃ vṛttavatāṃ caiva kecid icchanti paṇḍitāḥ
1.59ab śuśrūṣaiva tu śūdrasya brahmacaryaṃ prakīrtitam
1.59cd śuśrūṣā vā guror nityaṃ brahmacaryam udāhṛtam
1.60ab guravaḥ pañca sarveṣāṃ caturṇāṃ śruticoditāḥ
1.60cd mātā pitā tathācāryo mātulaḥ śvaśuras tathā
1.61ab eṣu mukhyāstriyaḥ proktā ācāryaḥ pitarau tathā
1.61cd eṣu mukhyatamas tv eka ācāryaḥ paramārthavit
1.62ab tam evaṃ brahmavic cheṣṭaṃ nityakarmaparāyaṇam
1.62cd śuśrūṣayārcaryen nityaṃ tuṣṭo ‘bhūd yena vā guruḥ
1.63ab dayā ca sarvabhūteṣu sarvatrānugrahaḥ smṛtaḥ
1.63cd vihiteṣu tadanyeṣu manovākkāyakarmaṇām
1.64ab pravṛttau vā nivṛttau vā ekarūpatvam ārjavam
1.64cd priyāpriyeṣu sarveṣu samatvaṃ yaccharīriṇām
1.65ab kṣamā saiveti vidvadbhir gaditā vedavādibhiḥ
1.65cd arthahānau ca bandhūnāṃ viyogeṣv api sampadām
1.66ab tayoḥ prāptau ca sarvatra cittasya sthāpanaṃ dhṛtiḥ
1.66cd aṣṭau grāsā muner bhakṣyāḥ ṣoḍśāraṇyavāsinām
1.67ab dvātriṃśac ca gṛhasthānāṃ yatheṣṭam brahmacāriṇām
1.67cd eṣām ayaṃ mitāhāro hy anyeṣām alpabhojanam
1.68ab śaucaṃ tu dvividhaṃ proktaṃ bāhyamābhantaraṃ tathā
1.68cd mṛjjalābhyāṃ smṛtaṃ bāhyaṃ manaḥśuddhis tathāntaram
1.69ab manaḥśuddhiśca vijñeyā dharmeṇādhyātmavidyayā
1.69cd ātmavidyā ca dharmaśca pitrācāryeṇa vānaghe
1.70ab tasmāt sarveṣu kāleṣu sarvair niḥśrayasārthibhiḥ
1.70cd guravaḥ śrutasampannā mānyā vāṅmanasādibhiḥ

2.1cd siddhāntaśravaṇaṃ caiva hrīr matiś ca japo vratam

// śrīparamātmane namaḥ//
atha svasthāya devāya nityāya hatapāpmane/
tyaktakramavibhāgāya caitanyajyotiṣe namaḥ//1//

nāndyante sūtradhāraḥ/ adya khalu bhagavataḥ kālapriyanāthasya yātrāyāmāryamiśrāḥ samādiśanti mahāpuruṣasaṃrambho yatra gambhīrabhīṣaṇaḥ/
prasannakarkaśā yatra vipulārthā ca bhāratī//2//
aprākṛteṣu pātreṣu yatra vīraḥ sthito rasaḥ/
bhedaiḥ sūkṣmairabhivyaktaiḥ pratyādhāraṃ vibhajyate//3//
sa sandarbho'bhinetavya iti/ 
(saharṣam/) mahāvīracaritaṃ tarhi prayoktavyamityādiṣṭamarthato bhavati/
vaśyavācaḥ kaveḥ kāvyaṃ sā ca rāmāśrayā kathā/
labdhaśca vākyaniṣyandaniṣpeṣanikaṣo janaḥ//4//
so'hametadvijñāpayāmi/ asti dakṣiṇāpathe padmapuraṃ nāma nagaram/ tatra kecit taittirīyiṇaḥ kāśyapāścaraṇaguravaḥ paṅktipāvanāḥ pañcāgnayo dhṛtavratāḥ somapīthina udumbaranāmāno brahmavādinaḥ prativasanti/ tadāmuṣyāyaṇasya tatrabhavato vājapeyayājino mahākaveḥ pañcamaḥ sugṛhītanāmno bhaṭṭagopālasya pautraḥ pavitrakīrternīlakaṇṭhasyātmasambhavaḥ śrīkaṇṭhapadalāñchano bhavabhūtirnāma jātūkarṇīputraḥ kavirmitradheyamasmākamityatrabhavanto vidāṅkurvantu/
śreṣṭhaḥ paramahaṃsānāṃ maharṣīṇāmivāṅgirāḥ/
yathārthanāmā bhagavān yasya jñānanidhirguruḥ//5//
tenedamuddhṛtajagattryamanyumūla- mastokavīragurusāhasamadbhutaṃ ca/
vīrādbhutapriyatayā raghunandanasya dharmadruhāṃ damayituścaritaṃ nibaddham//6//
tadidaṃ bhavantaḥ paripunantu/ uktaṃ ca tena śrotriyaputreṇa prācetaso munivṛṣā prathamaḥ kavīnāṃ yat pāvanaṃ raghupateḥ praṇināya vṛttam/
bhaktasya tatra samaraṃsata me'pi vāca- stāḥ suprasannamanasaḥ kṛtino bhajantām//7//

(praviśya naṭaḥ/) kṛtaprasādāḥ pāriṣadāḥ/ kintvapūrvatvāt prabandhasya kathāpradeśaṃ samārambhe jñātumicchanti//
sūtradhāraḥ/ sa tu bhagavān dīkṣiṣyamāṇaḥ kauśiko viśvāmitra eekṣvākasya rājarṣervasiṣṭhapurodhaso daśarathasya gṛhānupetya svameva tapovanaṃ pratyāgataḥ/ sa ca vijayi sahajamastrairvīryamucchrāyayiṣyañ jagadupakṛtibījaṃ maithilīṃ prāpayiṣyan/
daśamukhakulaghātaślāghyakalyāṇapātraṃ dhanuranujasahāyaṃ rāmadevaṃ nināya//8//
nimantritastena videhanāthaḥ sa prāhiṇod bhrātaramāttadīkṣaḥ/
kuśadhvajo nāma sa eṣa rājā sītormilābhyāṃ sahito'bhyupaiti//9//

(iti niṣkrāntau//)
//prastāvanā//

(tataḥ praviśati rathastho rājā sūtaḥ kanye ca/)

kanye/ jadhā kaṇiṭṭhatādā āṇavedi//
rājā/ turīyo hyeṣa medhyo'gnirāmnāyaḥ pañcamo'thavā/
athavā jaṅgamaṃ tīrthaṃ dharmo vā mūrtisañcaraḥ//10//
sūtaḥ/ sāṅkāśyanātha evametat/ na khalu viśvāmitrādṛṣermahattvena kaścidaparaḥ prakṛṣyate/ yasya bhagavatastraiśaṅkavaṃ śaunaḥśephaṃ rambhāstambhanaṃ cetyaparimeyamāścaryajātamākhyānavida ācakṣate/
tadasmin brahmādyaistridaśamunibhirnāthitaśame tapastejodhāmni svayamupanatabrahmaṇi gurau/
nivāse vidyānāmupahitakuṭumbavyavahṛti- rbhavāneva ślāghyo jagati gṛhamedhī gṛhavatām//11//
rājā/ sādhu bhoḥ sādhu sūnṛtaṃ hi sūta bhāṣase/ prakṛṣṭakalyāṇodarkasaṅgamā hyete bhagavantaḥ sākṣātkṛtabrahmāṇo maharṣayaḥ/
tamāṃsi dhvaṃsante pariṇamati bhūyānupaśamaḥ sakṛtsaṃvāde'pi prathata iha cāmutra ca śubham/
atha pratyāsaṅgaḥ kamapi mahimānaṃ vitarati prasannānāṃ vācaḥ phalamaparimeyaṃ prasuvate//12//
sūtaḥ/ dṛśyate haritaparisarāraṇyaramaṇīyaṃ kauśikīparikṣiptamāyatanamṛṣestasya siddhāśramapadaṃ nāma/ kiṃ bahunā/ sa evāyamātmanātṛtīyaḥ kuśikanandano bhavantamevābhyudgataḥ//
rājā/ yadyevamavatarāmo rathāt/ 
(kanyābhyāṃ sahāvatīrya/) sūta ucyantāṃ sainikāḥ na kaiścidāśramābhyarṇabhūmayaḥ kramyantāmiti//
sūtaḥ/ yathājñāpayasi//

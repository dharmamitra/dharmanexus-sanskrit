ṣpecial features of this text:
1. ṭhe text is accentuated according to Pāṇini's accentuation-rules
using Vedic svara-marks (i.e. only sannatara and svarita are marked).
2. āll vowel anubandhas (its) are marked with anunāsika-sign
according to Pāṇini's rules.
3. ṭhe avagraha after a dhātu is the sign of compound separation (as
in pada-pāṭha) and not of the elision of śort 'a' (as in
contemporary usage).
4. Verbal roots (dhātu) were enumerated according to the following
principle: every dhātu and every variation of dhātu must have its own
number, all known varieties of roots must be included in text and not
excluded. ṭhere are many styles of dhatu enumeration in different
editions and they don't agree with eac other. īn this enumeration
every dhātu has two numbers: a number of its gaṇa (class) an its
number within this gaṇa.
5. ṭhree kinds of brackets are used in this text:
() - words in suc brackets are variations of the text in other editions.
[] - words in suc brackets in index are anuvṛtti from preceding
lines. anuvṛtti is a part of a preceding rule wic must be remembered
in order to understand the following rules.
 - words in suc brackets are variations of dhatu exactly known
from other sources but not found in three editions on wic this text
is based.

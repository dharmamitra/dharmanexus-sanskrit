śivaṃ praṇamya paramaṃ nidhaneśamataḥ paramÝ

jñānadīkṣe pravakṣyāmi te śṛṇudhvaṃ samāhitāḥÝÝ 1ÝÝ

atra cādyena

pādena svayaṃbhūḥ sarvārthajñānakartṛtvalakṣaṇaśivatvenopetaṃ vighnavināśahetumaśivāpahaṃ śivapradaṃ cāṇubhyaḥ śivaṃ praṇamantiÝ
śivasya viśeṣalakṣaṇaṃ yadi hi muktātmanāmapyasti tena paramam yatasteṣāmādisiddhaṃ śivatvamÝ
yasyānādisiddhaṃ taṃ paramaṃ śivaṃ praṇamyaÝ
dvitīyena śivaprasādāt pradhvastabandhanamāvirbhūtasarvārthadṛkkriyaṃ sarvānugrāhakaṃ svaguruṃ nidhaneśaṃ praṇamyaÝ
tṛtīyena sakalatantrasthavastujñānadīkṣādikaṃ pratijānātiÝ
dvandvanirdeśaścātra jñānadīkṣe iti dvayorapi prādhānyajñāpanārthaḥÝ
kasyaciddīkṣeti dīkṣāvyākhyānayorārambhakarturjñānaṃ pradhānaṃ mumukṣostu dīkṣaivetiÝ

prayojanopanyāsaścātra purato .avasthitaḥÝ
tataḥ prayojanaṃ gauravasūtraṃ paśyato .atra prastutapratijñayā mamāpi kvacidarthakathanaṃ sūtravibhāgakaraṇe saṃkṣepakaraṇe ca kartṛtvamastīti jñāpayatiÝ
saṃkṣepakaraṇaṃ cātra na pūrvasya mahato granthasyālpena granthāntareṇārthakathanam, kiṃtu pūrvasyaiva granthasya prakṛtāvuktā[nāma]ṅgānāṃ vi[kṛ]tāvarthaprāptānāṃ yānyanuvādakāni sūtrāṇi teṣāmarthavādasūtrāṇāṃ parityāgenetaragranthakathanamÝ

nanu gaurave sūtre jñānamupanyastam, iha tu dīkṣājñānayorupanyāsaḥÝ
na paraṃ gauravasūtre jñānamevopanyastam, kintūpari dīkṣā ca bhaviṣyatītiÝ
ādisūtre jñānapadena dīkṣāpi saṃgṛhīteti jñāpayatiÝ

caturthena śivaśaktinipātād dīkṣayā cāpi saṃskṛtān jñānārthino .adhikārayogyān svaśiṣyān vālakhilyādīn samādhāyāvasthāpayatiÝ

samāhitacetaso hi śaivaṃ vastu yuktiyuktamavadhārayituṃ kṣamāḥÝ

evaṃ devagurunamaskriyāṃ pratijñāṃ ca [kṛtvā] svaśiṣyān vālakhilyādīn samādhāyāvasthāpya tadavabodhāya sakalatantrārthasaṃgrāhakaṃ gauravasūtraṃ paṭhati athetyādi

athātmamalamāyākhyakarmabandhavimuktayeÝ

vyaktaye ca śivatvasya śivājjñānaṃ pravartateÝÝ 2ÝÝ

atheti bhavināṃ cicchaktisaṃnirodhakartrādhikārakṣayābhimukhabhāvādanantaramanugrahāya jñānaṃ pravartata itiÝ
ātmā vyāpako nityaḥ śivasamaḥÝ
tasya śivatvasaṃnirodhako malaḥÝ
tena pratiba[ddha]śivatvo .asau nopāyaṃ vinā jñeyaṃ kṛtyaṃ vā jñātuṃ kartuṃ vā śaknotiÝ
tasyāpi bhogopāyāpekṣasya māyākhyo bandhaḥ kalādikaḥ pratipadyateÝ
māyā jagadyoniḥÝ
tatkāryo bandho yaḥ sa tayākhyāyateÝ
bhogasādhanenopetasya bhogāyābhipretasya bhogyaṃ karma dharmādharmasamākhyātamitiÝ
ayamātmano malādikastrividho bandhaḥ, yato hyanena yuktasya na sarvatra yugapacchivatvaṃ virājateÝ tasyātmano .asmāttrividhādbandhād vimuktaye śivatvasya ca vyaktaye śivād anujighṛkṣāto jñānaṃ pravartateÝ paśubhyaḥ pāśebhyaśca yajjñānaṃ pravartate tatpāśavyaṅgyatvāt pāśarūpatvācca pratyuta pāśānāṃ draḍhimānaṃ

janayatiÝ
śivastu paśupāśavilakṣaṇaḥ pāśapratipakṣībhūtaḥ sakalasāmarthyopacitaḥÝ
tasmādyatpravartate tatpāśānāṃ nivṛttaye paryāptaṃ dṛḍhaṃ caÝ
samānajātīyasya pratiba[ddha]śaktikasya samānajātīyamevāpratibaddhaśaktikaṃ śaktivyañjakamÝ
śivaścātmanaḥ samānajātīyaḥÝ
tasya pratibaddhaśivatvasyāpratibaddhaśivatvo .asau śivatvavyaktaye svaṃ jñānaṃ pravartayatīti yuktamÝ
tajjñānaṃ dvirūpaṃ śabdarūpamavabodharūpaṃ caÝ
tadavabodharūpaṃ śabdarūpārūḍhamartheṣu pravartateÝ

nanvidaṃ phalaṃ dīkṣāyāḥÝ satyam, kintu jñānamācāryasya sādhyaṃ sādhanaṃ ca jñāpayatiÝ sa ca tasmādviditasādhyasādhano .anugrāhyāṇāṃ sādhyasiddhaye sādhanaṃ prayoktuṃ śaknotiÝ

sarvatra ca muktivādino muktisiddhaye jñānameva pratijānanteÝ
na cānuṣṭhānaṃ vināsau bhavitumarhatiÝ
yatrāpi ca jñānādeveti tatrāpi ca heyādyanuṣṭhānamavaśyambhāvītiÝ

yadvā śivājjñānaṃ pravartata iti jñānaṃ śaktiḥÝ
sā ca dvirūpā, avabodharūpā dīkṣārūpā caÝ
yathā ca saviturgabhastirarthaprakāśikā dāhamapi kurvatī prakāśarūpatāṃ na jahātītyubhayarūpāpi prakāśa ityucyate, evaṃ śivaśaktirapyarthān prakāśayatīti bodha ityucyate, yadā pāśānāṃ vinivṛttimātmanaśca śivatvavyaktiṃ ca karoti tadā dīkṣetyucyate, tatrāpi jñānarūpatāṃ na jahātīti tenobhayarūpāpi jñānamityuktāÝ

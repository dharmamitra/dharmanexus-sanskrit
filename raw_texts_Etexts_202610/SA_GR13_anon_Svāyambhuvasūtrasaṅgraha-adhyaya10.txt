athācāryavaraḥ śrīmānagrajanmā supeśalaḥ|

heyopādeyatattvajñaḥ śivaśāsanatatparaḥ|| 1||

yathoktajñeyatattvajño yāyajūko jitendriyaḥ|

kṣamāvānabhimānīca sarvabhūtānukampakaḥ|| 2||

śraddadhānaḥ śucirdakṣastridīkṣādīkṣitaḥ sudhīḥ|

devāsuramanuṣyāṇāṃ bhayābhayavirodhakaḥ|| 3||

na hīnāṅgo .adhikāṅgo vā lakṣaṇaiśca śubhairyutaḥ|

deśakālanimittajñaḥ sthānabhūvarṇavittamaḥ|| 4||

tithinakṣatrayogajño bhūparigrahakarmavit|

adhivāsaviśeṣajñaḥ śivamaṇḍalakovidaḥ|| 5||

śāstrādhikārakarmajñastaduttaravidhānavit|

ṣaḍadhvakarmanipuṇaścaṇḍayāgavidhau paraḥ|| 6||

abhiṣekavidhānajñaḥ sthānapūjāntyakarmavit|

yogābhijño vratātīto vyutkrāntividhiśāstravit|| 7||

sadyotkrāntividhānajñaḥ śivakarmaṇi peśalaḥ|

mānase caiva yā  kamaṇḍaluvidhau tathā|| 8||

etaireva guṇairyuktaḥ sādhyakarmavrataḥ sudhīḥ|

ahiṃsānugraho nityamapramattaḥ sahāyavān|| 9||

sādhyamantrārpitamanāḥ sādhyamantravrate sthitaḥ|

sādhyamantrābhiṣiktaḥ san sādhakaḥ parikīrtitaḥ|| 10||

triparvādhvasu niṣkrāntaḥ śivāgnigurupūjakaḥ|

mantratantrasthito yo vā sa putraka iti smṛtaḥ|| 11||

saṃsāraduḥkhasantrastaḥ śivadhāmani baddhadhīḥ|

śivahastamanuprāptaḥ śivāgnigurupūjakaḥ|| 12||

gurvadhīno .asvatantraśca sarvakāryeṣu sarvadā|

samayī samayasthaḥ san śāstre .adhikriyate pumān|| 13||

lakṣaṇoktakrameṇaiva jyāyastvaṃ śāntacetasām|

vadhigaṃ  jñeyaṃ parṇajñānāvalambibhiḥ|| 14||

etānalakṣitākārān dūrādabhyāgatāsamān|

samayaistu yathā tattvaṃ ye te śāstrārtha   || 15||

te vai deśikamāsādya kṛtaśaucāḥ samāhitāḥ|

pūjācikīrṣayā dhīrā yadā tasyāgrataḥ sthitāḥ|| 16||

tadā tāndeśiko dṛṣṭvā liṅgamudrāṃ samāśrayet|

āsanaṃ ca satāmiṣṭamīśānaṃ manasā smaret|| 17||

ācāryaṃ pūjayitvā tu ācāryasamaye sthitaḥ|

niviśettyuttare tasya śaśakarṇikayā prabho|| 18||

badhvā tu mukuLīṃ paścāt puṣṭagarbhāntataḥ śanaiḥ|

śrotrābhyāśe nyasedvidvān īśānasya kalātrayam|| 19||

itarastu tataḥ śeṣaṃ ācāryāya kalādvayam|

tathaivoktā spṛśatpāṇiṃ pāṇibhyāmeva tasya tau|| 20||

padatrayaṃ trayaṃ cānye gadeyuḥ prathamasya hi|

śeṣāścaturthamuktvātha praṇatāṃstān samutkṣipet|| 21||

sāntvayitvā vacobhistānidaṃ brūyādvacaḥ śanaiḥ|

prabrūtha samayāntajñāḥ śaivaṃ panthānamāśritāḥ|| 22||

atha nindāṃ śivasyāgnergurośca parivarjayet|

sādhakācāryaśāstrāṇāṃ dīkṣitasyāparasya ca|| 23||

saṃspṛṣṭaṃ puṣṭavatyānnaṃ svaryātānāñca sautikam|

nirmālyalaṅghanaṃ dānaṃ bhojanaṃ ca vivarjayet|| 24||

tatrāviplavanaṃ dānamavinītabaleḥ paśoḥ|

nācarecchivamārgasthaḥ mahātayagato .api san|| 25||

evametān śivenoktān samayānparipālayet|

pālanācchivatāṃ yāti kravyādatvamatho .anyathā|| 26||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe sāmānyaviśeṣapaṭalo daśamaḥ||

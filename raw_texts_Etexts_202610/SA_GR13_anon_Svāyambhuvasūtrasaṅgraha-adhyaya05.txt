athādāvabhavacchabdaḥ kāraṇādakṣaraṃ tataḥ|

kāraṇaṃ mokṣadaṃ brahman brahma brahmavido viduḥ|| 1||

tasmātsarvaprado devaḥ śabdarāśiriti śrutaḥ|

navaparvaśatārdhātmā yonibījātmakaḥ paraḥ|| 2||

akārādivisargāntaṃ bījaṃ tat ṣoḍaśākṣaram|

śeṣā yoniścatustriṃśat avyayā hyakṣarātmikā|| 3||

sā śaktirdevadevasya tayā vyāptamidaṃ jagat|

jñānaṃ śaivaṃ paraṃ sūkṣmaṃ yattattārakamuttamam|| 4||

vedādijñānabhedena śivajñānavibhedataḥ|

cintāmaṇirivātrāsau sthitaḥ sarvasya kāraṇam|| 5||

ekameva śivajñānaṃ vibhinnaṃ daśadhā punaḥ|

tathāṣṭādaśadhā bhūyo bhedāntaravisarpitam|| 6||

kāmikaṃ prathamaṃ teṣāṃ yogodbhavamataḥ param|

acintyaṃ kāraṇaṃ jñānamajitaṃ dīptasaṃjñakam|| 7||

bhedāntaraṃ tathā sūkṣmaṃ sāhasramaparaṃ viduḥ|

aṃśumān suprabhaṃ caiva daśabhedamataḥ param|| 8||

vijayaṃ prathamaṃ tatra niśvāsamaparaṃ tathā|

svāyambhuvamathāgneyaṃ vīrabhadraṃ sarauravam|| 9||

mākuṭaṃ visaraṃ caiva canrahāsamataḥ param|

mukhabimbakamanyacca prodgītaṃ lalitaṃ tathā|| 10||

siddhaṃ santānasaṃjñaṃ ca śārvodgītaṃ nṛsiṃhakam|

kiraṇaṃ pārameśaṃ ca tantrabhedasya saṅgrahaḥ|| 11||

evametacchivajñānaṃ vibhinnaṃ bahudhā punaḥ|

upabhedena bhedānāmasya saṃkhyā na vidyate|| 12||

bhedāntarāṇi sarvāṇi mahānti na mahāntyapi|

dīkṣāsampratyayārthāni siddhimuktiphalāni ca|| 13||

kāraṇaṃ siddhimuktyoryaddīkṣā   raṃ dvyarthamiṣyate  |

mantrapradhānaṃ tadyasmāttasmāttadvaktumarhasi|| 14||

evamukto mahādevaḥ prasphuraccandraśekharaḥ|

mantrān sa kathayāmāsa yairotamakhilaṃ jagat|| 15||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe sṛṣṭipaṭalaḥ pañcamaḥ||

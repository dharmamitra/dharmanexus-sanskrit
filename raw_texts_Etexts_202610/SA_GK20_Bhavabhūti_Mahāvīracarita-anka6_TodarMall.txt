vāsavaḥ/ kīdṛśaḥ//

citrarathaḥ/ durbādho janidivasānmama pravṛddhaḥ ko'pyādhiḥ prabalatamo'thavā trilokyāḥ/

tasyedaṃ nidhanadinaṃ vidhervilāsāt kalyāṇī pariṇatirastu vānyathā vā//28//

tadavagantumahaṃ prahitaḥ//

vāsavaḥ/ sakulyānāmapyeṣa manorathaḥ//

citrarathaḥ/ kiṃ citram/ sahajāḥ kila te mithaḥ śatravaḥ/ kṛtrimatāpi nidhipuṣpakādiharaṇavṛttena durvṛttasya suprathitā/ athavā/

yāvattrilokyāṃ kila jantujātaṃ tat sarvamasyoddhataduścaritraiḥ/

kadarthitaṃ śrīraghunandanasya prītyā vidhatte vijayapratīkṣām//29//

vāsavaḥ/ 
(nirūpya/) gandharvarāja yadidamadhityakātaḥ suvelādrerakāṇḍaeva prabalakilakilākolāhalamukharitaharinmukhaṃ valīmukhacakramakramamevoccalitaṃ tathā manye patitameva praharaṇairiti//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja paśya paśya/

ayaṃ rakṣonāthaḥ kṣitidharaśirobandhuratare rathe tiṣṭhan praṣṭhaḥ pradhanarasaniṣṇātamanasām/

muhurjīvāghoṣairbadhirayati dikprāntaśikhari- pratidhvānādhmātairgaganavivarābhogamabhitaḥ//30//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja na tulādhṛtastāvadanayorvīrasamayocitaḥ parikaraḥ/ 
(sāvegam/) sūta sūta sāṅgrāmikaṃ me rathamupahara rāmabhadrāya/ ahamapi gandharvarājādhiṣṭhitaṃ vimānamevādhitiṣṭhāmi//

(iti tathā karoti//)

sūtaḥ/ yadājñāpayati devarājaḥ//

(iti niṣkrāntaḥ//)

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja kathamapratisandheyaṃ tumulam/ tathā hi/

rakṣobhirvipinaukasāṃ parivṛḍhaiścārādapāstakramaṃ muṣṭāmuṣṭi kacākaci praharaṇaprakṣepamūḍhātmabhiḥ/

prārabdhaṃ raṇakarma durdharamithoniṣpeṣaśīryadvapu- rniṣṭhyūtāsrajharībhireva saraṇirduḥsañcarābhūdyathā//31//

api ca/

vīrāṇāṃ ruṇḍaruṇḍapravighaṭanapaṭusphāradordaṇḍakhaṇḍa- vyāpārakṣipyamāṇapratibhaṭavikaṭāṭopavarṣmaprarūḍhaḥ/

kūṭaḥ ko'pyeṣa yuddhājirabhuvi jaraṭhaścitrakūṭānukārī līyante yatra śatruprapatanavivaśāḥ koṭiśaḥ śūrakīṭāḥ//32//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja ita itaḥ/

prāsaprotapravīrolvaṇarudhiraparāmṛṣṭabukkājighatsā- dhāvadgṛdhrādhirājāpratimatanuruhacchāyayā vāritoṣṇāḥ/

viśrāmyanti kṣaṇārdhaṃ pradhanaparisareṣveva muktābhiyogā dhīrāḥ śastraprahāravraṇabhararudhirodgāradigdhākhilāṅgāḥ//33//

ito'pi/

pratīkṣante dhīrāḥ pratimukhamurobhiḥ sarabhasaṃ vipakṣāṇāṃ hetīḥ pratiniyatadhairyānubhavataḥ/

vidīrṇatvagbhārā dalitapiśitāśchinnadhamani- prakāṇḍāsthisnāyusphuṭataravilakṣyāntranivahāḥ//34//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja apūrvo'yaṃ rakṣaḥpateḥ saṅgrāmāvataraṇasargaḥ/

tathā hi/

preṣyāḥ saṅgrāmasīmanyanujaśatavṛto meghanādo'pi pārśve vāme'nyatra pravīreṣvativiṣamamado bodhitaḥ kumbhakarṇaḥ/

kaikasyā bandhuvargo'pyayamativikaṭaḥ pṛṣṭhatastiṣṭhamāno' dhyāste madhyaṃ niṣaṇṇo rathaśirasi bhṛśaṃ vindhyavaddurvigāhaḥ//35//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja evamabhiyogoddhuraṃ dviṣantamabhivīkṣyāpi niṣprakampa eva rāmabhadraḥ/ athavocitamevaitat/ yataḥ na kampante jhañjhāmaruti kila vāti pratidiśaṃ samunmūrchatsārāḥ kulaśikhariṇaḥ kiñcidapi te/

na maryādāṃ te'pi pratijahati gāmbhīryagarima- sphuradvārbrahmāṇo'kalitamahimāno'mbunidhayaḥ//36//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja paśya/

bhaktiprahvaṃ kathamapi yavīyāṃsamutsṛjya cāpā- ropavyagrāṅgulikisalayaṃ meghanādakṣayāya/

lakṣyīkṛtya pradhanakuśalaṃ sānujaṃ rākṣasendraṃ jīvāṃ bhūyo raghupativṛṣā sparśataḥ saṃskaroti//37//

kathametadatiduṣkaramiva manye/ tathā hi/

ākramyaikaikamete rajanicarabhaṭāḥ koṭiśaḥ śastravarṣaai- rbhāsvadvaṃśaprarohaṃ pidadhati paritaḥ satkriyāyaugapadyāḥ/

athavā kiṃ nāma duṣkaram/

etāvapyutprabhāvāvakalitamahimaprābhavau yuddhabhūmā- vindhāte śatruśastrapravidalanaphalaspaṣṭabāṇābhiyogau//38//

(samantato'valokya/) aho kathamete vanaukaso'pi mahati sapatnasaṅghaṭṭe svābhidhānayogameva vyākhyānti/ pañcaṣāḥ kevalaṃ rāmabhadrapādamūlamāsevante/ tathā hi/

sugrīvaḥ syandanasyāgre sāṅgadāḥ pṛṣṭhataḥ punaḥ/

pañcaṣā jāmbavān bhāvī laṅkādhīśo'pi pārśvayoḥ//39//

(vicintya/) hanūmān punaḥ kanīyāṃsaṃ kākutstham/ 
(savimarśam/)

varameta evobhayathā rāmabhadrapādapadmopasevinaḥ/ yatastāvadeteṣāṃ svāmibhaktiśca dhairyaṃ ca vyākhyāte gātramakṣatam/

rakṣo'bhiyogastvanyeṣāṃ dṛśyate dainyamapyalam//40//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja mānuṣe loke vātsalyaṃ nāma kevalamakhilendriyavaśīkaraṇacūrṇamuṣṭiḥ/ yataḥ/

saumitriḥ kṛtahastatāprabhṛtibhirnyūno na kaiścidguṇaiḥ sāreṇāpi punaḥ prasiddhamahimā śauryoṣmaṇā rāvaṇiḥ/

itthaṃ tulyatare kila vyatikare rāmasya rakṣaḥprabho- ścānyonyaṃ śaravṛṣṭireva valate dṛṣṭistayorvatsalā//41//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja yuktamevaitat/ evaṃ vātsalyamanurudhyante kila mahātmānaḥ/ 
(sādbhutautsukyam/) paśyatu devarājaḥ/

saumitrervāṇavajrairadhikataramamī marmavedhaṃ praviddhā dhāvantaḥ kṣmādharendrā iva rajanicarāḥ śerate yuddhasīmni/

rakṣonātho'pi putrān katicana patitān vīkṣya rāmābhiyogaṃ santyajyāniṣṭaśaṅkī nipatati tarasā meghanādopakaṇṭham//42//

tadetadatyāhitamāśaṅke//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja kimatra nāmātyāhitam/ aparicchedyamahimānaḥ kilaite kakutsthakulaprarohāḥ/ tathā hi/

paraḥsahasraṃ rajanīcarendrā yathāsya vīrasya kilaikalakṣyam/

tathā raṇeṣvadbhutavīragoṣṭhī- bhūṣāyamāṇo daśakandharo'pi//43//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja bahubhirekasyābhiyoge'pi śubhodarkatetyetadabahuvyaktiniṣṭham/ 
(sacamatkāram/) ito'vadhattāṃ devarājaḥ/

rakṣonāthe sarabhasamito nirgate vigrahecchuḥ kṣubhyatyuccai raghupatiśaraiḥ kīlitaḥ kumbhakarṇaḥ/

kumbho'pyetāṃ piturupanatāṃ vīkṣya vasthāṃ vapuṣmān garvaḥ kiṃ vā nipatati javājjaṅgamaḥ kṣmādharendraḥ//44//

(sādbhutam/) aho chidrasañcāritā markaṭajāteḥ/ yataḥ/

uddiśyārāddaśarathakulāṅkūramādyaṃ patantaṃ dṛptaṃ kumbhaṃ mṛdhabhuvi kapiḥ ko'pi madhye rurodha/

(saviśeṣaṃ nirvarṇya/) kathaṃ sugrīva eva/ 
(savicikitsam/)

doḥstambhābhyāṃ sarabhasamathāpīḍaya vikṣipya bhūmau krāntvāpyenaṃ pratighavivaśaṃ māṣapeṣaṃ pipeṣa//45//

sāśaṅkam/

etannirīkṣya nipapāta ca kumbhakarṇaḥ sugrīvamugrataravidrutiragrahīcca/

unmocya so'pi nipuṇaḥ svamamuṣya nāsāṃ lajjāṃ svasuśca yugapat kila niścakartta//46//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja ita itaḥ//

laghuraghupatireṣa rākṣasānā- madhibhuvi kiṃ ca kumārameghanāde/

kimapi caritamadbhutaṃ vyatānīt sapadi yathā pratighāndhatāmadhātām//47//

ahaha/ idamatiduṣkaraṃ pratisaṃvidhānamāpatitamasya raghuśiśoḥ/

tathā hi/

yāvanmantraprabhāvādduradhigamagatīn meghanādapraṇunnān durbhedyānnāgapāśān vihagaparivṛḍhāstraprayogādvyadhūnot/

tāvadrakṣovinetrā punaratirabhasaṃ marmaṇi krodhabhūmnā gāḍhaṃ viddhaḥ śataghnyā hanumati sahasā mohanighno nyapaptat//48//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja ayamatrādbhutataro vimardaḥ/ yadā tu bhrāturmohamadhigamya bhāvilaṅkeśvarādakramameva karuṇavīrānubhāvabhāvitacittavṛttistathāvidhasyāpi darśanotsukaḥ samarudhyata paritaḥ kumbhakarṇapramukhayā rakṣaḥpṛtanayā tadā punaridameva pratyakārṣīt/ tathā hi/

purāṃ jetā pūrvaṃ tripuravijaye yāmudavahat sthitiṃ tāmevāyaṃ raghupativṛṣāśritya vapuṣā/

kṣaṇādrakṣonāthānujamiṣubhirācchidya kaṇaśa- ścamūṃ bhasmīkṛtyāpyanujamabhiyātyutsukatamaḥ//49//

(nirvarṇya/) aho vātsalyamahimā raghupuṅgavasya yena punarviṣayīkṛtamātrāmevānujasyāvasthāmabhijānāti sma/ 
(parito nirūpya/ saharṣam/) diṣṭayā svasti raghukulakumārābhyāmetābhyāmutpaśyāmi yatastāvadetayorasmin vyasanamahārṇave yātudhānādhīśenāpi saparivāreṇa kumbhakarṇavadhāt sambhrāntam/ 
(punaretau nirvarṇya/) kathamadyāpi pramugdhāveva/ viṣamo vyasanavyatikarastāvadāpatitaḥ/ yataḥ/

bahucchalāni rakṣāṃsi ripavastūvarau svayam/

eṣāvasthāpi kapayaḥ sahāyāste'pi viklavāḥ//50//

tat kimupakramaṃ daivamatreti na jāne//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja kimevamāśaṅkase/ paśya/ jāmbavatpratibodhitaḥ kilāyamacintyamahimaprathimā prābhañjaniḥ/ samprati utsphūrjadromakūpaḥ pralayaparimilatpāṃsuvarṣānukārī kiñcidbhugnāgrapucchāpratimavivalanāpāstanakṣatracakraḥ/

bhūmnautsukyānurūpavyavasitiradhikaṃ paryavaplutya gatvā kvāpi prājñaḥ kṣaṇārdhāt kamapi girimasāvāharannājagāma//51//

citrarathaḥ/ 
(vibhāvya/ sollāsam/) devarāja paśya paśya/

yathā candrālokaṃ kumudanivahaścumbakamaṇiṃ dṛṣatsārastattvāmṛtamapi bhavāmbhonidhigataḥ/

tathā sambhāvyaitau hanumadupanītādrimarutaṃ jhaṭityujjṛmbhete kimapi gahano vastumahimā//52//

(dakṣiṇato vibhāvya/) kathameṣa laṅkeśvaraḥ kalpāvasānanirmaryādaṃ pātha iva pāthonāthasya rākṣasabalamākarṣan punarabhyamitramabhyeti/ 
(vimṛśya/) samprati tu dharmayuddhasambhāvanā/ pratihatabahutarapradhānavyaktirāvaṇameghanādaśeṣametadrākṣasabalametāvapyubhau na gaṇyete kiṃ punaḥ paraḥsahasramapyasrapakīṭāḥ/ 
(punarlakṣmaṇaṃ nirvarṇya/) eṣa tu śāṇotkīrṇo maṇiriva ghanāmbhodamukto vivasvān niḥkośo'sirjhaṭitivigalatkañcukaḥ pannagendraḥ/

dīvyatyuccairlaghuraghupratiḥ kiṃ nu vā syāt kimanyad divyauṣadhyā jayati mahimā ko'pyacintyānubhāvaḥ//53//

(nirūpya/) kathaṃ prakrāntameva kapirākṣasanāsīracarayorbhaṭayoḥ punarāyodhanam/ tathā hi/

śitairvāṇaireke mṛdhabhuvi pare tīkṣṇanakharaiḥ kriyāsātatyenāpyahamahamikākrāntamanasaḥ/

mitho vidhyanti sma prabalatamasammardavidalat- kṣitikṣodaiḥ piṣṭātakasurabhivakṣastaṭabhṛtaḥ//54//

(saviśeṣaṃ niścitya/) tāvadantaramanayorbalayoradhigaṃsyamānaprātaḥsandhyayoryāvadandhatamasāruṇālokayoḥ/ tathā hi/

pratikṣaṇamiyaṃ rakṣaḥpṛtanā kṣīyatetarām/

tathā tathā plavaṅgānāṃ senānantaguṇaidhate//55//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja punarito mahat kadanamupakrāntam/

rakṣonātho raghūṇāṃ tvaritamadhibhuvā rāvaṇirlakṣmaṇena dvandvībhūya prahṛṣyadbhujabalamahimāviṣkṛteṣvāsaśikṣau/

divyāstrāṇāṃ prayogaṃ pratikṛtimucitāṃ cāśnuvānau mitho'mū mūrchatkalpāvasānajvalanaparibhavaṃ sainyayoḥ paryadātām//56//

citrarathaḥ/ devarāja duravabodho'yamanayormahāvīrayormithovimardaḥ/ tathā hi/

kṣveḍābhiḥ kakubhaḥ pṛṣatkanikarairvyoma dvidhākhaṇḍitair dehair vidviṣatāṃ dharātalam api pracchādayantau ciram/

kurvāte'śrujalāvilekṣaṇapathānyetāvakāṇḍoccara- dromāñcāni savepathūnyapi muhurvarṣmāṇi naḥ paśyatām//57//

(saviśeṣaṃ vibhāvya/) kathaṃ pratyakṣānumānābhyāmupalabhyamānamekameva vastu viprakṛṣṭāntaraṃ sampadyate/ tathā hi/

asmād rāvaṇavṛttād rāghavavṛttaṃ tu daśaguṇaṃ vīkṣe anumanye'nantaguṇaṃ pārśvapatatkauṇapendravinipātaiḥ//58//

(parito nirūpya/ sakutukāścaryam/)

yāvanto rajanīcarāḥ praharaṇodgūrṇadbhujāketavo yudhyanto'bhimukhāḥ sphuradbhujamadādhmātāḥ puro nirgatāḥ/

prakṣiptāśugajālapakṣapavanādhūte pratāpānale citraṃ dāśaratheḥ kṣaṇācchalabhatāṃ yānti sma sarve'pi te//59//

(savimarśam/) evaṃ kileyaṃ pāñcabhautikī sṛṣṭiḥ/

trailokyamapyaparyāptaṃ rakṣasāṃ sthātumapyadaḥ/

yeṣāṃ te kevalaṃ bhūmau vililyuḥ pañcatāṃ gatāḥ//60//

vāsavaḥ/ gandharvarāja paśya vismayanīyavipralambhau kilāmūḥ rāvaṇarāvaṇī/ yataḥ/

etābhyāṃ rāghavābhyāṃ sakutukamiṣubhiśchidyamāneṣu mūrdha- svekasyaiko'pyanantaḥ kimu sadṛśaguṇo varṇanīyaḥ parasya/

etatsampaśyatorapyaticiramanayoḥ ko'pyacintyaḥ prabhāvo yannotsāho na dhairyaṃ viramati na śiraśchedataḥ patriṇo'pi//61//

(nepathye/) bho bho rāmabhadra kimadyāpyupekṣase durvṛttamenaṃ kathaṃ caikakriyāsādhyametāvantamartham/ avadhatsva tāvat/

bhavān sītāṃ lokastribhuvanagataḥ prītimucitāṃ kanīyān paulastyaḥ puramamaratāṃ svāṃ punarayam/

kimatrānyat sākṣātkṛtaparamatattvo munigaṇaḥ prasādapronmīlanmudi manasi śāntiṃ ca  labhatām//62//

citrarathaḥ/ 
(niśamya/) kathameṣa divyarṣigaṇo'pyetayorvadhāya rāghavau tvarayati/ athavā durvṛttapraśāntiḥ kasya na manaḥprasattyai/ 
(sasambhramādbhutautsukyam/) devarāja paśya/

ābhyāṃ brahmācyutāsrasmaraṇasurabhibhirmārgaṇai rāghavābhyāṃ mūrdhānaścicchidāte rajanicarapate rāvaṇeścākrameṇa/

paścādrakṣaḥkabandho mṛdhabhuvi vivaśaḥ so'pi rakṣo'varodhaḥ kṣoṇyāṃ śrīdāśarathyoḥ śirasi ca viyataḥ puṣpavarṣaḥ papāta//63//

vāsavaḥ/ 
(nepathyābhimukhamavalokya/ sollāsam/) gandharvarāja paśya tāvat/ ete kila tribhuvanaśatrordaśakandharasya nidhanavṛttāntaśravaṇena pramodanirbharāḥ sahamaharṣayaḥ sumanasaḥ kamapi mahotsavamanububhūṣanto māmeva pratīkṣante/ tad gacchāmyeteṣāṃ manorathasampādanāya/ tvamapyetadvṛttāntanivedanena priyasakhamalakeśvaraṃ prīṇaya//

(iti parikramya niṣkrāntāḥ sarve//)

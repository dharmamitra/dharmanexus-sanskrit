(svagatam/) aho paraguṇotkarṣapariṇāmagrāhi saujanyapūtamantaḥkaraṇamasya rājanyapotasya/ pāramārthikavinayadurvibhāvastvatinipuṇabuddhigrāhyo mahānahaṅkāraḥ/

aprākṛtasya caritātiśayasya bhāvai-  ratyadbhutairmama hṛtasya tathāpyanāsthā/

ko'pyeṣa vīraśiśukākṛtiraprameya-  māhātmyasārasamudāyamayaḥ padārthaḥ//39//

āścaryam/

sambhāvyami bhuvanābhayadānapuṇya-  sambhāramasya vapuratra hi visphuranti/

lakṣmīśca sāttvikaguṇajvalitaṃ ca tejo  dharmaśca mānavijayau ca parākramaśca//40//

ayaṃ hi/

trātuṃ lokāniva pariṇataḥ kāyavānastravedaḥ  kṣātro dharmaḥ śrita iva tanuṃ brahmakośasya guptyai/

sāmarthyānāmiva samudayaḥ sañcayo vā guṇānā-  māvirbhūya sthita iva jagatpuṇyanirmāṇarāśiḥ//41//

(prakāśam/) he bhavatyaḥ praviśatviyaṃ vadhūrabhyantarameva//

rāmaḥ/ 
(svagatam/) evameva//

(nepathye/)

sīradhvajo dhanuṣpāṇirita evābhivartate/

gautamaśca śatānando janakānāṃ purohitaḥ//42//

sakhyaḥ/ bhaṭṭidārie parāgado evva tādo/ ehi abbhantaraṃ pavisa mha//

sītā/ 
(svagatam/) bhaavadi saṅgāmasirie eso de añjalī//

(iti niṣkrāntāḥ striyaḥ//)

jāmadagnyaḥ/

sa eṣa rājā janako manīṣī  purohitenāṅgirasena guptaḥ/

ādityaśiṣyaḥ kila yājñavalkyo  yasmai munirbrahma paraṃ vivavre//43//

sadvṛtta eṣaḥ/ tathāpi tu kṣatriya iti śiraḥśūlamutkopayati//

(tataḥ praviśataḥ sambhrāntau janakaśatānandau//)

śatānandaḥ/ rājan kimatra yuktam//

janakaḥ/ bhagavan/

ḷṣirayamatithiścedvi raḥ pādyamarghyaṃ  tadanu ca madhuparkaḥ kalpyatāṃ śrotriyāya/

atha tu ripurakasmād dveṣṭinaḥ putrabhāṇḍe  tadiha nayavihīne kārmukasyādhikāraḥ//44//

iti parikrāmataḥ/

rāmaḥ/ tat kimiti bāṣpāyitaṃ bhagavatā//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ na kiñcit/

sambhūyeva sukhāni cetasi paraṃ bhūmānamātanvate  yatrālokapathāvatāriṇi ratiṃ prastauti netrotsavaḥ/

sa tvaṃ nūtana eva kaṅkaṇadharaḥ śrīmān priyaścetaso  hantavyaḥ paribhūtavān gurumiti prāgeva dūyāmahe//45//

rāmaḥ/ bhārgava jñāyate māmanukampasa iti//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ are kimuta trāto'si/

amṛtādhmātajīmūtasnigdhasaṃhananasya te/

kuṭhāraḥ kambukaṇṭhasya ka ṃ kaṇṭhe patiṣpa[ya]ti//46//

rāmaḥ/ satyameva karuṇayā pratikṣipto'si//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ āḥ/ mayyeva bhrukuṭidharaḥ saṃvṛttaḥ/ are kṣatriyaḍimbha tvaṃ kila śiśurnavavadhūṭikāparigraha ityapūrvamupatapyate'smābhiḥ/

suprasiddhaḥ pravādo'yamitiheti ca gīyate/

jāmadagnyaḥ svayaṃ rāmo māturmūrdhānamācchinat//47//

api ca re mūḍha/

utkṛtyotkṛtya garbhānapi śakalayataḥ kṣatrasantānaroṣā-  duddhāmasyaikaviṃśatyavadhi vidhamataḥ sarvato rājavaṃśyān/

pitryaṃ tadraktapūrṇahradasavanamahānandamandāyamānaṃ  krodhāgniṃ kurvato me na khalu na viditaḥ sarvabhūtaiḥ svabhāvaḥ//48//

rāmaḥ/ nṛśaṃsatā hi nāma tatra puruṣadoṣaḥ/ tatra kā vikatthanā//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ ā kṣatriyabaṭo/ ati nāma pragalbhase/

prahara namatu cāpaṃ prākprahārapriyo'haṃ  mayi tu kṛtanighāte kiṃ vidadhyāt pareṇa/

dhagiti vitatavahanyūdgārabhāsvatkuṭhāra-  pravighaṭitakaṭhoraskandhabandhaḥ kabandhaḥ//49//

janakaśatānandau/ vatsa rāmabhadra visrabdhaṃ tāvadāsva//

rāmaḥ/ ka manujñānāpekṣaḥ saṃvṛtto'smi//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ api sukhamāṅgirasa//

śatānandaḥ/ viśeṣatastvaddarśanāt/ api ca/

tvaṃ naḥ pūjyatamo'tithiryadi punaḥ sajjātitheyā vayam/

jāmadagnyaḥ/ purodhaḥ/ sucaritavṛttaḥ śrotriyo gṛhamedhī yājñavalkyaśiṣyaḥ/ tatra sarvaṃ yujyate/ kintu nāhamātithyakāmaḥ//

śatānandaḥ/ kanyāntaḥpuramakramāt praviśatā sandūṣitā naḥ sthitiḥ/

jāmadagnyaḥ/ araṇyavāsī brāhmaṇo'hamanabhijñaḥ parameśvaragṛhācārasya//

rāmaḥ/ śobhata eva dattabhuvanaikadakṣiṇasya sāmanteṣvahaṅkārotprāsaḥ//

janakaḥ/ pāpaṃ vāñchasi karma rāghavaśiśāvasmatsanāthe katham/

(praviśya kañcukī/)

devyaḥ kaṅkaṇamocanāya militā rājanvaraḥ preṣyatām//50//

janakaśatānandau/

vatsa rāmabhadra śvaśrūjanastvāmāhvayati/ tad gamyatām/

rāmaḥ/ bhagavan jāmadagnyaḥ/ evamādiśanti guravaḥ//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ ko doṣaḥ/ kriyatāṃ lokadharmaḥ/ paśyantu ca tvāṃ jñātayaḥ/ kintu na ciraṃ janama[pa]deṣvāraṇyakāstiṣṭhanti/ gantukāmo'smi/ ato na kālaḥ pra[pa]rikṣeptavyaḥ//

rāmaḥ/ evam//

(praviśya sumantraḥ/) bhagavantau vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrau bhavataḥ sabhārgavānāhvayataḥ//

itare/ kva bhagavantau//

sumantraḥ/ mahārājadaśarathasyāntike//

rāmaḥ/ guruvacanād gacchāmaḥ//

(iti niṣkrāntāḥ sarve//)

likhettriṃśāṅgulaṃ khaḍgaṃ spaṣṭaṃ sāṅgaṃ manoramam|| 11||

pūrvoktena vidhānena tatra yaṣṭvā janārdanam|

muṣṭisthāne karaṃ sthāpya japellakṣaṃ samāhitaḥ|| 12||

yāvaduttiṣṭhate khaḍgo muṣṭinā grāhayettataḥ|

taṃ gṛhītvā tu sarveṣāṃ siddhānāmadhipo bhavet|| 13||

vidyādharāṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ cakravartitvamāpnuyāt|

pūrvoktena vidhānena pūjyayenmantranāyakam|| 14||

tāvatparijapenmantrī yāvajjvālāṃ pramuñcati|| 15||

tanmadhye dṛśyate kāntā kanyakārūpadhāriṇī|

siddhāsmīti vadetsā tu kuryātkāryaṃ yathepsitam|| 16||

anenaiva vidhānena añjanādīni sādhayet|

rocanāṃ kuṃkumaṃ caiva haratālaṃ manacchilām|| 17||

cakrāṅkamoṣadhīṃ tatra pañcamaṃ viniyojayet|

sūkṣmapiṣṭāṃ tu tāṃ kṛtvā āloḍya madhuraistribhiḥ|| 18||

ekādaśyāṃ samabhyarcya maṇḍale mantranāyakam|

tatrādhivāsayettāṃ tu gulikāṃ patrasaṃpuṭe|| 19||

śvetārkasya vidhānena dvādaśyāṃ tu yajettataḥ|

daśāyutān japenmantrī tataḥ siddhā tu sā bhavet|| 20||

tayā karasthayā mantrī vicaredgaganāntare|

pātāle vā mahīpṛṣṭe lokapālapure .api vā|| 21||

cakrapāṇiryathā viṣṇuradṛśyaḥ sarvadevataiḥ|

jāyate sādhakendrastu mantrasyāsya prabhāvataḥ|| 22||

brāhmaṇaṃ kṣatriyaṃ vāpi nirvraṇaṃ śavamānayet|

śūnye gṛhe samāveśya snāpayedarcayettataḥ|| 23||

yathāvidhānato mantrī mantraṃ saṃpūjyamaṇḍale|

upaviṣṭaṃ śavaṃ sthāpya mantraṃ tasyāgrato japet|| 24||

tāḍayetsarṣapāṇāṃ tu śatenāṣṭottarena tu|

tataḥ sa vegādutthāya bravīti praṇataṃ vacaḥ|| 25||

kiṃkaro .asmi tavādyāhaṃ mamājñā saṃpradīyatām|

padalepaṃ rasaṃ siddhaṃ rasāyanamathāpi vā|| 26||

yadanyanmanasāpīṣṭaṃ tanmadhyādapi kalpayet|

kauṣeyaṃ vastramānīya yakṣiṇīṃ tatra lekhayet|| 27||

sarvābharaṇasaṃyuktāṃ surūpāṃ manasepsitām|

yathāvidhānato mantrī mantraṃ saṃpūjya pūrvavat|| 28||

upoṣitasyārdharātre dadyāddhūpaṃ tu guggulum|

saptāhameva kurvīta japeccāpyayutaṃ sadā|| 29||

saptame hyardharātre tu saśabdaṃ kampate paṭaḥ|

yadā tu na kṣubhenmantrī tataḥ siddhā tu sā bhavet|| 30||

mātā vā bhaginī bhāryā kā bhavāmyadya bhāṣayet|

yatheṣṭaṃ tasyā vaktavyaṃ nirvikalpena cetasā|

sā siddhā sarvakāmāni sādhayatyāśu mantriṇaḥ||  || 31||

  iti jayottare siddhipaṭalaṣṭamaḥ||  ||

atha yogavibhūtyarthaṃ yogaṃ yuñjīta vaiṣṇavam|

sugupte vijane deśe nirdvandve śubhalakṣaṇe|| 1||

jitadarpāṃ matiṃ kṛtvā sarvabhūtahite sthitaḥ|

devāgnigurubhaktaśca sacchāstrābhirataḥ sadā|| 2||

bhūtadrohaparityāgī alepī saṃyatendriyaḥ|

āsane copaviṣṭastu suśubhe lakṣaṇānvite|| 3||

śubhadārusamutthe tu caturviṃśāṅgulāyute|

dvādaśāṅgulamānena kṛtotsedhe susaṃsthite|| 4||

kṛṣṇājinoparisthena sudhautenāpi vāsasā|

kuśaiśca mṛdubhiścānyairyute kenāpi vāvṛte|| 5||

tatropaviṣṭaḥ satataṃ yogāṅgāni samabhyaset|

recakādicaturbhistu mātrābhedakṛtaistu saḥ|| 6||

kanīyānmadhyamo jyeṣṭhaḥ prāṇāyāmaḥ prakīrttitaḥ|

pratyāhāraṃ tataḥ kuryāccittasaṃyamanaṃ tataḥ|| 7||

buddhirmanastvahaṅkārastribhiścittaṃ prakīrttitam|

tathāpi manasaḥ kuryātpratyāhāraṃ prayatnataḥ|| 8||

manaścaturvidhaḥ proktaḥ pratyāhāreṇa yojitaḥ|

tiṣṭhate lakṣyamārge tu nānyathā tu kadācana|| 9||

manaścaturvidhaścaiva sa ca kārtsnyena me śṛṇu|

saṃśliṣṭaśca sulīnaśca vikṣipto gatirāgatiḥ|| 10||

dhyānaṃ lakṣyasya niṣpattiścintanaṃ tatprakīrttitam|

dhāraṇāpi tato dhāryā śītamāpyamanuttamam|| 11||

caturvidhā ca sā dhāryā pratitantravyavasthayā|

japannimīlitākṣeṇa kuryānmantrasya vācakam|| 12||

vācyasya pratipattyarthaṃ piṇḍabījapadātmakam|

trividhaṃ tatprakurvīta upāṃśuvācyamānasam|| 13||

yogo .api trividhaḥ proktaḥ sa tu dhyānabalādbhavet|

prākṛtaṃ pauruṣaṃ proktamaiśvaryaṃ tu tṛtīyakam|| 14||

ūhasthaḥ procyate tarkaḥ sa ca siddhivicārakaḥ||

samādhistvātmalābhastu ānandaḥ parikīrttitaḥ|| 15||

sa tu lakṣyaṃ parityajya mantroccāraṇavarjjitaḥ|

yadā tu vyajyate brahma kalāṃśādivivarjitaḥ||	16||

samādhau pariniṣpanne paraṃ prāpnoti pauruṣam|

prāṇāyāmādito yāvatsamādhyantaṃ prakīrttitaḥ|

dviguṇatriguṇenaiva mātrābhedena saṃsthitaḥ|| 17||

yogāsanāni kurvīta vidhimenaṃ yathoditam||

yogāsanāni catvāri yogapaṭṭena bandhayet|| 18||

paryaṅkamacalaṃ cāpi bhadraṃ vā svastikaṃ dṛḍham|

eṣāmekatame sthitvā ṛjukāyorddhvataḥ kramāt|| 19||

sṛṣṭāṅgasandhiḥ kurvīta vistīrṇoraḥsukandharaḥ||

bāhū praśithile kṛtvā kiṃcidākuñcayecchiraḥ|| 20||

nābhau jaghanamadhye tu hastau kacchapisaṃsthitau||

savyasya copariṣṭhaṃ tu vāme savyamathāpi vā|| 21||

uttānau tu karau kṛtvā kacchapye pāṇi yojayet||

grīvāṃ tu vidhṛyādyatnānnātistabdhāṃ na kuñcitām|| 22||

kiñcinnimīlya netre tu nāsāgramavalokayan|

samudgavatpidhāyāsyaṃ dantairdantānna saṃspṛśet|| 23||

sagarbhaṃ yogamātiṣṭhe nirgarbhaṃ vā suyantritaḥ||

tatastu prerayedvāyuṃ prāṇākhyaṃ cittasaṃyutam|| 24||

recakādicaturbhedaiḥ prāṇāyāmaḥ prakīrttitaḥ|

cittaṃ jayetsadā yatnāddurjayaṃ devadānavaiḥ|| 25||

yadyapyabhyāsavairāgyaiścittaṃ saṃyamyate balāt|

tathāpi yatnamātiṣṭhedupādhīnāṃ tu varjane|| 26||

dvividhāstāstu vijñeyāḥ sannikṛṣṭā manotthitāḥ||

sannikṛṣṭāḥ sthāmabhūtā manotthā manasā kṛtāḥ|| 27||

na daṃśamaśakīrṇṇe tu nispande gandhavarjite|

nimīlitākṣaḥ saṃtiṣṭhedrasanendriyasaṃyataḥ|| 28||

evaṃ parityajetsarvānupādhīnsannikarṣajān|

vāsanotthāṃstu vividhān samyaglakṣyaṃ samāsthitaḥ|| 29||

pratyāharetsadā cittaṃ vikṣiptaṃ sarvavastuṣu|

tāmasaḥ sa tu boddhavyaścittaḥ sarvatra sarvadā|| 30||

gatirāgatistato jñeyo abhyāsādrājasastu saḥ|

sāttvikaḥ kathyate cittaṃ saṃśliṣṭaṃ lakṣyagocare|| 31||

saṃlīnaśca tato jñeyo guṇātītastapodhana|

dhyānamevaṃvidhaṃ kuryātsagarbhe prāṇanigrahe|| 32||

ucyate ca tato bhūyastrividhaṃ yogināṃ hitam|

sakalaṃ niṣkalaṃ viṣṇuṃ tṛtīyaṃ pararūpiṇam|| 33||

anyattu trividhaṃ jñeyaṃ śabdaṃ vyoma tvavigraham|

vigrahaṃ devadevasya lakṣyarūpaṃ vicintayet|| 34||

dhyānamevaṃ samuddiṣṭaṃ yāvadvyomāntiko bhavet|

tāvacca bhāvayellakṣyaṃ yāvallakṣyaṃ na bhāvayet|| 35||

bhāvaṃ hyabhāvamāpannaṃ paśyedbhāvaṃ paraṃ padam|

sthūlaṃ samabhyasetpūrvaṃ tataḥ sūkṣmaṃ tataḥ param|| 36||

evaṃ vilīyate cittaṃ lakṣyaṃ copahitaiḥ saha|

vigrahaṃ devadevasya dhyāyeddhṛtpadmagocare|| 37||

tato .anyaṃ cintayellakṣyaṃ sthūlaṃ sūkṣmamathāparam|

evamabhyasatastasya guṇotkarṣaḥ prajāyate|| 38||

tataḥ sūkṣmataraṃ bhindaṃstato nādamathāparam|

taṃ vidyātsarvamantrāṇāmūrdhvamākramya tiṣṭhati|| 39||

tatastu rājikābījapramāṇaṃ cintayetkramāt|

utpādakaṃ svaśaktyā tu yasyānte nādamāśritaḥ|| 40||

taṃ nilīya samutpannamaśvavālapramāṇakam|

yaḥ? sadā gacchate vyomni manaścāgacchate hṛdi|| 41||

tato vicintayetsūkṣmaṃ śirovālapramāṇakam|

brahmanāḍīmanenāśu dṛśyate dhyānasevanāt|| 42||

tato .aṅgaromamātraṃ tu bisavālapramāṇakam|

dhyāyenniyamamāsthāya brahmanāḍīprakāśakam|| 43||

yadā sā dṛśyate nāḍī sādṛśyarahitānanā|

tayā yāyātparaṃ sthānaṃ brahmarandhrapathena tu|| 44||

tatastu vyajyate sattā svakīyā brahmarūpiṇī|

halahetiṃ hayagrīvaṃ harirūpadharaṃ vibhum|

yena raudraṃ vapuḥ kṛtvā vidhṛtaṃ kāpilaṃ tanuḥ|

viśvarūpeṇa devānāṃ vidhṛtaṃ sarvadā vapuḥ|| 57||

saṃkarṣaṇa viśālārka pradyumna kusumāyudha|

aniruddha ca sarvajña tvameva jagataḥ prabhuḥ|| 58||

tvayā sṛṣṭaṃ jagatsarvaṃ brahmatvena trayī yathā|

sthityarthaṃ triṣu lokeṣu sādhūn pālayate .acyutaḥ|| 59||

yajñarūpeṇa devānāṃ havyaṃ vahnimukhādvibho|| 60||

ācāryasadṛśenaiva guṇaiḥ kurvīta sādhakām|?

dhyātvā tu tattvaniḥśreṇīṃ sakalāṃ niṣkalāṃ parām|

rājopacāravidhinā abhiṣekaṃ guroḥ smṛtam|| 9||

tritattvavitsarvajātīnāṃ dīkṣayedanulomataḥ|| 10||

sarvaratnaiḥ susaṃpūrṇṇān sarvauṣadhisamanvitān|| 11||

sarvabījaiḥ samākīrṇān kadalīdalaśobhitān|

evaṃvidhāṃstu kalaśān satyadaiśca pratiṣṭhitān|| 12||

etatsarvaṃ tathaikasmin kalaśe vāpi pūjayet|

sauvarṇe rājate tāmre mṛṇmaye vā guṇānvite|| 13||

phalamūlaiśca sahite candanādyaiḥ sucarcite|

śaṅkhasvastikapadmādyaiḥ svastikenopaśobhite|| 14||

pūrite tīrthatoyena mantreśaṃ tatra pūjayet|| 15||

ādau tu kalaśe pūjyaṃ tato maṇḍalamadhyagam|

vahnisthaṃ ca tato pūjyaṃ śiṣyadehe tataḥ punaḥ|| 16||

kalaśādiṣu sarveṣu gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ kramāt|| 17||

sahasraparijaptena samyagaṣṭādhikena tu|

dhyāyedamṛtasaṃpūrṇaṃ ratnauṣadhisamanvitam|| 19||

anena vidhinā kuryādācāryasyābhiṣecanam|

tasya mūrdhni śubhaṃ dattvā kalaśaṃ mantrapūjitam|| 20||

mantrapīṭhatvamāpanne lājāprakaraśobhite|| 23||

devyo .aṅgāni yathārcāyāṃ daleṣvevaṃ samālikhet|| 33||

  iti jayottare .abhiṣekapaṭalaḥ daśamaḥ||  ||

bhagavānuvāca|

idamanyatpravakṣyāmi muniśreṣṭha tapodhana|

mantrarājasya yadvīryaṃ prādhānyaṃ prabhaviṣṇutā|| 1||

yenedaṃ pūjitaṃ samyagjñātaṃ dhyātaṃ sakṛtsmṛtam|

tasya siddhiḥ parā divyā svarge vai sa ca tiṣṭhati|| 2||

na cāsya sadṛśo mantro mantrarājasya vidyate|

imaṃ mantreśvaraṃ japtvā sarvakāmānavāpnuyāt|| 3||

na cāsya sadṛśo mantro mantrakoṭiśatairapi|

etanmantraṃ paraṃ labdhvā tyaja sarvaṃ palālavat|| 4||

cintāmaṇiryathā loke yadabhīṣṭaṃ prayacchati|

evaṃ manuvaro hyeṣa sādhakasya prayacchati|| 5||

yasyāgrato devatāyā imaṃ mantreśvaraṃ japet|

sā tasya sarvakāmāni prasannā saṃprayacchati|| 6||

lakṣāhutipradānena tilānāṃ kṛṣṇavarcasām|

yastu saṃvatsaraṃ pūrṇaṃ hunedante ghṛtāhutīḥ|| 7||

ekādaśasahasrāṇi padmaviṣayutāni ca|

tasya kṣipraṃ dadatyeṣa mantreśo vipulaṃ dhanam|| 8||

grāmānaśvān hiraṇyaṃ ca vapuryacca kṛtākṛtam|

āyuḥ putrāṃstathārogyaṃ saubhāgyaṃ sarvatomukham|| 9||

bahunātra kimuktena pañcaviṣṇukasaṃyutam|

saṃtarpitaṃ japanmantraṃ sarvakāmānavāpnuyāt|| 10||

lakṣe lakṣāyute japte aṇimādyāni sādhayet|

prāṇāyāmāviyuktena japamātreṇa sādhakaḥ|| 11||

sākṣātpaśyati taṃ devaṃ viṣṇuṃ paramarūpiṇam|

iti te sarvamākhyātaṃ rahasyaṃ dvijasattama|| 12||

na deyaṃ pāpaśīlāya krūrakarmaratāya ca|

kapaṭenopapannāya nāstikāya ca suvrata|| 13||

gurubhaktāya dāntāya devakarmaratāya ca|

tasmai deyamidaṃ śāstraṃ gurvājñābhiratāya ca||	14||

guruṃ vinā na śāstrāṇāṃ prāptirasti mahītale|

na ca saṃskārasaṃprāptirna ca mantrādhikāritā|| 15||

tasmātsarvaprayatnena kāryā bhaktirguroḥ sadā|

ācārāṇāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ mūlamārādhanaṃ guroḥ|| 16||

iti te sarvamākhyātaṃ jayottaramidaṃ mune|

likhitaṃ yatra tiṣṭheta tatra lakṣmīḥ pravarttate|| 17||   ||

  iti jayottare ekādaśamaḥ paṭalaḥ samāptaḥ||   ||

  iti jayākṣarasaṃhitāyāṃ jayottaraṃ samāptamiti||

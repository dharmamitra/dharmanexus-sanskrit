athāṅgānāṃ śivasyāsya varṇalakṣaṇamucyate|

yajjñātvā śivatāṃ yāti dagdhamāyādibandhanaḥ|| 1||

anābhipūrvavaccakraṃ caturāvaraṇairvinā|

ālikhya vinyasettasya mārgamūle .akṣaraṃ param|| 2||

śivoddhārakramānnyāso mantroddhāraparibhramāt|

sarvātmasuśivādyo .asau netrāntaḥ parikīrtitaḥ|| 3||

oṃkāraśca yavau śivaśca te śubāśca punaśśa ca|

phadyavaśca tathā bhūyo bhūyaścaiva tathāpadaḥ|| 4||

śānte ca sakasābhūyo vaśvajāsavisargakaḥ|

bandharī ca tatassvāca ṭaśca paśca tathāntataḥ|| 5||

rvārarvārastathaireha śisau dīrgho dvitīyakaḥ|

ghūkāraśca rakāraśca hyakāraḥ pracivastataḥ|| 6||

tmakaukāvarvayārṇāśca ikāraḥ praṇavastataḥ|

cambhāvante punarna ca rayyanantrahi ghū tathā|| 7||

da ca praśca tathā ṭaśca he ca bhūyo .anta eva ca|

vapuprāmo visargeṇa makāraḥ pirṇaśāspṛkau|| 8||

monte ṣaṣṭhasya mārgasya rāruśāśi paṭāḥ karī|

rahau caiva tato hā ca yakumāvaśca rāpunaḥ|| 9||

laṅkiraspṛkadekārāḥ parunānavame pathi|

syajimāḥ kamarāścaiva bhaśca vā ca tato .antataḥ|| 10||

rasasāhaśca tamhā ca kinughovāśca yastataḥ|

meghadādadvitīyāśca kasūprāśca ramau ca dhīḥ|| 11||

svasau śiyapavā kṣmaśca vidhovāśca ma eva ca|

radyavajvārtavāssūmya ramāścaiva tataśca hi|| 12||

pabhapraliyya śikṣmāśca sataghātāścaturdaśa|

rāvasānī mavāpāśca rvaratannovirāmataḥ|| 13||

yahannāstvā ca hājñau ca hadatāyaḥ śireva ca|

yokāraśca vanau hāca ghoyādhaṣṭāśca nustataḥ|| 14||

ghāvonte punargācabhaśca mo ca tato .akṣaraḥ|

rambharastaṃ rutāḥ praśca yavotstuśi kamāstataḥ|| 15||

tathā tatra vakāraśca bhaśca paśca yavo .antataḥ|

yomatevāvahājñāśca yacau hrandau punastataḥ|| 16||

gapausmatmaucavandaśca śastambhaścaiva phaṭ cuvāk|

sañcasaśca tathā hā ca kaṃpiṃ yacca nibhau tathā|| 17||

cañca taśca tathoṃkāro bharvau praṇava eva ca|

magau bavau tathā yaśca ṭatmau juṅkāra eva ca|| 18||

ante vāde suhālandhajabhuṃ prahesa

sarvātmasaṃjñaṃ hṛdayamakṣarāṇi tu saptatiḥ|| 19||

oṃkārādi hakārāntamuddharetkramaśaḥ purā|

aṣṭākṣaraṃ śirastasmātsuśivākhyaṃ samuddharet|| 20||

jvālinīṃ paratastasmācchikhāmekādaśākṣarām|

ṣaḍuttaraṃ śataṃ tasmāt akṣaraṇāṃ tu piṅgalam|| 21||

aghorāstraṃ tatastriṃśaddvādaśākṣaralakṣitam|

caturviṃśatikāṃ devīṃ gāyatrīmuddharetkramāt|| 22||

netratryakṣaraṃ tasmādvidyānāmālayaṃ param|

catuṣṣaṣṭyuttaraṃ jñeyamakṣarāṇāṃ śatadvayam|

hṛdayādivibhāgena saṃsthitaṃ siddhimuktidam|| 23||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe śivāṅgalakṣaṇapaṭalaḥ saptamaḥ||

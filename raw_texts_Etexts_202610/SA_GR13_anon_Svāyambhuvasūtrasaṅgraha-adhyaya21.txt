athācāryavrataṃ śreṣṭhaṃ caradācārya eva hi|

sadyādiśivaparyantāmabhyasenmantrasaṃhitām|| 1||

pakṣatrayamadhaḥśāyī naktāśī carubhuk sadā|

maunī triṣavaṇasnāyī dvandvānudvignasaṃyamī|| 2||

śuklāmbaradharo nityaṃ śuklamālyānulepanaḥ|

yogapaṭṭākṣasūtrī ca mauñjyajinapa  || 3||

 kuryāt japānte śaktitaḥ kramāt|

ghṛtena vā ghṛtāktairvā tilaiḥ kṣīreṇa taṇḍulaiḥ|| 4||

vrīhibhirvā samidbhirvā dadhnāvāpi yavaistathā|

śrīphalairbilvapatrairvā puṣpairvā yājñikaiḥ śubhaiḥ|| 5||

tato vratasamāptau tu pūrvoktaṃ maṇḍalaṃ yajet|

vratagrahaṇakāle ca deśikaḥ sādhakastathā|| 6||

abhiṣicyātmanātmānamanantavijaye tataḥ|

carenmantravrataṃ mantrī sādhyamantroktavartmanā|| 7||

pāriyātre mahendre vā sahye vā malaye .api vā|

mantrī devagirau vāpi vindhye vā śuktiparvate|| 8||

gaṅgātaṭe samudre vā śrīśaile ṛkṣaparvate|

arbude nimnagāyāṃ vā yodadhau saṅgatā svayaṃ|| 9||

tatra puṣpaphalopetaṃ mṛtkuśopacitaṃ śubham|

prācyodakaṃ samitsphītaṃ niḥśabdaṃ sthānamāśrayet|| 10||

tasminsthitaḥ prasannātmā kāyaśuddhiṃ samāśrayet|

kṛcchracāndrāyaṇādyairvā śākādyaiḥ sampracoditaiḥ|| 11||

prāṇavṛttinirodhena praṇavaṃ tu samabhyaset|

sahasrāṇāṃ śataṃ pūrṇaṃ ṣaṇmāsakṛtaniścayaḥ|| 12||

śuddhakāyaḥ sunakṣatranimittopacite dine|

sampūjya devadeveśaṃ kuryāttilaṅgaparigraham|| 13||

sthāpitamṛṣibhiḥ pūrvaṃ sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitam|

āśrayetsiddhidaṃ liṅgaṃ svayaṃ vā parikalpitam|| 14||

kīlitaṃ varjayedviprāḥ skandaviṣṇvindramātṛbhiḥ|

uparyupari liṅgaṃ ca mukhaliṅgaṃ tathaiva ca|| 15||

athavā lakṣaṇairyuktaṃ sthaṇḍilaṃ parikalpayet|

sthaṇḍile .api hi mantrāṇāṃ siddhirāśu pravartate|| 16||

śreṣṭhaṃ paścānmukhaṃ liṅgamabhāve prāṅmukhaṃ smṛtam|

yāmyakauberavaktraṃ tu na siddhāviṣyate budhaiḥ|| 17||

parigṛhyepsitaṃ liṅgaṃ liṅgakalpoditaṃ śubham|

pūjayetsatataṃ mantrī matvā dehamaśāśvatam|| 18||

toyabudbudasaṃkāśaṃ divyaiśvaryavivarjitam|

sarvaduḥkhāśrayaṃ puṃsāmiti matvā śamaṃ vrajet|| 19||

gajavājirathastrīṇāṃ na spṛhettu kadācana|

chāyākrīḍanasārūpyāḥ kṣaṇikā bandhahetavaḥ|| 20||

etadrūpamadharmasya yatspṛśetsādhakaḥ striyam|

tasmāccittaṃ manau sthāpya caredvai vidhicoditam|| 21||

yadyākṛṣyeta viṣayairnāgagandharvayakṣiṇīḥ|

mantrairākṛṣya bhuñjīta na bhūstrīśca balātspṛśet|| 22||

tābhiḥ saha ramettāvadviṣayairyadi pīḍitaḥ|

dehapāto bhavedyāvattato nirvāṇamṛcchati|| 23||

vādyādiṣu prasaṅgeṣu nṛttodyeṣvapareṣvapi|

svalpāmapi matiṃ mantrī na kuryādaupasargikīm|| 24||

prādurbhavanti siddhasya sarvārthā vimalāḥ svayam|

tasmātsarvaṃ parityajya mantrasiddhiparo bhavet|| 25||

sarvāsāṃ mantrasiddhīnāṃ mūlaṃ vidyāṅgapañcakam|

atastamabhyasedādau kalpoktena krameṇa tu|| 26||

ayutāni daśaikaikaṃ vidhiśāstroktavartmanā|

japtvā vrataiśca vidhivat snātaḥ siddhyai japenmanum|| 27||

na vilambitamaspaṣṭaṃ na cāsvīkṛtamadrutam|

nāsaṃkhyaṃ na manobhrāntajapaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ|| 28||

sitavāsāḥ sitoṣṇīṣaḥ sitayajñopavītyapi|

sitānulepanaḥ sragvī caredvidyādhipavratam|| 29||

raktāmbaradharo mantrī raktamālyānulepanaḥ|

māsamekaṃ puroktaṃ ca caredbrahmaśirovratam|| 30||

pītavāsāścarenmāsaṃ pītamālyānulepanaḥ|

pītayajñopavītī ca rudrāṇyā vratamuttamam|| 31||

vrataṃ puruhūtasyāpi māsamevaṃ caredbudhaḥ|

sarvaṃ kṛṣṇopacāreṇa śivārcanarataḥ sadā|| 32||

citrāmbaradharo dhīraḥ citramālyānulepanaḥ|

sarvasiddhaparo mantrī caretpāśupatavratam|| 33||

vratavratasamāptau ca kalaśena śivāmbhasā|

svamantraparijaptena svātmānamabhiṣecayet|| 34||

evaṃ vidyāvrataḥ snātaḥ sarvato .adhikṛto .anaghaḥ|

japenmantramanudvignaḥ svakalpavidhinā tataḥ|| 35||

susaṃyamyendriyagrāmamaluptaniyamaḥ sadā|

lope vā saṃyamaṃ kāryaṃ vyuktamaśruticoditam|| 36||

na nindyā brāhmaṇā gāvo na nindyāḥ śivamūrtayaḥ|

na devā ṛṣayaścaiva na gaṇā na ca mātaraḥ|| 37||

eṣāmekatamasyāpi yadi nindāṃ samācaret|

snātvā sarvātmanā kuryātprāṇāyāmaṃ dviraṣṭakam|| 38||

retotsarge niśāyāṃ tu ṣaḍūnamidameva hi|

divāsvapne ca pūrvoktamaghoreṇa caredvratam|| 39||

saṃgamya yoṣitaṃ mantrī praṇavena mahātmanā|

prāṇavṛttinirodhena lakṣamāvṛtya śudhyati|| 40||

vidyāvratamatikrānto nirārambhavratastathā|

sahasraṃ pūrvavatsamyagācāryastriguṇaṃ hi tat|| 41||

ananujñātamantrastu na kuryānmāntrikaṃ vidhim|

kṛtvā mohājjapetsadya mantrakaḥ ṣaḍbhiranyathā|| 42||

kuśaśayyāsano maunī kuśasnāyī kuśāmbupaḥ|

kuśabhūrmedhyabhuk mantrī caredvidhyuditavratam|| 43||

nirmālyaṃ śāṅkaraṃ mohāt kuryādbrahmajapakramāt|

śatikaṃ triguṇaṃ kāmāt pañcagavyāṇyupoṣitaḥ|| 44||

etadeva vrataṃ kuryāttriguṇaṃ kevalāsya dhīḥ|

jānāno .anantayāgena padaśudhyā ca śudhyati|| 45||

itareṣāṃ tu sarveṣāṃ samayānāṃ vyatikrame|

japedekādaśātmānamamatyopoṣitastryaham|| 46||

matyā kṛcchramatiṃ dorvā mitaṃ piṇḍaṃ vrataṃ caret|

mahāpātakināṃ caiva budhvānīkṛtimādiśet|| 47||

sarveṣāmeva pāpānāṃ parāniṣkṛtirucyate|

ekādaśātmano vyastaṃ samastaṃ vā vidhau japet|| 48||

ācāryoktavacastathyaṃ kuryādvai hyavicāritam|

tasmātsarvamavāpnoti paralokaihikaṃ phalam|| 49||

sakṛjjapaṃ samārabhya śivasyaikādaśātmanaḥ|

lakṣaṃ yāvatparā niṣṭhā proktā pāpakṛtāmiha|| 50||

tasmādvyatikramaṃ jñātvā prāyaścittaṃ caretsudhīḥ|

mantrasiddhyupabhogārthaṃ lokasampratimātrataḥ|| 51||

nityaṃ sandhyāmupāsīta lope copavaseddinam|

nīrujaḥ sarujaścānte sadyojātaśataṃ japet|| 52||

deśiko .ahnikavicchinne triguṇaṃ tu tadeva hi|

kuryātsvātmavaśotthānaḥ prāguktaṃ tu śivaṃ japet|| 53||

bahudaivasike bhraṃśe ṣaḍguṇaṃ yadudīritam|

somaḥ sāhasriko vā syādbrahmabhiḥ śatiko .athavā|| 54||

upavāsastathā snānaṃ pañcagavyāśanaṃ japaḥ|

homaścaiva malaḥ hanti sarvasaṅkarajaṃ nṛṇām|| 55||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe prāyaścittapaṭalaḥ ekaviṃśatitamaḥ||

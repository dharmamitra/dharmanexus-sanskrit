athārcanaṃ śiva huḥ sarvavaiśeṣikaṃ budhā|

tada  ro? samāsena sāmānyavidhipūrvakam|| 1||

ijyādīśāntake vidvānuttarābhimukhaḥ sthitaḥ|

dagdhvā pāśātmakaṃ kāyaṃ praṇavenāgninā tataḥ|| 2||

dhyāyetsarvagataṃ sūkṣmamātmānaṃ praṇavātmakam|

tasya brahmakalākāyaṃ cintayedanupūrvaśaḥ|| 3||

tatra mudrāṃ namaskārāṃ baddhvā sañcintayetpurā|

śirāṃsi pañcabhiḥ pañca pañcamena tu kārayet|| 4||

pañcamudrāṃ tato baddhvā catvāri vadanāni tu|

kalākalitamantreṇa caturthenaiva cintayet|| 5||

niṣṭhurāṃ samprayogajño bahurūpaṃ tu pañcadhā|

cintayeddhṛdayaṃ grīvāmaṃsau pṛṣṭhaṃ vibhāgavit|| 6||

vāmadevaṃ tu navadhā baddhvā tu śaśakarṇikām|

liṅgaṃ pāyustathā coro jānunī ca tathā padau|| 7||

jaṅghe caiva tathā pārśve vāmaṃ dakṣiṇameva ca|

namaskārāntakalitaṃ mantraṃ mantrī niveśayet|| 8||

mukulīṃ tu tato baddhvā sadyojātaṃ tu pañcadhā|

kalākalitamātmānaṃ cintayet cintako budhaḥ|| 9||

pādau pāṇī tathā bāhū nāsikā ca śirastathā|

pūrvavatkalpayitvā tu śivaṃ vastupade nyaset|| 10||

śivamudrāprayogajñaḥ śivadhāma vibhāgavit|

vinyaseddhṛdaye paścāt sarvātmānaṃ ca śāntayā|| 11||

liṅgākhyayā ca suśivaṃ vinyasya śirasi kramāt|

śikhāyāṃ jvālinīmantradhvajena tu vicintayet|| 12||

nyasetsūryasamaprakhyamāvāhinyā (tadaṅgake)|

vinyasya sarvato devaṃ gāyatrīmurasi nyaset|| 13||

netreṇa netraṃ netreṣu hastayorastrameva ca|

tanmudrayaiva vinyasya śivārcanamathācaret|| 14||

yathopahārasampannaḥ śivāstreṇa viśodhya ca|

pūjāsthānaṃ tataḥ paścādarghyapātraṃ prapūrayet|| 15||

śarveṇa lokanāthasya dattvā puṣpāṇyanukramāt|

nirmālyaṃ tatpadāstreṇa tasmādapanayettataḥ|| 16||

liṅgaṃ ca piṇḍikāṃ caiva samprakṣālya śivāmbhasā|

arghyaṃ prakalpya pūrvoktamaravindaṃ tato nyaset|| 17||

dharmaṃ jñānaṃ ca vairāgyamaiśvaryaṃ ca tathāparam|

anantaṃ hṛdayenaiva vinyasedanupūrvaśaḥ|| 18||

rateṣu śaktayaḥ sthāpyāḥ sarvavyāpipadena tu|

sthāpayedvyomarūpeṇa karṇikāyāṃ manonmanīm|| 19||

tataḥ sarvagataṃ devaṃ śivaṃ praṇavasaṃsthitam|

pūrvavatsakalīkṛtya śivamāvāhayettataḥ|| 20||

sadyena padasaṃsthena vāmadevena vinyaset|

sānnidhyaṃ bahurūpeṇa puruṣeṇārghayetkramāt|| 21||

padeśānena puṣpāṇi dattvā dikṣu niveśayet|

akṣarāṇi śivoktāni kālātmāhyadhikāni tu|| 22||

anantavijayoktaṃ vā vidyāṅgāstrāntamaṇḍalam|

saṃsthāpya pūjayetkāmye nitye caiva niyogataḥ|| 23||

gandhodakaṃ tu sadyena snānārthamabhimantrayet|

vāmadevena vastrāṇi mandraṃ ca kaṭisūtrakam|| 24||

vilepanamaghoreṇa hārakeyūrakādikam|

śodhitaṃ pūrvamastreṇa dadyāttamabhimantritam|| 25||

madhusarpirdadhikṣīradhūpanaivedyadīpakam|

puruṣeṇa tu mantreṇa śeṣamīśena mantritam|| 26||

padātmanāṃ padaireva sadyādīnāṃ hi tatpadaiḥ|

gāyatryā hṛdayādīnāmitareṣāṃ svamūrtibhiḥ|| 27||

arcanaṃ ca prakartavyaṃ hṛdayaiścakravartinā|

aṣṭānāṃ pṛthagaṣṭābhiranantādyairanukramāt|| 28||

prarocanaṃ ca mantrāṇāṃ mudrābhiradhunocyate|

vidyāṅgāni tu catvāri kālakarṇyā prarocayet|| 29||

tathā paścātpadāstraṃca śodhanyaiva hi mantravit|

namaskāreṇa śeṣāṃstu vidyeśānpadmamudrayā|| 30||

evaṃ sampūjite deve sādhyamantraṃ niveśayet|

vidyāṅgopacitaṃ mantrī praṇave kamalāsane|| 31||

aghorāstrasya pūrveṇa vāruṇyāmadhipasya ca|

udakpāśupatāstrasya sādhyamantraṃ prapūjayet|| 32||

gandhapuṣpapavitrārghaiḥ manaḥ prītikaraiḥ sadā|

praṇamya devadeveśaṃ japakarma samācaret|| 33||

kalpoktaṃ tu japaṃ kṛtvā devāya vinivedayet|

sādhakaḥ sādhyasiddhyarthaḥ virāmāyetare janāḥ|| 34||

sarvamantrādimantreṇa gandhāmbu vidhipūrvakam|

japaṃ samarpayenmantrī sampuṭe nihitaṃ śive|| 35||

guhyātiguhyagoptā tvaṃ gṛhāṇāsmatkṛtaṃ japam|

siddhirbhavatu me yena tvatprasādādyathepsitā|| 36||

yatkiñcitkurmeha deva sadā sukṛtaduṣkṛtam|

tanme śivapadasthasya haṃ kṣva kṣapaya śaṅkara|| 37||

śivo dātā śivo bhoktā śivaḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat|

śivo yajati sarvatra yaḥ śivaḥ so .ahameva tu|| 38||

nivedyaivaṃ sadātmānaṃ japaṃ pūjāṃ ca bhaktitaḥ|

praṇamya śirasā devaṃ kṣamasveti visarjayet|| 39||

sadyojātādimantreṇa virāmapraṇavena tu|

dhyāyaṃstu praṇavenaiva manastanmayatāṃ nayet|| 40||

evaṃ sampūjayeddevaṃ niyatātmā sakṛcchivam|

sa yāti paramaṃ sthānamaprāpyamakṛtātmabhiḥ|| 41||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe śivapūjāpaṭalo .aṣṭādaśaḥ||

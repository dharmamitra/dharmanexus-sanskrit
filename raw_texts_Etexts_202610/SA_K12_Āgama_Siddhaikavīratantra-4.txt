caturtha paṭalaḥ
oṃ lavaṇāmbhosi tīkṣṇosi udagrosi bhayṃkara.
amukhasya daha gātrāṇi daha māṃsāni daha tvacam nakhāny api daha asthīni asthibhyo majjakaṃ daha.
lavaṇaṃ cchandati lavaṇaṃ bhindati lavaṇaṃ pacati kṣoṇitalavaṇe hriyamāṇe kuto nidrā kuto ratiḥ.
yadi vasati yojanaśate nadīnāṃ ca śatāntare.
nagare lohaprākāre kṛṣṇasarpakṛtākule.
tatraiva vaśam ānīhi lavaṇabandhapuraskṛta.
oṃ ciṭi ciṭi vikloli amukaṃ sadhanaparivāram eva samānaya svāhā.
pūrvasevāvidhānād ayutam ekaṃ [japtvā] paścāt sādhanam ārabhate.
dakṣiṇottarapārśve tārābhṛkuṭīdevīdvayasahitam āryāvalokiteśvarabhaṭṭārakavarṇaraktamālāvastrālaṅkāranulepanaṃ pāśāṅkuśadhanurbāṇakaraṃ caturbhujaṃ śṛṅgāraikarasaṃ kusumānvitāśokataror adhastāt avasthitam ātmānaṃ vicintya trisaṃdhyaṃ lavaṇākṛtīnām aṣṭottaraśataṃ juhuyāt. saptāhāt puruṣaṃ striyaṃ vā vaśīkaroti.
trisaptāhāt mahāpuruṣaṃ vaśam ānayati.
lavaṇasahitena madanena sādhyapratikṛtiputtalikāṃ caturaṅgulapramāṇāṃ kṛtvā khaḍirāṅgāre nirdhūme mantram uccārayan trisaṃdhyaṃ tāpayet yasya māmnā sa vaśībhavati.
vajrodakabhāvitaṃ lavaṇaṃ parijapyāṣṭaśatavāraṃ dātavyaṃ bhakṣaṇamātreṇa vaśībhavati. 53
oṃ lavaṇāmbhosi tikṣṇosi udagrosi hṛdayaṃgama amukasya hṛdayaṃ pītaṃ nāsti loke cikitsakaḥ oṃ ciṭi ciṭi vikloli vikloli mahāvikloli mahāvikloli amukaṃ me vaśam ānaya svāhā.
pūrvavat pūrvasevāvidhānaṃ kṛtvā lavaṇodakaculukatrayaṃ trisaṃdhyaṃ yasya nāmnā pibati sa vaśībhavati. 
lavaṇarājikāṃ samabhāgaṃ kṛtvā yasya nāmnā juhoti sa vaśībhavati.
oṃ kurukulle svāhā.

āryatārābhaṭṭārikāyā hṛdayaṃ apratihataśaktiḥ tribhuvanavaśakarmaṇi prathitā.
pūrvoktasamādhisthaḥ akṣaralakṣaṃ japet.
paścāt puṣpadhūpavilepanacūrṇatāmbūlādi yasya dīyate sa vaśībhavati.
sugandhidhūpenātmānaṃ dhūpayet.
sarvajanapriyo bhvati.
raktakusumāni homayet yasya nāmnā sa vaśībhavati.
khāne pāne vābhimantrya samādīyate sa vaśībhavati.
mahāpuruṣavaśīkaraṇe śvetasarṣapā hotavyāḥ.
paścāt sutithinakṣatrādinā bhagavatyāḥ pūjāṃ kṛtvā kuṅkumagorocanakarpūrādyaiḥ bhūrje vastre vā ṣoḍaśāram utpalapatrākāraṃ cakram abhilikhya cakramadhye mantrasahitaṃ sādhyanāma likhanīyam.
patreṣu kurukulleti akṣaracatuṣṭayayantritaṃ sādhyasya nāma lekhanīyam.
bahiḥ oṃkāreṇa triguṇīkṛtacakraṃ raktasūtreṇa saṃveṣṭya madanamayyāḥ puttalikāyā hṛdayamadhye vinikṣipya khadirāṅgāreṇa tāpayan trisaṃdhyaṃ pādākṛṣṭiṃ kurute.
puruṣasya striyo vā vaśīkaraṇe tāmrasūcyā pādau viddhvā tāpayet vaśībhavati.
yaṃkāreṇa vāyumaṇḍalaṃ pariniṣpannaṃ vicintya tasyoparisthaṃ sādhyaṃ muktaveśaṃ vivastraṃ kaṇṭhe pāśena baddhaṃ hṛdaye aṅkuśenākramya vāyuvegena vidyāmantreṇākṛṣya sādhakasya pādamūle nikṣiptaṃ vicintya sarvaṃ kartavyam.
abhyāsāt piṇḍākṛṣṭim api karoti dhyānamātreṇa.
ghṛtamadhuśarkarāmadhye mantraṃ nikṣipya trisaṃdhyaṃ kusumādibhir abhyarcya mantraṃ japan yam icchati taṃ vaśīkaroti.
śvetapadmatantubhir vartiṃ kṛtvā kapilāgoghṛtenāpakvaśarāve kajjalaṃ pātayitvā aṣṭaśatābhimantritena kajjalenāñjitena nayane sarvajanapriyo bhavati.
mahātailena mahākapāle śvetapadmatantuvartyā pitṛvane kajjalaṃ prasādhitaṃ kṛṣṇāṣṭamyāṃ caturdaśyāṃ vā rātrau kartavyam.
tena kajjalena nayane 'ñjitena sarvajanapriyo bhavati. 55
amale vimale kuṅkume samayena baddhosi bindūn bindūn icchayā devo varṣati vidyotayati garjati garjati vismayamahārāja samāyita vardhayita huṃ.

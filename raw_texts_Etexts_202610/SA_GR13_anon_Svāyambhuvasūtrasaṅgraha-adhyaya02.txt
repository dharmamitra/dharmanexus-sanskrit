athānādimalaḥ puṃsāṃ paśutvaṃ parikīrtitam|

tuṣakambukavajjñeyaṃ māyāvāpāṅkurasya tat|| 1||

tatsadbhāvavaśo .ajñādipāśaughaḥ pauruṣaḥ sthitaḥ|

tasmāttattattvato jñeyaṃ mokṣamakṣayamicchatā|| 2||

patijñānaikagamyaṃ taditareṣāmagocaram|

tena tajjñānibhirmukto .aśuddhātmā socyate budhaiḥ|| 3||

yadyaśuddhirna puṃso .asti saktirbhogeṣu kiṃkṛtā|

śuddhe puṃsi na tadbhogo jāghaṭīti vipaścitaḥ|| 4||

atha cedyuktimānbhogaḥ sā yuktirnopapadyate|

muktasaṃsāravartmasthaviśeṣāpratipattitaḥ|| 5||

tasmādabhyupagantavyo viśeṣaḥ puṃmalātmakaḥ|

yasminnasati muktātmā baddhātmā ca satīṣyate|| 6||

tadbhāvabhāvino bhāvāḥ sarve māyātmakāḥ paśoḥ|

dvividhaṃ sādhayantīha puruṣārthaṃ parāparam|| 7||

māyātattvaṃ jagadbījamavināśyaśivātmakam|

vibhvekamakalaṃ sūkṣmamanādyavyayamīśvaram|| 8||

tasmātkālakale rāgavidyāvyaktaṃ guṇāndhiyam|

buddhitattvādahaṃkāraṃ tanmātrāṇīndriyāṇi ca|| 9||

tanmātrebhyaśca bhūtāni sarvaṃ ca kramaśo ṣṛjat|

vidyāvidyeśvareśānaḥ śivaśaktisamarthitaḥ|| 10||

bhāvān pratyayasargāṃśca bhūtāni bhuvanāni ca|

so .asṛjadbhagavānīśaḥ sarvaṃ māyātmakaṃ jagat|| 11||

karmataḥ pariṇāmo .asya jagatastriguṇātmakaḥ|

śāntaghoravimūḍhātmā nitāntaviṣamastataḥ|| 12||

karmataśca śarīrāṇi vividhāni śarīriṇām|

karmataḥ sarvamevedaṃ sukhaduḥkhātmakaṃ phalam|| 13||

tena karma prakurvanti labdhadehāṇavo .asakṛt|

paśutvādanvitātmāno bhogaviplutacetasaḥ|| 14||

asadbhāve tayoḥ puṃsāṃ bandhamuktī na jātucit|

bhavetāṃ bhavataste vai pramāṇātpratipattitaḥ|| 15||

karmāśuddhiśivatvāni teṣu satsu bhavantyamī|

trayo .arthāḥ sargajagato bhogabhoktṛtvamuktayaḥ|| 16||

karma taddvividhaṃ bhogyaṃ dharmādharmātmakaṃ paśoḥ|

 taddvividhaṃ
         ; tadvividhaṃ

ābhogasthāyi tadvidyātphaladvayavirodhi ca|| 17||

dhatte lokānimānsarvān prītiṃ ca puruṣe parām|

dharmastenocyate tajjñairadharmastadviparyayāt|| 18||

dharmasādhanasaṃvittirāmnāyādeva jāyate|

tanmūlatvātsmṛteścaiva tābhyameva parasya ca|| 19||

unnatyavanatī yadvattulākoṭyorvyavasthite|

karmaṇorvilayotpattī tadvaduttarapūrvayoḥ|| 20||

asaṃsthā muktiparyantā dvikarmapratirodhataḥ|

procyate ṣāvaṇorbandhaḥ sukaivalyanirodhikāḥ|| 21||

anādiḥ pauruṣī kārā muktyantāvikṛtā nijā|

māyeyā kārmikī naiva muktyantāpi vidharmataḥ|| 22||

māyeyamāṇavaṃ kārmametāvadaṇubandhanam|

etaccoktaṃ mayā sarvaṃ saṃkṣepātkanakāṇḍaja|| 23||

tasmātpravitatādbandhātparāsaṃsthānirodhakāt|

dīkṣaiva mocayatyūrdhvaṃ śaivaṃ dhāma nayatyapi|| 24||

śivadhāmārpitasyāsya bhogabhokturna jātucit|

bhoktṛtvamadhikāritvaṃ patikṛtyānukāritā|| 25||

bhavodbhavapadātīto niṣkampārciriva sthitaḥ|

mukto vyaktaśivatvo .asau kṛtakṛtyo yatastataḥ|| 26||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe pāśavicārapaṭalo dvitīyaḥ||

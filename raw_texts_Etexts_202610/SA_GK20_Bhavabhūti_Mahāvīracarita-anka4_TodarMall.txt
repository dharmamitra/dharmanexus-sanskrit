oṃ
(nepathye/) bho bho vaimānikāḥ/ pravartyantāṃ maṅgalāni/

kṛśāśvāntevāsī jayati bhagavān kauśikamuniḥ sahasrāṃśorvaṃśe jagati jayati kṣatramadhunā/

vinetā kṣatrārerjagadabhayadānavratadharaḥ śaraṇyo lokānāṃ dinakarakulendurvijayate//1//

(tataḥ praviśataḥ sambhrāntau vimānena śūrpaṇakhāmālyavantau//)

mālyavān/ vatse dṛṣṭastvayā divaukasāmekāyanībhāvo yadindrādayaḥ svato vanditvamupagatāḥ//

śūrpaṇakhā/ ṇa hi tumhehiṃ ṇirūvidaṃ visaṃvadaï/ sampadaṃ ca ukkampida mhi/ tā kiṃ ettha karaṇijjam//

mālyavān/ yāsau rājñā daśarathena prākpratiśrutavaradvayā rājñī bharatamātā kaikeyī tasyā mantharā nāma paricārikā daśarathasya vārtāhāriṇī mithilāmayodhyāyāḥ sampreṣitā mithilopakaṇṭhe vartata iti sampratyeva me niveditaṃ cārakaiḥ/ tasyāstvayā śarīramāviśya evamevaṃ ca kartavyam// 
(iti karṇe kathayati//)

śūrpaṇakhā/ kiṃ maṇṇadha karissadi evvaṃ ṇāma tti//

mālyavān/ bhidyeta no sadvṛttamikṣvākugṛheṣu viśeṣatastādṛśasya vijigīṣoḥ//

śūrpaṇakhā/ tado kim//

mālyavān/ tato'nena yogācāranyāyena sudūramākṛṣya rakṣasāmaṅkamupanītasya vindhyakāntāreṣvadeśajñasya vicarataḥ sukarāṇyavaskandanāni syuḥ/ virādhadanukabandhaprabhṛtayo hi daṇḍakāraṇyasatreṣu tīkṣṇāścariṣyanti/ śakyā hi luptaprabhuśakterutsāhaśaktiśchadmanābhisandhātum/ anivartanīyaśca rāvaṇasya sītāsvīkāragrahaḥ/ sa caivamīṣatkaraḥ saprayojanaśceti//

śūrpaṇakhā/ aha lakkhaṇasahāattaṇe vi kiṃ paāaṇam//

mālyavān/

vīro'strapāragaścintyo yathā rāmastathaiva saḥ/

chadmadaṇḍaprayogastu yathaikasmiṃstathā dvayoḥ//2//

śūrpaṇakhā/ mama  duaṃ evva edaṃ ṇa juttaṃ paḍikhabhādi/ jaṃ ca dūraṭṭhidassa dāsarahiṇo saṇṇidhāṇakaraṇaṃ jaṃ ca aṇāvaddhaverassa appaḍisamādheaṃ itthiāveraṃ ti//

mālyavān/ sa tāvad bhūmyānantaryataḥ pratyāsanna eva/ sānucarasundopasundaputropaplavāt tāṭakāriḥ kathamanābaddhavairaḥ/ apratividheyaṃ ca rāmarāvaṇayoritarathāpi vairam/ paśya/

pālyaṃ tasya jagadvayaṃ tu jagato nityaṃ haṭhādeśinaḥ sāmaivaṃ sati kīdṛgapriyakṛtā śaśvadviruddhātmanā/

kānarthān raghunandano mṛgayate devaiḥ patiryāe vṛta- stasmāddhānamapīha nāsti na bhidā tasyeṣavaḥ sādhanam//3//

na daṇḍo'pyadhike śatrau na prakāśaḥ prakāśate/

tūṣṇīndaṇḍastu kartavyastasya cāyamupakramaḥ//4//

tathā sati sītāpahārataḥ kimaparaṃ kuryāt/ kiñca/

hṛtajānirarātibhiḥ salajjo yadi mṛtyoḥ śaraṇaṃ gato'nyathāpi/

mṛdito mṛta eva niṣpratāpaḥ paritapyāpyathavā ghaṭeta sandhau//5//

uttiṣṭheta vadhāya naḥ paribhavaprauḍhena cenmanyunā neṣṭe tatprasaraṃ niroddhumudadhistigmāṃśuvīryāe hi saḥ/

kintu prākpratipannarāvaṇasuhṛdbhāvena bhīmaujasā śatrurvajradharātmajena hariṇā ghoreṇa ghāniṣyate//6//

anena ca prasaṅgena bahvanusandhātavyam//

śūrpaṇakhā/ kiṃ via//

mālyavān/ rāvaṇapriyāsi vatse kāryajñā ca ato niḥśaṅkamāvedyate hṛdayakhedaḥ/

kṣiterānantaryādapakṛdapakṛtyaśca satataṃ dvidhā rāmaḥ śatruḥ prakṛtiniyataḥ kṣatriya iti/

tṛtīyo me naptā rajanicaranāthasya sahajo ripuḥ pratyāsatterahiriva bhayaṃ no janayati//7//

kumbhakarṇastu sannapyasatsamaḥ/ kṛtrimasvāpavyasanādavinayācca/ vibhīṣaṇastvābhigāmikātmaguṇasampanna iti anuraktāḥ prakṛtayaḥ/

kharadūṣaṇatriśirasastu kulasadṛśavṛttayo rājānamupatiṣṭhante yataste vatseneva dhenuṃ rājānamarthān duhanti/ upajāpitāśca pratidruhyanti prakṛtayaḥ/ tadidamantarbhedajarjaraṃ rājakulamabhiyuktamātraṃ rāmeṇa bhedyate/ yathoktaṃ laghvapi vyasanapadamabhiyuktasya kṛcchrasādhyaṃ bhavatīti/ tatra ca vibhīṣaṇāvagrahataḥ pratividhānam/ na tu prakāśadaṇḍastūṣṇīndaṇḍaḥ saṃrodhanamapasāraṇaṃ vā syāt/ tatra prakāśadaṇḍamabhinnasambandhāḥ kathaṃ rākṣasāstitikṣeran/ tūṣṇīndaṇḍo'pi prājñairanumīyamānaḥ prakṛtikopako rāme'bhiyoktari durantaḥ syāt/

saṃrodhane tvabhibhavādvihite tadeka- maitryā kharaprabhṛtayaśca tadā vikuryuḥ/

nirvāsyamānamapi taṃ parivārayeyu- stasmāt kharaprabhṛtayaḥ purato vicintyāḥ//8//

tatra rāmasyopayoga iti//

śūrpaṇakhā/ aho aṃṇujīvittaṇassa agaruadā jaṃ rāvaṇassa kharappamuhāṇaṃ ca tulle vi kulasambandhe evvaṃ mādāmaho cintedi//

mālyavān/ īdṛśaḥ kulaputrakācāraḥ//

śūrpaṇakhā/ viṇā kharappamuhehiṃ vibhīsaṇassa kā paḍivattī//

mālyavān/ prājñaḥ khalvasāvutprekṣitaviprakāraḥ svayamevāpasarpeta/ upekṣaṇīyastvasmābhirna caitanmantavyamaurasaṃ bhayamiti/ yataḥ/

bālyātprabhṛtyeva virūḍhasakhyaḥ sugrīvameṣa dhruvamāśrayeta/

vāliprasādīkṛtabhūmibhāge kumārabhuktau sthitamṛṣyamūke//9//

tataśca vālinā yatiṣyate/ na rāmopaśrayeṇa rāmopaśleṣaṇena vā nopekṣeta vālī//

śūrpaṇakhā/ adha rāmo parasurāmavijaī jaṇidavirodhaṃ vāliṇaṃ vāvādedi tado vibhīsaṇarāmasañjoā aṇattho tti sambhāvemi//

mālyavān/ nanu vatse/

yo vālinaṃ hanti hatā vayaṃ sma- stena dhruvaṃ tatra tu sarvanāśe/

ekaḥ sa jīvyāt kulatanturasmai rāmaḥ śriyaṃ dharmamayo dadāti//10//

śūrpaṇakhā/ 
(sāsram/) evvaṃ pi dāva hodu//

mālyavān/ gamyatāmidānīṃ vatse yatra preṣitāsi/ sukaraṃ caitat prayojanaṃ yadi janakadaśarathāntike vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrau na syātām/ ahamapi laṅkāmeva gacchāmi//

śūrpaṇakhā/ hā amba tue vi dukkhaṃ pekkhidavvam//

mālyavān/

hā vatsāḥ kharadūṣaṇaprabhṛtayo vadhyāḥ stha pāpasya me hā hā vatsa vibhīṣaṇa tvamapi me kāryeṇa heyaḥ sthitaḥ/

hā madvatsala vatsa rāvaṇa mahatpaśyāmi te saṅkaṭaṃ vatse kaikasi hā hatāsi na cirāttrīnputrakān paśyasi//11//

(iti niṣkrāntau//)

//viṣkambhakaḥ//

(tataḥ praviśato vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrābhyāṃ saha janakadaśarathau// rājānāvanyonyaṃ pariṣvajya/)

janakaḥ/ rājan diṣṭyā vardhase yadīdṛśaste vatso rāmabhadraḥ/

aprākṛtāni ca guṇaiśca nirantarāṇi lokottarāṇi ca phalaiśca mahodayāni/

vīrasya yasya mahataścaritādbhutāni nāsmākameva jagatāmapi maṅgalāni//12//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ 
(viśvāmitraṃ pariṣvajya/) sakhe kuśikanandana/

asmābhirapyanāśāsyo rāmasya mahimānvayaḥ/

yat kṛtāstena kṛtino vayaṃ ca bhuvanāni ca//13//

viśvāmitraḥ/ prakṛṣṭapuṇyaparipākopādāna eṣa mahimā/ ke vayametāvataḥ prakarṣasya//

daśarathaḥ/ bhagavan kuśikanandana mā maivam/

ādityāḥ/ kuladevatāmiva nṛpāḥ pūrve dilīpādaya- stejorāśimarundhatīpatimṛṣiṃ bhaktyā yadāśiśrayan/

pākaḥ ślāghyataraśca bhūritapasāṃ satyāśiṣāmāśiṣa- stāsāmapyayameva maṅgalanidhiryannaḥ prasanno bhavān//14//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ satyamīdṛśo viśvāmitraḥ/

yadvācāṃ viṣayamatītya cetasāṃ vā paryāyātparamatiśāyanasya vā yat/

brahmarṣāai tadiha durāsade samiddhaṃ tejobhirjvalati mahattvamaprameyam//15//

viśvāmitraḥ/ bhagavan maitrāvaruṇa/

sanatkumārāṅgirasau gurū vidyātapomayau/

stauṣi cetsatya evāsmi satyasandhā hi te giraḥ//16//

rāmabhadre tu nāścaryametat/ mahārājadaśaratho hi tasya prasavitā/

sākṣātpuṇyasamucchrayā iva manorvaaivasvatasyānvaye rājānastvadapekṣitena vidhinā gopāyitāraḥ prajāḥ/

ye bhūtāḥ prathame pavitracaritāsteṣāmayaṃ dhūrdharo vīraḥ kṣatriyapuṅgavo guṇanidhiḥ ślāghyo dharitryāḥ patiḥ//17//

api ca/

ariṣṭastvāṣṭrasya praśamanavidhau jambhadamanaḥ sa viśveṣāmīśaḥ patirapi nikāyasya marutām/

vijetāraṃ senāḥ satatamapahantāramasurān amuṃ vīraṃ vavre bahuṣu samanīkeṣu maghavā//18//

so'yamīdṛśaḥ kathamanīdṛśaṃ prasūte//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ kathamatrāścaryaṃ nāma/

marutvantaṃ devaṃ ya iha bhagavantaṃ vijayate vijigye taṃ rājā yudhi daśamukhaṃ haihayapatiḥ/

nihantāraṃ tasya prathitamahimānaṃ tribhuvane mahāvīraṃ jitvā kimiva hi na vatsena vijitam//19//

daśarathaḥ/ tat kimityayaṃ dvidhā bhidyate lokaḥ//

viśvāmitraḥ/ eṣa vatso rāmabhadraḥ sajāmadagnya ita evābhivartate/ ya eṣaḥ vīraśriyā ca vinayena ca rocamāno mānye munāvavanataśca guṇonnataśca/

lajjāṃ vahan bhṛgupatau hṛtavīryadarpe śiṣyo gurāviva kṛtaprathamāpacāraḥ//20//

(tataḥ praviśato rāmajāmadagnyau//)

rāmaḥ/

yad brahmavādibhirupāsitavandyapāde vidyātapovratanidhau tapatāṃ variṣṭhe/

daivāt kṛtastvayi mayā vinayāpacāras tat tvaṃ prasīda bhagavannayamañjaliste//21//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ aparāddhameva tvayā jāmadagnyasya nopakṛtam/

puṇyā brāhmaṇajātiranvayaguṇaḥ śāstraṃ caritraṃ ca me yenaikena hṛtānyamūni haratā caitanyamātrāmapi/

ekaḥ sannapi bhūridoṣagahanaḥ so'yaṃ tvayā preyasā vatsa brāhmaṇavatsalena śamitaḥ kṣemāya darpāmayaḥ//22//

rāmaḥ/ kathaṃ nāparāddhaṃ mayā yadāyayudhaparigrahaṃ yāvadārūḍho duryāegaḥ//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ eṣa eva vo nyāyaḥ/

asādhyamanyathā doṣaṃ paricchidya śarīriṇām/

yathā vaidyastathā rājā śastrapāṇirbhiṣajyati//23//

rāmaḥ/ ko'hamuktipratyuktikāyāṃ bhagavataḥ/ tasmādita ito bhagavān/

jāmadagnyaḥ/ kva punarmayā vatsa gantavyam/

rāmaḥ/ yatra tātaśca tātajanakaśca/ athavā śāntam/ uta yatra bhagavantau maitrāvaruṇakauśikau//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ idamidānīmaśakyam/ anatikramaṇīyaśca rājanirdeśaḥ/ 
(parikramya/) he bhagavantau/

sa eṣa rāmaḥ saumyatvādacaṇḍaścaṇḍavikramaḥ/

yasya pratiṣṭhitaṃ jaitraṃ jāmadagnye'pi śāsanam//24//

rājānau/ atigambhīraḥ saujanyodgāraḥ//

rāmaḥ/ eṣa vo rāmaśirasā praṇāmaparyāyaḥ//

sarve/ ehyehi vatsa// 
(iti pariṣvajante//)

jāmadagnyaḥ/ bhagavan maitrāvaruṇa/ eṣa jamadagniputraḥ praṇamya kauśikena sārdhamatra bhagavato vijñāpayati/

vṛddhātikramasambhṛtasya mahato nirṇiktaye pāpmanaḥ prāyaścetanamādiśantu guravo rāmeṇa dāntasya me/

prāgdharmasya bhavanta eva paramaṃ draṣṭāra āsan guro- rlabdhvā jñānamanekadhā pravacanairmanvādayaḥ prāṇayan//25//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ adya naḥ śrotriyāṇāṃ gṛhe jāto'si/

durvinīte tvayi vayaṃ duḥkhitāḥ sukhino'nyathā/

nisargo hyeṣa vṛddhānāṃ yattu śreyastathaiva tat//26//

tat paripūta evāsi//

viśvāmitraḥ/ apahataṃ te vidmaḥ pāpmānaṃ rāmabhadreṇa/ yataḥ prāyaścittamiva rājadaṇḍamapyenaso niṣkrayamāmananti dharmācāryāḥ/ kiṃ punaryadatra bhagavān vasiṣṭhaḥ prajāpālasannidhau praśāsti//

rāmaḥ/ etāni tāni bhavatāṃ sākṣātkṛtadharmāṇāmṛṣīṇāṃ prasannagambhīrapāvanāni vacanāni//

daśarathaḥ/ bhagavan jāmadagnya/

nisargataḥ pavitrasya kimanyatpāvanaṃ tava/

tīrthodakaṃ ca vahniśca nānyataḥ śuddhimarhataḥ//27//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ 
(ātmagatam/) bhagavati vasundhare prasīda randhradānena//

janakaḥ/ bhagavan yadi prasanno'si tadvisrabdhopaveśanāt paripunīhi gṛhān/ etat pūtamāsanaṃ bhagavataḥ//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ yadabhirucitaṃ sūryaśiṣyāntevāsine rājanyaśrotriyāya//

sarva upaviśanti//

daśarathaḥ/ janapadabahirniṣṭhā yūyaṃ gṛhasya parigrahād vayamapi nijairvyagrāḥ kāryaaistato na babhūva yaḥ/

sa iha bhavatāmadyāsmābhirmanorathavāñchitaḥ sucaritaparīpākātprāptaścirasya samāgamaḥ//28//

atra ca/

kā te stutiḥ stutipathādativṛttadhāmnaḥ kiṃ dīyatāmavikalakṣitidāyine ca/

śāntasya kiṃ parijanena mune tathāpi putraiḥ samaṃ daśaratho'dya vaśaṃvadaste//29//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ yūyamīdṛśā iti kimatrāścaryam/

preddhaṃ dhāma yadāmananti sudhiyaḥ so'yaṃ nidhirjyotiṣāṃ devo vaḥ savitā kulasya kimato bhūtyai praśaṃsāpadam/

yajvānaḥ paramārtharājaḷṣayaste yūyamikṣvākavo yeṣāṃ veda ivāprameyamahimā dharme vasiṣṭho guruḥ//30//

api ca/

saṅgrāmeṣṭasamāptidaṃ diviṣadāṃ bharturdhanuḥ sādhanaṃ saptadvīpaniviṣṭayūpanicayaśreṇyaṅkitā bhūgṛhāḥ/

śaśvatkīrtinibandhanaṃ bhagavatī bhāgīrathī sāgaraḥ prakhyātāni ca tāni tāni bhavatāṃ bhūmānamācakṣate//31//

vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrau/ 
(apavārya/) etadapi śikṣitaṃ vatsena//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ rāmabhadra māmanumodasvāraṇyagamanāya//

viśvāmitraḥ/ māmapyatha bhavanto'nujānantu/

raghujanakagṛheṣu garbharūpa- vyatikaramaṅgalavṛddhayo'nubhūtāḥ/

bhṛgupatidamana-/ 
(ityardhokte viramya/)

bhṛgupatividitonnatiṃ ca vatsaṃ priyamabhinandya sukhī gṛhānupeyām//32//

daśarathaḥ/ vatsa rāmabhadra prasthitaste bhagavān kauśikaḥ//

viśvāmitraḥ/ sāsraṃ rāmamāliṅgya/ ahameva saumya na tvāṃ moktumutsahe/

kintvanuṣṭhānanityatvaṃ  svātantryamapakarṣati/

saṅkaṭā hyāhitāgnīnāṃ pratyavāyairgṛhasthatā//33//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ gṛhāt  svagṛhaṃ gantumāgantuṃ ca kāmacāraḥ//

viśvāmitraḥ/ bhagavan yadyanurudhyase tadehi siddhāśramapadamubhau gacchāvaḥ/ tvāṃ puraskṛtya gacchan madhucchandaso mātuḥ satkāryo bhaviṣyāmi//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ kimetāvatyapi bhavānasmāsu na prabhavati//

rājānau/ ramaṇīyapāvano brahmarṣisaṅgamaḥ/

anyonyamāhātmyavidoranyairaviditātmanoḥ/

virājate virodho'pi nāma snehe tu kā kathā//34//

(nepathye/) eṣā rāmavadhūrgurūn vandate//

ḷṣayaḥ/ vatse jānaki/

vīreṇa te vinayamāṅgalikena patyā vṛtradruhaḥ praśamiteṣu mahābhayeṣu/

kṣatraprakāṇḍagṛhiṇībahumānapūjām ūrjasvalāmapi śacī manasā karotu//35//

rāmaḥ/ 
(svagatam/) acirāt samūlakāṣaṃ kaṣiteṣu rākṣaseṣvevaṃ syāt//

ḷṣayaḥ/ svastyevamevāsatāṃ bhavantaḥ/ 
(uttiṣṭhanti//)

itare/ 
(utthāya/) namo namo vaḥ//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ bhavantau jāmadagnyo'bhivādayate//

vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrau/

sthiraste praśamo bhūyāt pratyagjyotiḥ prakāśatām/

acchinnaśivasaṅkalpamantaḥkaraṇamastu te//36//

iti niṣkrāntau//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ kiñcit parikramya sthitvā ca/ vatsa rāmabhadra itastāvat//

rāmaḥ/ 
(upaśritya/) ājñāpaya//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ yanmayā kṣatriyocchedaviśrāntenāvadhāritam/

tadetadadhunā dhatte dhanuḥ kāraṇaśūnyatām//37//

idhmādiprayojanamastu paraśuḥ/

puṇyānāmṛṣayastaṭeṣu saritāṃ ye daṇḍakāyāṃ vane bhūyāṃso nivasanti teṣu satataṃ laṅkāsado rākṣasāḥ/

vidhvaṃsāya caranti tatpramathaneṣvasyopayogo bhavet sampratyeṣa sahāmunaiva dhanuṣā vatse'dhikāraḥ sthitaḥ//38//

(arpayati//)

rāmaḥ/ 
(praṇamya/) pratigṛhīteyamājñā//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ 
(sāsraṃ parikramya/) āyuṣman pratinivartasva iti niṣkrāntaḥ//

rāmaḥ/ 
(sabāṣpam/) gato bhagavān bhārgavaḥ/ 
(vicintya/) tadapi nāmānyena kenacidupāyena daṇḍakāraṇyānīṃ pratiṣṭheyam/ kathaṃ rāmapriyād gurujanādevaṃ syāt/

nyastaśastre bhṛgupatau paratantre tathā mayi/

kaṣṭamutsāritāḥ krūrairyātudhānaistapodhanāḥ//39//

(nepathye/) ārya ārya/

madhyamāyāḥ priyasakhī māturvo manthareti yā/

sā prāpteyamayodhyāyāstava rāma didṛkṣayā//40//

rāmaḥ/ sādhu yadasyāṃ śiśujanapravāsavaimanasyaṃ vicchidyeta/ tad vatsa lakṣmaṇa upasarpaya//

(tataḥ praviśati lakṣmaṇaḥ mantharāveṣā śūrpaṇakhā ca//)

śūrpaṇakhā/ āvesiamantharāsarīrā suppaṇahā aham/ vasiṭṭhavissāmittagamaṇeṇa susamāhidam/ aho eso parasurāmavijaī khattiakumāraā rāmo/ 
(nirvarṇya/) aho samaggasohaggalacchīpariggaheṇa loaṇarasāaṇaṃ sommaṃ se sarīraṇimmāṇam/ bho dāṇiṃ ciraālavehavvadukkhapamhusidasaṃsārasokkhassa vi jaṇassa cārittadhīrasupūridaṃ puṇo vi hiaaṃ paripphurāvedi//

rāmaḥ/ 
(praṇipatya/) api manthare kuśalamambāyāḥ//

śūrpaṇakhāḥ/ suhaṃ kusalaṃ ca/ vaccha sadā esā paṇhudatthaṇī majjhmā de mādā parissajia āṇavedi/ puttaa purā paḍiṇṇāde duve vare mahārāaṃ jāṇāvemi/ tassa me viṇṇattihāraā hohi/ esa tādassa kajjaleho//

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ 
(gṛhītvā vācayati/)

astvekena vareṇa vatsabharato bhoktādhirājyaśriyaḥ 
(svagatam/)

kathametadārye tiṣṭhati kaniṣṭhasyāryabharatasya rājyaprārthanam//

(prakāśam/) yātvanyena vihāya kālaharaṇaṃ rāmo vanaṃ daṇḍakām/

(svagatam/) hā amba kiṃ kṛtaṃ tvayā āryagamanavaraṇena//

(prakāśam/) tasyāṃ cīradharaścaturdaśa samāstiṣṭhatvasau taṃ punaḥ sītālakṣmaṇamātrakātparijanādanyo na cānuvrajet//41//

(svagatam/) hā pāparaṇḍe mātṛbandhu/

sadā bharataśatrughnalakṣmaṇāryamukhaiḥ kṛtām/

ambeti vyāhṛtiṃ hitvā kiṃ kṛtaṃ pāpayā tvayā//42//

rāmaḥ/ aho prasādaprakarṣaḥ/

tatraiva gamanādeśo yatra paryutsukaṃ manaḥ/

na caiṣa viraho jātaḥ sa ca vatso'nujo nṛpaḥ//43//

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ diṣṭyānumodito'hamāryeṇa//

rāmaḥ/ ārye manthare eṣa prasthito'smi//

śūrpaṇakhā/ ṇamo dāṇiṃ bhaavado saṃsārassa jattha īrisā vi kappadumā parohanti//

(iti niṣkrāntā//)

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ aye mātulo yudhājidāryabharatasahacarastātamupasarpati//

rāmaḥ/ diṣṭyā kaṣṭaṃ ca/

apariṣvajya bharataṃ nāsti me gacchato dhṛtiḥ/

asmatpravāsaduḥkārtaṃ na tvenaṃ draṣṭumutsahe//44//

(praviśya yudhājidbharatau/ daśarathamupasṛtya/) deva śrūyatāṃ yadekāyanībhūya prakṛtayastvāṃ vijñāpayanti/

trayyāstrātā yastavāyaṃ tanūja- stenādyaiva svāminaste prasādāt/

rājanvatyo rāmabhadreṇa rājñā labdhakṣemāḥ pūrṇakāmāścarāmaḥ//45//

daśarathaḥ/ sakhe janaka/

priyakalyāṇakāmābhiḥ prajābhiścoditā vayam/

kintu rāmapriyau neha maitrāvaruṇakauśikau//46//

janakaḥ/

parokṣe sukṛtaṃ karma tayoḥ prītiṃ kariṣyati/

mantrajño vāmadevastu bhagavānāsta eva hi//47//

daśarathaḥ/ yadyevaṃ tadayameva jāmadagnyavijayamahotsavaḥ prasajyatāmabhiṣekamahotsavena//

rāmaḥ/ idamidānīṃ katham//

daśarathaḥ/ sumantra sannidhāpyantāmābhiṣecanikāḥ sambhārāḥ/ yaśca yenārthī sa tena paryāptakāmaḥ kartavyaḥ//

rāmaḥ/ 
(upasṛtya praṇamya ca/) ahaṃ tāvadarthī//

daśarathaḥ/ vatsa kena//

rāmaḥ/

yo'sau varadvayanyāsastaṃ tvāṃ mātādya madhyamā/

yatheṣṭaṃ nāthate tāta tatprasādārthino vayam//48//

daśarathaḥ/

satyasandhā hi raghavaḥ kiṃ vatsa vicikitsase/

tvayi dūte'pi kastasyāḥ prāṇānapi dhanāyate//49//

rāmaḥ/ vatsa vācyatām//

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ 
(astvekena vareṇetyādikaṃ vācayati//)

itare/ kathamanyadeva kimapi/ hā hatāḥ smaḥ//

(rājā mūrchati//)

rāmalakṣmaṇau/ tāta samāśvasihi/

janakaḥ/

ikṣvākuvaṃśatilakasya nṛpasya patnī tasmin viśuddhimapi rājakule prasūtā/

atyāhitaṃ kimapi rākṣasakarma kuryā- dāryā satī kathamaho mahadadbhutaṃ naḥ//50//

rāmaḥ/ tāta/

satyasandhāḥ stha yadi vā rāmo vā yadi vaḥ priyaḥ/

tatprasīdatu mātā naḥ pūrṇakāmāstu madhyamā//41//

daśarathaḥ/ evamastu/ kā gatiḥ//

janakaḥ/ hā vatsa rāmabhadra hā vatsa lakṣmaṇa/

putrasaṅkrāntalakṣmīkairyadvṛddhekṣvākubhirdhṛtam/

tvayā tat kṣīrakaṇṭhena prāptamāraṇyakaṃ vratam//52//

vatse jānaki/ dhanyāsi yasyāste guruniyogādeva bharturanugamanam//

daśarathaḥ/ hā vatse jānaki/ kaṅkaṇadharaiva rakṣasāmupanītāsyupahāratām//

(ityubhau mūrchataḥ//)

rāmaḥ/ atyāpanno gurujanaḥ/ kathaṃ nāmaitat//

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ ārya īdṛśo'yamāpātakaruṇasnehasaṃvegaḥ/ kimatra kriyate/ pratiṣiddhaṃ ca naḥ kālaharaṇaṃ bharatajananyā/ tadalamatisnehakātaryeṇa//

rāmaḥ/ sādhvācāraniṣṭhura sādhu/ amanuṣyasadṛśaste cittasāraḥ/ tadvatsa vaidehīmānaya//

(lakṣmaṇo niṣkrāntaḥ//)

bharataḥ/ mātula yuktametadvo gṛhasya//

yudhājit/ vatsa udbhrāntaḥ saṃvṛtto'smi/

patirmṛtyorvaktre vrajati vanametatsutayugaṃ vadhūṭī rakṣobhyo valiriva varākī praṇihitā/

nirālambo lokaḥ kulamayaśasā tacca nihataṃ svasurme daurātmyaṃ jagadavikalaṃ viklavayati//53//

(tataḥ praviśati lakṣmaṇaḥ sītā ca//)

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ ārya iyamāryā//

rāmaḥ/ ita itaḥ//

sītā/ diṭṭhiā aṇumada mhi ajjeṇa//

rāmaḥ/ 
(sītālakṣmaṇābhyāṃ saha gurujanaṃ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya/) mātula/

eṣa tātaśca tātaśca priyāpatyāśca mātaraḥ/

āśvāsanīyāḥ śoke'smin bhavataiva gatā vayam//54//

(iti parikrāmanti//)

yudhājit/ 
(sāvegam/) kathaṃ vāraṇye tyajāmi// 
(utthāyānudhāvati//)

bharataḥ/ 
(anugacchan/) mātula brūhi kimidānīṃ karomi//

yudhājit/ rāmabhadra apekṣasva pādaparicārakamaraṇyānucaraṃ bharatam//

rāmaḥ/ nanvasyāpi varṇāśramarakṣaṇe guruniyogaḥ//

bharataḥ/ lakṣmaṇasya śatrughnasya vā tadastu//

rāmaḥ/ kimatra kasyacit svaruciḥ//

bharataḥ/ etāvatī mama svaruciḥ//

rāmaḥ/ āḥ/ śakyaṃ nāma mayi tiṣṭhati tvayānyena vā yuktamullaṅghayitum//

bharataḥ/ hā hā kathaṃ parityakto'smi mandabhāgyaḥ// 
(iti mūrchati//)

yudhājit/ vatsa samāśvasihi//

bharataḥ/ 
(āśvasya/) mātula dhārayasva mām//

yudhājit/ vatsa evaṃ tāvat// 
(bharatasya karṇe kathayitvā/) rāmabhadra evamayaṃ vijñāpayati/ yadetad bhagavatā śarabhaṅgena preṣitaṃ tapanīyopānadyugalaṃ tadāryaḥ prasādīkarotviti//

rāmaḥ/ 
(tadunmucya/) gṛhāṇedaṃ vatsa//

bharataḥ/ 
(śirasyāropya/) ārya//

rāmaḥ/ 
(pariṣvajya/) matpādaspṛṣṭikayā pratinivartasva/ samprati sambhāvaya cirapramūḍhau tātau//

bharataḥ/ ayamidānīmahaṃ nandigrāme jaṭāṃ bibhradabhiṣicyāryapāduke/

pālayiṣyāmi pṛthivīṃ yāvadāryo nivartate//55//

(sītārāmau pradakṣiṇīkaroti//)

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ ārya bharata lakṣmaṇaḥ praṇamati//

(bharataḥ pariṣvajya vāṣpastambhāṃ nāṭayati//)

rāmaḥ/ vatsa tātau sambhāvaya//

bharataḥ/ kaṣṭamadyāpi nocchvasitaḥ// 
(iti vījayati//)

janakaḥ/ 
(samucchvasya sarvato vilokya/) hā hā muṣito'smi//

daśarathaḥ/ 
(ucchvasya/) vatsa rāmabhadra na gantavyam/

prāṇāḥ patanti paritastamasā vṛto'smi marmacchido mama rujaḥ prasarantyapūrvāḥ/

akṣṇormukhendumupadhehi giraṃ ca dehi hā putra mayyakaruṇaḥ sahasaiva mā bhūḥ//56//

(sonmādamiva nirbharam/) bhoḥ kva praviśāmīdānīṃ mandabhāgyaḥ//

(iti viklavo bharatajanakābhyāṃ nīyamāno niṣkrāntaḥ//)

yudhājit/ vatsa rāmabhadra/

ekībhūya śanairanekasamayavyutpannamekakriyaṃ muktākrandamitastataḥ kimidamityudbhrāntanārīnaram/

etat tvatpuramanyathaiva sahasā sañjātamāvedyate yasmin kardamiteṣu vartmasu ghanairbāṣpāmbubhirdurdinam//57//

rāmaḥ/ mātula pratinivartasva/ ayaṃ ca vo haste bharataḥ//

yudhājit/ anurudhyasva māmanugacchantam//

rāmaḥ/ śāntam/ guravo yūyaṃ nānugantāraḥ/ ātmanā tṛtīyena gantavyamityambāyā ādeśaḥ//

yudhājit/ kimahameko'nugacchāmi/ api tu sabālavṛddhāḥ prakṛtayaḥ/ kiṃ na paśyasi/

skandhāropitayajñapātranicayāḥ svairvājapeyārjitai- śchatrairvārayituṃ tavārkakiraṇānete mahābrāhmaṇāḥ/

sāketāḥ saha sainikairanupatatpatnīgṛhītāgnayaḥ prākprasthāpitahomadhenava ime dhāvanti vṛddhā api//58//

rāmaḥ/ mātula mātula gurubhireva śiśavo dharmalopāt pālayitavyāḥ/ tat prasīda naḥ/ pratinivṛtya nivartyatāmayaṃ mahājanaḥ// 
(iti praṇamati//)

yudhājit/ vatsottiṣṭhottiṣṭha/ eṣa bodhayitvā prajāḥ kvāpi mandabhāgyo gacchāmi/

tvāṃ lakṣmaṇa mahābāho tvāṃ ca vaidehanandini/

āmantraye nivṛtto'smi pāpaḥ kalyāṇamastu vām//59//

(rudan pratinivṛtya/) aho nu khalu bhoḥ pratimanvantaraṃ bhūtairgīyamānā cariṣyati/

prātaḥpavitraṃ lokānāmiyaṃ cāritrapañjikā//60//

(iti niṣkrāntaḥ//)

lakṣmaṇaḥ/ kathitamāryasya śṛṅgaverapuravāstavyena niṣādapatinā guhena tatpradeśaparyantāvaskandino virādharākṣasasya durvilasitam//

rāmaḥ/ tena hi virādhahatakonmathanāya sannikṛṣṭaprayāgamanuṣaktamandākinīpavitramekhalaṃ citrakūṭaṃ nāmācalamupetya munibhirupajuṣṭatīrthāṃ rakṣāṃsi nihatya daṇḍakāṃ prāpya/

sannihitagṛdhrarājaṃ krameṇa yāmo janasthānam//61//

(iti niṣkrāntāḥ sarve//)

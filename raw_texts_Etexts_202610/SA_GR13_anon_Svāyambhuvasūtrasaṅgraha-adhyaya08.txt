atha sadyādimantrāṇaṃ śivāṅgānniḥsṛtākṣaraiḥ|

lakṣaṇaṃ sampravakṣyāmi yathāvattannibodhata|| 1||

padabrahma svanāmāni pañcānāṃ pañca vinyaset|

caturthyantāni trīṇyatra jñeyāni kanakāṇḍaja|| 2||

ādyante karmanirdeśo vibhaktyante tu nāmani|

aṣṭaṣaṣyaraṃ śaivaṃ taṅkārātparatāsthitam|| 3||

hārdantu pañcamantasmāttriṃśadā dīrghamūrcchitam|

ikāraṃ mūrcchitaṃ śaivaṃ tṛtīyaṃ tu tataḥ param|| 4||

pañcakṣaracaturthyantaṃ nāmāsyaiva tataḥ param|

ekonaviṃśakaṃ vāmamaikārāntamato natiḥ|| 5||

śivasarvātmanā yugmaṃ phalamekacaturthakam|

ekārasvarasambhinnaṃ vakāradvayakaṃ nyaset|| 6||

dvirabhyāsapadādekamaṣṭamācchaivamakṣaram|

amantraṇapadāntyārṇamimūrchitamataḥ param|| 7||

pūrvameva dvirabhyāse padamantyākṣaraṃ vinā|

yogādhipapadasyādi padāntyamaparaṃ tataḥ|| 8||

antyabindudvayakṣīṇaṃ nāpadādaṣṭakaṃ padam|

jākṣarātsaptamaṃ śaivamāmūrchitamato .akṣaram|| 9||

tato mantrasamāptyarthamuñca muñca padātpadam|

pañcamaṃ vinyaseddhīmān bījasya ṣoḍaśaṃ hṛdi|| 10||

nyasedvāmapadādūrdhvaṃ padaṃ sūkṣmadvitīyakam|

ekāreṇa tato bhinnaṃ rudrāṇyāḥ prathamākṣaram|| 11||

anantaṃ paiṅgaLaṃ tasmātsamāpternavamākṣaram|

taṭhakārikṛtaṃ yañca nyasetsūkṣmapadaṃ punaḥ|| 12||

rudranāmacaturthyantaṃ trimūrtinanimeva ca|

tābhyāṃ ca paiṅgaLaṃ dīrghaṃ kalakārākṣaradvayam|| 13||

yatatī pūrvavattābhyāṃ yathāsaṃsthaṃ tu paiṅgaLam|

gāyatryāmakṣaraṃ ṣaṣṭaṃ paiṅgaLaṃ pūrvamakṣaram|| 14||

dvitīyaṃ paratastasmādvidyāṅgasiraso .akṣaram|

apayujya ṣakārasthaṃ śaivārṇamayutaṃ nyaset|| 15||

yanatītu tatastasmādvāmadevapadānuge|

saṃsthāpya vinyasedvarṇaṃ bakāraṃ piṅgaLātmakam|| 16||

lakārādyaṣṭakaṃ bhūyatsthāpya tatra tṛtīyakam|

ikārasvarasambhinnaṃ kuryānmantrārṇavittamaḥ|| 17||

balakārākṣaradve .anye punaḥ saṃsthāpya paiṅgaLe|

nyasedādyastatastābhyāṃ vidyāṅgāstrākṣaratrayam|| 18||

pañcātmakācchivāt ṣaṣṭhamaṣṭamañca tato .akṣaram|

sarvātmano dvitīyaṃ ca padaṃ tatparato nyaset|| 19||

brahmāṇḍādestṛtīyasya pūrvamardhaṃ padasya tu|

vinyaseditarasyāpi padārthasyāntimo .akṣaram|| 20||

tṛtīyaṃ tu tataḥ śaivaṃ saptaviṃśakameva ca|

tṛtīyasya padasyāntaṃ padaṃ sūkṣmākṣarānugam|| 21||

idameva padaṃ saukṣmaṃ bījaṃ tatparivartitam|

gāyatryāmakṣaraṃ caiva dvitīyaṃ vinyasedbudhaḥ|| 22||

trimūrtyanatṃ natistasmāt  nādhanāvākṣaradvayam|

nyasenmantrasamātpyarthaṃ punaryadupavarṇitam|| 23||

vidyāṅgāstratṛtīyaṃ tu bhyokārātparato .akṣaram|

vinyasettu punastasmāt ghokārāditrikaṃ nyaset|| 24||

aghorāstrātmakān varṇān saptamāṣṭamaghoratā|

rebhyo .akāramatho bhāvaṃ punarevākṣaradvayam|| 25||

paiṅgalaṃ śākṣaraṃ sthāpya tacca kāroparisthitam|

vinyasedekarudrāntaṃ sarvakārākṣaradvayam|| 26||

ekādaśamaghorāstrāttakāraṃ savisargakam|

karmendriyapadaṃ tasmātpunaḥ sarvaśca bhāyutam|| 27||

bhyokāraśca navistasmāt jñeyāste śaivameva ca|

rudrakāśā tato rucaṃ ṣecabhyaśca tataḥ punaḥ|| 28||

caturthasya tu gāyatryāṃ ṣaḍvarṇādyakṣaratrayam|

nyasedevaṃ caturthyantaṃ mahādevapadaṃ punaḥ|| 29||

padatrayottaraṃ tāntāṃ gāyatryārṇacatuṣṭayam|

antyaṃ cāntye tayormadhye so rudrāstu śivātmakāḥ|| 30||

sarvadevottare hardi ṣaṣṭhaṃ gāyatryamakṣaram|

saptamaṃ sāyametāni pañcamasyāpi vinyaset|| 31||

aṣṭamaṃ nākṣaraṃ śaivaṃ anacakasamanvitam|

yavadeśānādi bhūyo .api vinyasetparato budhaḥ|| 32||

sparśatanmātrakasyāpi padasyārdhamathottaram|

savisargaṃ punarhārde bhūtākārākṣaradvayam|| 33||

śaivātmā bindusaṃyuktaṃ brahma śaivamataḥ param|

nyasedokārasambhinnarṇakāraṃ vāmadevataḥ|| 34||

viyatpadedhipau tābhyāmaṣṭamaṃ saukṣmyamakṣaram|

brahmabhūyaḥ tayorekaṃ pūrvaṃ rākāntakā nyaset|| 35||

ananteśaṃ caturthyantaṃ bāhordarme kṣaraṃ tathā|

stukārantu tataḥ śaivamānanteśaṃ ca saptamam|| 36||

vinyasedekarudrāntāmāmūrcchitamato .akṣaram|

tataḥ pañcamaṣaṣṭhe tu sarvānte suśivātmake|| 37||

anuṣṭupchandasā yuktāstrayo mantrāḥ prakīrtitāḥ|

yajuṣā vāmadevastu gāyatreṇa caturthakam|| 38||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe brahmamantrapaṭalo .aṣṭamaḥ||

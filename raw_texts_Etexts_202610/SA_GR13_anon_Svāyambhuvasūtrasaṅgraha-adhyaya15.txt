athānte .ahani kartavyo yāgaścaṇḍeśvarasya tu|

tasyādau maṇḍalaṃ proktamardhacandraṃ taducyate|| 1||

āmnātasya hi mānasya bhāgaṃ tyaktvā tu pañcamam|

śeṣamānasamaṃ sūtraṃ bhrāmayedardhacandravat|| 2||

tasya madhye likhetpadmamaṣṭapatraṃ sakarṇikam|

dvādaśāṅgulasaṃyuktaṃ karamānamathāpi vā|| 3||

yogapīḍhapadaṃ tatra pīṭhaṃ vinyasya vinyaset|

śarvaśarvapadasthaṃ tu devaṃ caṇḍeśvaraṃ guruḥ|| 4||

vidyāṅgāni tatastasya vinyasya vidhivatpunaḥ|

āgneyādidaLeṣveva dikṣvastraṃ vinyasetpunaḥ|| 5||

sampūjya gandhapuṣpādyaiḥ bhakṣyabhojyairvibhūṣaṇaiḥ|

tarpayedyajñaśeṣeṇa aṣṭottaraśatena tu|| 6||

hṛdayādīni sarvāṇi paścādekaikaśaḥ kramāt|

viṃśatyāhutihomena tarpayitvā kṣamāpayet|| 7||

caṇḍeśvarocitaṃ vastu pūjāyaiva prakalpayet|

rajorekhāntarasthaṃ tu na bhojyaṃ vidhimicchatā|| 8||

tasmādanyadapi dravyaṃ devāya viniveditam|

agādhe .ambhasi tatsarvaṃ caṇḍeśāyaiva nikṣipet|| 9||

ato .anyathāpravṛtānāṃ śivājñābhaṅgakāriṇām|

śāstā caṇḍeśvarasteṣāṃ dīkṣitānāmapi prabhuḥ|| 10||

caṇḍeśvaraṃ prapannā ye na teṣāṃ bhayakāraṇam|

tasmādārādhanākṣīṇāḥ śaivaṃ panthānamāśritāḥ|| 11||

tato yāmāsamāptau tu bhūdeśe svastikāṃkite|

kuryādavabhṛthasnānaṃ ātmano dīkṣitasya ca|| 12||

kalaśena śivasthena sarvaduṣkṛtahāriṇā|

snātaḥ sampūjayetpaścādguruṃ sānucaraṃ budhaḥ|| 13||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe caṇḍayāgapaṭalaḥ pañcadaśaḥ||

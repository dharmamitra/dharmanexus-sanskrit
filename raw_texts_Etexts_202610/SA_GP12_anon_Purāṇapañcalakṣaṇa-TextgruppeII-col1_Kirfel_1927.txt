## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 1. Kapitel  (Mt. 4.33—55).

svāyambhuvo manur dhīmāṃs tapas taptvā suduścaraṃ |
patnīm evāpa rūpāḍhyām anantāṃ nāma nāmataḥ. || 33 ||
priyavrat-ottānapādau manus tasyām ajījanat. |
dharmasya kanyā caturā sūṇṛtā nāma bhāminī || 34 ||
uttānapādāt tanayān prāpa mantharagāminī. |
apasyatim apasyantaṃ kīrtimantaṃ dhruvaṃ tathā || 35 ||
uttānapādo 'janayat sūṇṛtāyāṃ prajāpatiḥ. |
dhruvo varṣasahasrāṇi trīṇi kṛtvā tapaḥ purā || 36 ||
divyam āpa tataḥ sthānam acalaṃ brahmaṇo varāt, |
tam eva purataḥ kṛtvā dhruvaṃ saptarṣayaḥ sthitāḥ. || 37 ||
dhanyā nāma manoḥ kanyā dhruvāc chiṣṭam ajījanat, |
agnikanyā tu succhāyā śiṣṭāt sā suṣuve sutān: || 38 ||
kṛpaṃ ripuṃjayaṃ vṛttaṃ vṛkaṃ ca vṛkatejasaṃ. |
cakṣuṣaṃ brahmadauhitryāṃ vīriṇyāṃ sa ripuṃjayaḥ, || 39 ||
vīraṇasyātmajāyāṃ tu cakṣur manum ajījanat. |
manur vai rājakanyāyāṃ naḍvalāyāṃ sa cakṣuṣaḥ || 40 ||
janayāmāsa tanayān daśa śūrān akalmaṣān: |
ūruḥ pūruḥ śatadyumnas tapasvī satyavāk kaviḥ || 41 ||
agniṣṭud atirātrāś ca sudyumnaś cāparājitaḥ |
abhimanyus tu daśamo naḍvalāyām ajāyata. || 42 ||
ūror ajanayat putrān ṣaḍāgneyī tu suprabhān: |
aṅgaṃ sumanasaṃ khyātiṃ kratum aṅgirasaṃ gayaṃ. || 43 ||
pīṭṛkanyā sunīthā tu venam aṅgād ajījanat. |
venam anyāyinaṃ viprā mamanthus, tatkarād abhūt |
pṛthur nāma mahātejāḥ, sa putrau dvāv ajījanat. || 44 ||
antardhānas tu mārīcaṃ śikhaṇḍinyām ajījanat. |
havirdhānāt ṣaḍāgneyī dhiṣaṇājanayat sutān: |
prācīnabarhiṣaṃ sāṅgaṃ yamaṃ śukraṃ balaṃ śubhaṃ. || 45 ||
prācīnabarhir bhagavān mahān āsīt prajāpatiḥ, |
havirdhānāt prajās tena bahavaḥ saṃpravartitāḥ. || 46 ||
savarṇāyāṃ tu sāmudryāṃ daśādhatta sutān prabhuḥ, |
sarve pracetaso nāma dhanurvedasya pāragāḥ. || 47 ||
tattaporakṣitā vṛkṣā babhur loke samantataḥ, |
devādeśāc ca tān agnir adahad ravinandana. || 48 ||
somakanyābhavat patnī mārīṣā nāma viśrutā, |
tebhyas tu dakṣam ekaṃ sā putram agryam ajījanat. || 49 ||
dakṣād anantaraṃ vṛkṣān auṣadhāni ca sarvaśaḥ |
ajījanat somakanyā nadīṃ candravatīṃ tathā. || 50 ||
somāmśasya ca tasyāpi dakṣasyāśītikoṭayah, |
vakṣye tāsāṃ tu vistāraṃ, loke yaḥ supratiṣṭhitah: || 51 ||
dvipadaś cābhavan kecit, kecid bahupadā narāḥ, |
valīmukhāḥ śaṅkukarṇāḥ karṇaprāvaraṇās tathā, || 52 ||
aśvaṛkṣamukhāḥ kecit, kecit siṃhānanās tathā, |
śvasūkaramukhāḥ kecit, kecid uṣṭramukhās tathā. || 53 ||
janayāmāsa dharmātmā mlecchān sarvān anekaśaḥ, |
sa sṛṣṭvā manasā dakṣaḥ striyaḥ paścād ajījanat. || 54 ||
dadau sa daśa dharmāya, kaśyapāya trayodaśa,
saptaviṃśatīṃ somāya dadau nakṣatrasamjñitāḥ; |
devāsuramanuṣyādi tābhyāḥ sarvam abhūj jagat. || 55 ||

## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 2. Kapitel | A.  (L. 63.1—21; Mt. 5.1—32; P.1. I. 6.1—33a; P.2.V. 6.1—33. cfr. Kū. 16.1—16.)

devānāṃ dānavānāṃ ca gandharvoragarakṣasāṃ |
utpattiṃ vistareṇaiva sūta brūhi yathātathaṃ! || 1 ||
sūta (pulastya) uvāca:
saṃkalpād darśanāt sparśāt pūrveṣāṃ sṛṣṭir ucyate, |
dakṣāt prācetasād ūrdhvaṃ sṛṣṭir maithunasaṃbhavā. || 2 ||
prajāḥ sṛjeti vyādiṣṭaḥ pūrvaṃ dakṣaḥ svayaṃbhuvā |
yathā sasarja caivādau, tathaiva śṛṇuta dvijāḥ! || 3 ||
yadā tu sṛjatas tasya devarṣigaṇapannagān |
na vṛddhim agamal lokas, tadā maithunayogatas || 4 ||
dakṣaḥ putrasahasrāṇi tad-asiknyām ajījanat. |
tāṃs tu dṛṣṭvā mahābhāgān sisṛkṣur vividhāḥ prajāḥ || 5 ||
nāradaḥ prāha haryaśvān dakṣa-putrān samāgatān: |
bhuvaḥ pramāṇaṃ sarvatra jñātvordhvam adha eva ca || 6 ||
tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ viśeṣeṇa kurudhvam ṛṣiṣattamāḥ! |
te tu tadvacanaṃ śrutvā prayātāḥ sarvatodiśaṃ, || 7 ||
adyāpi na nivartante samudrād iva sindhavaḥ. |
haryaśveṣu pranaṣṭeṣu punar dakṣaḥ prajāpatiḥ || 8 ||
vairiṇyāṃ eva putrāṇāṃ sahasram asṛjat prabhuḥ; |
śabalā nāma te viprāḥ sametāḥ sṛṣṭihetavaḥ. || 9 ||
nārado 'nugatān prāha punas tān pūrvavan muniḥ: |
bhuvaḥ pramāṇaṃ sarvatra jñātvā bhrātr̄n atho punaḥ || 10 ||
āgatyā cātha sṛṣṭiṃ vai kariṣyatha viśeṣataḥ! |
te 'pi tenaiva mārgeṇa jagmur bhrātṛpathā tadā. || 11 ||
tataḥ prabhṛti na bhrātuḥ kanīyān mārgam icchati, |
anviṣyan duḥkham āpnoti, tena tat parivarjayet. || 12 ||
tatas teṣu api naṣṭeṣu ṣaṣṭiṃ kanyāḥ prajāpatiḥ |
vairiṇyāṃ, janayāmāsa dakṣaḥ prācetasas tadā. || 13 ||
prādāt sa daśa dharmāya, kaśyapāya trayodaśa, |
saptaviṃśati śomāya, catasro 'riṣṭanemaye || 14 ||
dve caiva bhṛguputrāya, dve kṛśāśvāya dhimate |
dve caiv-aṅgirase tadvat; tāsāṃ nāmāni vistarāt || 15 ||
śṛṇudhvaṃ devamātṝṇāṃ prajāvistāram āditaḥ: |
arundhati vasur yāmī lambā bhānur marutvatī || 16 ||
saṃkalpā ca muhūrtā ca sādhyā viśvā ca bhāminī |
dharmapatnyaḥ samākhyātas, tāsāṃ putrān nibodhata! || 17 ||
viśvedevās tu viśvāyāḥ, sādhyā sādhyān ajījanat, |
marutvatyāṃ marutvanto, vasos tu vasavas tathā, || 18 ||
bhānos tu bhānavo jātā, muhūrtāyāṃ muhūrtakāḥ, |
lambāyāṃ ghoṣanāmāno, nāgavīthī tu yāmijā || 19 ||
pṛthivītalasaṃbhūtam arundhatyām ajāyata, |
saṃkalpāyās tu saṃkalpo; vasu-sṛṣṭiṃ nibodhata! || 20 ||
jyotiṣmantas tu ye devā vyāpakāḥ sarvatodiśaṃ, |
vasavas te samākhyāṭās, teṣāṃ sargaṃ nibodhata: || 21 ||
āpo dhruvaś ca somaś ca dharaś caiv-anilo 'nalaḥ |
pratyūṣaś ca prabhāsaś ca vasavo 'ṣṭau prakīrtitāḥ. || 22 ||
āpasya putrāś catvāraḥ: śānto vaitaṇḍa eva ca |
śāmbo 'tha munibabhruś ca yajñarakṣādhikāriṇaḥ. || 23 ||
dhruvasya kālaḥ putras tu, varcāḥ somād ajāyata, |
draviṇo havyavāhaś ca dharaputrāv imau smṛtau. || 24 ||
kalyāṇinyāṃ tataḥ prāṇo ramaṇaḥ śīśiro 'pi ca, |
manohard dharāt putrān avāpātha hareḥ sutā. || 25 ||
śivā manojavaṃ putram avijñātagatiṃ tathā |
avāpa c-analāt putrāv agniprāyaguṇau punah. || 26 ||
agniputraḥ kumāras tu śarastambe vyajāyata, |
tasya śākho viśākhaś ca naigameyaś ca prṣṭhajāḥ. || 27 ||
apatyaṃ kṛttikānāṃ tu kārttikeyas tataḥ smṛtaḥ, |
pratyūṣasya ṛṣiḥ putro munir nāmātha devalaḥ. || 28 ||
viśvakarmā prabhāsasya putraḥ śilpī prajāpatiḥ |
prāsādabhavanodyānapratimābhūṣaṇādiṣu || 29 ||
taḍāgārāmakūpeṣu smṛtaḥ so 'maravardhakiḥ. |
ajaikapāḍ ahirbudhnyo virūpākṣo 'tha raivataḥ || 30 ||
haraś ca bahurūpaś ca tryambakaś ca sureśvaraḥ |
sāvitraś ca jayantaś ca pinākī cāparājitaḥ, || 31 ||
ete rudrāḥ samākhyāṭā ekādaśa gaṇeśvarāḥ. |
eteṣāṃ mānasānāṃ tu triśūlavaradhārīṇāṃ || 32 ||
koṭayaś caturaśitis, tatputrās cāḥṣayā matāḥ |
dikṣu sarvāsu ye rakṣāṃ prakurvanti gaṇeśvarāḥ. |
ete vai putrapautrāś ca surabhīgarbhasambhavāḥ. || 33 ||

## 2. Abschnitt. Vaṃśa | 2. Kapitel | B.  (L. 63.22—41; Mt. 6.1—47; P.1 I. 6.33b—79; P.2 V. 6.34—80;cfr. Kū. 16.17—20, 18.11, 12.)

kaśyapasya pravakṣyāmi patnībhyāḥ putrapautrakān: |
aditir ditir danuś caiva ariṣṭā surasā tathā || 1 ||
surabhir vinatā caiva tāmrā krodhavaśā irā |
kadrūḥ khasā munis tadvat, tāsāṃ putrān nibodhata! || 2 ||
tuṣitā nāma ye devāś cākṣuṣasyāntare manoḥ, |
vaivasvate 'ntare caite hy ādityā dvādaśa smṛtāḥ: || 3 ||
indro dhātā bhagas tvaṣṭā mitro 'tha varuṇo 'ryamā |
vivasvān savitā pūṣā aṃśumān viṣṇur eva ca || 4 ||
ete sahasrakiraṇā ādityā dvādaśa smṛtāḥ, |
mārīcāt kaśyapāj jajñe putrān āditir uttamān. || 5 ||

ditiḥ putradvayaṃ lebhe kaśyapād iti naḥ śrutaṃ. || 7 ||
hiraṇyakaśipuṃ caiva hiraṇyākṣaṃ tathaiva ca. |
hiraṇyakaśipos tadvaj jātaṃ putracatuṣṭayam: || 8 ||
prahlādāś c-anuhlādaś ca samhlādo hlāda eva ca. |
prahlādaputra āyuṣmāñ śibir bāṣkala eva ca || 9 ||
virocanaś caturthaś ca, sa baliṃ putraṃ āptavān. |
baleḥ putraśataṃ tv āsīd bāṇajyeṣṭhaṃ tato dvijāḥ: || 10 ||
dhṛtarāṣṭras tathā sūryaś candraś caudrāṃśutāpanaḥ |

evamādyās tu bahavo bāṇajyeṣṭhā guṇādhikāḥ, |
bāṇaḥ sahasrabāhuś ca sarvāstragaṇasaṃyutaḥ, || 12 ||
tapasā toṣito yasya pure vasati śūlabhṛt, |
mahākālatvam agamat sāmyaṃ yaś ca pinākinaḥ. || 13 ||
hiraṇyākṣasya putro 'bhūd ulūkaḥ śakunis tathā |
bhūtasaṃtāpanaś caiva mahānābhas tathaiva ca. || 14 ||
etebhyaḥ putrapautrāṇāṃ koṭayaḥ saptasaptatiḥ |
mahābalā mahākāyā nānārūpā mahaujasaḥ. || 15 ||
danuḥ putraśataṃ lebhe kaśyapād baladarpitaṃ; |
vipracittiḥ pradhāno 'bhūt teṣāṃ madhye mahābalaḥ, || 16 ||
dvimūrdhā śakuniś caiva tathā saṅkuśirodharaḥ |
ayomukhaḥ śambaraś ca kapilo vāmanas tathā || 17 ||
mārīcir meghavāṃś caiva irāgarbhaśirās tathā |
vidrāvaṇaś ca ketuś ca ketuvīryaḥ śatahṛadaḥ || 18 ||
indrajit saptajic caiva vajranābhas tathaiva ca |
ekacakro mahābāhur vajrākṣas tārakas tathā || 19 ||
asilomā pulomā ca bindur bāṇo mahāsurah |
svarbhānur vṛṣaparvā ca, evamādyā danoh sutāh. || 20 ||
svarbhānos tu prabhā kanyā śacī caiva pulomajā, |
upadānavī mayasyāsīt tathā mandodarī kuhūh, || 21 ||
śarmiṣṭhā sudarī caiva candrā ca vṛṣaparvaṇāh. |
pulomā kālakā caiva vaiśvānarasute hi te || 22 ||
bahvapatye mahāsattve mārīcasya parigrahe. |
tayoh ṣaṣṭih ṣahasrāṇi dānavānām abhūt purā. || 23 ||
paulomān kālakeyāṃś ca mārīco 'janayat purā, |
avadhyā ye 'marāṇāṃ vai hiraṇyapuravāsinaḥ; || 24 ||
caturmukhāl labdhavarās te hatā vijayena tu. |
vipracittih saiṃhikeyān siṃhikāyām ajījanat, || 25 ||

ye hatā balam āśritya tv arjunena raṇājire. || 29 ||

saudāmanī tathā kanyā yeyaṃ nabhasi viśrutā. || 34 ||
sāṃpātīś ca jaṭāyuś ca aruṇasya sutāv ubhau, |
sāṃpātīputro babhruś ca śīghragaś cāpi viśrutaḥ. || 35 ||
jaṭāyuṣaḥ karṇikāraḥ śatagāmī ca viśrutau, |
sāraso rajjuvālāś ca bheruṇḍaś cāpi tatsutāḥ. || 36 ||
teṣām anantam abhavat pakṣiṇāṃ putrapautrakaṃ. |
śurasāyāḥ sahasraṃ tu sarpāṇām abhavat purā. || 37 ||
sahasraśīrasāṃ kadrūḥ sahasraṃ prāpa suvratā, |
pradhānās teṣu vikhyātāḥ ṣaḍvīṃśatir arīṃdamāḥ: || 38 ||
śeṣa-vāsuki-karkoṭa-śaṅkh-airāvata-kambalāḥ |
dhanaṃjaya-mahānīla-padm-āśvatara-takṣakāḥ || 39 ||
elāpatra-mahāpadma-dhṛtarāṣṭra-balāhakāḥ |
śaṅkhapāla-mahāśaṅkha-puṣpadaṃṣṭra-śubhānanāḥ || 40 ||
sañkhuromā ca nahuṣo vāmanah pāṇinas tathā |
kapilo durmukhaś cāpi patañjalir iti smṛtāḥ. || 41 ||
eṣām anantam abhavat sarveṣāṃ putrapautrakaṃ, |
prāyaśo yat purā dagdhaṃ janamejayamandire. || 42 ||
rakṣogaṇaṃ krodhavaśā sunāmānam ajījanat, |
daṃṣṭriṇāṃ niyutaṃ teṣāṃ bhīmasenād agāt kṣayaṃ. || 43 ||
rudrāṇāṃ ca gaṇaṃ tadvad gomahiṣyau varāṅgaṇā |
surabhir janayāmāsa kaśyapāt saṃyatavratā. || 44 ||
munir munīnāṃ ca gaṇaṃ gaṇaṃ apsarasāṃ tathā, |
tathā kinnaragandharvān ariṣṭājanayad bahūn. || 45 ||
tṛṇavṛkṣalatāgulmam irā sarvam ajījanat, |
khasā tu yakṣarakṣāṃsi janayāmāsa koṭiśaḥ. || 46 ||
ete kaśyapadāyādāḥ śataśo 'tha sahasraśaḥ |
eṣa manvantare rājan sargāḥ svārociṣe smṛtaḥ. || 47 ||
tata ekonapañcāśan marutāḥ kaśyapād ditiḥ |
janayāmāsa dharmajñān sarvān amaravallabhān. || 48 ||

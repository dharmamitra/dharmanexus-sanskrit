saṅkīrṇāatiṣu strīṣu vicārya bahulaṃ guṇam|
kuryāddattā(ttada)nusāreṇa sambhogaṃ ruciraṃ nṛpaḥ||

rajanyādi(dau)kaphāḍhyāyāḥ sapittāyā mahāniśi|
rātre(trau)rataḥ (taṃ) savātāyāḥ kālo 'yaṃ ratilekha(khela)ne||

devasattvāsu yoṣāsu śucau deśe manohare|
anindyaṃ surataṃ kuryāttāsāṃ śuci sukhāvaham||

gandharvasattva gandhā(rāddhā)su gītenānandya mānasam|
nityaṃ ca (niṣkṛpaṃ) cāṭubhūyiṣṭhaṃ ramyadeśe rataṃ caret||

yakṣasattvāsu rāmāsu kṛtvā surabhilepanam|
udyāne tuṅgaharmye haṭhākramya(ma)rataṃ caret||

nāgasattvāsu nārīṣu bhūgṛhe vā vanisthale|
vṛkṣādhirūḍhakāśleṣasthityā sambhogamācaret||

vidhāyaivaṃ ratakrīḍāṃ krīḍāṃ(ḍā) kāmasya (stu)kurvati(bhūpatiḥ)|
saubhāgyarūpasampannaṃ mohayedvanitājanam||

dattvā mukhaṃ mṛgākṣīṇāṃ hareccittaṃ samanmatham|
kuṭṭanīnāṃ dhanaṃ dattvā haredbandhakamarpitam||

itīyaṃ suratakrīḍā niḥśeṣajanarañjinī|
nṛpāṇāṃ sukhasiddhyarthaṃ varṇitā somabhūbhujā||

iti suratakrīḍā samāptā||20||
idaṃ śṛṇoti yaḥ śāstraṃ tasya dharme ratirbhavet|
kauśalaṃ cārthaśāstreṣu rājanītirabhīpsi(tāvabhijña)tā||

sevāyāṃ dakṣatā caiva sāhitye ni(nai)puṇaṃ tathā|
tarkeṣu pāṭavaṃ samyak chandolaṅkāraveditā||

vaidagdhyaṃ śā(śa)stravidyāyāṃ gajāśva(śvā)rohaṇajñatā|
vijñānaṃ mallavidyāyāṃ yuddhajñānaṃ tathaiva ca||

saṅgītavidyākauśalyaṃ jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ|
kāmadhenuridaṃ śāstraṃ mānasollāsasaṃjñitam||

nāstyetat(styanena)samaṃ śāstraṃ sarvavastuprakāśane|
brāhmaṇo labhate jñānaṃ  kṣatriyo rājyamāpnuyāt||1374||

vaiśyo vibhavamatyarthaṃ śūdro vṛttiṃ ca bhuñjati(pūjitām)||1375||

iti śrīmahārājādhirājasatyāśrayakulatilakacālukyābharaṇaśrīmadbhūlokamallaśrīsomeśvaradevaviracite abhilaṣitārthacintāmaṇau rājamānasollase sukhopapādikākrīḍākathane pañcamaṃ prakaraṇaṃ samāptam|

svasti śrīnṛpaśālivāhanaśake netrāṅkatithyunmite
prāntye māsi site trayodaśatithau puṣye śanervāsare|
śrīmadyādavarājabhūpatimude kherḍānvayolīlikha-
drāmaḥ satkṛtirājamānasasukhollāsābhidhaṃ pustakam||1||

akṣarāṇīha yāvanti rājamānasapustake|
tāvadyādavabhūpālo jīvyāddivyaśaracchatam||2||

hara hara hara hara mahādeva||

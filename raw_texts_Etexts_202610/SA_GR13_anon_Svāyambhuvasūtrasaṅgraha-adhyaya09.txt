atha mudrāprasiddhyarthaṃ tallakṣaṇamihocyate|

yajjñātvā sarvamantrāṇāṃ vidhānaphalamaśnute|| 1||

śodhanī kālakarṇī ca mukuLī śaśakarṇikā|

niṣṭhurā padmamudrā ca namaskārā manoramā|| 2||

śāntā ca liṅgamudrā ca dhvajā cāvāhanī tathā|

gāyatrī ca tathā netramantraṃ caiva prakīrtitam|| 3||

pāṇimūle tu saṃlagne hṛddeśe tu parasparam|

kṛtvaivaṃ tāḍayetpāṇiṃ mudreyaṃ śodhanī bhavet|| 4||

kṛtvā muṣṭī tatotthāne tadanaṅguLiyo jane|

aṅguṣṭhāgre tu saṃlagne kālakarṇīṃ tu darśayet|| 5||

dvau hastāvekataḥ kṛtvā madhye tu suṣiraṃ tayoḥ|

sarojamukulākārāṃ mukuLīṃ tu pradarśayet|| 6||

tarjanyau kuñcite kṛtvā tayoragre nidhāpayet|

dvayoraṅguṣṭhaśirasī śeṣā ṛjvyo ghanāḥ samāḥ|| 7||

kṛtvā tu maṇibandhe tu vāme savyaṃ tathā nyaset|

mudreyaṃ śaśakarṇī tu vijñeyā yogakarmaṇi|| 8||

hastābhyāmañjaliṃ kṛtvā dvimuṣṭī sādhayetsudhīḥ|

aṅguṣṭhau garbhanikṣiptau niṣṭhurāṃ samprayojayet|| 9||

pūrvoktā mukuLī yātu hṛddeśe nisṛtāṅguLiḥ|

vyākośakamalākāraṃ padmamudrāṃ tu darśayet|| 10||

sakalāṃgulisaṃlagnau hastau hṛddeśamāśritau|

sarveṣāṃ vandanī kāryā vāmāṅguṣṭhanipīḍitam|| 11||

valite hastayoḥ kārye uttāne tu kaniṣṭhike|

tayorvāmā bahirjñeyā valite madhyame .api vā|| 12||

kuñcite .anāmikāgre ca svapāṇitalayojite|

deśinyau prasṛte kṛtvā tayoragre niyojayet|| 13||

aṅguṣṭhau mūlaparvasthau tayoreva ha kuñcitau|

manorameti vikhyātā śivasāyujyadāyikā|| 14||

tarjanyaṅguṣṭhalagnāgrā śeṣā ṛjvyo ghanāḥ samāḥ|

sthāpate sarvamantrāṇāṃ śānteyaṃ samprakīrtitā|| 15||

śāntāṃ baddhvā kṣipedvāmamaṅguṣṭhaṃ tadadhomukham|

veṣṭayeddakṣiṇāṅguṣṭhaṃ vāmāṅguṣṭhatalena tu|| 16||

vāmahastāṅguLībhistu savyaṃ muṣṭiṃ ca veṣṭayet|

liṅgamudreti vikhyātā śivarūpadharī sadā|| 17||

agraṃ vāmaprakośinyā veṣṭayetsavyapāṇinā|

kṛtvo  toṅguṣṭhaṃ hṛdaye sanniyojayet|| 18||

vāmāṅguṣṭhaṃ ca vāmābhistisṛbhirveṣṭayettataḥ|

dhvajamudrā samākhyātā saṃyojyā yāgakarmaṇi|| 19||

hastābhyāmañjaliṃ kṛtvā kaniṣṭhāparvamūlayoḥ|

aṅguṣṭhau sthāpayedeṣā mudrā tvāvāhanī smṛtā|| 20||

anyonyāntaragāḥ sarvāḥ karapṛṣṭhāgrayojitāḥ|

aṅguṣṭhāṅgulayaḥ kuryātsuṣiraṃ ca karāntare|| 21||

pūjākarmaṇi gāyatrīṃ gāyatryāstu prayojayet|

netraṃ netreṣu netreṇa śāntāvādhomukhī bhavet|| 22||

dvau muṣṭī saṃhṛte kṛtvā śīghraṃ hastau prasārayet|

astramudreti vikhyātā sarveṣāṃ tu bhayaṅkarī|| 23||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe mudrālakṣaṇapaṭalo navamaḥ||

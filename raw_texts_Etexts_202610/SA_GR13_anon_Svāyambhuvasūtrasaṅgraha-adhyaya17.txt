atha snānavidhiṃ vakṣye parameśaprakāśitam|

yena siddhimavāpnoti snāto mucyeta kilbiṣāt|| 1||

nadīnadataṭākeṣu snātaḥ suplāvaneṣu ca|

snānaṃ samācarennityaṃ sāmānyavidhikalpitam|| 2||

tatrādau śucirācāntaḥ śucau deśe samāhitaḥ|

gṛhṇīyātsadyamantreṇa mṛdamastreṇa śodhitām|| 3||

jaloddeśe tataḥ sthāpya vāmadevena mantravit|

prokṣyedbahurūpeṇa padeśānena mantrayet|| 4||

bhāgatrayaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā puruṣasya padaistribhiḥ|

kṣipeddikṣu jale caikabhāgamastrābhimantritam|| 5||

bhāgārdhena malasnānamapsu cārdhaṃ nidhāpayet|

bhāgena śivatīrthaṃ tu kuryādekādaśātmanā|| 6||

padasthenordhvakāyastu prāgārabhya śivaḥ kramāt|

īśānādyaiḥ padaiḥ paścātsnāyādāmalakādibhiḥ|| 7||

abhiṣekaṃ ca taireva prāṇāyāmān śivena tu|

kṛtvācamya purā brāhmīṃ bhajetsandhyaṃ dvirāśramī|| 8||

itarastu virāgātmā laukikārthaparāṅmukhaḥ|

kuryādbrāhmīṃ navā kuryāt śaivīmeva niyogataḥ|| 9||

sadyena vāmadevena tato .aghoreṇa mārjanam|

prāṇāyāmaṃ caturthena śeṣamīśena yojayet|| 10||

śrīkaṇṭhenopasaṃhṛtya kuryāddhṛdyātmano vidhim|

yāyādāyatanaṃ paścāddhyātvā rūpaṃ sadojjvalam|| 11||

snānamāgneyamaparaṃ bhasmasnānamudāhṛtam|

jalasnānottaraṃ nityamanuttaramathāpi vā|| 12||

jātisaṅkarasaṃsparśakarmabhāvaparigrahaiḥ|

adṛṣṭaṃ kīrtitaṃ bhasma bhasmasnānavicakṣaṇaiḥ|| 13||

sitaṃ pūtamaromañca niṣparigrahameva ca|

tena praśasyate snānamāgneyamadhikaṃ guṇaiḥ|| 14||

āraṇyagomayotthena pūtenākarajena vā|

sadyādimantritenaiva snāyānnityamudaṅmukhaḥ|| 15||

śiro mukhaṃ tathā grīvā skandhau bāhū urastathā|

kramāduddhūlayedaṅgaṃ caramaṃ ca kaṭiṃ tataḥ|| 16||

trirācamyodakaṃ paścāddviḥpramṛjya mukhaṃ śanaiḥ|

śiraścāpi śīrṣaṇyāni ṣaḍdvārāṇi tataḥ spṛśet|| 17||

aṃsau ca hṛdayaṃ nābhiṃ caramaṃ ca śirastataḥ|

pādāvabhyukṣya kartavyamidamatra yathoditam|| 18||

tale tvaṅguṣṭhamūlasya tīrthaṃ brāhmaṃ pratiṣṭhitam|

tenācāntaḥ śucirnityaṃ prāṅmukho vāpyudaṅmukhaḥ|| 19||

daivaṃ kaniṣṭhikāmūlatale tīrthaṃ praśasyate|

tena praśasyate cānamapāmeva vicakṣaṇaiḥ|| 20||

aṅgulyagre sthitaṃ tīrthamṛṣīṇāṃ tarpaṇaṃ param|

paitryaṃ ca pitṛkāryeṣu tarjanyaṅguṣṭhamūlataḥ|| 21||

āgneyaṃ pāṇimadhye tu saumyaṃ vāme pratiṣṭhitam|

tābhyāṃ sthānābhiṣekaṃ ca kartavyamaghanāśanam|| 22||

māhendraṃ snānamanyatsyādvṛṣṭau satyāṃ taducyate|

sūryasya raśmisaṃsparśī syātpraśastataraṃ hi tat|| 23||

gajasammīlitākṣeṇa prāṅmukhenordhvabāhunā|

īśānaṃ japatā kāryaṃ padasaptakamṛcchatā|| 24||

vāyavyaṃ ca tathā snānaṃ gokṛtena tu rajasā|

kāryaṃ tatpuruṣeṇaiva vidhiṃ snānasya jānatā|| 25||

sadyādiparipūtena salilena tu mārjanam|

māntraṃ tattu vijānīyādvāruṇe .api tadiṣyate|| 26||

mānasaṃ manasā kāryaṃ ṣaṣṭhaṃ sārvatrikaṃ ca tat|

sarvamantrādimantreṇa prāṇāyāmagatena ca|| 27||

snātvācamya yathānyāyaṃ karma kṛtvā yathoditam|

pūjayedatha deveśaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile .api vā|

agnikuṇḍe .athavā dehe nityamevaṃ prakīrtitam|| 28||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṃgrahe snānapaṭalaḥ saptadaśaḥ||

athādhivāsanaṃ kuryāt prāguktamakhilaṃ vidhim|

kalaśasya niveśaṃ ca puṣpāḍhyavikirottamam|| 1||

haimaṃ vā rājataṃ tāmraṃ pārthivaṃ kalaśaṃ dṛḍham|

saṃskṛtya vinyasettasminnanantavijayaṃ śivam|| 2||

sarvādhvaraśarīrasya aiśānyāṃ diśi saṃsthite|

kalaśe sati kurvīta garbhamantragaṇārcanam|| 3||

tato .analavidhānaṃ ca mantrāṇā tarpaṇaṃ tataḥ|

carorapi vidhānaṃ ca gāyatryā hṛdayena vā|| 4||

caruṃ gavyena payasā śāliṣāṣṭkataṇḍulaiḥ|

śivāgnau śrāpayeddhīmān sukhinnamavatārayet|| 5||

apanīya tatastasya devāyāgraṃ nivedya ca|

sakṛddhomaṃ tataḥ kuryātsarvamantragaṇena tu|| 6||

khaṭikā śaṅkusuparṇā kartarīrajasāṃ tathā|

ājyādīnāṃ ca sarveṣāṃ gandhapuṣpādikasya ca|| 7||

sārdrasya dantakāṣṭhasya kṣīravṛkṣamayasya ca|

sarveṣāṃ prokṣaṇaṃ caiva kruśaiścālambhanaṃ tataḥ|| 8||

nava dyavi  phalaiḥ saha|

tataḥ śrīkaṇṭhadevena bali  || 9||

 devadevaṃ praṇamya ca|

vāriṇā mantrāpūtena śiṣyādi prokṣayediti|| 10||

dakṣiṇāstreṇa tanmūrdhni bhasmanā tāḍayetpaśum|

ānayeddakṣiṇāmūrtim muñca muñca padena tu|| 11||

uttarābhimukhaṃ kṛtvā prāṅmukhaṃ cātra deśikaḥ|

kuśaiḥ sarvātmanā kuryāt sparśanaṃ śirasi kramāt|| 12||

upaveśya tamālabhya brahmabhiḥ kusumaiḥ sitaḥ|

gāyatryā pūjayetsamyak gandhadhūpapavitrakaiḥ|| 13||

pātayetpadayoḥ pūrvaṃ śivāgnyoścaramaṃ guroḥ|

dantakāṣṭhaṃ caroḥ śeṣaṃ tasmai dadyāttato guruḥ|| 14||

sataṃ prāpya samaśnīyāduttarābhimukhaḥ śanaiḥ|

ācāryaḥ sarvaśeṣaṃ tu prāśayeccaramaṃ carum|| 15||

agnau naivedyahomaṃ tu prāyaścittamathācaret|

ājyāhutibhiraṣṭābhiraghoreṇa praśāntaye|| 16||

visṛjya devadeveśamagnimadhyasthitaṃ purā|

caramaṃ sthaṇḍile bhūmau saśiṣyaṃ tu guruḥ svapet|| 17||

śivādhivāsanaṃ viprāḥ kathitaṃ tu mayādhunā|

sāmānyasya punarmā bhūdabhāsoktirapārthikā|| 18||

vyāsoktirapi paryastā samāsoktirabhīpsitā|

sā caivaṃ syānnayadyatra granthasya gurutā bhavet|| 19||

athocyate samāsena nidhaneśavacaḥ śrutam|

sādhanaṃ vividhārthasya padārthasya ca niścitam|| 20||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe .adhivāsanapaṭalo dvādaśaḥ||

atha sarvādhvaśuddhyarthamanantavijayaṃ purā|

ālikhenmaṇḍalaṃ mukhyaṃ śivadhāmaphalapradam|| 1||

asya darśanamātreṇa mahāpātakino .api ye|

nirmalāste .api rudratvaṃ prāpya modantyaśaṅkitāḥ|| 2||

devadānavagandharvayakṣarakṣorageśvarāḥ|

paśyantyanantavijayaṃ yāgabhūmerbahiḥsthitāḥ|| 3||

śāṇādyekatamaṃ dīrghaṃ maṇḍalāddviguṇottaram|

tatra sūtraṃ trivṛtproktaṃ granthyādirahitaṃ samam|| 4||

chāyāpraveśaniṣkrāmamatsyairāveditāsu ca|

dikṣu teneṣyate nyāsaḥ śaṅkunā śaṅkitātmabhiḥ|| 5||

śaṅkavaḥ śaiṃśapāḥ kāryāḥ khādirāḥ plakṣasārajāḥ|

caturaśrā hyadho bhāve vṛttāḥ śirasi śākhinām|| 6||

pūrvapaścimaśaṅkusthaṃ yāmyasaumyagataṃ tataḥ|

aiśānanairṛtasthaṃ ca sūtramāsphālayecchanaiḥ|| 7||

evaṃ pāvakasāmīraśaṅkusthaṃ deśikastataḥ|

dvihastapīṭhagarbhasthaṃ madhye padmaṃ samālikhet|| 8||

ṣaḍaṅgule purobhāge prathame karṇikāṃ likhet|

kesarāṇi dvitīye tu dalānāṃ sandhayo .apare|| 9||

caturthe tu daLāgrāṇi padmamevaṃ prakalpya tu|

dvihastāmālikhedvīthīṃ pīṭhasya paritaḥ sthitām|| 10||

vīthyardhena pṛthakkāryaṃ devatāvaraṇaṃ bahiḥ|

vīthīmukhāṣṭavicchedabhāgāṣṭakavikalpitam|| 11||

vīthīmukhārdhanirvṛttabahirdvārāṣṭasaṃyutam|

sūtrayitvā tataḥ paścādrajāṃsi vinipātayet|| 12||

tāni samprathitānyeva yathārthavibhavāni tu|

pātayetsūtramārgeṇa sitādīnanupūrvaśaḥ|| 13||

sitarekhāsu nirvṛttaṃ raktādyābhirbahirvṛtam|

ṛjumārgāṣṭakopetamanantavijayaṃ śubham|| 14||

uktamānārdhamadhyardhamānadviguṇamānakam|

kartavyaṃ parameśasya vitānādisuśobhitam|| 15||

tasya madhye nyaseddevaṃ karṇikāyāṃ paraṃ śivam|

īśānaṃ mūrdhni vinyasya vaktraṃ pūrvadaLe nyaset|| 16||

dakṣiṇe tu tathāghoraṃ vāmadevaṃ tathottare|

paścime tu daLe sadyaṃ padavyūhakramātpunaḥ|| 17||

aṅgādīnāmanenaiva vidiśāsu samārabhet|

āgneyadaLamūle tu praṇavaṃ hṛdayaṃ nyaset|| 18||

īśāne tu śiraścaiva nairṛte ca śikhāṃ nyaset|

vāyavyāṃ kavacaṃ tadvaddaLāgre .astraṃ tathottare|| 19||

sarvātmānaṃ śiro .aṣṭārṇaṃ jvālinīṃ piṅgaLaṃ tathā|

netraṃ pūrvadaLāgre tu dakṣiṇe .astraṃ pravinyaset|| 20||

gāyatrīṃ paścime sthāpya vidyāṅgāni tu pūrvavat|

daLāntareṣu sarveṣu tataḥ pāśupataṃ nyaset|| 21||

aindre pīṭhe tato .anantaṃ yāmye sūkṣmaṃ ca vinyaset|

āvāhanārghapādyaiśca vāruṇe ca śivottamam|| 22||

ekanetraṃ tato   deva  muttare sthāpya pūjya ca|

aiśānye tu nyaseddhīmānekarudraṃ samāhitaḥ|| 23||

trimūrtiṃ makhamukhe tu śrīkaṇṭhaṃ nairṛte tataḥ|

śikhaṇḍinaṃ ca vāyavye pīṭhe sampūjayetkramāt|| 24||

tato vidyeśvarī śaktirmūlavidyeti viśrutā|

ādyā mūrtiḥ śivasyaiṣā sampūjyā yāgakarmaṇi|| 25||

vargadvayamanantasya dakṣiṇottarapārśvayoḥ|

vibhinnaṃ pūjayeddevyāḥ sūkṣmādīnāṃ tataḥ kramāt|| 26||

evaṃ vargāṣṭakāvasthāḥ caturdikṣu vyavasthitāḥ|

sampūjya pūjayedastraṃ dvārasthānaṣṭake sthitam|| 27||

tasyottaraṃ tataḥ karma kṛtvā śiṣyasya coditam|

guruḥ praṇamya deveśamidaṃ vijñāpayecchivam|| 28||

prasīda mama deveśa tavājñānuvidhāyinaḥ|

tvatprasādātparaṃ dhāma yātvayaṃ bhogabhugdvijaḥ|| 29||

evamasyāpyanujñāte samīhitaphale sati|

ni  rṣaraṇamutkarṣamadhvano mūlamāśrayet|| 30||

yenecchati tathā netuṃ tasya mūlapadāśrayam|

sampūjya yojayettasmin vāmadevena sādhakaḥ|| 31||

gāyatryā tatra sambhūtiḥ sadyena jananaṃ tataḥ|

kṛtvā ghoreṇa kartavyamadhikāraviśodhanam|| 32||

bhogaṃ ca puruṣeṇātra nirvartya vidhivattathā|

īśānena layaṃ kuryādadhvaśuddhau tu deśikaḥ|| 33||

aghorāstreṇa tatpāśaṃ cchitvā sarvātmanā kṣipet|

dadyādvyastasamastena śivenādyāhutīstu ṣaṭ|| 34||

kālāgnyādi śivānteṣu sarvasthāneṣvanukramāt|

pradadyādāhutīretā nava ṣaṭ ca yathoditāḥ|| 35||

itarādhvaviśuddhyarthaṃ pañcakaṃ homamārabhet|

svena svenaiva mantreṇa padasaṃkhyāsthitena tu|| 36||

sādhāraṇo vidhirjñeyo bhuvanādhvapadādhvanoḥ|

sadāśive pade viprā bhuvane dviguṇākṛtiḥ|| 37||

tattvavarṇādhvanorhomaḥ prāguktāddviguṇaḥ samaḥ|

sadāśive paṭe tattve tattvādhvani caturguṇaḥ|| 38||

sa eva syāddaśaguṇaḥ mantrādhvanyakṣarādike|

mantrādhvani ca yo homo dviguṇaḥ sakalādhvani|| 39||

anena kramayogena homasāmyaṃ dvayordvayoḥ|

adhvanorveditavyaṃ tu śivayogāhutiṃ vinā|| 40||

śivayoganimittastu homaḥ sādhāraṇo .akṣare|

tataḥ pañcakahomaṃ ca homānte cākṣarāhutīḥ|| 41||

tryuttaraṃ ca sahasraṃ syāttathā śatacatuṣṭayam|

āhutīnāmiyaṃ saṃkhyā dīkṣākarmaṇi bhauvane|| 42||

padadīkṣāvidhau tadvaditarādhvavidhiṃ vinā|

sarvatraivocyate saṃkhyā yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ|| 43||

aṣṭapañcāśatāmiśraṃ sahasraṃ tāttvike .adhvani|

āhutīnāṃ tu vijñeyamevaṃ varṇādhvakarmaṇi|| 44||

sahasraṃ dve śate .aṣṭau ca mantrādhvavidhicoditā|

saṃkhyā saiva kalā mārge dīkṣāyāṃ gaditā purā|| 45||

evaṃ yo .anantavijaye ṣaḍadhvavidhidīkṣayā|

dīkṣitaḥ so .adhunā bandhādvicchinnastu bhavātmakāt|| 46||

subhāṣitaratnabhāṇḍāgāram
saptamaṃ saṅkīrṇakaprakaraṇaṃ

The text here is taken from the seventh book of the Subhāṣitaratnabhāṇḍāgāra, (ed.) Narayana Ram Acharya, Kavyatirtha, as volume 106 in the Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series (Varanasi: Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series Office, 1998). It is, of course, a reprint of the Nirnaya Sagar Press edition which went through a number of different editions, the last in 1952.

This last section appears to contain odds and ends that did not fit in easily or were missed in the preparation of the earlier sections, which are organized according to subject matter. References throughout SRB are not given consistently, and that is reflected in this edition, though we have made some attempt to place the references next to the verse for the convenience of the reader. However, there are inconsistencies in the numbering, due to the differences between the editions used by the original editor and myself. Here are some of the abbreviations used here:

In keeping with GGM's policy of quantity before quality, the text here has not been adequately proofread or corrected against original texts, etc. Nevertheless, it is, I believe, a useful addition for those interested in subhāṣita ("witty, epigrammatic, instructive and descriptive verses").
saptamaṃ saṅkīrṇakaprakaraṇaṃ

śīlaṃ pradhānaṃ puruṣe tad yasyeha praṇaśyati |
na tasya jīvitenārtho na dhanena na bandhubhiḥ ||1|| [ma.bhā. 5.34.46]

mahato hi kṣayaṃ labdhvā ślāghāṃ nīco'pi gacchati |
dānārthī madhupo yadvad gajakarṇasamāhataḥ ||2|| [bhāva. 31]

mahadbhiḥ spardhamānasya vipad eva garīyasī |
dantabhaṅgo hi nāgānāṃ ślāghyo girividāraṇe ||3|| [pañca. 1.404]

pañcāsyasya parābhavāya bhaṣako māṃsena gorbhūyasā |
dadhyannair api pāyasaiḥ pratidinaṃ saṃvardhito yo mayā ||4|| [?]

pañcendriyasya martyasya chidraṃ ced ekam indriyam |
tato'sya sravati prajñā dṛteḥ pātrād ivodakam ||5|| [ma.bhā. 5.2047]

pañcaiva pūjayan loke yaśaḥ prāpnoti kevalam |
devān pitèn manuṣyāṃś ca bhikṣūn atithipañcamān ||6|| [ma.bhā. 5.2045]

yannimittaṃ bhavec chokas trāso vā duḥkham eva vā |
āyāso vā yato mūlas tadekāṅgam api tyajet ||7|| [śā.pa. 1461]

yan niḥśabdajalā ghanāśmaparuṣe deśe'tighorāravā 
yac cācchāḥ samaye payodamaline kāluṣyasandūṣitāḥ |
dṛśyante kulanimnagā api paraṃ digdeśakālāv imau 
tat satyaṃ mahatām api svasadṛśācārapravṛttipradau ||8|| [rā.ta. 4.308]

yamo vaivasvatas tasya niryātayati duṣkṛtam |
hṛdi sthitaḥ karmasākṣī kṣetrajño yasya tuṣyati ||9|| [ma.bhā. 1.3018]

bhītavat saṃvidhātavyaṃ yāvad bhayam anāgatam |
āgataṃ tu bhayaṃ dṛṣṭvā prahartavyam abhītavat ||10|| [ma.bhā. 1.5622]

bhuñjānāḥ pavanaṃ sarīsṛpagaṇāḥ prakhyāpitā bhogino 
gāyadbhṛṅganivārakā nigaditā vistīrṇakarṇā gajāḥ |
yaś cābhyantarasambhṛtoṣmavikṛtiḥ proktaḥ śamī sa drumo 
lokeneti nirargalaṃ pralapatā sarvaṃ viparyāsitam ||11|| [rā.ta. 3.194]

pativratā patiprāṇā patyuḥ priyahite ratā |
yasya syād īdṛśī bhāryā dhanyaḥ sa puruṣo bhuvi ||12|| [pañca. 3.144]

saṃkṣepāt kathyate dharmo janāḥ kiṃ vistareṇa tu |
paropakāraḥ puṇyāya pāpāya parapīḍanam ||13|| [?]

śubhraṃ sadya savibhramā yuvatayaḥ śvetātapatrojjvalā 
lakṣmīr ity anubhūyate sthiram iva sphīte śubhe karmaṇi |
vicchinne nitarām anaṅgakalahakrīḍātruṭattantukaṃ
muktājālam iva prayāti jhaṭiti bhraśyaddiśo dṛśyatām ||14|| [bhartṛ. 1.95]

yan namraṃ saralaṃ cāpi yac cāpatsu na sīdati |
dhanur mitraṃ kalatraṃ ca durlabhaṃ śuddhavaṃśajam ||15|| [pañca. 2.188]

yan nave bhājane lagnaḥ saṃskāro nānyathā bhavet |
kathācchalena bālānāṃ nītis tad iha kathyate ||16|| [hi. 0.8]

yad apy uccair vijānīyān nīcais tad api kīrtayet |
karmaṇā tasya vaiśiṣṭyaṃ kathayed vinayānvitaḥ ||17|| [?]

sakṛj jalpanti rājānaḥ sakṛj jalpanti sādhavaḥ |
sakṛt kanyāḥ pradīyante trīṇy etāni sakṛt sakṛt ||18|| [ve. 34]
 
mukhaṃ prasannaṃ vimalā ca dṛṣṭiḥ 
kathānurāgo madhurā ca vāṇī |
sneho'dhikaḥ sambhramadarśanaṃ ca 
sadānuraktasya janasya cihnam ||19|| [hi. 2.59]

pañca tvānugamiṣyanti yatra yatra gamiṣyasi |
mitrāṇy amitrā madhyasthā upajīvyopajīvinaḥ ||20|| [ma.bhā. 5.1046]

pareṣām ātmanaś caiva yo'vicārya balābalam |
kāryāyottiṣṭhate mohād āpadaḥ sa samīhate ||21|| [pañca. 3.87]

balīyasā hīnabalo virodhaṃ 
na bhūtikāmo manasāpi vāñchet |
na vadhyate'tyantabalo hi yasmād 
vyaktaṃ praṇāśo'sti pataṅgavṛtteḥ ||22|| [pañca. 3.126]

balopapanno'pi hi buddhimān naraḥ 
paraṃ nayen na svayam eva vairitām |
bhiṣaṅ mamāstīti vicintya bhakṣayed 
akāraṇāt ko hi vicaksaṇo viṣam ||23|| [pañca. 3.111]

mitrasvajanabandhūnāṃ buddher dhairyasya cātmanaḥ |
āpannikaṣapāṣāṇe naro jānāti sāratām ||24|| [bho.pra. 156]

mitrāṇy eva hi rakṣanti mitravān nāvasīdati |
mitrād utpāditaṃ vairam api mūlaṃ nikṛntati ||25|| [rā. 4.20.18]

mitrārthe bāndhavārthe ca buddhimān yatate sadā |
jātāsv āpatsu yatnena jagādedaṃ vaco manuḥ ||26|| [pañca. 1.346]

pratyāsattiṃ madakaraṭino dānagandhena vāyur 
garjodbhūtiṃ prakaṭitaruciś cañcalevāmbudasya |
ceṣṭā spaṣṭaṃ vadati matimannaipuṇonneyatattvā 
jantor janmāntaraparicitāṃ niścalāṃ cittavṛttim ||27|| [rā.ta. 4.354]

prasādaḥ kurute patyuḥ sampattiṃ nābhijānatām |
kālimā kālakūṭasya nāpaiti harasaṅgamāt ||28|| [hi. 3.19]

prasādo niṣphalo yasya krodhaś cāpi nirarthakaḥ |
na taṃ bhartāram icchanti ṣaṇḍhaṃ patim iva striyaḥ ||29|| [bho.pra. 57|

prajāvṛddhaṃ dharmavṛddhaṃ svabandhuṃ 
vidyāvṛddhaṃ vayasā cāpi vṛddham |
kāryākārye pūjayitvā prasādya 
yaḥ sampṛcchen na sa muhyet kadācit ||30|| [ma.bhā. 5.155]

yathā grāmāntaraṃ gacchan naraḥ kaścit kvacid vaset |
utsṛjya ca tam āvāsaṃ pratiṣṭhetāpare'hani ||31||
evam eva manuṣyāṇāṃ pitā mātā gṛhaṃ vasu |
āvāsamātraṃ kākutstha sajjante nātra sajjanāḥ ||32|| [rā. 2.108.56]

yathā chāyātapau nityaṃ susambaddhau parasparam |
evaṃ karma ca kartā ca saṃśliṣṭāv itaretaram ||33|| [pañca. 2.136]

yathā phalānāṃ pakvānāṃ nānyasya patanād bhayam |
evaṃ narasya jātasya nānyatra maraṇād bhayam ||34|| [?]

yathā bījaṃ vinā kṣetram uptaṃ bhavati niṣphalam |
tathā puruṣakāreṇa vinā daivaṃ na sidhyati ||35|| [ma.bhā. 13.301]

yo'nyathā santam ātmānam anyathā satsu bhāṣate |
sa pāpakṛttamo loke tenātmāpahārakaḥ ||36|| [manu. 4.255]

śānte'nantamahimni nirmalacidānande taraṅgāvalī
nirmukte'mṛtasāgarāmbhasi manāṅ magno'pi nācāmati |
niḥsāre mṛgatṛṣṇikārṇavajale śrānto vimūḍhaḥ pibaty 
ācāmaty avāgahate'bhiramate majjaty athonmajjati ||37|| [?]

vaśaṃ prāpte mṛtyoḥ pitari tanaye vā suhṛdi vā 
śucālaṃ tapyante bhṛśam udaratāḍaṃ jaḍadhiyaḥ |
asāre saṃsāre virasapariṇāme tu viduṣāṃ
viyogo vairāgyaṃ draḍhayati vitanvan śamasukham ||38|| [pra. 95]

vidhir eva hi jāgarti bhavyānām arthasiddhaye |
asaṃcetayamānānāṃ sadbhṛtyāḥ svāminām iva ||39|| [?]

līlāyantyaḥ kulaṃ ghnanti kulānīva saridvarāḥ 
doṣān sarvāṃś ca matyāśu prajāpatir abhāṣata ||40|| [ma.bhā. 13.1475]

rāmo hemamṛgaṃ na vetti nahuṣo no yān yunakti dvijān 
viprād eva savatsadhenuharaṇe jātā matiś cārjune |
dyūte bhrātṛcatuṣṭayasvamahiṣīṃ dharmātmajo dattavān
prāyaḥ satpuruṣo vināśasamaye buddhyā parityajyate ||41|| [ve. 15]

purā vidvattāsīd amalinadhiyāṃ kleśahataye 
gatā kālenāsau viṣayasukhasiddhau viṣayiṇām |
idānīṃ samprekṣya kṣitilavabhujaḥ śāstravimukhān 
aho kaṣṭaṃ sāpi pratidinam adho'dhaḥ praviśati ||42|| [bhartṛ. 3.100]

rājā dharmavinā dvijaḥ śucivinā jñānaṃ vinā yoginaḥ 
kāntā satyavinā hayo gativinā jyotir vinā bhūṣaṇam |
yoddhā śūravinā tapo vratavinā chando vinā gāyanaṃ
bhrātā snehavinā naro harivinā muñcanti śīghraṃ budhāḥ ||43|| [saptaratna 2]

priyavacanakṛto'pi yoṣitāṃ 
dayitajanānunayo rasād ṛte |
praviśati hṛdayaṃ na tadvidāṃ 
maṇir iva kṛtrimarāgayojitaḥ ||44|| [?]

māno vā darpo vā vijñānaṃ vibhramaḥ subuddhir vā |
sarvaṃ praṇaśyati samaṃ vittavihīno yadā puruṣaḥ ||45||

parāṃ vinītaḥ samupaiti sevyatāṃ 
mahīpatīnāṃ vinayo vibhūṣaṇam |
pravṛttadāno mṛdusañcaratkaraḥ
karīva bhadro vinayena śobhate ||46|| [kā.nī. 1.65]

prīṇāti yaḥ sucaritaiḥ pitaraṃ sa putro
yad bhartur eva hitam icchati tat kalatram |
tan mitram āpadi sukhe ca samakriyaṃ
yad etat trayaṃ jagati puṇyakṛto labhante ||47|| [bhartṛ.saṃ. 279]

bhāryāyāṃ janitaṃ putram ādarśeṣv iva cānanam |
hlādate janitā prekṣya svargaṃ prāpyeva puṇyakṛt ||48|| [?]

hiṃsakāny api bhūtāni yo hinasti sa nirghṛṇaḥ |
sa yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ kiṃ punar yaḥ śubhāni ca ||49|| [pañca. 3.16]

sukhāsvādaparo yas tu saṃsāre satsamāgamaḥ |
sa viyogāvasānatvād duḥkhānāṃ dhuri yujyate ||50|| [hi. 4.77]

pūjyo bandhur api priyo'pi tanayo bhrātā vayasyo'pi vā 
mohād anavadyakāryavimukho heyaḥ sa kāryārthinā |
loke hi prathitā nanu śrutir iyaṃ nāryo'pi gāyanti yāṃ
kiṃ kāryaṃ kanakena tena bhavati cchedāya karṇasya yat ||51|| [pañca. 1.240]

sudhāśubhraṃ dhāma sphuradamalaraśmiḥ śaśadharaḥ 
priyāvaktrāmbhojaṃ malayajarajaś cātisurabhi | 
srajo hṛdyāmodās tad idam akhilaṃ rāgiṇi jane
karoty antaḥkṣobhaṃ na tu viṣayasaṃsargavimukhe ||52||

bhāryāvantaḥ kriyāvantaḥ sabhāryā gṛhamedhinaḥ |
bhāryāvantaḥ pramodante bhāryāvantaḥ śriyānvitāḥ ||53|| [ma.bhā. 1.3029]

mantre tīrthe dvije deve daivajñe bheṣaje gurau |
yādṛśī bhāvanā yasya siddhir bhavati tādṛśī ||54|| [vikrama. 64]

bāhū dvau ca mṛṇālam āsyakamalaṃ lāvaṇyalīlājalaṃ
śroṇī tīrthaśilā ca netraśapharaṃ dhammillaśaivālakam |
kāntāyāḥ stanacakravākayugalaṃ kandarpabāṇānalair
dagdhānām avagāhanāya vidhinā ramyaṃ saro nirmitam ||55|| [śṛ.ti. 1]

bhāryā putraś ca dāsaś ca traya evādhanāḥ smṛtāḥ |
yat te samadhigacchanti yasya te tasya tad dhanam ||56|| [manu. 8.416]

priyā hitāś ca ye rājño grāhyavākyā viśeṣataḥ |
āśrayet pārthivaṃ vidvāṃs taddvāreṇa nānyathā ||57|| [pañca. 1.32?]

bhavāraṇyaṃ bhīmaṃ tanugṛham idaṃ chidrabahulaṃ 
balī kālaś cauro niyatam asitā moharajanī |
gṛhītvā jñānāsi viratiphalakaṃ śīlakavacaṃ 
samādhānaṃ kṛtvā sthirataradṛśo jāgrata janāḥ ||58|| [?]

pratyupasthitakālasya sukhasya parivarjanam |
anāgatasukhāśā ca naiva buddhimatā nayaḥ ||59|| [ma.bhā. 12.5280]

prathamaṃ saṃsthitā bhāryā patiṃ pretya pratīkṣate |
pūrvaṃ mṛtaṃ ca bhartāraṃ paścāt sādyanugacchati ||60|| [ma.bhā. 1.3033]

prathame nārjitā vidyā dvitīye nārjitaṃ dhanam |
tṛtīye nārjitaṃ puṇyaṃ caturthe kiṃ kariṣyate ||61|| [cā.nī.sā. 93]

rāgī bimbādharo'sau stanakalaśayugaṃ yauvanārūḍhagarvaṃ 
nīcā nābhiḥ prakṛtyā kuṭilakamalakaṃ svalpakaṃ cāpi madhyam |
kurvantv etāni nāma prasabham iha manaś cintitāny āśu svedaṃ 
yan māṃ tasyā kapolau dahata iti muhuḥ svacchakau tan na yuktam ||62|| [pañca. 1.221]

yo nānādyutimatpadārtharasiko'sāre'pi śakrāyudhe 
sapremā sa vilokya barham iha me kiṃ kiṃ na kuryāt priyam |
ity āviṣkṛtabarharāji naṭate yo barhiṇo'mbholavān
nānyan muñcati taṃ vihāya jaladaṃ ko'nyo'sti śūnyāśayaḥ ||63|| [rā.ta. 3.218]

śarīram evāyatanaṃ sukhasya duḥkhasya cāpy āyatanaṃ śarīram |
yad yac charīreṇa karoti karma tenaiva dehī samupāśnute tat ||64|| [ma.bhā. 12.5478]

vinayena vinā kā śrīḥ kā niśā śaśinā vinā |
rahitā satkavitvena kīdṛśī vāgvidagdhatā ||65|| [?]

śaśinā saha yāti kaumudī saha meghena taḍit pralīyate |
pramadāḥ pativartmagā iti pratipannaṃ hi vicetanair api ||66|| [ku.saṃ. 4.33]

varaṃ maunaṃ kāryaṃ na ca vacanam uktaṃ yad anṛtaṃ 
varaṃ klaibyaṃ puṃsāṃ na ca parakalatrābhigamanam |
varaṃ prāṇatyāgo na ca piśunavākyeṣv abhirucir 
varaṃ bhikṣāśitvaṃ na ca paradhanāsvādanasukham ||67|| [hi. 1.137]

puro revāpāre giriratirādurohaśikharo 
dhanur bāṇaiḥ paścāc chabarakavaro dhāvati bhṛśam |
saraḥ savye'savye davadahanadāhavyatikaro
na gantuṃ na sthātuṃ hariṇaśiśur evaṃ vilapati ||68|| [nītiratna 5]

līlāvatīnāṃ sahajā vilāsās 
ta eva mūḍhasya hṛdi sphuranti |
rāgo nalinyā hi nisargasiddhas 
tatra bhramaty eva sudhā ṣaḍaṅghriḥ ||69|| [?]

buddhiś ca hīyate puṃsāṃ nīcaiḥ saha samāgamāt |
madhyamair madhyatāṃ yāti śreṣṭhatāṃ yāti cottamaiḥ ||70|| [ma.bhā. 3.30]

vistīrṇavyavasāyasādhyamahatāṃ snigdhaprayuktāśiṣāṃ
kāryāṇāṃ nayasāhasonnatimatām icchāpadārohiṇām |
mānotsekaparākramavyasaninaḥ pāraṃ na yāvadgatāḥ
sāmarṣe hṛdaye'vakāśaviṣayā tāvat kathaṃ nirvṛtiḥ ||71|| [pañca. 3.253]

loko vahati kiṃ rājan na mūrdhnā dagdhum indhanam |
kṣālayann api vṛkṣāṅghrīn nadīvego nikṛntati ||72|| [hi. 4.60]

varuṇena yathā pāśair baddha evābhidṛśyate |
tathā pāpān nigṛhṇīyād vratam etad dhi vāruṇam ||73|| [rā. 2.122.12]

lākṣālakṣma lalāṭāṭṭam abhitaḥ keyūramudrā gale
vakre kajjalakālimā nayanayos tāmbūlarāgodayaḥ |
dṛṣṭvā kopavidhāyi maṇḍanam idaṃ prātaś ciraṃ preyaso
līlātāmarasodare mṛdadṛśaḥ śvāsāḥ samāptiṃ gatāḥ ||74|| [amaru. 60]

lajjā snehaḥ svaramadhuratā buddhayo yauvanaśrīḥ
kāntāsaṅgo yajanasamatā duḥkhahānir vilāsaḥ |
dharmaḥ śāstraṃ suragurumatiḥ śaucam ācāracintā
pūrṇe sarve jaṭharapiṭhare prāṇināṃ sambhavanti ||75|| [pañca. 5.91]

puṇye grāme vane vā mahati sitapaṭacchannapālīkapālīm 
ādāya nyāyagarbhaṃ dvijahutahutabhugdhūmadhūmropakaṇṭham |
dvāraṃ dvāraṃ praviṣṭo daram udaradarīpūraṇāya kṣudhārto
mānī prāṇī sanātho na punar anudinaṃ tulyakulyeṣu dīnaḥ ||76|| [bhartṛ. 3.24]

adeśakālārtham anāyatikṣamaṃ 
yad apriyaṃ lāghavakāri cātmanaḥ |
vicintya buddhyā muhur apy avaimy ahaṃ 
na tadvaco hālāhalaṃ hi tad viṣam ||77|| [pañca. 3.112]

adṛṣṭapūrvān ādāya bhāvān apariśaṅkitān |
iṣṭāniṣṭān manuṣyāṇām astaṃ gacchanti rātrayaḥ ||78|| [ma.bhā. 12.12519]

satkṣetrapratipāditaḥ priyavacobaddhālavālāvalir
nirdoṣeṇa manaḥprasādapayasā niṣpannasekakriyaḥ |
dātus tat tad abhīpsitaṃ kila phalan kāle'ticālo'py asau
rājan dānamahīruho vijayate kalpadrumādīn api ||79|| [su. 3028]

atha nityam anityaṃ vā neha śocanti tadvidaḥ |

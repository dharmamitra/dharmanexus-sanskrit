athāgnau yajanaṃ puṇyaṃ dravyābhinayanaṃ tathā|

āpyāyanaṃ ca mantrāṇāṃ pravakṣyāmi yathākramam|| 1||

iṣṭvā devaṃ yathānyāyamāgneyyāṃ diśi vinyaset|

dvādaśāṅgulivistīrṇāṃ gāyatryarṇāṅgulāyatām|| 2||

araṇīṃ hyucchrayeṇoktāṃ hyaṅgulāni tathaiva ṣaṭ|

sarvadoṣavinirmuktāṃ śamīgarbhasamudbhavām|| 3||

gāyatrīmiti tāṃ dhyātvā tathaiva susaparyayā|

sarvātmānaṃ tathā tasyāṃ vinyaseduttarāraṇīm|| 4||

aṣṭāṅgulāgre jananīṃ kṣetraṃ syāddvādaśāṅgulam|

upilyā dvādaśa proktānyaṅgulāni na saṃśayaḥ|| 5||

vyāmamātreṇa yoktreṇa gopālatrivṛtena tu|

āyasena susannaddhameṣa yantravidhiḥ smṛtaḥ|| 6||

sarvaṃ sarvātmadevena parijaptamiheṣyate|

sarvātmānaṃ japedeva janayejjātavedasam|| 7||

jātaṃ pātre samādhāya kuṇḍe tu viniveśayet|

caturaśraṃ bhavetkuṇḍaṃ vṛttaṃ vā hastasammitam|| 8||

nitye naimittike caiva kāmye kalpapracoditam|

caturaśraṃ bhavet procyāmāgneyyāṃ tu bhagākṛtim|| 9||

yāmyāyāmardhacandraṃ ca nairṛtyāṃ ca trikoṇakam|

vṛttakuṇḍaṃ tu vāruṇyāṃ vāyavyāṃ pañcakoṇakam|| 10||

pañcākāraṃ hi saumyāyāmaiśānyāṃ saptakoṇakam|

iṣṭakābhiḥ supakvābhirapakvābhirasambhave|| 11||

kuryātkuṇḍaṃ tu śāstroktaṃ mantrahomānurūpataḥ|

mantrāṇāṃ vāruṇānāṃ tu kumbhākāraṃ vidhīyate|| 12||

vāyavyānāṃ patākeva mahendrāṇāṃ tu vajravat|

saptajihvākṛtiḥ proktamāgneyyānāṃ gurūttamaiḥ|| 13||

madhyamottamavīryāṇāṃm caturaśraṃ phalapradam|

trikoṇamalpavīryāṇāṃ strīrūpāṇāṃ bhagākṛtiḥ|| 14||

ardhacandraṃ tu raudrāṇāṃ saumyānāṃ vṛttameva hi|

pañcakoṇaṃ ca dūtīnāṃ kinnarāṇāṃ tathaiva ca|| 15||

vidyāvidyeśvarāṇāṃ tu padmākāramihottamam|

śate śatādhike home dṛṣṭiratnipramāṇataḥ|| 16||

sāhasrake tu kartavyaṃ samantāddhastasammitam|

daśasāhasrike home dvihastaṃ sarvataḥ samam|| 17||

tato lakṣamite mantrī caturhastaṃ prakalpayet|

koṭihome paraṃ śreṣṭhamaṣṭahastapramāṇataḥ|| 18||

kuryātkuṇḍavidhānajñaḥ pakveṣṭakacitaṃ samam|

sarveṣāmeva mantrāṇāṃ vṛttaṃ syātsārvakāmikam|| 19||

tena vṛttaṃ prakurvanti vidhau śāstravido janāḥ|

muṣṭiratnipramāṇe tu piṇḍikrā dvyaṅgulā smṛtā|| 20||

hastamānasamāne ca syādasau caturaṅgulam|

ṣaḍaṅgulā dvihastānāmaṣṭau taddviguṇātmanām|| 21||

aṣṭahastapramāṇānāṃ smṛtā sā dvādaśāṅgulā|

piṇḍikārghena dairghyaṃ syādyonistārā hyaṃśakaḥ ??|| 22||

sarveṣāmeva kuṇḍānāmiti śrutinidarśanam|

athavā pārthivaṃ kuṇḍaṃ pācayeddṛḍhamavraṇam|| 23||

sadyādyaiḥ brahmabhiḥ kuryāt hastamātrapramāṇataḥ|

catuṣpathe nadītīre girau govṛṣaśṛṅgataḥ|| 24||

taṭākatīre kedāre kuśamūle mṛdaḥ śubhāḥ|

śmaśānabilavalmīkacoṣarāmṛdaśobhanā|| 25|| ??

aghorāstreṇa saṃśuddhāṃ paṭena ca pariṣkṛtām|

kaṣāyodakasaṃklinnāṃ triphalācūrṇasaṃyutām|| 26||

gandhai rasaiśca sammiśrāṃ bilvāmbupariṣecitām|

rukmagairikasindūratālāñjanaśilānvitām|| 27||

iti saṃhṛtya sadyena vāmadevena kalpayet|

ādīpayedaghoreṇa vanyagomayaveṣṭitām|| 28||

īśānena tu gṛhṇīyāt prokṣitāṃ puruṣeṇa tu|

tatastāṃ hṛdayenaiva khātvā bhūmau vidhāpayet|| 29||

yatnena pācayetkuṇḍaṃ yathā lohitakaṃ bhavet|

apakvaṃ vā prakurvīta sudṛḍhaṃ hi manoramam|| 30||

ullekhanaṃ tu kartavyaṃ aghorāstreṇa madhyataḥ|

samidagreṇa pūrvāgramuttarāgramathāpi vā|| 31||

tisraḥ pūrvamukhā rekhā rekhaikā cottarāmukhā|

vākyārthapratilomeva bhavedekārtha eva hi|| 32||

aṅguṣṭhānāmikāgreṇa kṛtvā karaṇameva ca|

nikuṭṭanaṃ ca dattvā tu prokṣyet kuśavāriṇā|| 33||

catuṣpathamathāstīrya gāyatrīṃ viṣṭare nyaset|

 dyenagāyatryāmaraṇyagniṃ niveśayet|

sūryakāntodbhavaṃ vāpi dvijādevāgṛhotthitā|| 35||

astreṇa śodhitāvetau pañcabrahmābhimantritau|

garbhādhānavidheryogyāvanukalpavidhitvataḥ|| 36||

gāyatryā nihitaṃ darbhaiḥ sadyenāgniṃ tu cchādayet|

tato vidyādhipenāsya dadyādarghyādikaṃ kramāt|| 37||

garbhādhāne kṛte tvevaṃ deyaṃ puṃsavanaṃ jalam|

vāmadevena mantreṇa rudrāṇyā ca tato .arcanam|| 38||

sīmante garbhasīmante kṛtvāghoreṇa mantravit|

tato vai brahmaśirasā pūjākarma samācaret|| 39||

jātakarmāṇi sandīpya puruṣeṇa vidhānataḥ|

arghyaṃ pāśupatāstreṇa dadyādgandhādikaṃ kramāt|| 40||

īśānena ca puṣpaṇi dattvā nāmavidhau tataḥ|

śivasaṃsparśanaṃ kuryātkavacena ca pūjanam|| 41||

śivāgnistu bhavedevaṃ pañcasaṃskārasaṃskṛtaḥ|

dīkṣāyāṃ naityake caiva kāmye .api ca vidhau param|| 42||

tataścāstaraṇaṃ kuryātkuṇḍasya paritaḥ kuśaiḥ|

viṣṭarāṇāṃ niveśaṃ ca paridhīnāṃ tathaiva ca|| 43||

hṛdayena śivotthena sarvamantrānuvartinā|

sūpādīnāṃ ca saṃskāramājyasya ca tathaiva ca|| 44||

brahmaviṣṇupadotthaiśca mantrairbrahmādikānnyaset|

krameṇa vidhivatpaścātsruvasaṃskāramācaret|| 45||

vaikaṅkatamayo vātha khādiro vā sruvastataḥ|

aṅguṣṭhaparvavatsthūlo gāyatryarṇāṅgulāyataḥ|| 46||

aṅguṣṭhadvayavistīrṇakaṣāpūrakaviṣkaraḥ|

hastena vidhṛtaścāsau manaḥ prītikaraḥ paraḥ|| 47||

ṣaṭtriṃśadaṅgulāyāmā daṇḍanāha ṣaḍaṅgulān|

saptāṅgulāgrā ṣaḍvṛttapuṣkārābhogamaṇḍalā|| 48||

agrapuṣkarayormadhye sukṛtāṅgulaśobhanā|

puṣkarasthacatuṣkoṇāttanmānāṅgulasaṃsthitā|| 49||

khātaṃ cātraṃ mukhaṃ caiva dvyaṅgulaṃ kīrtitaṃ srucaḥ|

dviraṣṭakaṃ sruco nālaṃ samūlaṃ viṃśatiḥ param|| 50||

pratāpyau sruksruvāvagnau acchināgrakuśaiḥ spṛśet|

agramagreṇa madhyaṃ ca mūlaṃ mūlena ca kramāt|| 51||

niṣkṛtyaivaṃ puroktena mantreṇaiva vidhānavit|

tato .adhiśrayaṇaṃ kuryātparyagnikaraṇaṃ tataḥ|| 52||

pavitrīkaraṇaṃ paścāttathā cotplavanaṃ tataḥ|

kuryātsaṃplavanaṃ darbhairavadyotanapaścimam|| 53||

prokṣaṇaṃ ca pavitreṇa kṛtvāghoreṇa homayet|

sadyena vāmadevena bahurūpeṇa cakṣuṣi|| 54||

vaktraṃ vaktreṇa mūrdhānamīśānena tataḥ punaḥ|

homayedbahurūpeṇa śaktito .agnestadāhutim|| 55||

evaṃ sarvamidaṃ kṛtvā vidhānamanalodbhavam|

paścātsantarpayeddevamagnau saṃsthāpya pūrvavat|| 56||

agnervarṇāṃśca gandhāṃśca śikhāḥ śabdāṃśca lakṣayet|

siddhyasiddhyorabhivyaktyai bhāvitenāntarātmanā|| 57||

taptakāñcanavaiḍūryapadmarāgendracāpavat|

bandhūkadāḍimīpuṣpaharitālendrakopavat|| 58||

udaye sūryasaṃkāśaḥ kāśmīrajalajadyutiḥ|

nityamūrdhvagamocchiṣṭān sampiṇḍitaśikhaḥ śubhaḥ|| 59||

snigdhaḥ pradakṣiṇāvarta śrutipracchāditadhvaniḥ|

kṣīrājyabilvahome ca yo bhavatyatha gandhavān|| 60||

durgandhaḥ kṛṣṇavarṇāśca nīlaḥ pāṇḍara eva ca|

kuśabdo .atha mumorūkṣo nistejā bhayado .aśubhaḥ|| 61||

apradīptena hotavyaṃ sadhūme cāpyanindhane|

pradīpte lelihāne ca homayetkarmasiddhaye|| 62||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe agnikāryapaṭalaḥ ekonaviṃśatitamaḥ||
iti śrī kriyākāṇḍaḥ samāptaḥ||

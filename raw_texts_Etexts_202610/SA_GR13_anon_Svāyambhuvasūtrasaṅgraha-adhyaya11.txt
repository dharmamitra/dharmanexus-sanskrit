atha yāgaprasiddhyarthaṃ brahmāvartādikaṃ bhajet|

deśaṃ bhūdevasañjuṣṭaṃ samitpuṣpodakottamam|| 1||

dharmaniṣṭhaprapākrānataṃ varṇānāmanupadravam|

mṛtkuśāḍhyauṣadhīgāḍhamanāstikamaninditam|| 2||

śaradvasantayoryogo dīkṣākarmavidhau varaḥ|

tayorasambhave varṣā vinānyatrāpi śasyate|| 3||

dīkṣāyāmabhiṣeke ca tathā mantraparigrahe|

vratagrahaṇamokṣe ca dravyālambhanakarmaṇi|| 4||

kārtikyāṃ caiva vaiśākhyāṃ svarbhānorapi darśane|

ayane viṣuve krāntau saṃsthitau ca guroryajet|| 5||

mahendre malaye sahye śrīśaile himavadgirau|

pāriyātrerbude vindhye śuktimadrakṣaparvate|| 6||

gaṅgāyāṃ yamunāyāṃ ca tayorantara eva ca|

sarasvatyāṃ dṛṣadvatyāṃ godāvaryāmapāṃ nidhau|| 7||

narmadā kṛṣṇavarṇāyāṃ sandhau pañcanade tathā|

tuṅgabhadropakaṇṭhe ca candrabhāgopakaṇṭhataḥ|| 8||

guhyāyatanatīrtheṣu nadīnāṃ saṅgameṣu ca|

bhavaneṣu ca ramyeṣu vaneṣūpavaneṣu ca|| 9||

bhūmiṃ tatra parīkṣeta nimnonnatavivarjitām|

anūṣarāmasaṃklinnāṃ tyaktakardamavālukām|| 10||

tuṣakeśāsthirahitāṃ bhasmāṅgāravivarjitām|

duṣṭaśalyavinirmuktāṃ snigdhāṃ bhāravatīṃ dṛḍhām|| 11||

śūrpādyākṛtinirmuktāṃ prāgudakpravaṇāṃ mahīm|

śodhitāṃ karaśākhena karakhātaparīkṣitām|| 12||

parṇavarṇādyavikalāṃ bhūmiṃ kāmaphalapradām|

manaḥ prītikarīṃ muktyai siddhyai vāpi yathoditām|| 13||

khānitāṃ paramāstreṇa bhūpadena ca pūritām|

pañcagavyena saṃśuddhāṃ kaṣāyodakasevitām|| 14||

darpaṇodarasaṅkāśāṃ sarvatra supariśritām|

ūrvīmevaṃvidhāṃ prāpya yajenmaṇḍalamuttamam|| 15||

paurṇamāsyaṣṭamī śuklā tathā caiva caturdaśī|

bhūtidīkṣāsu tithayaḥ sakṛṣṇā muktikarmasu|| 16||

dinaścidrādyayuktāśca ye cānye tithayaḥ smṛtāḥ|

te .api yāgāṅgabhāvitvāt saṅgrāhyā yāgakarmaṇi|| 17||

trīṇyuttarāṇi re  saumyaṃ ca gurudaivatam|

maitraṃ sauraṃ ca nakṣatraṃ pāvanaṃ pitryameva ca|| 18||

nairṛtaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ brāhmaṃ tathā pūṣādhidaivatam|

etāni śivayāgeṣu praśastāni caturdaśa|| 19||

nāmnaivāvoditānyogāt ekādaśa parityajet|

śeṣeṣu prārabhedyāgaṃ yo  samāhitaḥ|| 20||

yāgabhūmiṃ viśuddhāṃ ca vitānācchāditāmbarām|

abhūgatagṛhītena gomayenopaleyapayet|| 21||

sarvopahārasaṃyuktaḥ snātaḥ śuklāmbaraḥ śuciḥ|

soṣṇīṣaḥ sāṅgulīyaśca sakalīkṛtavigrahaḥ|| 22||

candanāliptagātraśca sragābharaṇabhūṣitaḥ|

vighānuccāṭayenmantrī yāgabhūmau vyavasthitān|| 23||

śivāmbhasā tu samprokṣya pañcagavyena tāntataḥ|

aghorāstrābhijaptena secayedgandhavāriṇā|| 24||

puṣpākṣatatilairbhūmimavatīrya śivaṃ yajet|

homaṃ kuryāttato vidvān śivamantragaṇena tu|| 25||

adhivāse kṛte tvevaṃ bhūmyagnyoḥ samparigrahe|

dvitīye .ahani devasya tṛtīye vātha kārayet|| 26||

iti svāyambhuvasūtrasaṅgrahe deśikādideśanirdeśapaṭala ekādaśa||

vasiṣṭhaviśvāmitrau/ jāmadagnya iṣṭāpūrtavidheḥ sapatnaśamanātpreyān maghonaḥ sakhā yena dyauriva vajriṇā vasumatī  vīreṇa rājanvatī/

yasyaite vayamagrataḥ kimaparaṃ vaṃśaśca vaivasvataḥ so'yaṃ tvāṃ tanayapriyaḥ pariṇato rājā svayaṃ yācate//1//

tad vīra virama śuṣkakalahāt/ idaṃ tv astu/

sañjñapyate vatsatarī sarpiṣyannaṃ ca pacyate/ 
śrotriya śrotriyagṛhānāgato'si juṣasva naḥ//2//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ atra vo vijñāpayāmi kiṃ na kṣame/ yadi rāmaḥ prakṛṣṭavīryo na syāt paśyantu bhavantaḥ/

rāmaḥ karmabhiradbhutaiḥ śiśurapi kyātastato bhārgavaḥ kasmāt prāpya tiraskriyāmasahano'pyasthāditi prastute/

ko vidyād gurūgauravāditi bhavej jñātāpi vaktā  punar na tvevāsti yathāsthitasya sulabhadveṣaṃ hi vīravratam//3//

api ca/

yaśasi niravakāśe viśvataḥ  śvetamāne kathamapi vacanīyaṃ prāpya yatkiñcideva/

kṛtavitatirakasmāt prākṛtairuttamānāṃ viramati na kathañcit kaśmalā kiṃvadantī//4//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ ayi vatsa kimanayā yāvajjīvamāyudhapiśācikayā/

śrotriyo'si jāmadagnya/ pūtaṃ bhajasva panthānamāraṇyakaścāsi/

tat pracinu cittaprasādanīścatasro maitryādibhāvanāḥ/ prasīdatu hi te viśokā jyotiṣmatī nāma cittavṛttiḥ samāpayatu paraśuṃ ca/ tatprasādajamṛtambharābhidhānamabahiḥ sādhanopādheyasarvārthasāmarthyamapaviddhaviplavoparāgamūrjasvalamantarjyotiṣo darśanaṃ prajñānamabhisambhavati/ taddhyācaritavyaṃ brāhmaṇena tarati yena mṛtyaṃ pāpmānam/ anyatra cābhiniviṣṭo'si/ paśya pariṣadiyamṛṣīṇāmeṣa vṛddho yudhājit saha nṛpatiramātyairlomapādaśca vṛddhaḥ/

ayamaviratayajño brahmavādī purāṇaḥ prabhurapi janakānāmadruho yācakāste//5//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ evametat/ kintu śatrumūlamanutkhāya na punardraṣṭumutsahe/

tryambakaṃ devamācāryamācāryānīṃ ca pārvatīm//6//

viśvāmitraḥ/ yadi gurūṣvanurudhyase cetayasva māmapi tataḥ/ kiñca hiraṇyagarbhādṛṣayo babhūvurvasiṣṭhabhṛgvaṅgirasastrayo ye/

yo'yaṃ vasiṣṭho bhṛgunandanastvamayaṃ hi tasyāṅgirasaḥ prapautraḥ//7//

jāmadagnyaḥ/

prāyaścittaṃ cariṣyāmi pūjyānāṃ vo vyatikramāt/

na tvevaṃ dūṣayiṣyāmi śastragrahamahāvratam//8//

tathā hi/

yato vimukterapi mānarakṣaṇaṃ priyaṃ nisargeṇa tathā ca paśya me/

sanābhayo yūyamayaṃ ca karkaśaḥ śarāsanajyākiṇalāñchano bhujaḥ//9//

viśvāmitraḥ/ 
(svagatam/)

sampūjitaṃ hi māhātmyamudgirantyaḥ pade pade/

api marmāvidho vācaḥ satyaṃ vismāyayanti mām//10//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ api ca bhagavan kuśikanandana/

brahmaikatānamanaso hi vasiṣṭhamiśrāstvaṃ brūhi vīracariteṣu guruḥ purāṇaḥ/

vaṃśe viśuddhimati yena bhṛgorjanitvā śastraṃ gṛhītamatha tasya yadatra yuktama[m]//11//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ 
(svagatam/)

kāmaṃ guṇairmahāneṣa prakṛtyā punarāsuraḥ/

utkarṣāt sarvatovṛtteḥ sarvākāraṃ hi dda[dṛ]pyati//12//

viśvāmitraḥ/ vatsa etad bravīmi/

ekavyaktyaparādhakopavikṛtastvaṃ kṣatrajāterapi prāgādhānaniranvayapramathanāducchedamevākaroḥ/

triḥsaptāvadhi vipraśukrajamapi kṣatraṃ tadevoddharan vṛddhaiḥ svaiścyavanādibhirniyamitaḥ krodhādvyaraṃsīriti//13//

jāmadagnyaṃ/ vyaraṃsiṣameva pitṛvadhaprayuktāt kṣatravadhamahādhikārāt/

kimatra nihnavaḥ/

paraśuraśanikhaṇḍaḥ kṣatraghātaṃ vihāya priyamapi samididhmavraścanaḥ kiṃ na jātaḥ/

nibhṛtaviśikhadaṃṣṭraścāpadaṇḍo'pi dhatte vigalitaviṣavahneḥ sāmyamāśīviṣasya//14//

evaṃ mayā niyamitaścyavanādivākyaiḥ kopānalaśca paraśuśca punaryathā taiḥ/

kālena pārthivakulaiḥ kakubho mahadbhirdagdhotthitairiva vanairgahanāḥ kriyante//15//

nimittāntarāttu śīrṣacchedyo rāma iti/ athedānīm etasya rāghavaśiśoḥ kṛtacāpalasya lūtvā śiro mayi vanāya punaḥ prayāte/

svasthāścirāya raghavo janakāśca santu mā bhūt punarvata kathañcidatiprasaṅgaḥ//16//

śatānandaḥ/ āḥ śaktirasti kasyacid videharājasya rājarṣeryājyasya me preyasaśchāyāmapyavaskanditum/ kimaṅga jāmātaram/

vayamiha yathā gṛhyo vahnistathaiva ciraṃ sthitāḥ sucaritagurustambhādhāre gṛhe gṛhamedhinaḥ/

yadi paribhavastatrānyasmādupaiti dhigastu naḥ priyamapi tato dhig brāhmaṇyaṃ dhigaṅgirasaḥ kulam//17//

viśvāmitraḥ/ sādhu vatsa gautama sādhu/ kṛtakṛtya eṣa rājā sīradhvajastvayā purohitena/

na tasya rāṣṭraṃ vyathate na bhraśyati na jīryati/

tvaṃ vidvān brāhmaṇo yasya rāṣṭragopaḥ purohitaḥ//18//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ gautama tvayeva bahubhiḥ kṣatriyapurohitairbrahmatejasā sphuritamāsīt/ kintu prākṛtāni tejāṃsyaprākṛte jyotiṣi praśamyanti//

śatānandaḥ/ 
(sakrodham/) are anaḍvan/ niraparādharājanyakulakadanamahāpātakin/ aśiṣṭavikṛtaceṣṭa/ bībhatsakarman/ apūrvapāṣaṇḍa/ kāṇḍīra/ kāṇḍapṛṣṭha/ kathamasyāmapi diśi pragalbhase/ nanu ca re tvamasi kiṃ brāhmaṇa eva/ aho mahābrāhmaṇasyācāraḥ/

mātureva śiraśchedo garbhāṇāṃ cāpakartanam/

rājñāṃ ca savanasthānāṃ brahmahatyāsamo vadhaḥ//19//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ āḥ svastivācanika duṣṭa/ sāmantapurohita/ api ca re ahalyāyāḥ putra/ ahaṃ tava kāṇḍapṛṣṭhaḥ//

śatānandaḥ/ āḥ duṣṭa durmukha/ bhṛguprasavapāṃsana/

rājāno guravaścaite mahimnaiva mahākṣamāḥ/

kṣamantāṃ nāma na tvevaṃ śatānandaḥ kṣamiṣyate //20//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ kaḥ ko'tra bhoḥ/ prasādyatāṃ prasādyatāmayaṃ dhavitranirdhūta ivāgniḥ praṇītapṛṣadājyābhighāraghorastanūnapāt samidhyamānadāruṇabrahmavarcasajyotirāṅgirasaḥ//

śatānandaḥ/ 
(sasaṃrambham/ śāpodakaṃ gṛhītvā/) bho bhoḥ sabhāsadaḥ/ paśyantu bhavantaḥ/

sakrodhaḥ prasabhamahaṃ parābhighātādudbhūya drutataramātatāyinaṃ vaḥ/

utpātakṣubhitamarudbighaṭyamāno vajrāgnirdraumamiva bhasmasātkaromi//21//

(nepathye/) prasīda bhagavan/ gṛhānupagate praśāmyatu durāsadaṃ tapastejaḥ/

ślāghyo guṇairdvijavaraśca nijaśca bandhustasmin gṛhānupagate sadṛśaṃ kimetat/

vidvānapi pracalitastu yadeṣa mārgāt kṣātraṃ hi tatra vijayāya śamaṃ bhaja tvam//22//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ 
(śāpodakamapaharan/) vatsa śatānanda/ yathāha sambandhī mahārājadaśarathaḥ/ anyacca/

yatkalyāṇaṃ kimapi manasā tadvayaṃ vartayāmastvaṃ jābāliprabhṛtisahitaḥ śāntimadhyagni kuryāḥ/

jetuṃ jaitrānatha khalu japan sūktasāmānuvākān asmacchiṣyaiḥ saha sa bhagavān vāmadevo vṛṇotu//23//

(pariṣvajya niṣkrāmayati//)

jāmadagnyaḥ/ paśyata vaṭoḥ kṣatriyāvaṣṭabdhasya garjitāni/ tat kimanena/ bho bhoḥ kosaleśvaravidehaprasādopajīvino brāhmaṇāḥ saptakulaparvatadvīpagocarāśca sarvakṣatriyāḥ vadāmaḥ/

tapo vā śastraṃ vā vyapadiśati yaḥ kaścidiha vaḥ sa darpāduddāmadviṣamasahamānaḥ kalayatu/

arāmāṃ niḥsīradhvajadaśarathīkṛtya jagatīmatṛptastatkulyānapi paraśurāmaḥ śamayati//24//

(nepathye/) bhārgava ati hi nāmāvalipyase//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ asūyati nāmāyamasmadavalepāya janakaṃ saṃrabdhaśca//

(praviśya janakaḥ/)

śatrudhvaṃsātpariṇativaśād gṛhyatantrapradhāne nairantaryādatha ca paramabrahmatattvopalambhāt/

kṣātraṃ tejo vijayi sahajaṃ yadvyaraṃsīdidaṃ tat pratyudbhūya tvarayati punaḥ karmaṇe kārmukaṃ naḥ//25//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ bho janaka/

tvaṃ brahmaṇyaḥ kila pariṇataścāsi dharmeṇa yuktas& tvāṃ vedānteṣvacarama ḷṣiḥ sūryaśiṣyaḥ śaśāsa/

ityācārādasi yadi mayā praśrayeṇopajuṣṭas&tatkiṃ mohādaviditabhayaḥ karkaśāni bravīṣi//26//

janakaḥ/ aṇḍabhedanaṃ kriyate praśrayaśceti/ śṛṇuta bhoḥ sabhāsadaḥ/

bhṛgorvaṃśe jātastapasi ca kilāyaṃ sthita iti dviṣatyapyasmābhiściramiha titikṣaiva hi kṛtā/

yadā bhūyobhūyastṛṇavadavadhūnotyanibhṛtas&tadā vipre'pyasminnamatu dhanuranyāsti na gatiḥ//27//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ 
(saroṣahāsākṣepam/) kimāttha bhoḥ kimāttha dhanuriti/ āścaryam/

kṣatrālokakṣubhitahutabhukprasphuliṅgāṭṭahāsaṃ hāsyaḥ paśyannapi ripuśiraḥśāṇaśātaṃ kuṭhāram/

dattotsekaḥ pralapati mayā yājñavalkyānurodhān&mithyādhmātaḥ kimapi jarasā jarjaraḥ kṣatrabandhuḥ//28//

(nepathye/) virama narapate kathaṃ dvije'smin&naviratayajñavitīrṇagosahasraḥ/

tava palitanirantaraḥ pṛṣatkaṃ spṛśati purāṇadhanurdharasya pāṇiḥ//30//

janakaḥ/ sakhe mahārāja daśaratha/

asmānadhikṣipatu nāma na kiñcidetat kasya dvije paruṣavādini cittabhadeḥ[bhedaḥ]/

vatsasya maṅgalaviruddhamayaṃ tu pāpaḥ karṇe raṭan kaṭu kathaṃ nu vaṭurviṣahyaḥ//31//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ āḥ durātman kṣatriyāpasada vaṭurityadhikṣipasi/

uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha yāvadviśakalitayakṛtklomavakṣoruhāntras&nāyugranthyasthiśukravyatikaritajaratkandharādantakaṇṭhaḥ/

(praviśyāntare daśarathaḥ/) bho bhārgava/

eṣa no narapatiryathā sthitaḥ svaṃ śarīramapi na sthitaṃ tathā/

tatra vākparibhavaiḥ kṛtairvayaṃ sarvathaiva nanu duḥkhamāsmahe//33//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ tataḥ kim/

daśarathaḥ/ tataśca na kṣamyate//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ tvamapyaparaḥ prabhaviṣṇuriva māmavaskandayasi/ cetayasva nityaniravagrahaḥ prakṛtyaiva rāmo'smi jāmadagnyaḥ kṣatriyaśca bhavān//

daśarathaḥ/ ataḥ khalu nopekṣyase/

durdāntānāṃ damanavidhayaḥ kṣatriyeṣvāyatante durdāntastvaṃ vayamapi ca te kṣatriyāḥ śāsitāraḥ/

sadyaḥ śānto bhava kimaparaṃ damyase so'dhunaiva kva brahmāṇaḥ praśamanaparāḥ kṣatradhāryaṃ kva cāstram//34//

jāmadagnyaḥ/ 
(vihasya/) cirasya khalu kālasya jāmadagnyaḥ sanātho vartate yasya yūyaṃ kṣatriyā rājāno vinetāraḥ//

daśarathaḥ/ are kimatra kācid bhrāntiḥ/

ajño vā yadi vā viparyayagato jñāne'tha sandehabhṛd dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavirodhi karma kurute yastasya goptā guruḥ/

nissandehaviparyaye sati punarjñāne viruddhakriyaṃ rājā cetpuruṣaṃ na śāsti tadayaṃ prāptaḥ prajāviplavaḥ//35//

viśvāmitraḥ/ yuktamāha bho mahārājaḥ/

anutpannaṃ jñānaṃ yadi yadi ca sandehavidhuraṃ viparyastaṃ vā syāt paricara vasiṣṭhasya caraṇau/

dhruvaṃ jñāne doṣaḥ kathamaparathā durvyavahṛtir&viśuddhau cet pāpaṃ carasi na sahante nṛpatayaḥ//36//

jāmadagnyaḥ/

dharme brahmaṇi kārmuke ca bhagavānīśo hi me śāsitā sarvakṣatranibarhaṇasya vinayaṃ kuryuḥ kathaṃ kṣatriyāḥ/

sambandhastu vasiṣṭhamiśraviṣaye mānyo jarāyāṃ na tu spardhāyāmadhikaḥ samaśca tapasā jñānena cānyo'sti me//37//

vasiṣṭhaḥ/ bhṛguprasavāt parājaya iti priyaṃ naḥ/ kiṃ tu khalu asmābhireva pālyasya praśastatvātpriyasya naḥ/

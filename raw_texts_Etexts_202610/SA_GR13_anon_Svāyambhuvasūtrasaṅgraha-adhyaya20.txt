atha yogaprasiddhyarthaṃ niḥśabdaṃ sthāmāśrayet|

suguptaṃ yogināṃ sevyamakīlitamarakṣitam|| 1||

tatra yogāsanaṃ baddhvā vitatyorasthaṃ tataḥ|

āharedindriyārthebhyo .akṣeśvaraṃ hṛtsamudrake|| 2||

nāsāvaṃśāgradṛṣṭiḥ san citte vyāpakasaṃsthite|

recakādiprabhedena prāṇāyāmān ṣaḍācaret|| 3||

āgneyīṃ dhāraṇāṃ paścāt dhārayennābhimaṇḍale|

yayā dahati pāpāni śṛṅgakāryāṇi susthiteḥ|| 4||

hṛdaye dhārayetsaumyāṃ sadā somasamāśraye|

āpyāyayati sarvatra yayā yogapade sthitaḥ|| 5||

dhārayenmūrdhanīśānīṃ sarveśānīṃ vicakṣaṇaḥ|

yayā tu yoginaḥ sarve prayāti paramaṃ padam|| 6||

amṛtādhāraṇā tu syātsā sarvatra vyavasthitā|

vibhūyātvāṃ tathaivātra dharmajñānāmṛtapradām|| 7||

pāpapaṅkānulipto vā śītenābihatenāpi vā|

kumbhakastho .abhyasedbījaṃ dhārayedvahnidhāraṇam|| 8||

saṃhāraṃ vahnivāyusthaṃ caturthena pradīpitam|

āgneyaṃ dhāraṇābījaṃ prajvalantaṃ vicintayet|| 9||

śoṣatiyvā paraṃ toyamagninānena nābhitaḥ|

utthitena tataścaivaṃ pare .agnau sanniyojayet|| 10||

tadarcirmukhasamprīte hṛdisthe somamaṇḍale|

kāyamāpūrayettena nirvighnena nirantaram|| 11||

uddhūmaraśmijālena romakūpaprapūritam|

prapātayedanekarṇāstatastadvaktrakoṭibhiḥ|| 12||

tenāpūrya jagatsarvamuddhātānte pratīyate|

nāśayetsarvapāpāni pareṣāmātmano .api vā|| 13||

nivīcīkaraṇe caiva himasya ca vināśane|

nāgoddhāre ripūtsāde dhāraṇāṃ dhārayedimām|| 14||

vāruṇasthaṃ dvitīyena pūrvoktaṃ tu prabhedayet|

susaumyā dhāraṇā bījaṃ pūrvavadbindusaṃyutam|| 15||

idamabhyasanaṃ dhyāyedidameva tvadhomukham|

hṛdayādvāruṇe yāvadgranthiṃ granthīśabheditam|| 16||

tasmādadbhiḥ suśubhrābhiḥ pare .agnau suvināśite|

some copabitekābhiḥ somaṃ dhyāyetsunirmalam|| 17||

tasya raśmimukhaiḥ śuddhairadho ninnābhimaṇḍale|

nāśayitvā paraṃ toyaṃ paraṃ toyaṃ niveśayet|| 18||

ekībhūte tatastoye kāyamāpūrya pūrvavat|

niḥsṛte romakūpebhyaḥ pūrayeta jaganmahat|| 19||

evaṃ dhyātvā caturthe syāduddhātānte sunirmalaḥ|

yogīva yogayogyaḥ syāddhāraṇāsu kṛtaśramaḥ|| 20||

dhārayeddhāraṇāṃ saumyāmāpyāyanavidhau paraḥ|

viṣāpaharaṇe tāpe sarvasmin bījamāśritaḥ|| 21||

saṃhāraṃ vahnivāyusthaṃ pauruṣeyasamanvitam|

ṣaṣṭhena dīpitaṃ dhyāyedīśānī dhāraṇā smṛtā|| 22||

āgneyadhāraṇāśuddhaṃ kāyamāpūrya vāyunā|

aṅguṣṭhāgrātsamākṛṣya dhārayenmūrdhani kramāt|| 23||

tato nāsāpuṭenaiva recayedaniśaṃ śanaiḥ|

yāyādvā brahmamārgeṇaiva brahmārpitamanāḥ param|| 24||

amṛtāṃ vyāpakenaiva vyāpinīṃ dhārayetsadā|

yasyāṃ vidhīyamānāyāṃ yogo jñānaṃ ca jñāyate|| 25||

śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśamātmānaṃ ca jagattathā|

ātmasaprabhavaṃ dhyāyettaptaṃ śāntatvamāgatam|| 26||

etāsu yogamārgasthaḥ parāyāścittasaṃsthiteḥ|

padāni pratyanīkāni pravṛtto vilayaṃ nayet|| 27||

kāraṇāni samṛddhiṃ ca mantrasiddheśca sādhakaḥ|

āścaryasvavidheścaiva parānugrahatatparaḥ|| 28||

ūhottagāyogasya tenodhvāvadhṛte sthiteḥ|

sādhāraṇo .apyasau yukte bhūyaścāpakaroti hi|| 29||

cittavṛttiṃ sthitāṃ mārge nudannūhaḥ pravartate|

prāpayitvā paraṃ sthānamūhogre vinivartate|| 30||

anena lakṣayedyogī yogasiddheḥ pravartakam|

nivartakaṃ ca yadvastu bahudhāsaṃvyavasthitam|| 31||

śivatattvārthaviṣayacittādhyānamudāhṛtam|

tadbījālambanaṃ jñānaṃ pravāhātmakamiṣyate|| 32||

tena tatra sthito yogī samādhiṃ vindate .acirāt|

samādhau pariniṣpanne phalaṃ prāpnotyabhīpsitam|| 33||

vijñānālambanaṃ vastu bhāvayaṃstanmayo bhavet|

tanmayatve nirālambaṃ bhaveccittamabhāvayat|| 34||

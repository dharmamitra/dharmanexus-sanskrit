vyavahārakṣamaṃ śuddhaṃ karmayogyaṃ bhavecca tat||

tadrasena rasenāpi mañjiṣṭhāsahitena ca||

suvarṇai rajatai ratnairvastrairābhara.aistathā|

iti dhātuvāda-rasāyanam||

puṭīkṛtyānale taptaṃ yat svarṇaṃ nirupakṣayam|
tat syāt ṣoḍaśavarṇākhyaṃ kośe sthāpyaṃ tadeva hi||
vālārkadyutisaṅkāśaṃ vidyuddīptisamaprabham|
śuddhaṃ bhūṣaṇayogyaṃ syāt tridaśānāṃ mahībhujām||
kṛtaṃ vā niṣkarūpeṇa ghanaṃ vā piṇḍarūpakam|
bhūṣaṇatvena vā siddhaṃ śuddhaṃ kośe vidhārayet||
dharmasyārthasya kāmasya sādhakaṃ janarañjanam|
śuddhaṃ suvarṇakośasthamāyurlakṣmīpradaṃ bhavet||

dhātvantarasamāyuktamaśuddhaṃ naiva dhārayet|
aśaktaṃ svarṇakāryeṣu gṛhalakṣmīvināśakam||
nāgena miśritaṃ raupyaṃ vahninā pariśodhitam|
candramaṇḍalasaṅkāśaṃ kośe sthāpyaṃ mahībhujā||
yaśaḥ-saubhāgyajananaṃ śreṣṭhaṃputrapradāyakam|
gṛhe vidhāritaṃ raupyaṃ malinaṃ cāsukhapradam||
ratnāni dhārayet kośe śuddhāni guṇavanti ca|
smbhavaṃ ca tathā jātiṃ guṇaṃ teṣāṃ parīkṣya ca||
kṛte yuge kaliṅgeṣu kosale vajrasambhavaḥ|

pauṇḍrake ca surāṣṭre ca dvāpare yā ca santatiḥ|
vairāgare ca sopāre kalau hīrakasambhavaḥ||
guṇāḥ pañca samākhyātā doṣāḥ pañca prakīrtitāḥ|
chāyāścatasro vijñeyā vajrāṇāṃ ratnakovidaiḥ||
ṣaṭkoṇatvaṃ laghutvaṃ ca samāṣṭadalatā tathā|

malo bindustathā rekhā trāsaḥ kākapadaṃ ca yat|
ete doṣāḥ samākhyātāḥ pañca vajreṣu kovidaiḥ||
śvetā raktā tathā pītā kṛṣṇā chāyā caturvidhā|
viprakṣatriyavaiśyānāṃ śūdrajāteryathākramam||
yajñairdānaistapobhiśca yadāpnoti tadāpnuyāt|
guṇayuktasya vajrasya viprajātervidhāraṇāt||

jayaḥ parākramastasya śatrunāśaśca jāyate|
guṇavat kṣatrajātīyaṃ vajraṃ vasati yudgṛhe||
kalākuśalatā dravyaṃ prajñā kṣema yaśo mahat|
guṇinaḥ paviratnasya vaiśyajātervidhāraṇāt||
paropakāritā dākṣyaṃ dhanadhānyasamṛddhayaḥ|
guṇayuktasya vajrasya śūdrajātervidhāraṇāt||
male malinatā khyātā dhāraṇād daṃṣṭriṇo bhayam|
koṇe vyādhibhayaṃ proktaṃ madhye vahnibhayaṃ bhavet||

caturddhaivaṃ samākhyātā bindavo vajrasaṃśrayāḥ||

iti vajradoṣa-guṇāḥ||

amūlyaṃ tad vinirdiṣṭaṃ ratnalakṣaṇavedibhiḥ|

susnigdhaṃ madhuracchāyaṃ mauktikaṃ siṃhalodbhavam|
āravāṭasamutpannaṃ pītacchāyaṃ sunirmalam||
pārasīkodbhavaṃ svacchaṃ sitaṃ muktāphalaṃ śubham|
īṣacchyāmaṃ ca rūkṣaṃ ca mauktikaṃ barbarodbhavam||
catvāraḥ syurmahādoṣā madhyamāḥ ṣaṭ prakīrtitāḥ|
evaṃ daśa samākhyātāsteṣāṃ vakṣyāmi lakṣaṇam||

dāridryajananaṃ yasmāt tasmāt tat parivarjayet||

tasmin sandhārite mṛtyurjāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ||

iti mauktikaparīkṣā||

aśvagandhā kaṇā rātriḥ kṛmighnaṃ rasapañcakam|
pṛthak pṛthak palānyaṣṭau variṣṭho muṣṭimātrakaḥ||

trikaṭu trāyamāṇā ca pauṣkaraṃ gajapippalī|
dhātakī ceti cūrṇo 'yaṃ madhunā kaṇṭharoganut||
sindhavaṃ nāgaraṃ kuṣṭhaṃ vacā śigru niśādvayam|
siddhārthaka-yavakṣārau sarvamekatra cūrṇitam||
dadhnā viloḍitaṃ koṣṇaṃ kaphavātajaśophajit|
svedaṃ ca kārayecchophe pracchannaṃ vā yathocitam||
śophe vipāṭite pakve dvipaviṭ saindhavānvitā|

ghṛtamākṣikapūrṇaṃ tad vidhātavyaṃ dinatrayam||

katakasya phalaṃ rodhraṃ madhukaṃ candanaṃ ghanam|
prapauṇḍarīkaṃ mañjiṣṭhā vālakośīrakaṃ tathā||
ebhirviracitā varttiścūrṇamāścotanaṃ tathā|
sarveṣāmakṣirogāṇāṃ śasyate sāmajanmanām||
kṣīradrumāṇāṃ niṣkvāthe trivṛtaṃ nikṣipedanu|
triphalā rocanaṃ lākṣā sindūraṃ rodhraguggulam||
bhallāta-rajanī-ghoṇṭāphalakāsīra(sa)-saindhavam|
saurāṣṭrikāñjanaṃ caiva rasaṃ sarjjasamudbhavam||

iti balādhyāyaḥ||

kulīnaṃ ca suśīlaṃ ca sukhe duḥkhe ca sammatam|

nītijñaṃ śaucasampannaṃ vyasaneṣvaparāṅmukham|
kurvīta nṛpatirmitraṃ dharmārthaṃ sukhasiddhaye||

iti suhṛdadhyāyaḥ||

ātmāyatto nṛpaḥ śreṣṭho dvayāyattastu madhyamaḥ|
kaniṣṭhaḥ sacivāyatta iti nītivido viduḥ||
madadhīnamidaṃ rājyaṃ rājā ca vaśago mama|
mayā yat kriyate kāryaṃ tat kāryaṃ kena laṅghyate?||
ityātmagarveṇādhmātaḥ sacivo yatra jalpati|
kaniṣṭhaṃ tatra tad rājyaṃ sacivāyattamucyate||
vijñaptaṃ yanmayā kāryamavaśyaṃ manyate prabhuḥ|
ityāropya dvayoḥ sāmyaṃ sacivo yat tu bhāṣate|
sukhāsukhapradaṃ caivamubhayādhīnasiddhitaḥ|
tadrājyamubhayāyattaṃ madhyamaṃ parikīrtitam||

iti prabhuśaktyadhyāyaḥ||

kāryasya sādhanopāyāḥ sahāyāstatkṛtau kṣamāḥ|
deśakālavicāraśca vighnānāṃ ca pratikriyā||
kāryasiddhau sukhaṃ ceti pañcāṅgo mantra iṣyate|

bahubhirbhidyate mantro dvābhyāṃ mantro na naśyati|
ātmana vihito mantro brahmaṇā 'pi na bhidyate||
satataṃ guptavākyasya nityaṃ gupteṅgitākṛteḥ|
phalairjñeyāḥ sadārambhāḥ puṇyaṃ pūrvārjitaṃ yathā||

āruhya connataṃ sthānaṃ prāsādaṃ vā rahaḥsthitam|

abhiprāyaṃ viditvaiṣāṃ guṇadoṣau vicārayet||
mantribhirmantrite mantre guṇadoṣau vicārya ca|
svayaṃ niścityānyatarat kāryaṃ kuryānmahīpatiḥ||
kiṃ kṛtaṃ karaṇīyaṃ kiṃ kriyamāṇaṃ ca kiṃ mayā|

bahulābhaṃ susādhyaṃ ca dharmyaṃ kīrttivivardhanam|

ityutsāhaśaktyadhyāyaḥ||

anyo 'nyasyopakārārthaṃ yatra sandhīyate nṛpaḥ|
parasparopakārākhyaḥ sa sandhiḥ procyate budhaiḥ||
gajānaśvāṃstathā ratnaṃ suvarṇaṃ bhūmimeva ca|

iti sandhyadhyāyaḥ||11||

suyatnena sahāyena sāmarthyena balena ca|

daivopahatakośaśca vyādhinā bādhito 'pi vā|

riporvināśamuddiśya tyaktvā cānyapramādakam|
sahasā kriyate yātrā sā bhavecchīghragā bhṛśam||

iti yātrābhedāḥ||

deśaṃ kālaṃ tathā mitraṃ nimittaṃ balamātmanaḥ|
vicārya kurvato nityaṃ yātrā bhavati siddhidā||
mūler 'ke śravaṇe saumye bhaumāhirbudhnyadaivate|
kṛttikāyāṃ budho yukto vākpatistu punarvasau||
bhāgye śukraḥ śaniḥ svātau tiṣṭhantaḥ sarvasiddhidāḥ|
tasmādete samākhyātāḥ siddhiyogā manīṣibhiḥ||
sahasrakiraṇo haste mṛgāṅko mṛgaśīrṣake|
aśvinyāṃ ca dharāputre maire candramasaḥ sutaḥ||
divaukasāṃ guruḥ puṣye revatyāṃ bhṛgunandanaḥ|
rohiṇyāṃ śanivāre tu sudhāyogāḥ prakīrtitāḥ||
riktāsu sūryaje sūrye nandāsu bhṛgunandane|
jayāsu dharaṇīputre bhadrāsu ca budhe vidhau||
pūrṇāsu tridaśācārye sthite siddhā tithirbhavet|
sarvakāryakarī nR̥ṇāṃ yātrāyāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ||
pratipad gamane śastā dvitīyā kṣemadā bhavet|
tṛtīyā dhanalābhāya caturthī ninditā sadā||

pañcamī śrīkarī jñeyā ṣaṣṭhī yāne 'tikutsitā|
saptamī savalābhāya kutsitā cāṣṭamī tithiḥ||
navamī ca sadā nindyā bhūmidā daśamī bhavet|
vaiṣṇavī jayadā nityaṃ dvādaśī parivarjitā||
kiṃ punarbahunoktena sarvasiddhā trayodaśī|
asite ca site pakṣe varjanīyā caturdaśī||
pūrvabhāgaṃ parityajya pūrṇimā gamane varā|
amāvāsyā viśeṣeṇa śaśihīnā vivarjitā||
uttarātritayaṃ puṣyo ravivāre śubhapradaḥ|
rohiṇī śravaṇaṃ citrā somavāre sukhāvahāḥ||
ahirbudhnyaṃ ca pauṣṇaṃ ca bhaumavāre praśasyate|
punarvasurgurorvāre bhāgyaṃ ca śravaṇe sthite||
haridaivaṃ dvidaivaṃ ca vahnidaivaṃ śanerdine|
dhiṣṇyānyetāni śastāni vāreṣveteṣu sarvadā||
hastaḥ punarvasuḥ puṣyo revatī mṛgaśīrṣakam|
anurādhāśvinī śrotraṃ śastāni gamane nṛṇām||
caralagne prayātavyaṃ dvisvabhāve 'thavā nṛpaiḥ|
lagne sthire na gantavyaṃ yātrāyāṃ kṣemamicchubhiḥ||

āpya-mārutayorvairaṃ balavān mārutastayoḥ|
madhyashāvāmbarāgneyau nābhasastu tayorvalī||
naiva śatrū na mitre ca nābhasāpyau parasparam|
tathāpi nābhaso nādaḥ prabalaḥ parikīrtitaḥ||
pārthivāgneyayormaitrī tatra bahumo balī svaraḥ|
analānilayormaitrī balavānanilastayoḥ||
jalamārutajau śabdau prāguktau bhaumavairiṇau|
tāveva paścādudbhūtau mitre bahumasya kīrtitau||
caturṇāmapi śabdānāṃ nābhasaḥ śatruriṣyate|
evaṃ svarāṇāmuddiṣṭaṃ mitrāmitrabalābalam||
svasyāṃ diśi phalaṃ pūrṇaṃ tadardhaṃ mitrasadmani|
śatrukṣetre phalābhāvo viparītamathāpi vā||
bhaumādanantaraścāpyaḥ sarvasiddhiprado bhavet|
viparītakrameṇaiva phalanāśakarau svarau||
prathamaṃ pārthivo nāda staijasastadanantaram|
vāñchitārthapradau syātāṃ viparītau tato 'nyathā||
ādau cet pārthivo nādaḥ paścānmārutajo yadi|
lābhapūrvā tadā hānirhānerlābho viparyayāt||
prathamo bhūmijo nādo dvitīyo nābhaso yadi|
sukhapūrvaṃ bhaved duḥkhaṃ duḥkhāt saukhyaṃ viparyayāt|
āpyasya paścādāgneyaḥ phalaṃ kṛtvā vināśayet|
vyatyāsena samudbhūto vyatyastaphalakāriṇau||
prathamo jalajaḥ śabdastataśced vāyusambhavaḥ|
bhayaṃ vittavināśāya vaiparītye 'pi tādṛśau||

jalajād vyomajaḥ paścānitrayuddhād bhavenmṛtiḥ|
viparītakramodbhūtau śatruto mṛtisūcakau||
āgneya-mārutau śabdau kramājjātau dhanāpahau|
mṛtisaṃsūcakau jñeyau viparītakramoditau||
krameṇa vyutkrameṇāpi nādau taijasa-nābhasau|
kurutaḥ kalahaṃ cograṃ bhayaṃ ca sumahattaram||
vyatyāsāt kramaśo vā 'pi śabdau vāyavya-nābhasau|
vittanāśaṃ raṇe mṛtyuṃ narasya kurute dhruvam||
anyairabhihito bhaumaḥ kāryasiddhivighātakṛt|
adhikaḥ pārthivo nādaḥ sarvakāmaphalapradaḥ||
trayāṇaṃ vā caturṇāṃ vā pañcānāṃ śravaṇe sati|
paścācchruto 'dhiko vā 'pi parthivo dhvaniruttamaḥ||
pārthivaṃ salilaṃ nādaṃ svasthānādūrdhvagastaroḥ|
kurvan lābhaṃ sukhaṃ kīrtiṃ jayaṃ śaṃsati piṅgalaḥ||
avatīrya nijasthānāt kurvan nādau śubhāvahau|
kaniṣṭhaṃ phalamācaṣṭe kleśāllābhaṃ vihaṅgamaḥ||
vṛkṣasyāgre ravaṃ kurvan pārthivaṃ vārijaṃ khagaḥ|
puṣkalaṃ phalamākhyāti madhye madhyamadho laghu||
vinyasya vadane bhakṣyaṃ bhuktvā vā tad vihaṅgamaḥ|
ramyasthānasthitaḥ kurvan śubhau nādau śubhaṃ diśet||
bhakṣyamutsṛjya vaktrasthaṃ yadā vakti śubhau dhvanī|
tadā śaṃsati sampatterhāniṃ piṅgavihaṅgamaḥ||

arcitaṃ pādapaṃ tyaktvā pādapāntaramāśritaḥ|
racayan śobhanaṃ śabdaṃ diśed deśāntare sukham||
ambare śuṣkavṛkṣe vā bhagnaśākhe ca koṭare|
kurvan nādaṃ śubhaṃ piṅgo bhayamugraṃ praśaṃsati||
śubhasthānasthito vāpi gatvā vā 'tha pradakṣiṇam|
kurvan śānte śubhādanyanādaṃ śaṃsatyaśobhanam||
vāmataḥ śāntadigvartī kurvāṇaḥ śobhanaṃ dhvanim|
śobhanaṃ phalamācaṣṭe cirakālena piṅgalaḥ||
bhaumāpyataijasairnādairjalpantī śubhadeśagā|
piṅgalā prītimācaṣṭe sukhaṃ lābhaṃ jayaṃ yaśaḥ||

iti piṅgalāśakunam||

arcayitvā gaṇādhīśaṃ sarvavighnavināśanam|
kumāryā sahitā nāryastisraḥ supte jane 'khile||
akṣataiḥ pūrayeyustā yat kiñcit kuḍavādikam|
caṇḍikāyai namaḥ kṛtvā saptakṛtvo 'bhimantritam||
sammārjanīkṛtāveṣṭe sthāpayeyurguṇādhipam|
vrajeyustaṃ samādāya rajakasya niketanam||
tadgehasya purobhāge nikṣipeyuḥ sitākṣatān|
manogataṃ samuddiśya śṛṇuyuḥ susamāhitāḥ||
śrūyate vacanaṃ kiñcid rajakālayamadhyagam|
nāryā nareṇa bālena proktamanyena kenacit||
svairasaṃlāpanodbhūtaṃ śubhaṃ vā yadi vā 'śubham|
śṛṇvantībhiḥ phalaṃ jñeyaṃ tadvākyārthavicārataḥ||

caṇḍālanilaye 'pyevaṃ śravaṇe bodhane kramaḥ|
yad brūyurvacanaṃ tatra tat tathā na tadanyathā||
śākunaṃ śāstramālokya bhāvikāryāvabodhakam|
śakunaṃ varṇitaṃ samyak someśvaramahībhujā||

ityupaśrutiśakunam|| iti yātrādhyāyaḥ||13||

avṛṣṭyā toyarahite durbhikṣe marakānvite|
pareṣaṃ duḥsthite deśe naiva yāyāt kathañcana||
parasparavirodhena kṣīyamāṇeṣu śatruṣu|
tadvināśo bhaved yāvat tāvat tiṣṭhatu buddhimān||
svasthāsanamupekṣākhyaṃ mārgarodhāsanaṃ tathā|
durgasādhyāsanaṃ caiva rāṣṭrasvīkaraṇāsanam||
ramaṇīyāsanaṃ cānyat tathā ca nikaṭāsanam|
dūramārgāsanaṃ caiva pralobhākhyaṃ tathaiva a||
parādhīnāsanaṃ caitad daśamaṃ parikīrtitam|
ito 'dhikaṃ na dṛśyeta nītijñairanyadāsanam||
niṣkaṇṭake tathā rājye saṃhate vairimaṇḍale|
svasthāne yā sthitī rājñāṃ svasthāsanamiti smṛtam||
yadasmābhiranuṣṭheyaṃ daivena kriyate hi tat|
ugradaṇḍaprayogācca vyasanād vṛttyadarśanāt||
durbhikṣānmarakāpātād vināśaṃ yāsyati dhruvam|
ityupekṣya nṛpasthānamupekṣāsanamucyate||
nadyāḥ pūrṇapravāheṇa gamanaṃ rodhitaṃ yadi|
tena doṣeṇa yat sthānaṃ mārgarodhāsanaṃ smṛtam||

durgaṃ grahītumudyukto vārayan yavasādikam|
tadupānte vased yastu durgasādhyāsanaṃ hi tat||
baladupārjite rāṣṭre  vaśārthaṃ yatra tiṣṭhati|
tat sthanaṃ kathitaṃ rājñāṃ rāṣṭrasvīkaraṇāsanam||
vijigīṣurnṛpo gatvā ripuṃ hatvā raṇāṅgaṇe|
ramyāṃ vasatimālokya yatra tiṣṭhati sānugaḥ||
yavasendhanabhūyiṣṭhe jaladhānyasamanvite|
tat sthānaṃ nītitattvajñai ramaṇīyāsanaṃ smṛtam||
dūrasthasya ripo rājā vināśāya samudyataḥ|
samīpe yat sthitiṃ kuryāt tad bhavennikaṭāsanam||
dūradeśaṃ nṛpo gatvā kṛtvā kāryamaśeṣataḥ|
vijñāya svapuraṃ dūraṃ yatra tiṣṭhatyanākulam||
varṣarttuvyatyayo yāvaccharatkālasya sambhavaḥ|
tāvad yaducitaṃ sthānaṃ dūramārgāsanaṃ hi tat||

hastyaśca-dhana-ratnāni durgaṃ rāṣṭraṃ tathaiva ca|
kiñcitkālaṃ sthitiḥ kāryā ripuṃ hatvā dadami te||
iti pralobhito 'nyena yatra tiṣṭhati bhūmipaḥ|
tat pralobhāsanaṃ nāma kathitaṃ nītivedibhiḥ||
māse pakṣe daśāhe vā ṣaḍahe pañcarātrake|
prābhṛtaṃ te dadāmīti pralobhya sthāpayennṛpam||
āśayā dhāryamāṇastu tatra tiṣṭhati yacciram|
pralobhāsanamevaṃ vā tadākhyātaṃ manīṣibhiḥ||

snehād vā vairibhāvād vā gantuṃ deśaṃ svakaṃ na yaḥ|
labhate tasya tat sthānaṃ parādhīnāsanaṃ smṛtam||

ityāsanādhyāyaḥ||14||

svayaṃ hīnabalo rājā jayahetu. na paśyati|
balinā pīḍyamāno yaḥ kṣemasthānaṃ samāśrayet||
satsaṃśrayo 'nyasaṃsargo durgasaṃśrayaṇaṃ tathā|
iti bhedāstrayaḥ proktaścaturtho nopalabhyate||
mahatā śatruṇā yastu pīḍito 'lpabalo nṛpaḥ|
satyasandhaṃ parijñāya tamevāśrayate budhaḥ||
bahūn vā sadguṇān vīkṣya śatruṃ yastu samaśrayet|
taṃ guṇaṃ nītitattvajñāḥ satsaṃśrayamuśanti hi||
śatruṇā bādhyamāno hi svayaṃ vā 'pyanupāyakaḥ|
guṇahīnaṃ ripuṃ jñātvā śrayedanyaṃ guṇādhikam||
balinaṃ kriyayā yuktaṃ dharmajñaṃ satyavādinam|
āśrayedīdṛśaṃ yastu so 'nyasaṃśrayaḥ ucyate|
vairiṇā balayuktena pīḍito hyasamo ripuḥ|
yat tu saṃśrayate durgaṃ durgasaṃśraya iṣyate||

ityāśrayādhyāyaḥ||15||

balinordviṣatormadhye vācā ' 'tmānaṃ samarpayan|
dvaidhībhāvena varteta kākākṣivadalakṣitaḥ||

lābhād vā 'pi bhayād vā 'pi yogakṣemārthamudyataḥ|
dvayormadhye ciraṃ kālaṃ dvaidhībhāvena yāpayet||
nītiśāstrārthasārajñairnāmabhedārthayatnataḥ|
mithyācittaḥ samādiṣṭo mithyāvacana eva ca||

mithyākaraṇa ityanyastathā cobhayavetanaḥ|
yugmaprābhṛtakaśceti dvaidhībhāvo 'tra pañcadhā||
citte virodhamādhatte vacasā priyabhāṣaṇam|
dvaidhībhāvo 'yamākhyāto mithyācittor 'thakovidaiḥ||
vacasā priyamākhyāti karmaṇā varttate 'nyathā|
dvaidhībhāvo 'paraḥ prokto mithyāvacanasaṃjñakaḥ||
stokaṃ stokaṃ kimapyādau kāryaṃ viśvāsakāraṇāt|
karoti bhāvaduṣṭo 'sau mahat kāryaṃ vināśayet||
evaṃ yaḥ kurute mithyā ripornāśāya pārthivaḥ|
mithyākaraṇanāmā 'sau dvaidhībhāvaḥ prakīritaḥ||
guptaṃ vetanamekatra tathā 'nyatra prakāśitam|
gṛhītvā yaścaran māyī guptaṃ dasyuhitecchayā||
alakṣito ripormantra bhittvā svasvāmine 'rpayet|
yatra tiṣṭhati dvaividhyāt sa syādubhayavetanaḥ||
madripuṃ sādhayasveti ḍhaukitaṃ prābhṛtaṃ mahat|
anyenāpi tamuddiśya dattaṃ vāji-gajādikam||
saṃvāhaṃ preṣayiṣyāmi sādhayiṣyāmi te ripum|
iti bruvan viruddhābhyāṃ dvābhyāmarthaṃ samāharet||
tayorajñātarūpaḥ san yatra rājā pravartate|
dvaidhībhāvaṃ tamapyāhuryugmaprābhṛtakaṃ budhāḥ||
dvaidhībhāvaṃ samālambya ya evaṃ vartate nṛpaḥ|
vipakṣalakṣmīmādatte mantrarakṣāvicakṣaṇaḥ||

iti dvaidhībhāvādhyāyaḥ||16||

sandhiṃ ca vigrahaṃ caiva yānamāsanamāśrayam|
dvadhībhāvaṃ ca yo vetti prayoktuṃ śatrumaṇḍale||
balavān kośasampanno guṇaṣaṭkakriyābudhaḥ|
sa kṛtsnāṃ pṛthivīṃ bhuṅkte deśakālasahāyavit||
upāyeṣūttamaṃ sama bhedo madhyama iṣyate|
upapradānamadhamaṃ daṇḍaḥ kaṣṭatamaḥ smṛtaḥ||
apāyarahitatvācca dravyahānerabhāvataḥ|
siddhimāyānti kāryāṇi tataḥ sāmottamaṃ matam||
kutsitā bhedamāyānti na te viśvāsabhūmayaḥ|
ataḥ sandehaūpatvād bhedo 'yaṃ madhyamaḥ smṛtaḥ||
vittasya saṃkṣayaḥ pūrvaṃ siddhirdaive vyavasthitā|
upapradānamadhamaṃ tena proktaṃ manīṣibhiḥ||
lābhārthaṃ kriyate yuddhaṃ raṇe saṃśayito jayaḥ|
rājyaṃ ca jīvitaṃ caiva daṇḍaḥ kaṣṭamastataḥ||
kupitasya priyaṃ vākyaṃ kopavṛddhau prajāyate|
yathā ' 'jyasya prataptasya jvālāyai jalabindavaḥ||
kulīneṣu kṛtajñeṣu sārdracitteṣu sādhuṣu|
kāryārtheṣu ca medhāvī pūrvaṃ sāma prayojayet||
prathamaṃ karṇasubhagaṃ dvitīyaṃ daivikaṃ tathā|
tṛtīyaṃ smārakaṃ proktaṃ caturthaṃ lobhajaṃ smṛtam||
tathaiva pañcamaṃ sāma nijārpaṇamiti smṛtam|
upāyeṣūttamaṃ sāma kathitaṃ nirupadravam||
madhuraiḥ sukhasaṃlāpairanyaiśca hṛdayaṅgamaiḥ|
evaṃ vacorbhayat sāma tat karṇasubhagaṃ smṛtam||

viśvāsajananopāyaiḥ śapathairdevapūrvakaiḥ|
kriyate yacca nītijñaiḥ sāma tad daivikaṃ viduḥ||
yadāsīd bāndhavaṃ pūrvaṃ sāmprataṃ vismṛtaṃ tvayā|
iti saṃsmāryate yatra smārakaṃ sāma tat smṛtam||
grāmaṃ puraṃ tathā rāṣṭraṃ vājiaṃ vāraṇaṃ dhanam|
dāsyāmīti ca yat sāma smṛtaṃ tadiha lobhajam||
bhavatkārye madīyaṃ svaṃ śarīraṃ ca mayārpitam|
iti vāgarpite sāmni kathitaṃ tannijārpaṇam||

iti sāmādhyāyaḥ||17||

sāmopāyairasādhyā ye śatravo madamohitāḥ|
bhedopāyena te sādhyā nṛpeṇa vijigīṣuṇā||
kṣīraṃ nīraṃ yathā haṃso viśleṣayati saṃhatam|
tathā susaṃhatān śatrūn bhedād viśleṣayet tu tān||
śatrusthairātmapuruṣairgūḍhairubhayavetanaiḥ|
bhītāpamānitān kruddhān bhedayecca nṛsaṅgatān||
prāṇāpaho mānabhaṅgo dhanahāniśca bandhakaḥ|
dārābhilāṣo 'ṅgbhaṅga iti bhedo 'tra ṣaḍvidhaḥ||
mantraḥ samyag mayā jñāto nṛpastvāṃ hantumudyataḥ|
adya śvo vā 'titīkṣṇaiśca kiñcinnādyāpi budhyase||
paiśunyenāpyanākhyāpyahetubhiḥ pratyayātmakaiḥ|
bhītirutpadyate yatra sa bhedaḥ prāṇahā smṛtaḥ||
tvaddviṣatprerito rājā tava mānaṃ hariṣyati|
evaṃ vidveṣya yo bhedo mānabhaṅgaḥ sa varṇitaḥ||

dhaniko 'yaṃ mahāneva tava kiñcinna ditsati|
kenāpyupāyena tasmād dhanamāhartumarhati||
iti sammantrya bhūpena tad brūte dhanikāya ca|
arthalubdho hyayaṃ rājā tava dravyaṃ hariṣyati||
yatraivaṃ piśunatvena bhinattyubhayavetanaḥ|
dhanahānistvayaṃ bhedo nītijñaiḥ samudāhṛtaḥ||
na karoti tava svāmī viśvāsaṃ tvayi karhicit|
bandhayitvā tu nigaḍaiḥ kārāgare nidhāsyati||
iti viśvāsamudbhāvyya nijasvāmihite rataḥ|
yad bhinatti paraṃ buddhyā sa bhedo bandhakaḥ smṛtaḥ||
vilāsī capalo bhogī rūpavāṃstaruṇo viṭaḥ|
antaḥpure madīyāṃ strīṃ kaṭākṣaiḥ spraṣṭumīkṣate||
vadhyo 'yamiti bhūpālo mayā sārddhamamantryat|
iti yaṃ kurute bhedaṃ sa syād dārābhilāṣakaḥ||
tvadbhāryāṃ rūpasampannāṃ navayauvanaśālinīm|
mamāgre varṇayan rājā sāpekṣa iti lakṣitaḥ||
tvayi jīvatyalabhyatvāt tava drohaṃ vidhāsyati|
dārābhilāṣabhedo 'yamevaṃ vā parikīrttitaḥ||
ayamasmina kule jātaḥ kadācid rājyamicchati|
aṅgulī vā tathā netre santataḥ sādhanāni ca||
chettuṃ niścitavān rājā sarvathā tat kariṣyati|
iti yo nirmito bhedaḥ so 'ṅgabhaṅgaḥ prakīrtitaḥ||
samyagbhedena śatrūṇāṃ bhinatti prakṛtiṃ tu yaḥ|
bhedavajreṇa tad bhinnaṃ hṛdayaṃ pratibhūbhṛtaḥ||

iti bhedādhyāyaḥ||18||

arthabhārapradānena svādhikārakulena ca|
alabdhavetanatvena dravyāpaharaṇena ca||
dviṣatāṃ kāraṇairebhirviraktā ye ca mantriṇaḥ|
amātyāḥ sacivāścaiva tathā sāmantamānyakāḥ||
bhṛtyāśca bāndhavāścaiva ye cāntarvarttino janāḥ|
yuddhabuddhisahāyā ye teṣāṃ dānaṃ prayojayet||
anyeye lobhasaṃyuktā vyasanāsaktacetasaḥ|
tebhyo 'pi dānaṃ yuñjīta nṛpaḥ śatrujighāṃsayā||
evaṃ rāgavihīnā ye svakīyāḥ sacivādayaḥ|
teṣāmapi prayuñjīta dānaṃ rājyābhivṛddhaye||
abhīṣṭaṃ hāyanaṃ deśyaṃ karajaṃ danti-saptijam|
grāmajaṃ śāsanaṃ bhūṣā vasanaṃ pratipattijam|
ākaraṃ rukmajaṃ kanyā vaiśyaṃ velākaraṃ tathā|
upapradānamevaṃ tu proktaṃ ṣoḍaśadhā budhaiḥ||
yasmai yad rocate vastu tasmai yogyānusārataḥ|
yad dīyate tadākhyātamabhīṣṭaṃ dānamuttamam||
ekaṃ saṃvatsaraṃ yāvat kuṭumbabharaṇakṣamam|
yasya yad dīyate tasya tad dānaṃ hāyanaṃ smṛtam||
rāṣṭraṃ pradīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ deśyamucyate|
rāṣṭrotthakaradānaṃ yat tat smṛtaṃ karajaṃ budhaiḥ||
vāraṇasya pradānaṃ yat tad dānaṃ dantisaṃjñitam|
hayasya dānamākhyātaṃ saptijaṃ nītikovidaiḥ||
agṛhītakaro vā 'pi pragṛhītakaro 'pi vā|
grāmaḥ pradīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ grāmajaṃ matam||

putrapautraprapautreṇa dattaṃ naiva vilupyate|
acālyatvena yat kiñcit tad dānaṃ śāsanaṃ smṛtam||
ratnena jātarūpeṇa ghaṭitaṃ cāru bhūṣaṇam|
dīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ bhūṣākhyaṃ samudāhṛtam||
nānāvarṇavicitrāṇi nānāsūtramayāni ca|
nānādeśasamutthāni sūkṣmāṇi ca ghanāni ca||
vastrāṇyatimanojñāni prabhūtāni prasādataḥ|
dīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ vasanaṃ parikīrttitam||
āsanaṃ cāmaraṃ chatraṃ yānaṃ sammānanocitam|
mānavad dīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ pratipattikam||
raupya-kāñcana-ratnāni sañjāyante yato bhuvaḥ|
sā khanirdīyate yat tu tadākaramitīritam||
dīyante yara niṣkāṇi prabhūtāni varāṇi ca|
tad dānaṃ rukmajaṃ proktamarthaśāstravicakṣaṇaiḥ||
bhuṣaṇairbhūṣitā kanya lakṣaṇaiśca samanvitā|
vidhivad dīyate yat tu kanyādānaṃ tu tad viduḥ||
rūpayauvanasampannā nṛtyagītaviśaradā|
veśyā pradīyate yat tu tad dānaṃ vaiśyamucyate||
yatrāḍhyā bahavaḥ santi vahitrasyopajīvinaḥ|

daṇḍo rakṣati maryādāṃ daṇḍo dharmaṃ pravarttayet|
nivārayedadharmācca tasmād daṇḍaṃ prayojayet||
daṇḍahīne yato rāṣṭre mātsyo nyāyaḥ pravartate|
tasmād daṇḍaṃ prayuñjīta duṣṭānāṃ dhārmiko nṛpaḥ||
daṇḍapātabhayālloko dharme tiṣṭhati sūtritaḥ|
karīva vijayo matto 'pyaṅkuśena vaśīkṛtaḥ||
tīvradaṇḍabhayālloke bhṛśamudvijite janaḥ|
tasmānmṛduprayogeṇa prajāpālanamācaret||
yathoktadaṇḍavinyāsād bhūpaterdharmacāriṇaḥ|
yaśo dharmastathā rāṣṭraṃ kośaśca parivardhate||

evamaṅgāni rājyasya sapta śaktitrayaṃ tathā|
ṣāḍguṇyaṃ ca tathā proktamupāyaśca caturvidhaḥ||
rājyasthairyanimittāni prāptarājyasya bhūpateḥ|
viṃśatiḥ somabhūpālaḥ kṛtavān nītikovidaḥ||

iti daṇḍādhyāyaḥ||20||

iti mahārājādhirāja-satyāśrayakulatilaka-cālukyābharaṇa-
śrīmadbhūlokamallaśrīsomeśvaradevaviracite
mānasollāse 'bhilasṣitārthacintāmaṇau rājyasthirīkaraṇopāyakathane
dvitīyaṃ prakaraṇam||

prathamo bhāgaḥ samāptaḥ|

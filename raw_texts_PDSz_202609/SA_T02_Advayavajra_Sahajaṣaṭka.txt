śāśvatocchedanirmuktaṃ tattvaṃ saugatasaṃmatam | 
prakṛtyājātadharmeṣu vidhapohau paśor vacaḥ || 1 || 

astīti vadato brūmo nāsti sarvaṃ vicārataḥ | 
nāstīti vadato brūmaḥ sarvam asty avicārataḥ || 2 || 

yathā yathā samāropā jāyante tattvayoginaḥ | 
tathā tathā samāropā hanyante tattvayoginā || 3 || 

^S p.63. 

sahajo 'kṛtrimo yasmāt tasmāt saṅgo na sāhajaḥ | 
sukhaṃ na sahajād anyat sukhaṃ cāsaṅgalakṣaṇam || 4 || 

jñātvā niḥsaṅgatātmānaṃ niveśāgatasatsukham | 
viśvaṃ svasamayaṃ kṛtvā magnaḥ sahajasāgare || 5 || 

mantratattvasthito yogī niḥsaṅgārthapratiṣṭhitaḥ | 
bhavāṃś cāsau gurūn kṛtvā niḥsaṅgaviṣayo bhavet || 6 ||

|| kṛtir iyaṃ paṇḍitāvadhūtaśrīmadadvayavajrapādānām iti || 
|| sahajaṣaṭkāḥ samāptāḥ ||

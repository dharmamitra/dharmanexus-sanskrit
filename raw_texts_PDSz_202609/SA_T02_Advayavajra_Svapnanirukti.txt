12. Svapnanirukti 

edited by MM Hara Prasad Shastri 
in Advayavajrasaṃgraha GOS 1927 Baroda

edited by Mikkyou-seiten-kenkyūkai  4.
in Taisho Daigaku Sougou Bukkyou Kenkyūjo Nenpou

^S p.45. 
^M (68/269) 

namaḥ sarvajñāya | 

vinaye cābhidharme ca sūtreṣu jinapuṅgavaiḥ | 
dharmāḥ svapnopamāḥ khyātā vispaṣṭaṃ tat prakathyate || 1 || 

svapnaṃ satyam asatyaṃ vā ciccitraṃ vā cid eva vā | 
māyā vāthāpratiṣṭhānaṃ satāṃ kim iha saṃmatam || 2 || 

svapnaṃ satyam abodhena tad bodhe yāty alīkatām | 
prathame śāśvatajñānaṃ dvitīye nāstitā bhavet || 3 || 

prabodhāt kalpitāpohe nānubhūtir apohyate | 
sacciccitraprakāśātmā yad asty anubhavo mahān || 4 || 

^M (70/267) 

kṣaṇadhvaṃsyālayād maulāt pravṛttibhyo vicitratā | 
tāś ca tatra tadākāraś cid evaitat tad iṣyate || 5 || 

svapnāt svapnaṃ yadaiva syāc ciccitrālikatā tadā | 
naiva satyam asad vitter na cocchedaḥ prakāśataḥ || 6 || 

kim anāmni kṛtaṃ nāma nāmni māyeti vā kṛtam | 
nāmni na yujyate nāma na pratiṣṭhā tv anāmani || 7 || 

ṣaṭsv iha dvitayaṃ heyaṃ catvāro bodhaye matāḥ | 
svapnatulyam idaṃ viśvam ittham uktaṃ jinottamaiḥ || 8 || 

kiṃ cāsthānato viddhi buddhasarvasvam uttamam | 
saṃvittyā sadguror yatnāc caryayā ca viśeṣataḥ || 9 || 

|| svapnaniruktiḥ samāptā ||

namo buddhāya | 

kalyāṇamitravācā ko 'pi samahimā jayaty anavagāhyaḥ | 
karatalagatam iva tattvaṃ yad vācām aviṣayaṃ dhatte || 1 || 

vajrācāryottamajñānāḥ sekatātparyasaṃgraham | 
viditvā kurmahe 'nekasekagranthād yathāgamam || 2 || 

prathamaṃ kalaśābhiṣeko dvitīyaṃ guhyam uttamam | 
prajñājñānaṃ tṛtīyaṃ vai caturthaṃ tat punas tathā || 3 || 

asyārthaḥ | prathamaṃ kalaśābhiṣeka iti | udakamukuṭavajraghaṇṭānām ācāryalakṣaṇāḥ ṣaṭ kalaśābhiṣekāḥ | bāhyavariṇeva bāhyamalasyāvidyāmalakṣālanāya sicyate 'neneti sekaḥ | eṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ kalaśavyāpārāt kalaśābhiṣekasaṃjñā | avaivartikābhiṣekāś caite ṣaṭ tathāgatasvabhāvāt | tathā hi udakābhiṣeka ādarśajñānātmako 

^ (110/235) 

'kṣobhyasvabhāvaḥ | mukuṭābhiṣekaḥ samatājñānātmako ratnasaṃbhavasvabhāvaḥ | vajrābhiṣekaḥ pratyavekṣaṇājñānātmako 'mitābhasvabhāvaḥ | adhipatyabhiṣekaḥ kṛtyānuṣṭhānarūpo 'moghasiddhisvabhāvaḥ | nāmābhiṣeko 'vidyānirodhād vidyānugatasuviśuddhadharmadhātujñānātmako vairocanasvarūpaḥ | ācāryābhiṣekas tu vajrasattvasvabhāvaḥ || 

atra ca pañcābhiṣeko vidyābhiṣekaḥ pañcasu locanādividyāyā vyāpārāt | 

atra cāvidyāmalakṣālanāyākṣobhyarūpeṇa vajrācāryeṇa vairocanarūpālambini śiṣye salilābhiṣeko deyaḥ | evaṃ sarvatrāhaṃkāraḥ || 

āgāmibuddhabhāvaś citoṣṇīṣabījabhūto

mukuṭābhiṣekaḥ | 

^ (112/233) 

dvādaśāṅguliparimāṇena dvādaśāṅgapratītyasamutpādaviśuddhyā vajram | yatra madhyavṛtte niruttaradharmatāsūcako hūṃkāraḥ | tasyārthaḥ | ha iti hetuviyuktaḥ | ū ity ūhāpagataḥ | aṃ ity apratiṣṭhitaḥ sarvadharma iti | yatra bhavasarojamukhāt sphuranti pañcārarūpiṇo munayaḥ pañcaskandhaviśuddhyā niṣkrāntā bhavaśarīreṇa | iha ca madhyārābhimukhatayā paryantārāṇāṃ sarvarūpādayo vijñānātmakā iti sūcyante | ete ca sarve sarvātmakā iti nivedayituṃ sarvatraiva caturaśrī || 

atha te dharmasya budhā hūṃnādaniveditātmarūpasya pañcāsivimuktakāyāḥ paryante cānyatra visphuritāḥ | śūnyatānimittāpraṇihitatāvabodhanārthaṃ sarvatra phullakātrayam | ete cādarśasamatāpratyavekṣaṇākṛtyānuṣṭhānasuviśuddhadharmadhātulakṣaṇapañcajñānātmakā gurūpadeśato jñeyāḥ || 

abhedyajñānasūcakaṃ ca samudāyārthaḥ |

adāhī avināśī ca śūnyatā vajram ucyate || 

	
abhedyaṃ vajram iti Hevajre | 

asya ca vajrābhiṣekasya vidhānam utpadyamānābhedyajñānabījādhānam eva vidhātum || 

evaṃ vajraghaṇṭāpi pūrvābhisaṃdhānena dvādaśāṅguliparimāṇādhomukhāmbhojavajrasamāpattir niḥsvabhāvena sarvadharmasvabhāvaṃ pratipādayitum | abhedyajñānanidānatāṃ dharmodayasya bodhayitum ūrdhvādhobhāge ca vajrāvalī yugalamālinī | tridhātukasvabhāvakūṭāgāratām asya khyāpayituṃ hārārdhahāraprakārāḥ | ata eva pañcatathāgatāṅkitaḥ | śūnyatākaruṇābhinnajñānakāraṇatām āvedayitum upari prajñāmukhākhyānam | asya ca dharmadhātusvarūpasya jñānasya vairocanādipañcatathāgatātmakatāṃ ca

rūpādipañcaskandhasvabhāvatāṃ ca pṛthivyādipañcadhāturūpatāṃ ca kathayitum ūrdhvaṃ pañcāraviracanā | atrāpi madhyasthitārābhimukhādyabhisaṃdhānaṃ pūrvavat | athābhiṣekam anayā svanantyā vajraghaṇṭayā kurvītānuttarāśeṣadharmabodhyadhikārakam | iha prādhānyakhyāpanāya hetukatāpratipādanāya ca kāraṇabhūtam api vajraghaṇṭābhiṣekam ullaṅghya prathamaṃ vajrābhiṣekadānam |

sarvadharmā nāmata iti pratipādanārthaṃ bhaviṣyanmunīndrapadocitanāmanidānāvadānārthaṃ ca pūrvanāmavyapanayena svadevatākulagotrānusāreṇa nāmābhiṣekaḥ | 

ācāryābhiṣekaś ca vajrasamayaghaṇṭāsamayamudrāsamayabhavyatānujñāvratavyākaraṇāśvāsalakṣaṇaḥ | itaḥ prabhṛty asaṃskṛtābhedayuganaddhavāhibodhasamayas tvam iti bodhayituṃ vajrasamayaḥ | caturaśītidharmaskandhasahasraparas tvam iti pratipādanāya ghaṇṭāsamayaḥ | sveṣṭadevatāsvabhāvas tvam iti khyāpayituṃ mudrāsamayaḥ | maṇḍalatattvaṃ maṇḍalaviśuddhilakṣaṇaṃ devatātattvaṃ devatāviśuddhilakṣaṇam ācāryaparikarma ca maṇḍalasādhanajñānaṃ pañcapradīpapañcāmṛtabhakṣaṇaṃ ca bhavyatā | tattvaṃ ca naiḥsvabhāvyam eṣām utpannakramapakṣataḥ | dharmacakrapravartanārtham anujñā |

^ (118/227)

bāhyavratanirākaraṇārthaṃ vajravratadānam | pṛthivyādisvabhāvatāsūcanāya vyākaraṇam | tathā hi bhuvo dharādeḥ svaḥ svarūpaṃ bhūr bhūyā iti bhūr bhuvaḥ svar ity asyārthaḥ | sarvāvaraṇavinirmuktaḥ sarvabuddhabodhisattvasamas tvam itaḥ prabhṛti bodhanārtham āśvāsaḥ | 

prajñāśraddhākṣetrīkaraṇārthaṃ samayarakṣaṇārthaṃ ca samānakālobhayasaṃpāditabodhicittapradānaṃ guhyābhiṣekaḥ | prajñopāyaguhyābhyāṃ dāpayata iti vyutpattiḥ | 

prajñājñānam ity atra

vyutpattidvayam | prajñāyā jñānam, prajñaiva bāhyā jñānam | tatra grāhyagrāhakākāradhāraṇī buddhiś caturdhātupañcaskandharūpādiṣaḍviṣayātmikāṅganāsvabhāvā prajñā | tasyā nimittabhūtāyā bodhicittajñānam iti pūrvā vyutpattiḥ | ākāradvayaśūnyā saiva vijñānam ity aparā vyutpattiḥ |

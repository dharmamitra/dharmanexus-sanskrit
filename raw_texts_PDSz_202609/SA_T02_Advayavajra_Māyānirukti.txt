11. Māyānirukti 

edited by MM Hara Prasad Shastri 
in Advayavajrasaṃgraha GOS 1927 Baroda

edited by Mikkyou-seiten-kenkyūkai  3.
in Taisho Daigaku Sougou Bukkyou Kenkyūjo Nenpou

^S p.44. 
^M (52/313) 

namo buddhāya || 

māyeva niḥsvabhāvaṃ ced jagad adhyakṣato budhāḥ | 
kimartham iha muhyanti jānanto 'pi sukhāśayāḥ || 1 || 

māyāvī kurute māyāṃ jvaladāgārabhāsvarām | 
kasyacit satyam ābhāti māyā māyaiva tadvidaḥ || 2 || 

māyātuṣṭer vinābhogaṃ ye kāmāḥ svayam āgatāḥ | 
māyayā tān asau bhuṅkte yad māyā sarvasaṃgatā || 3 || 

alīkatām ṛte hanta māyāyāṃ śāśvataṃ vada | 
dharmadhātor ajātatvād na cocchedaḥ prasajyate || 4 || 

dharme 'jātānirodhe vai jaḍatvaṃ nānugacchati | 
śūnyato jāyate dharmas tasmād anyā na dharmatā || 5 || 

ata eva hi sārvajñyaṃ buddhasya na vihanyate | 
anāropavaśāt sarvaṃ dharmacakraṃ pravartate || 6 || 

^M (54/311) 

pādaprasārikāṃ tyaktvā kṣiptvā mānādikalpanām | 
caryayā vicaret yogī susthito 'sthānayogataḥ || 7 || 

khānapānarasaṃ prāpya śuddhuam udghuṣya bhāṣayā | 
caryāṃ na vicared yas tu nāsau saṃbodhibhājanaḥ || 8 || 

vācā vakti janas tattvaṃ caryām apy anumodate | 
vittyanuṣṭhānasaṃpattyā saṃpanno durlabho janaḥ || 9 || 

mahī śayyā diśo vāso bhikṣābhaktaṃ ca bhojanam | 
ajātadharmatākṣāntiḥ kṛpānābhogavāhinī || 10 || 

janaṃ jayanti ye dharmā jīyante te 'pi caryayā | 
etat phalam ihādhyakṣam āstāṃ tāvad anuttaram || 11 || 

māyāṃ vivṛtya yat puṇyaṃ sulabdhaṃ sādhucetasā | 
tenādvaitapadaṃ yāntu lokā lokottare sthitāḥ || 12 || 

|| māyāniruktiḥ samāptā ||

Title: Saptākṣarasādhana 
Author: Durjayacandra 
Source: Sinclair 2014 
Tibetan: D1461 
Input: PDSz, silent corrections

namo vajraḍākāya | 

vistaro durgrahaḥ prāyo bālastrīṇāṃ bhaved iti | 
saṃkṣiptam api saṃkṣipya tat saptākṣaram uddharet || 1 
pūjādiśūnyatābodhimantraparyantam antarā | 
kṛtavān aṅgam adhyaṅgaṃ vajraḍākaṃ prasādhayet || 2 
yaṃ raṃ vaṃ laṃ caturbhūtamaṇḍalopari cintayet | 
suṃkārasambhavaṃ meruṃ saptaratnāṣṭaśṛṅgakam || 3 
oṃ medini vajrībhava vajrabandha hūṃ | 
oṃ vajraprākāra hūṃ vaṃ hūṃ | 
oṃ vajrapañjara hūṃ paṃ hūṃ | 
oṃ vajravitāna hūṃ khaṃ hūṃ | 
oṃ vajraśarajāla trāṃ śaṃ trāṃ | 
oṃ vajrajvālānalārka hūṃ hūṃ hūṃ | 
vidhāya vajrabhūmyādiṣaṭkaṃ ṣaḍāram ālikhet | 
viśvābjasaṃsthitaṃ cakraṃ viśvavajrāvalīvṛtam || 4 
hūṃbhavaṃ dhyāyed atraiva vajraḍākaṃ mahāsukham | 
ṣaḍbhujaṃ trimukhaṃ tryakṣaṃ sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitam || 5 
vyañjanāśītisaṃyuktam ālikālyudbhavaṃ prabhum | 
vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannaṃ naracarmordhvadhāriṇam || 6 
vāme kapālakhaṭvāṅgaṃ triśūlaṃ dakṣiṇe kare | 
kapālamālāmukutaṃ viśvavajrajaṭādharam || 7 
ardhenduśekharaṃ caiva ṣaṇmudrādehabhūṣaṇam | 
nīlapītaharitavaktraṃ vyāghracarmāmbarāvṛtam || 8 
ālīḍhākrāntasūryasthabhairavakālarātrikam | 
yathā nāthasya tathā vajravārāhyāpi bhujādikam || 9 

devī jānū samāveṣṭya paramānandavihvalā || 10 
jñānākarṣaṇādikaṃ kuryāt | jaḥ hūṃ vaṃ hor ebhir akṣaraiḥ || 
ṣaḍ yoginyaḥ ṣaḍāreṣu vāmāvartavivartanaiḥ | 
herukī prathamā devī dvitīyā vajrabhairavī || 11 
tṛtīyā ghoracaṇḍī ca caturthī vajrabhāskarī | 
pañcamī vajraraudrī ca ṣaṣṭhī syād vajraḍākinī || 12 
nīlā ca pītā raktā haritā dhūmrā sitā devyaḥ | 
muktakeśā mahāraudrās trinetrāś ca digambarāḥ || 13 
raṇaḍḍamarughaṇṭāś ca hastetarakaradvaye | 

dadhānā naracarmāṇi pretasūryopari sthitāḥ || 14 
kapālamālāmukuṭā ālīḍhāsanasaṃsthitāḥ | 
ṣaḍakṣarabhavā bhavyā devyaḥ sarvā yathākramam || 15 
lāṃ māṃ pāṃ tāṃ khaṃ ity ebhiḥ pañcadhātubhir adhiṣṭhitāḥ | oṃ aṃ hrīṃ hūṃ hoḥ hrīḥ kāraiḥ skandhān rūpādikān api | bhrūṃ hūṃ khaṃ āḥ ho haṃ kārair āyatanāni śodhayet | oṃ haḥ | namaḥ hiḥ | svāhā hūṃ | vauṣaṭ he | hūṃ hūṃ hoḥ | phaṭ phaṭ | uraḥśiraḥskandhanetrādiṣu yojayet | oṃ vaṃ | hāṃ yoṃ | hriṃ moṃ | hreṃ hrīṃ | hūṃ hūṃ | phaṭ phaṭ | nābhihṛdvaktreṣu mūrdhni śikhāyām anyatra vinyaset | kāyavākcitteṣu oṃ āḥ hūṃ kāram arpayet |
ṣaṭcakravarticakrasthadevīvṛndakarasthitaiḥ | 
pañcāmṛtadravāpūrṇaiḥ kapālair abhiṣecayet || 16 
pūjāstutyamṛtāsvādaṃ kṛtvā ca bhāvanāṃ caret | 
saptākṣaraṃ japen mantrī nirviśaṅkena cetasā || 17 
oṃ hrīḥ ha ha hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ | 
dhyānajapād yadā khinnaḥ tadā śuddhim anusmaret || 18 
smṛtisambodhyaṅgaṃ śrīherukaḥ | dharmapravicayasambodhyaṅgaṃ herukī | vīryasambodhyaṅgaṃ vajrabhairavī | prītisambodhyaṅgaṃ ghoracaṇḍī | prasrabdhisambodhyaṅgaṃ vajrabhāskarī | samādhisambodhyaṅgaṃ vajraraudrī | upekṣāsambodhyaṅgaṃ vajraḍākinī | saptabodhyaṅgabhāvanā | 
kaṇṭhikā rucakaṃ ratnaṃ kuṇḍalaṃ bhasma sūtrakam | 
ṣaḍ vai pāramitā etā mudrārūpeṇa yojitāḥ || 19 || 

durjayacandroddhṛtaṃ saptākṣarasādhanaṃ samāptam ||

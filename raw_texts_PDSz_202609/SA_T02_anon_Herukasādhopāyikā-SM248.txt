Sādhanamālā 248 [= Tōh. 1627]
Mahāmāyāsādhana (anon) 
[ = NGMPP B 24/12, DSCN9071 = 952 ff.]

vajraḍākaṃ namaskṛtya mahāmāyātmakaṃ jinam | 
āmnāyasaṅgatas tasya sūcyate sādhanakramaḥ || 
śrāddho maitrīkṛpāyogāt saṃbodhau kṛtamānasaḥ | 
dānaśīlādiyukto 'ṣṭalokadharmaiḥ samāśayaḥ || 

mantratantrādisiddhijño yogyācārakṣame kecit |

sthāne sukhopaviṣṭaḥ san hṛtsūrye nyastahūṃkṛtiḥ || 
tatprabhodbhavapūjābhiḥ saṃpūjya purato dhiyam | 
kṛtvāghadeśanādyaṃ tu śūnyatāṃ bhāvayet tataḥ || 

oṃ śūnyatājñānavajrasvabhāvātmako 'ham iti manasā paṭhet || 

vyomni raktacatuṣpatrapadmasthe sūryamaṇḍale | 
pañca bindūn sitān dhyātvā spharasaṃhārakārakān || 
caturdigbindusaṃbhūtā nṛtyantaḥ saṅgamotsukāḥ | 

pūrābhyastamahāmaitrīkaruṇādisvabhāvikāḥ | 
catasro 'pi ca ḍākinyaś codayantīti cintayet ||

hale sahi viasia kamalu pabbohiu vajjeṃ | 

alalalala ho mahāsuheṇa ārāhiu nacceṃ | 

ravikiraṇa pahulliu kamalu mahāsuheṇa || 

tataḥ saṃsāraduḥkhebhyaḥ sattvābhyuddharaṇāśayaḥ | 
māyopamaṃ jagattattvaṃ yathābhūtaṃ vilokya saḥ | 
madhyabindūdbhavaṃ nīlaṃ hūṃbījāt karuṇāmayam | 

pañcajñānatrikāyātmaparamānandasundaram | 
caturmukhaṃ caturbāhuṃ bhasmoddhūlitam asya ca |

mūlaṃ śarīravan nīlaṃ mukhaṃ pītaṃ tu dakṣiṇam | 
pṛṣṭhataḥ sitaraktābhaṃ vāmaṃ marakatojjvalam | 
trinetraṃ naracarmādimudrāpañcakabhūṣitam | 
piṅgordhvamūrdhajākṣobhyaṃ kapālamukuṭotkaṭam | 
kapālapāṇinaṃ savye vāme khaṭvāṅgacāpinam | 
raśmikoṭisphuraccitrabuddhaiḥ kṛtajagaddhitam | 

svābhayā buddhaḍākinyā saṃśliṣṭaṃ vā ratotsave | 
nṛtyantaṃ herukaṃ dhyāyāc caturdevīkṣitānanam | 

ḍākinīnāṃ caturdikṣu cihnādikam ucyate || 

tatra pūrvadale vajraḍākinī nīlavarṇikā | 
nīlapītasitāraktaharitāsyacatuṣṭayā | 

pṛthivyādarśajñānādiśuddhā vajraṃ kapālakam | 

savye dadhāti vāme tu ghaṇṭākhaṭvāṅgadhārikā |

dakṣiṇe samatājñānatoyadhātvābhirūpikā | 
śrīratnaḍākinī ratnacchaṭātriśūlahastikā || 
vāme nipīḍitagrīvajambukasatpatākikā |

pītanīlasitāraktaharitābhacaturmukhī | 
paścime tu dale padmaḍākinī dhavalāruṇā | 
syāt tejaḥpratyavekṣādisvabhāvā navayauvanā || 
sitāruṇadrutasuvarṇanīlaharitanibhānanā | 

viśvapadmaṃ śaraṃ savye kapālaṃ dhanur anyataḥ ||
uttare haritapītaśuklaraktāsitānanā | 
kṛtyānuṣṭhānavāyvādiviśuddhā viśvaḍākinī | 

khaṭvāṅgaḍamarukaṃ savye vāme pāśaṃ kapālakam | 

satrinetrāś caturvaktrāḥ sarvā devyaś caturbhujāḥ | 
śṛṅgārādirasāsaṅgā lakṣaṇavyañjanojjvalāḥ | 
raudrāsanasthā nṛtyantyo nāthābhā mudrikādibhiḥ |

kuryāt ṣaḍaṅgavinyāsaṃ nāthasyāvāhanādikam | 
abhiṣekaṃ jinaiḥ pūjāṃ stutyanurāgaṇādikam |  
lalāṭakaṇṭhayoś cakṣuḥśrotraghrāṇe hṛdi nyaset | 
oṃ āḥ a khe ci ra ity evaṃ rephād ūrdhvaṃ ca hūṃkṛtiḥ ||

vajracchaṭāsamāyogair ānīya jñānamaṇḍalam | 
arghadānādipūrvaṃ ca praveśyaikīkuryāt svayam ||

sārdhaṃ samayacakreṇa viśiṣṭaphalasiddhaye | 
rasapraveśāt tāmrādirajatībhāvasiddhavat ||

saṃcodya diggatān nāthān hṛdayasthahṛdarciṣā | 

tatprabhodbhavavidyābhir bhṛtakumbhāmṛtāmbubhiḥ ||
abhiṣekāt prabhor maulāv akṣobhyo divyayoṣitām | 

punaḥ śāśvataratneśāmitābhāmoghasiddhayaḥ | 
samādhyutsṛṣṭadevībhiḥ satpūjāvyagrapāṇibhiḥ | 

pūjayet yoginīyuktam ātmānaṃ herukaṃ stuyāt ||

daṃṣṭrotkaṭamahābhīmamantrasragdāmabhūṣitam | 

bhakṣamāṇamahāmāṃsaṃ śrīherukaṃ namāmy aham 

iti stutigītiḥ ||

śirohṛnnābhiguhye syād ūruyugmādike kramāt | 

oṃ hūṃ svā āḥ hā bījaṃ dhyātvā pañcakulātmikām | 
hrīḥkāraraktaguhyābjāṃ bhrūṃbhūtapītakarṇikām | 
śrībuddhaḍākinīṃ svābhāṃ vīkṣya raktāṃ ratotsukām | 
gītyā saṃcodyamānaḥ san caturdevībhir āryayā ||

oṃjātamaṇihūṃvajro vicitrakaraṇādibhiḥ | 

jinān anurāgayāmīty ārabhed raticintayā | 
vajrābjasphālaphaṭśabdair ānīyāsyaiḥ praveśitam | 

jinavṛndaṃ sukhāveśāt drutaṃ vajravinirgatam |

padme saccittam āsvādya jihvayā vidhivat tataḥ | 

caturbhūtasvabhāve hṛdraktapadme caturdale | 
sūryasthabinduś candreṇa saṃpuṭitaḥ suveṣṭitaḥ |

mṛṇālasūtropamasārdhasapta-
rekhābhir āptākṣarapaṅktibhiḥ saḥ | 

niścārya nādaṃ tata ūrdhvam indu-
cchidre praveśyānugamāgamaḥ syāt ||

uccārayan spharet prāṇair mantramāyām asaṃhṛtam | 

tryakṣaraṃ ca tathā kuryān nādapraveśanirgamau | 

ūrdhvaṃ nirgacchati vāyau tryakṣaradhvanir nirgacchati | 

praviśati sati praviśati | 
tiṣṭhati sati vāyau sūkṣmāsitabindumadhye tiṣṭhati | 
evaṃ granthitasvaravyañjanāḥ sārdhasaptaguṇarekhāpraveśasthitinirgamāḥ |

mandamandam anyatrāgatapracāracittena nidhyeyāḥ | 

oṃ āḥ hūṃ iti tryakṣarajapyamantraḥ |

utthātukāmaḥ pūjādyaṃ kṛtvā dṛṣṭvā ca duḥkhitam | 
jagat tattvadṛśā muktaṃ kuśalaṃ pariṇāmayet || 

yan mayopacitaṃ puṇyaṃ vajraḍākasya bhāvanāt | 
tenāhaṃ vajraḍākaḥ syāṃ jagannāthapadāptaye ||
utthāya piṇḍapātādiśrutavyākhyādikāṃ kriyām | 
svaparaherukākāro māyākāra ivācaret || 
hūṃkārādhiṣṭhitaṃ bhojyaṃ āḥkārajakapālagam |

adho vāyvagnicakreṇa jvālitaṃ nirmalīkṛtam | 
ūrdhvam oṃkāram ācintya abhimantryāmṛtāyitam | 
jihvāvajrāṃśunalikākṛṣṭenānena tarpayet || 

oṃ āḥ hūṃ guhyābhakṣā bhakṣe 'ham iti amṛtāsvādamantraḥ ||

prāg iva snānavelāyām abhiṣekavidhiṃ bhajet |

catasṛṣv api sandhyāsu dhyāyād evaṃ samāsataḥ ||

sādhanaṃ vajraḍākasya kṛtvā labdhaṃ śubhaṃ mayā | 

yat tenādyajano bhūyāt tadahaṃkṛtibhūṣaṇaḥ || 

mahāmāyātantrānusāreṇa herukasādhanopayikā samāptā ||   ||

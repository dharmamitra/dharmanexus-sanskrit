tataḥ śiṣyo 'pi gurūpadeśena yāvadicchaṃ sattvān saṃsphārya bodhicittaṃ saṃlakṣyotsṛjya svayaṃ padmasthaṃ jihvayā saṃgṛhya pibet | prajñaiva mukhena mukhe dadyād iti <kecit> | 

tato 'nurūpasatpūjādakṣiṇābhyarthitas tayoḥ samutkuṭukayoḥ prāgvat svavāmakaragau karau kṛtvā tanmūrdhni savyaṃca dadyād vidyāvrataṃ guruḥ—

adyārabhya mayā samyag dattā kanyāsya sādhake | 
sādhanāya mahābodher vijānantu tathāgatāḥ ||

iyaṃ te dhāraṇī ramyā sevyā buddhaiḥ prakalpitā | 
nānyopāyena buddhatvaṃ śuddhaṃ cedaṃ jagattrayam ||
advayāḥ sarvadharmās te dvayabhāvena lakṣitāḥ | 

tasmād viyogaṃ saṃsāre na kāryaṃ bhavatānayā ||
idaṃ tat sarvabuddhānāṃ vidyāvratam anuttaram | 
atikrāmati yo mūḍhaḥ siddhis tasya na cottamā ||

iti vidyāvratam dadyād iti ||   ||

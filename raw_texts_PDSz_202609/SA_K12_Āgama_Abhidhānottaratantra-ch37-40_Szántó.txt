Abhidhānottaratantra ch. 37 [Tib. & Ś. 40] 

 = Lokesh Chandra facsimile ed. 
 = 1138 AD palm-leaf codex

[ 129-132 desunt, likely incipit *133]

athāparaṃ pravakṣyāmi ḍākinināṃ tu lakṣanaṃ ||
yena samyagvijānīyāt ḍākinyaḥ samaye sthitāḥ ||
raktagaurā tu yā nārī padmagandhaṃ vimuñcati ||
saumyadṛṣṭi pratyekaṃ saṃraktadarśānāntayā ||
nakhā hi yasyā nāryāyāḥ saṃrakte nayane tathā ||
gṛhe ca likhitaṃ padmaṃ padmanarte kulodbhavā ||
bhrūmadhyagataṃ triśūlaṃ syāmāpāṇḍalasarīrañ ca satataṃ vajrakulavibhāvitāṃ ||

nityaṃ snānaṃ ratā yā ca vacayā na ca bahubhāṣiṇīṃ |

vajraṃ gṛha pūjyate satataṃ likhitaṃ śubhaṃ [|]

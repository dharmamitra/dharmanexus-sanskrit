buddhaṃ jagac chuddhaṃ buddhabodhena bandhunā | 
ādiśuddha mahābuddha kiṃ buddhaṃ buddhaśāsane || 1 || 

idaṃ nivṛttapratipakṣatattvaphalavikalpasya sahajanirvedhabhājaḥ svābhāvikaṃ vacaḥ | 

jñānam anāvilaṃ śūnyam anābhogakṛpātmakam | 
pratītya jāyate tac ca svabhāvābhāvavarjitam || 2 || 

anena nirvedhapratirūpam ākhyāti | 

dharmāṇāṃ śūnyatā vāyu kṛpā vāyu garīyasī | 
vāyu saṃvarasāmarthyaṃ vāyu śuddhā ca saṃvṛtiḥ || 3 || 

^M (84/253)

anena nirbharasahajodgāram udgirati | 

hā kiṃ brūmaḥ kathaṃ brūmo brūmo vā kva nu te janāḥ | 
buddhāvṛttir bhaved yeṣām anābhogārthaśālinī || 4 || 

etena karuṇayā 'pratiṣṭhitanirvacanīyā dharmagambhīranayādhimuktikapuruṣadurlabhatām āvedayati | 

svakarmasūtrabījād dhi marmacchedi phalaṃ mama | 
nāparebhyo mayā hiṃsro 'py evaṃ sahite + + + || 5 || 

anena svakarmasūtragrathitopahatamatisakalasattvaparigrahaṃ kurvāṇo bodhisattvānāṃ hṛdayam ācaṣṭe |

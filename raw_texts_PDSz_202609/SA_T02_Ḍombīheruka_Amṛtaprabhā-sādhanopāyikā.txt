Amṛtaprabhāsādhanopāyikā of Ḍombīheruka

 = Matsunaga 307 IV.
 = Sādhanamālā 228. 
 = readings reported by  editor

 p. 443.

[ 20r] oṃ namo nairātmāyai || 

ḍombīherukapādair devīcakrasya bhāvanā kathitā | 
saṃpiṇḍya sakalaṃ tattvaṃ hevajrād yoginītantrāt || [1*]

nabhasi bhaṭṭārakaṃ dṛṣṭvā gurukuliśeśvaraprabhum | 
pūjayed vandayed dhīmānn akṣobhyaṃ pratibimbakam || [2*] 

svabījaṃ hṛdaye dhyātvā pūjāmeghaṃ prakalpayet | 
aṣṭābhir mātṛbhir nityaṃ pañcāmṛtaprayogataḥ || [3*]

gauryādyāḥ pañca yoginyaḥ pañcāmṛtadharā matāḥ | 
śavarīcaṇḍālīḍombyaḥ rasaḍarvāliṅganaiḥ(?) || [4*] 

arghaṃ ca deśayen nityaṃ puṇyaṃ caivānumodayet | 
triratnaṃ śaraṇaṃ yānti bodhau citto bibharti ca || [5*] 

 p. 444.

prathamaṃ maitrīṃ dhyātvā tadanu karuṇāṃ vibhāvayet | 
punar api muditāṃ dhyātvā upekṣāṃ sarvaśeṣataḥ || [6*]

tadanu paṭhij(?) jinamantrai (|?) [7*]

oṃ śūnyatājñānavajrasvabhāvātmako 'haṃ || [8*] 

svaparaṃ vibhāvya śū[ 21r]nyaṃ praṇidhānam anusmared yogī | [9*]

repheṇa sūryaṃ purato vibhāvya 
tasmin ravau hūṃbhavaviśvavajram | 
tenaiva vajreṇa vibhāvayec ca 
prākārakaṃ pañjarabandhanaṃ ca || 

khadhātau cintayed dhīmān prajñām ekārarūpiṇīm | 
tasyāṃ vibhāvayet pṛthvīṃ caturasrāṃ harītikām || 

pṛthvīmaṇḍalamadhyasthāṃ abmaṇḍalaṃ vibhāvayet | 
apsu tejaḥ punar dadyād agnau ca marunmaṇḍalam || 

marunmaṇḍalamadhyastham ātmānaṃ cintayet tataḥ | 
miśrībhūya bhaved cakram ekākāreṇa cātmanā || 

catuṣkoṇaṃ caturdvāraṃ aṣṭastambhopaśobhitam | 
hārārdhahārasaṃyuktaṃ vajrasūtrair alaṃkṛtam || 

tasmiṃś cakre gataprāṇān pañcadaśaṃ vibhāvayet | 
yoginīkakubhoddeśāt āsanaṃ pañcadaśaṃ matam || 

āsanopari candraṃ ca candrasyopari bhāskaram | 
anayor madhyagataṃ bījaṃ mūlasvarādikaṃ nyaset || 

ādarśasamatājñānau candrasūryau prakīrtitau | 
aṃkārasambhavā kartriḥ pratyavekṣaṇam ucyate || 

 p. 445. 

eṣām aikyam anuṣṭhānaṃ niṣpattiḥ śuddhadharmatā | 
pañcajñānā[ 21v]tmakaṃ dehaṃ yoginīnāṃ prakīrtitam || 

candrārkābhyāṃ ca kartryā śabalībhūtaṃ tathā jalam | 
miśrībhūya punaḥ paścāt jñeyā nairātmyayoginī || 

kṛṣṇavarṇā mahāghorā pañcamudrāvibhūṣitā | 
savye kartrir mahābhīmā 'vasavye padmabhājanam |
avasavye vajrakhaṭvāṅgaṃ vyāghracarmāvṛtā kaṭau || 

a ā i ī u ca kathitāni tu nairātmyādīnām, 
u ṛ Ṛ ḷ iti ca proktāni pukkasyādīnām, 

Ḷ e ai o bījānīti gauryādīnām, 
au aṃ bīje punar api kathite adhordhvavāsinyāḥ | 

cittaśāśvataratneśāmitābhāmoghasiddhibhiḥ tān mudrayet nairātmyādīn prajñāpuṭe vyavasthitān | 

cittaśāśvataratneśaamitāyus tathāgataiḥ | 
vidiksthān pukkasyādīn mudrayed anulomataḥ || 

dveṣamohapiśunais tu rāgavajreṇa mudraṇam | 
yoginīnāṃ catasṚṇāṃ dvārasthānāṃ sadaiva tu || 

kāyavāṅmudraṇaṃ kuryāt adhordhveṣu samāhitaḥ | 
mudryate lakṣaṇeneti mudraṇaṃ tena bhaṇyate ||

indre vajrā yame gaurī vāruṇyāṃ vāriyoginī | 
kaubere vajraḍākī ca madhye nairātmyayoginī || 

aiśāne pukkasī khyātā pāvake śabarī tathā | 
caṇḍālī rākṣasāśāyāṃ vāyavye ḍombinī tathā || 

 p. 446.

pūrvadvāre punar gaurī dakṣiṇe caurikā tathā | 
vetālī paścime dvāre uttare ghasmarī tathā || 

adhare bhūcarī khyātā ūrdhve ca khecarī smṛtā | 
paramānandadāḥ sarvā yathā nairātmyayoginī || 

svabījaṃ hṛdaye dhyātvā raśmīn niścārayet tataḥ | 
ākṛṣya tena sambuddhān pūjayed aṣṭamātṛbhiḥ || 

paścād anunāthanāṃ vidadhīta bhagavatsu mahātmasu | 
bhagavantaḥ sarvatathāgatā abhiṣekapadaṃ māṃ dadantu || 

paścād amṛtabhṛtaiḥ kumbhair abhiṣicyate budhaḥ | 
secayan sarvasambuddhaiḥ citteśamukuṭo bhavet || 

prathamaṃ bhāvayet kṛṣṇāṃ dvitīye raktāṃ vibhāvayet | 
tṛtīye bhāvayet pītāṃ caturthe haritāṃ tathā || 

pañcame nīlavarṇābhāṃ ṣaṣṭhe suśukladehikām | 
ṣaḍaṅgāṃ bhāvayed yogī paścād varṇavivarjitām || 

rūpaskandhe bhavet vajrā gaurī vedanāyāṃ smṛtā | 
saṃjñāyāṃ vāriyoginī saṃskāre vajraḍākinī || 

vijñānaskandharūpeṇa sthitā nairātmyayoginī | 
maṇḍale sā sthitā bhīmā bhayasyāpi bhayaṅkarī || 

pṛthivī pukkasī khyātā abdhātuḥ śabarī matā | 
tejaś caṇḍālinī jñeyā vāyur ḍombī prakīrtitā || 

rūpe gaurī sadā khyātā śabde caurī prakīrtitā | 
vetālī gandhaviṣaye rase ca ghasmarī tathā || 

 p. 447.

sparśe ca bhūcarī khyātā khecarī dharmadhātutaḥ | 
sadā hy asāraviśuddhyā vai sidhyanti tattvayoginaḥ || 

cakṣuṣor mohavajrī ca karṇayor dveṣavajrikā | 
ghrāṇe mātsaryakī khyātā vaktre ca rāgavajrikā | 
sparśe īrṣyāvajrī ca mano nairātmyayoginī || 

kavacam ebhir mahāśuddhyā indriyāṇāṃ viśuddhaye |
navamāryaṃ ghṛtaṃ muṇḍaṃ kartrikā devacchedinī || 

kapālakleśamuṇḍena skandhasyopari cāśanam |
gurvācāryeṣṭadevasya namanārthaṃ cakrikā dhṛtā || 

durbhāṣasyāśravaṇāya guror vajradharasya ca | 
kuṇḍalaṃ śravaṇayor dhāryaṃ mantraṃ japtuṃ ca kaṇṭhikā || 

rucakaḥ prāṇivadhaṃ tyaktuṃ mudrāṃ bhajituṃ ca mekhalām | 
pañcabuddhasya sūtreṇa śarīraṃ mudritaṃ sadā || 

mantraḥ | oṃ a ā i ī u ū ṛ Ṛ ḷ Ḷ e ai o au aṃ aḥ svāhā | 

mātrāviśuddhiṃ kathayāmi ––

akṣobhyaś cakrirūpeṇāmitābhaḥ kuṇḍalātmakaḥ | 
ratneśaḥ kaṇṭhamālāyāṃ haste vairocanaḥ smṛtaḥ || 

mekhalāyāṃ sthito 'moghaḥ pañcaite śuddhamātrikāḥ | 
doṣaparihāṇito dhāryā yoginībhiḥ sadā dhṛtāḥ || 

adhyātmarūpaṃ kathayāmi –– 

strīrūpeṇa vaśaṃ loke krodharūpeṇa trāsanam | 
ālikālisamāpattyā bāhyadvandvaṃ niṣevitam || 

 p. 448.

purakṣobhamantraḥ –– akacaṭatapayasa svāhā | 

sārvabhautikabalimantraḥ –– oṃ akāro mukhaṃ sarvadharmāṇām ādyam anutpannatvāt oṃ āḥ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā | 

nairātmyayoginyā[ś caiva] saṃvaraṃ kathayāmi te |
mahāmudrā sthitā nābhau mahāsukhakarā śubhā || 
tīkṣṇatvād agnirūpeṇa ruṣitā sā tu mātṛbhiḥ || 

ādisvarasvabhāvā sā dhīti buddhaiḥ prakalpitā |
vyāpya tiṣṭhati traidhātuṃ na jñātā sā pṛthagjanaiḥ ||

nairātmyakamadhyasthaṃ ca anāhatam adhomukham | 
sravaty anirvṛttakayogena bodhicittaṃ sadaiva tu || 

tasmāt kāye 'tra ye [nāḍyo] lalanārasanāvadhūtayaḥ | 
kāyavākcittarūpeṇāvatīrṇāḥ kartrimudrikāḥ || 

lalanā prajñāsvabhāvena rasanopāyena saṃsthitā | 
avadhūtī madhyadeśe tu mahāsukhādhārarūpiṇī || 

akṣobhyāvahā lalanā rasanā raktapravāhinī | 
avadhūty amitanāthasya ādhārabhāvinī sadā || 

karmamudrāsukhāvatyāṃ trayo nāḍyaḥ svakālataḥ | 
sravanti kāyavākcittaṃ vajraratnāñjajanminam || 

mūtrakālas tu vajrasya vamantaṃ ratnadhāriṇam | 
abhisaṃbuddharūpeṇāmitanāthasya kathyate || 

sadaivotpadyate jñānam uktaṃ vāvasare 'pi vā | 
sahajaṃ paramaṃ saukhyaṃ pitror api sukhāvaham || 

 p. 449.

yathā niśāyāṃ hi bhunakti kaścit 
veśmāntare dīpitadīpakaḥ san | 
paryeṣṭikartus tanubhis tamovat 
mohāndhakāreṇa sukhaṃ tathā syāt || 

jñātvaiṣaṃ bhavasāgaraṃ viṣajalaṃ rāgādinakrākulaṃ 
gambhīrālaharīlatāvighaṭitaṃ saukhaikatīraṃ mahat | 
kasmāt bodhidhiyo janāḥ kṣaṇam api svarthaikaniṣṭhodyatāḥ 
pāraṃ yānti na mohapāśavasanaṃ tyaktvā prayānty adbhutam || 

utpattikramabhāvanāṃ prathamataḥ kuryāt sudhīḥ śāśvataṃ 
mudrāmantraviśuddhimātrakavacaṃ sarvān na vai vismaret | 
paścād utkarṣam anvitaḥ pratimuhus tattvaikacetāḥ sudhīr 
utpannakramabhāvanābhavam idaṃ dhyāyāt samastaṃ sadā || 

vyāghrāsīnaś cared yo vividhaphaṇivarair bhūṣito yaḥ kṣamāvān 
bāḍhaṃ sambhogayute vividhamadhuyute kaṅkagehākhyanāmni | 
tenedaṃ sādhanendraṃ parahitarucinā guhyaprajñāṅgasaṅgaṃ 
lokārthodbhāsitaṃ yat kṣamata jinasutā bhāṣitaṃ yan mayedam || 

|| amṛtaprabhā nāma sādhanopāyikā samāptā ||

Title: Mañjuśrīparipṛcchā (a.k.a. *Dharmaśaṅkhasūtra) (incomplete)
Source: Vinītā 2010, no. 20
Tibetan: D 172 
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó 
	% NB some of the lost portions are available in the Kriyāsamuccaya and extracts! 

1. evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavāñ śrāvastyāṃ viharati sma samantaprāsāde mahatā bhikṣusaṃghena sārdham ardhatrayodaśabhir bhikṣuśatair aprameyair bodhisattvair mahāsattvair anekaiś ca vaineyaśatasahasrair avalokiteśvarapramukhair bodhisattvaiḥ | aniñjyamānena kāyena bhagavān ratnasiṃhāsane niṣaṇṇaḥ | 

2. atha khalu mañjuśrīr bodhisattvo mahāsattvo buddhānubhāvenotthāyāsanād ekāṃsam uttarāsaṅgaṃ kṛtvā dakṣiṇaṃ jānumaṇḍalaṃ pṛthivyāṃ pratiṣṭhāpya yena bhagavāṃs tenāñjaliṃ praṇamya bhagavantam etad avocat |

3. kiṃ bhagavan katipramāṇaṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ yena mahāpuṇyenānekakoṭīniyutaśatasahasravaineyābhiprāyaparipūraṇas tathāgatasya dharmaśaṅkho bhavati |

4. evam ukte bhagavān mañjuśriyaṃ kumārabhūtaṃ bodhisattvaṃ mahāsattvam etad avocat | acintyam idaṃ mañjuśrīḥ prajñāmayaṃ mahākaruṇāparibhāvitaṃ mahāpuṇyaṃ yena mahāpuṇyenānekakoṭīniyutaśatasahasravaineyābhiprāyaparipūraṇas tathāgatasya dharmaśaṅkho bhavati |

5. yāvanto mañjuśrī jāmbudvīpakāḥ sattvāḥ sarve te daśakuśalakarmapathasamanvāgatā bhaveyuḥ, teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ sattvānāṃ yaḥ puṇyaskandhas tenaiva puṇyaskandhena śataguṇitena rājā bhavati jambudvīpeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ |
	% NB no Tib. 

6. yāvanto mañjuśrī jāmbudvīpakāḥ sattvāḥ sarve te jambudvīpeśvarasya puṇyaskandhena samanvāgatā bhaveyuḥ, teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ sattvānāṃ yaḥ puṇyaskandhas tenaiva puṇyaskandhena śataguṇitena rājā bhavati cakravartī caturdvīpeśvaraḥ prabhuḥ saptaratnasamanvāgataḥ | tasyemāni saptaratnāni bhavanti | tadyathā cakraratnaṃ hastiratnam aśvaratnaṃ maṇiratnaṃ strīratnaṃ gṛhapatiratnaṃ pariṇāyakaratnam eva saptamam | sahasraṃ cāsya putrāṇāṃ bhavanti śūrāṇāṃ vīrāṇāṃ varāṅgarūpiṇāṃ parasainyapramardakānām | evaṃ mañjuśrīr maharddhiko mahānubhāvo rājā bhavati cakravartī |

7. yāvanto mañjuśrīś cāturdvīpikāyāṃ lokadhātau sattvāḥ sarve te cakravartipuṇyaskandhena samanvāgatā bhaveyuḥ, teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ sattvānāṃ yaḥ puṇyaskandhas tenaiva puṇyaskandhena sahasraguṇitena śakro bhavati devānām indraḥ | evaṃ maharddhiko mahānubhāvo devānām indraḥ |

8. yāvanto mañjuśrīś cāturdvīpikāyāṃ lokadhātau sattvāḥ sarve te śakrasya puṇyaskandhena samanvāgatā bhaveyuḥ, teṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ sattvānāṃ yaḥ puṇyaskandha tenaiva puṇyaskandhena śatasahasra[explicit Ms]

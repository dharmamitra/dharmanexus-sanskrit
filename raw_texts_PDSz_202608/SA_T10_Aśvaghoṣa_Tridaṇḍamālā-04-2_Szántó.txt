Title: Tridaṇḍamālā (4.2 Anusmṛti) 
Author: Aśvaghoṣa/scriptural 
Source: Ueno & Fukita 2023 
Tibetan: Ø 
Input: PDSz 30.xi.2025 Budapest 

[1] evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavāñ chrāvastyāṃ viharati, jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme. tatra bhagavān bhikṣūn āmantrayate sma. 

[2] yaḥ kaścid bhikṣavo bhikṣur vā bhikṣuṇī vā stokastokaṃ muhūrtamuhūrtam antato 'cchaṭāsaṃghātamātrakam api tathāgatam ākārataḥ samanusmarati. ity api sa bhagavān tathāgato 'rhan samyaksaṃbuddho vidyācaraṇasaṃpannaḥ sugato lokavid anuttaraḥ puruṣadamyasārathiḥ śāstā devamanuṣyāṇāṃ buddho bhagavān iti. 

[3] sa evaṃ tathāgatam ākārataḥ samanusmaran, labhate cetasaḥ prasādaṃ cittasyaikāgratām, cittaṃ sthāpayati saṃsthāpayaty upasthāpayati damayati śamayaty upaśamayaty ekotīkaroti samādhatte. śraddhāṃ pratilabhate, vīryaṃ smṛtiṃ samādhiṃ prajñāṃ pratilabhate. 

[4] ayam ucyate, bhikṣur bhikṣuṇī vā śāstuḥ śāsanakaro 'nirākṛtadhyāyī vidarśanayā samanvāgato bṛṃhayitā śūnyāgārāṇāṃ svārthayogam anuyukto viharaty agarhito vijñaiḥ sabrahmacāribhir amoghaṃ rāṣṭrapiṇḍaṃ paribhuṅkte. 

[5] tasmāt tarhi bhikṣava evaṃ śikṣitavyam. yat stokastokaṃ muhūrtamuhūrtam antato 'cchaṭāsaṃghātamātrakam api tathāgatam ākārataḥ samanusmarati. ity api sa bhagavān tathāgato 'rhat samyaksaṃbuddho vidyācaraṇasaṃpannaḥ sugato lokavid anuttaraḥ puruṣadamyasārathiḥ śāstā devamanuṣyāṇāṃ buddho bhagavān iti. lapsyāmahe cetasaḥ prasādaṃ cittasyaikāgratām, cittaṃ sthāpayiṣyāmaḥ saṃsthāpayiṣyāma upasthāpayiṣyāmo damayiṣyāmaḥ śamayiṣyāma upaśamayiṣyāma ekotīkariṣyāmaḥ samādhāsyāmahe, śraddhāṃ pratilapsyāmahe, vīryaṃ smṛtiṃ samādhiṃ prajñāṃ pratilabhapsyāmaha ity evaṃ vo bhikṣavaḥ śikṣitavyam. 

[6] idam avocad bhagavān.

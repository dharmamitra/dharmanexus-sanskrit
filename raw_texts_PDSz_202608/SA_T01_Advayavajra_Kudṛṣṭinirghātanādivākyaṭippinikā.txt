tatrāvasthāḥ trayaḥ– 

hetvavasthā nāma bodhicittāt prabhṛti bodhimaṇḍaniṣadanaṃ yāvat |

phalāvasthā nāma samyaksaṃbodhijñānotpattau sarvakleśaguṇaprahāṇiprāptyavasthā | 
sattvārthakriyāvasthā nāma prathamadharmacakrapravartanāt prabhṛti ā śāsanāntardhānaṃ yāvat ||

tatra hetvavasthā trividhā–

āśayāvasthā, prayogāvasthā, vaśitāvasthā ceti |
	% vaśitāvasthā \M] vasitāvasthā \S
	
tatrāśayāvasthā sattvanirmokṣapraṇidhānaṃ taddhetavaś catvāraḥ | tadyathā–
	% sattvanirmokṣa \M] sattvānirmokṣa \S
	
	gotrasanmitrakāruṇyaduḥkhābhīrutvahetavaḥ |
	caturbhiḥ pratyayair ebhir bodhicittaḥ prajāyate || 1 ||
		% bodhicittaḥ \M] bodhicittaṃ \S
		
^\M 40/195

iti vacanāt | daśabhir arthair āśayāvasthā | 

tatra prayogo dvividhā | adhimukticittasya pāramitāḥ sapta | tatrādhimukticittasya pāramitā daśa–
	% ādhimukticittasya \M] ādhimukticaritasya \S
	
	dānaṃ śīlaṃ kṣamā vīryaṃ dhyānaṃ prajñā upāyatā |
	praṇidhānaṃ balaṃ jñānam etāḥ pāramitā daśa || 2 ||

bhūmiprāptasya catasṛbhiḥ saṃpadbhiḥ saṃpannam āśayaprayogapratigrāhakadeyasaṃpadbhir iti dānaśīlakṣāntivīryadhyānaprajñopāyā etāḥ sapta pāramitāḥ | adhimukticaryācaritapāramitābhyo viśiṣyata iti dvividhā prayogāvasthā |
	% dānaśīlakṣāntivīryadhyānaprajñopāyā \M] dānaṃ śīlaṃ kṣāntir vīryaṃ dhyānaṃ prajñā upāyaḥ \S
	
tatra vaśitāḥ pañca, tadyathā– kleśopapattikarmopāyasattvaparipākāvasthā ceti | 

tatra hetvavasthāsthitena sarvam ādikarma kartavyam | phalāvasthāsattvārthakriyāvasthāsthitasya ca śākyamuner ivānābhogena ādikarma pravartata eveti | vistareṇa kudṛṣṭinirghāte jñeyam.

|| kudṛṣṭinirghātādivākyaṭippinikeyaṃ śrīmatpaṇḍitāvadhūtādvayavajrapādānāṃ kṛtir iyam ||

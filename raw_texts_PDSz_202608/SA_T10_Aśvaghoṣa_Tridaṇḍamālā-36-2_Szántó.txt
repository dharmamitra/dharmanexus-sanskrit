Title: Tridaṇḍamālā (36.2 Asārasūtra) 
Author: Aśvaghoṣa/scriptural 
Source: Ueno 2022 
Tibetan: Ø 
Input: PDSz 30.xi.2025 Budapest 

evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavāñ chrāvastyāṃ viharati jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme | tatra bhagavān bhikṣūn āmantrayate sma | 
tribhyo bhikṣavo 'sārebhyaḥ sāram ādeyam | katamebhyas tribhyaḥ | kāyād bhogebhyo jīvitāc ca |

[1] katham asārāt kāyāt sāram ādeyam | iha kulaputro 'bhivādanavandanaparyutthānāñjalisāmīcīkarmārheṣv abhivādanavandanapratyutthānāñjalisāmīcīkarmakartā bhavati | evam asārāt kāyāt sāram ādeyam |
	% anne: paryutthāna?

[2] katham asārebhyo bhogebhyaḥ sāram ādeyam | iha kulaputro dānaṃ dātā bhavati śramaṇebhyo brāhmaṇebhyaḥ kṛpaṇebhyo vanīyakebhyo 'dhvagebhyo yācanakebhyaḥ | tadyathānnaṃ pānaṃ khādyaṃ bhojyaṃ yānaṃ vastraṃ mālāgandhavilepanam apāśrayaṃ pradīpadānaṃ ca | evam asārebhyo bhogebhyaḥ sāram ādeyam | 

[3] katham asārāj jīvitāt sāram ādeyam | iha kulaputra idaṃ duḥkham āryasatyam iti yathābhūtaṃ prajānāti, ayaṃ duḥkhasamudayaḥ, ayaṃ duḥkhanirodhaḥ, iyaṃ duḥkhanirodhagāminī pratipad āryasatyam iti yathābhūtaṃ prajānāti | evam asārāj jīvitāt sāram ādeyam | 

[4] idam avocad bhagavān idam uktvā sugato hy athāparam etad uvāca śāstā ||

kāyasya ca satkāraṃ bhogānāṃ jīvitasya ca | 
tad ādadati saprajñā jinasya vacane ratāḥ || 1 ||

dharmam anucaranti ye sadā satpuruṣān pratimānayanti ca | 
te jīvitasāram adhyagur ye yuktā jinavacane maharṣayaḥ || 2 ||

ye yuktā jinavacane saṃghagatāṃ dakṣiṇāṃ dadati ye ca | 
tair iha lābhā labdhā bhavanty amoghaṃ ca mānuṣyam|| 3 || 

idam avocat ||

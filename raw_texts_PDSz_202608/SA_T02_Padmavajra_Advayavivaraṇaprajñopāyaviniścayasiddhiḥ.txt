idānīṃ prajñeti, upāya iti bhāgadvayaṃ vicāryate | prajñā kim ucyate? dharmato pravicala(ya)lakṣaṇā śrutacintābhāvanāmayī | dvividhā prajñā — pratyavekṣaṇā, nairvedhikī ceti | prati prati pratyavekṣate anviṣyate jñāyate cakṣurvajreṇa gṛhyate sā pratyavekṣaṇā prajñā | nairvedhikī nirviddhyate 'nayā pratyavekṣaṇā sā nairvedhikī prajñā | sā nairvedhikī prajñā uttaratra spaṣṭam asya sannairātmā sarvasattvajīvādikam eva buddhyā niḥsukhaṃ jñānaṃ karoti, sākṣāt pratipattiṃ karoti, sudurjayaś ca bhavati, ajayo bhavatīti yāvat |

upāyo 'pi caturvidho bodhivajreṇa varṇitaḥ | buddhavajreṇa varṇita iti yāvat | 

sevāvidhānaṃ prathamaṃ dvitīyam upasādhanam | 
sādhanaṃ tṛtīyaṃ caiva mahāsādhanaṃ caturtham iti || 1 || (Gu. Sa. pṛ. 162)

aparaṃ cedam—

prathamaṃ śūnyatābodhiḥ dvitīyaṃ vajrasaṃhṛtiḥ | 
tṛtīyaṃ bimbaniṣpattiś caturthaṃ cā(nyā)sam akṣaram ||
ebhir vajracatuṣkeṇa jñānaniṣpattir ucyate || 2 || (Gu. Sa. pṛ. 163)

[p. 212]

ata eva abhāvalakṣaṇā prajñā, bhāvalakṣaṇa upāya iti | tathā coktam— niḥsvabhāvalakṣaṇā prajñā, svabhāvalakṣaṇā upāyaś ceti | 

tasmāt prayoginas tena (dharmeṇa?) saṃsāro 'nuvivardhate | 
sukhāḥ kāmaguṇāḥ pañca bālānāṃ bo(bā)dhane matāḥ || 3 ||

yoginas te(gī tu te)na dharmeṇa buddhatvaṃ sādhayet punaḥ | 
rā[go] harṣas tathā prītir maitrī ca karuṇā tathā || 4 ||

upekṣā muditā caiva balam utsāha eva ca |
sau(śau)ryaṃ vīryaṃ tathā raudraṃ haṭhatvaṃ sāhasaṃ tathā | 
svarūpāḥ prakṛtīś cānyā[ḥ] svādhiṣṭhānena saṃcaret || 5 ||

ekamudrāsamāyuktaś caturmudrāyuto 'thavā |
antaḥpuragato vāpi svādhiṣṭhānam anusmaret || 6 ||

rūpaṃ śabdaṃ tathā gandhaṃ rasaṃ sparśaṃ ca śobhanam | 
anubhūyāt sadā yogī mahāsukhapade sthitaḥ || 7 ||

tataś cai(taṃ cai)vāvanaddhaṃ ca ghaṭaṃ śuṣiram eva ca |
vādyaṃ caturvidhaṃ yogī svādhiṣṭhānena nirviśet || 8 ||

śṛṅgārādīn rasān sarvān navanāṭya(ṭaka)saṃjñakān | 
anubhūyāt sadā yogī māyopamasamādhinā || 9 ||

yogatantre samuddiṣṭā[ḥ] sthomā(stobhā)dyā ye ca vistarāḥ | 
tāṃś ca pravartayed yogī māyayā 'nupalambhataḥ || 10 ||

unmiṣan nimiṣan vāpi hasan jalpaṃs tathaiva ca | 
gacchaṃs tiṣṭhan svapan bhuñjan māyādeham anusmaret || 11 ||

[p. 213]

jagaty anyāś ca yāḥ krīḍā nānāsaṅkalpakalpitāḥ | 
vicaret tāś ca tattvajño mahārāganaye sthitaḥ || 12 ||

svādhiṣṭhānam idaṃ prāpya sarvadṛṣṭividāraṇam | 
sarvaṃ kuryān na kuryād vā yathārucitaceṣṭitaḥ || 13 ||

caityakarma na kurvīta na ca pustakavācanam | 
karotu vācayec cāpi svādhiṣṭhānakrameṇa tu || 14 ||

devān na vandayed evaṃ bhikṣūṃś cāpi na vandayet |
athavā vandayet sarvān svādhiṣṭhānakrameṇa tu || 15 ||

mantranyāsaṃ na kurvīta mudrābandhaṃ tathaiva ca | 
mantrajāpaṃ na kuryād vā kuryāc ca pratibimbavat || 16 ||

madīya(yaṃ) dṛśyate cittaṃ bāhyam artha na vidyate |
evaṃ vai bhāvayet māṣān (bhāvān) tathatām apy anusmaret || 17 ||

anantadhyānavidhinā mantramūrtisamādhinā | 
yadi māyā na labdhaḥ(bdhā) syāt sarvaṃ bhūmi(ri) phalāya vai || 18 ||

dehabhogapratiṣṭhā ca bhūmipāramitādayaḥ | 
buddhaṛddhivikurvādi sarvaṃ māyāviceṣṭitam || 19 ||

ādarśabimba(mbe) sakalāṃ(laṃ) sa(su)yuktaṃ 
rūpaṃ yathā svacchataraṃ vibhāti | 
aśītyanuvyañjanalakṣaṇādyo(ḍhyo) 
dehas tathā vajradharaḥ sadaiva || 20 ||

indrāyudhaṃ viyati dṛṣṭam anekavarṇaṃ lokasya darśayati karma śubhāśubhāya | karaṇavidhānena bheditāḥ | 

tataḥ kurvanti sattvārthaṃ viśvarūpo maṇir yathā || iti |

[p. 214]

tena tāvat prajñeti, upāya iti vyavasthitaṃ bhāgadvayam |

idānīṃ nirucyate— prajñayā kevalayā kiṃ buddhatvaṃ syāt? no ced upāyamātreṇāpi? ucyate cedam— na kevalaṃ prajñāmātreṇa buddhatvaṃ bhavati, nāpy upāyamātreṇa, kintu yadi punaḥ prajñopāyalakṣaṇau samatāsvabhāvau bhavataḥ, etau dvāv abhinnarūpau bhavataḥ, tadā bhuktir mukti[ś ca] bhavatīti |

tathā coktam—

upanayaty abhimataṃ yasmān naukevānukūlaya(lya)taḥ |
tadānukūlayogena saivopāyaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||

ubhayor mīlanaṃ yac ca salilakṣīrayor iva |
advayākārayogena prajñopāyaṃ tad ucyate ||

prakṣeptum apanetuṃ ca śakṣa(kya)te yatra naiva hi | 
prajñopāya ca yat tyaktaṃ dharmatattvaṃ tad ucyate ||

cintāmaṇir ivāśeṣajagataḥ sarvadā sthitaḥ |
bhuktimuktipadaṃ samyak prajñopāyasvarūpataḥ ||

idam eva samāgamya saṃbuddhāḥ sugatāḥ purā |
sambudhyante ca sarvatra sambhotsyante jagaddhitāḥ || iti | (Pra. Vi. Si. 1.16-18, 25-26)

yadi punar iyaṃ prajñeti, idaṃ copāyam iti, yady evaṃ bhāgadvayaṃ kriyate, tadā buddhatvaṃ na bhavet | yadi punar anayoḥ śūnyatāsvabhāvam ekākārarūpatayā samyak prakarṣaparyantavartino vijñānam utpadyate, tadā niyataṃ yoginām ihaiva janmani buddhatvaṃ vajradharatvaṃ ceti | na caivaṃ prajñopāyarahitaṃ jñānam utpadyate,

prajñopāyātmakaṃ jñānam utpadyate | kiṃ bahunā, prajñopāyadvayaparijñānena yogināṃ muktir iti | tasmāt prajñopāyajanitātmakāḥ sarvatraidhātukādayaḥ sattvāḥ | ata eva buddho bhagavān mahākaruṇāpraṇidhānabalena yo yaḥ prapañcarūpeṇa dharmanairātmyayogena ca jñānajñeyavibhāgadvayena vyavasthitaḥ san ekākāraṃ jñānaṃ muktidharmam upadarśayati | ato 'rtham ādiśuddhāḥ sarvadharmāḥ prakṛtiprabhāsvarāḥ śrutvā(tā) iti bhagavato(tā) buddhenedam uktam | tathā  coktaṃ saṃvarākhye tantre—

pūrvakalpe punar jātā vāsanākleśahetutaḥ || iti |

tata(da)rtha ... ... na satye(ne?)kaputrapremātiśayatayā sarvasattvāḥ parigṛhītā iti | tathā coktam— ``ko 'smākaṃ bhaṇati buddho bhagavān anuttarāṃ samyaksambodhim abhisambuddhaḥ'' iti | yāvac ca traidhātukasattvān buddhatve pratiṣṭhāpayāmas tāvad buddha iti nāmadheyaṃ na lebhire | tathā coktaṃ bhadracaryāpraṇidhāne—

yāvata niṣṭha na tasya bhaveyyā sattva aśeṣata niṣṭha tathaiva |
karmatu kleśatu yāvata niṣṭhā tāvata niṣṭha mama praṇidhānam || (Sa. Bha. Ca. Pra. ślo. 46, Gaṇḍavyūhasūtre pṛ. 434)

tathā coktaṃ samādhirājatantre(sūtre)—

buddha bhaviṣyati sarvajano 'yaṃ nāsti ha kaścid abhājanasattvaḥ || iti | (Sa. Sū. pari˚ 1, ślo. 72, pṛ. 317)

ato 'rthaṃ sarvabhāvasvabhāvamūrtir bhagavān mahākaruṇāpraṇidhānabalena prajñopāyavibhāgadvyaṃ vyavasthitam | dharmasambhoganirmāṇarūpeṇa sākāranirākārarūpeṇa vyavasthitaḥ san bhavyasattvānāṃ prati vicitraprajñopāyanāṭakaṃ prakaṭayati | yena prajñopāyanāṭakaparijñānena ihaiva buddhatvaṃ vajradharatvaṃ ceti |

atropadeśo melāpyate | prajñā kim ucyate | prajñā padmaṃ vidhīyate | prajñāśabde śaṅkate | tena padmasvabhāvena locanāmāmakīpāṇḍarātārāsamantabhadrāpañcadevīśūnyatāsvabhāvā prajñā svabhāvenoktā iti | pṛthivyāptejovāyvākāśādayaḥ pañcabhūtā śūnyatārūpeṇa vyavasthitā iti yāvat | tena śūnyatāsvabhāvarūpeṇaiva locanāmāmakīpāṇḍaravāsinītārāsamantabhadrāḥ pañcadevatyaḥ tatra pariṇatā iti | akṣobhyaratnasambhavāmitābhāmoghasiddhiśrīmadvairocanādayo 'pi devatā dharmanairātmyayogena śrīrūpadharo(rā) bhūtvā locanādipañcadevīrūpeṇa pariṇatā iti | yā ca samantabhadrā devatā śūnyatārūpātmikā, sā prajñāpāramitā caturdevī jñānātmikā sphāraṇasaṃharaṇasvabhāvena vyavasthitā | śrīmahāvairocanākārajñānapariṇatasvabhāvā samantabhadrā prajñāpāramitāśabdenoktā iti | kiṃ bahunā, yā samantabhadrā prajñāpāramitā sā'bhāvarūpā ākāśalakṣaṇatvena śūnyatākārā viviktā prakṛtiprabhāsvarā jñānajñeyarahitā niḥsvabhāvalakṣaṇā dharmakāyapariṇatā satī vaineyajanaṃ prati sambhogakāyena sā vyavasthitā satī nirmāṇādikaṃ pratibhāsate(sayati) | sambhogakāyaś cāpi śūnyatālakṣaṇaṃ(ḥ) | śūnyatā ca sarvākāravaropetatvena vyavasthitā | māyāmarīcisvapnapratibimbapratiśrutyo(tko)dakacandranirmitopamasvabhāvatayā pratipadyata iti | tathā coktam— ``kāyaṃ sāmbhogikaṃ śūnyaṃ dharmakāyaṃ nairmāṇikaṃ kṛtvā bhāṣitaṃ sarvabuddhaiḥ'' [iti] |

prajñopāyaśabdena kim ucyate? prajñayā upāyaḥ prajñopāya iti, prajñājanita upāya ity arthaḥ | upāya ity anena śabdena ko 'rtho bhaṇyate? śrīvajrasattvaḥ saṃsārabhūtaḥ karuṇāsvabhāvaḥ sthiravastusvabhāvamūrtiḥ upāyaśabdenokta iti | upāyaḥ śrīvajrasattva iti | pañcakāyātmakaṃ pañcatathāgatātmakaṃ ca sphāraṇasaṃharaṇasvabhāvena vyavasthitam iti | vajrapadmasamāyogena

prajñāliṅgita ekākārarūpeṇa jñānasvabhāvaṃ bhāvayed iti | prajñopāya ity anayor dvayoḥ kaḥ sambandhaḥ? locanādipañcadevatyo bhūtādisvabhāvena śūnyatākārarūpeṇa pariṇatā iti | upāyo 'pi śrīvajrasattvaḥ sarvabhāvasvabhāvaḥ sthiracalasarvabhāvasvabhāvasya yā śūnyatā sā śūnyatākārayogena pariṇatā iti | oṃ hūṃ ity anenākṣaradvayena ekākāram iti | prajñopāyasthāpanārthaṃ dvidhārūpaṃ gataḥ | na ca vajrasattvo dvidhābhāvaṃ yāti | ta(ya)dā śūnyena śūnyatayā''krāntaṃ bhavati, tadā ekākāraṃ pratibhāsata iti | anyac ca prajñopāyātmakāḥ sarvasattvā iti ye prajñopāyasvabhāvadvaidhībhāvaṃ kurvanti tebhyo vidyāgrahagṛhītāḥ sambhoga(gaṃ) salīlam avalokya pārapatitās tiṣṭhanti | ye punar advayākāraṃ viśuddhākāratayā tattvarūpaṃ paśyanti bhāvayanti ca, te 'nuttarabodhiphalalābhina iti | tena prajñopāyobhayasvabhāvatayā na kaścid buddhānāṃ bhagavatāṃ bandhanam | kaścit paṇḍitaḥ samatāyogena muktir bha(kto bha)vati | tathā coktam—

bajjhanti jeṇa jatā(ḍā) parimuṃcanti teṇa budhā |
bodhibibhāvanayā viparītam idaṃ sakalam ||

punaś coktam—

yena yena hi bhāvena tatra tatrābhyāsabalād viśiṣṭatarasvabhāvam āviṣkaroty apunardṛṣṭadharmatālakṣaṇam | tathā coktam—

yena yena hi bhāvena manaḥ saṃyujyate nṛṇām |
tena tanmayatāṃ yāti viśvarūpo maṇir yathā ||

punaś coktam—

yena yena hi badhyante jantavo raudrakarmaṇā |
sopāyena tu tenaiva mucyante bhavabandhanāt || (He. Ta. 2.2.50)

tathā coktam—

kāmaśokabhayonmādacaurasvapnādyupaplutāḥ |
abhūtān api paśyanti purato 'vasthitān iva ||

tasmād bhūtam abhūtaṃ vā yad yad eveti bhāvyate |
bhāvanābalaniṣpattau tasya sākalyadhīḥ phalam ||

karuṇa uṇe viṇumuṇḍuhiṃ laggo | ṇaü so pāvaï uttima maggo ||
ahavā karuṇā kevali bhāvaï | so saṃsāramokṣa na pāvaï ||

yo puṇa vaṇṇi viyoga ṇa sakkaï |
i naü bhava naü nivvāṇa hi thākkaï ||

ayam arthaḥ— karuṇa iti | karuṇārahito yadi śūnyatāyāṃ lagati, tadāsau yogī uttamamārgaṃ na labhate, buddhatvamārgaṃ na labhata iti yāvat | athavā śūnyatārahito yadi karuṇākevalī bhāvyate, tadā saṃsārasya mukti na labhyate | yaḥ punar yogī bhāvadvayaṃ jñātvā prajñopāyarāśidvayaṃ yojayituṃ śakyate, ekākāraṃ kartuṃ śakyate | na tadbhavaṃ na nirvāṇam iti bhavaṃ saṃsāraṃ nirvāṇaṃ buddhatvam iti |

ekārākṛti yad divyaṃ madhye vaṃkārabhūṣitam |
ālayaḥ sarvabuddhānāṃ buddharatnakaraṇḍakam || (He. Ta. 2.3.4)

ekāraṃ padmam ity uktaṃ vaṃkāraṃ vajram ity āhuḥ |
tatra buddhāḥ samutpannās tatra vai parinirvṛtāḥ || (~ Gu. Si. 2.56)

ity advayavivaraṇaprajñopāyaviniścayasiddhiḥ ||
kṛtir iyam ācāryapadmavajrapādānām ||



@ [namaḥ] śrībhūtaḍāmarāya ||

kāyāḥ kṛtā yena jagaddhitārthaṃ 
kulāni devyaḥ kulanāyakāś ca |
tanmūrtimudrānusṛtaikatattvaḥ 
sa mṛgyatāṃ vajradharaḥ śivāya ||
	
	
	
	

atilaghu mahāmāyātantraṃ girā guru cārthato
jagati katicit tasyedānīṃ nirantaravedinaḥ |
pratipadam atas tatra jñānaṃ śuci śrutaśālināṃ
nibhṛtasulabhaṃ loke kartuṃ mamaiṣa pariśramaḥ ||
	

vividhaḥ sugatena bodhimārgaḥ 
kathito bhinnaruciṃ vilokya lokaṃ |
rucito bahavaḥ svayaṃ pravṛttā 
vivṛtir me guṇavaty athātra karma (?) ||
	
	
	

	

athetyādi | naitat tantraṃ muktakaṃ | kiṃ tarhi [|] tantrāntaraiḥ saṃyuktakaṃ | tasmād ayam athaśabdaḥ pūrvatantrāpekṣayānantaryam asya tantrasya dyotayati | ata eva nāsyādau nidānavākyam evaṃ mayetyādikaṃ prayuktaṃ | prāg eva prayuktatvāt | tad yathā śrīvajraśikhare |
	
	
	
	

ata iti | yasmād atisaṃkṣeparucīnām idam eva bodher āśu sādhanaṃ | ataḥ pravakṣye iti sambandhaḥ | pravibhajya vakṣye pravakṣye | 

tantram iti prabandhaṃ | 
	

trividhaṃ tantraṃ | hetutantraṃ | phalatantraṃ | upāyatantraṃ ca | 

tatra prakṛtiprabhāsvaram anādinidhanaṃ cittaṃ bodhicittaṃ | sa hetus tadbījaṃ | kasya bījaṃ | bodheḥ |
	

samyaksambodhiḥ phalaṃ niruttaraphalatvāt | sā punas tasyaiva prakṛtiprabhāsvarasya cittasyāgantukasarvāvaraṇakṣayalakṣaṇā viśuddhiḥ | sā buddhānāṃ dharmakāyaḥ | saṃbhoganirmāṇakāyasaṃgṛhītānām anantānāṃ buddhadharmāṇām āśraya ity arthaḥ | saiva buddhānāṃ bodhir dharmakāyo mahāvajradharapadaṃ | 

tasmād dhetos tasya phalasya pariniṣpattaye sādhanam upāyaḥ | sa punar bodhisattvānāṃ trikalpāsaṃkhyeyabhāvitaḥ saparikaro mārgaḥ |

mantrayāne tu tasyaivātimahato bodhimārgasya saṃkṣeparūpaḥ kṣiprataraṃ sukhataraṃ ca bodhisādhano maṇḍalacakrādyākāraḥ saparikaro mārga upāyaḥ |
	

etān hetuphalopāyān adhikṛtya ye deśanāprabandhās te yathākramaṃ hetutantraṃ phalatantram upāyatantraṃ ca | tad etat trividhaṃ tantraṃ pravakṣye |
	

tat tarhi tantraṃ kiṃnāmety āha | paramaguhyaṃ nāmeti | nāmaśabdo 'vyayaṃ prasiddhau vartate | paramaguhyam ity anena nāmnā prasiddham ity arthaḥ | 
	
	

keṣāṃ paramaguhyam ity āha | vajraḍākinīnāṃ |
	
	
ataś ca sākalyena vajraḍākinīparamaguhyaṃ nāmedaṃ tantraṃ | pūrvapadalopāt tu paramaguhyaṃ nāma | satyabhāmā bhāmeti yathā |

ḍai vaihāyasagamane | aikārasyātvam | ḍānaṃ ḍāḥ | ākāśagamanam ity arthaḥ | ḍāśabdāt tṛtīyā | ak | ag |  kuṭilāyāṃ gatau | atra sarvato gamanaṃ kuṭilā gatiḥ | ḍā akituṃ śīlam asyā iti ḍākinī | ṛddhyā kāyakoṭīniyutaśatasahasrair yugapat sarvato gāminīty arthaḥ |
	
	

tathā coktam—

ḍai vaihāyasagamane dhātur atra vikalpitaḥ |
sarvākāśacarā siddhir ḍākinīti milicchayeti ||
	
	

vajragrahaṇaṃ bāhyaḍākinīvyavacchedārtham |

vajrāś ca niṣprapañcajñānamayatvāt | ḍākinyaś ceti | vajraḍākinyaḥ | 
	
	

tāsāṃ kīdṛśīnām ity āha | guhyeśvarīṇām iti | atra pañca kulāni | pañca guhyāni | teṣām īśvaryaḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārakārikāḥ | tatra tathāgatakulasyeśvarī 

buddhaḍākinī | vajrakulasya vajraḍākinī | maṇikulasya ratnaḍākinī | padmakulasya padmaḍākinī karmakulasyeśvarī viśvaḍākinī | tāsām apīśvarī mahāmāyā |

yasmād asau tāsāṃ guhyātiguhyānāṃ guhyaṃ tasmāt paramaguhyam | 
	

tadabhidhāyakaṃ tantram api tathocyate | sītāharaṇaṃ kāvyam iti yathā || 3 ||
	

	
	
		

imam eva nāmārthaṃ darśayitum āha | yayetyādi | 

bṛha bṛhi vṛddhau | manin | nairukto varṇavikāraḥ | evaṃ brahmeti bhavati | iha tu sarvalokagurutvāt sarvato vṛddhā iti brahmāṇas tathāgatāḥ saṃbhoganirmāṇakāyasaṃgṛhītāḥ | teṣām aṇḍam utpattisthānaṃ | dharmakāya ity arthaḥ | athavā mokṣātiśayatvād brahma ca tat kāyadvayotpattisthānatvād aṇḍaṃ ceti brahmāṇḍaṃ dharmakāya ity arthaḥ | 
	

pariśuddheṣu buddhakṣetreṣu yaḥ sattvalokaḥ so 'tra caraḥ | yas teṣu bhājanalokaḥ so 'trācaraḥ | saha tābhyāṃ vartata iti sacarācaraṃ brahmāṇḍaṃ | tad yayā vyāptam iti sambandhaḥ | 

sakalam iti | sarvaṃ | sarvatāthāgatam ity arthaḥ | 

tatra kathaṃ brahmāṇḍaṃ tayā vyāptaṃ | yasmāt tanmayam eva sarvabuddhānāṃ brahmāṇḍaṃ | kasmāc carācarau lokau tayā vyāptau | tadudbhavatvāt tayoḥ | 

etad evāha | utpattir ityādinā | utpattiḥ kāraṇaṃ | sarvadevānām iti | pañcānāṃ kulaḍākinīnāṃ | brahmādīnām iti | pañcānāṃ tathāgatānāṃ | tatkulānāṃ ca pratyekam anantānāṃ | 
	

maharddhiketi | mahāprabhāvā || 4 || 

	
	

kāsāv ity āha | mahāmāyeti | mahāmāyāśabdena bhagavato vajradharasya paramarahasyā mūrtir bhagavān heruka uktaḥ | 

maheśvarīti | brahmādayo hi devīnāṃ sṛṣṭir atas tā īśvaryaḥ | tā apy asyāḥ sṛṣṭir ata iyaṃ maheśvarī |
	

yata iyaṃ maheśvarī | ata eva tāsāṃ param iyaṃ guhyaṃ | tāsām iti | vajraḍākinīnāṃ | paraṃ guhyam iti | paramaṃ guhyaṃ | iyam iti mahāmāyā | 
	

tathā hi sūkṣmaṃ rūpam audārikāṇāṃ rūpāṇāṃ guhyam ucyate | 
	

tataś ca pañcānāṃ kulānāṃ kulādhipāḥ pañca guhyāni | tanmayatvāt pañca kulānāṃ | guhyānām api guhyāni pañca ḍākinyaḥ | 
	

tathā hi svakulavyāpakaprakāśalakṣaṇāḥ kuleśāḥ | tena te audārikāḥ | bhāvāṃśapradhānatvāt | tasyaiva prakāśasya dvayaśūnyatālakṣaṇā ḍākinyaḥ | tatas tāḥ sūkṣmā abhāvāṃśapradhānatvāt |
	

tāsāṃ guhyātiguhyānāṃ guhyaṃ mahāmāyā | tathā hi ekaikakuladharmatālakṣaṇatvād audārikyas tāḥ | iyaṃ tu pañcakulasādhāraṇī dharmatā pañcānām api tāsāṃ samatā | tatas tāsām apīyaṃ guhyam iti paramaguhyam eṣā | tadvācakatvāt tantram apīdaṃ paramaguhyam ucyate |

ucyatāṃ paramaguhyaṃ | mahāmāyā tu katham ucyata ity āha | mahāmāyetyādi | 
	
sā hi mahāraudrā | paramahiṃsratvāt | kutaḥ | yasmād bhūtasaṃhārakāriṇī | avidyāsaṃbhūtatvāt bhūtaṃ buddhabodheḥ sarvam āvaraṇaṃ | tasya saṃhārakāriṇī pralayakartrī | sa hi pralayaḥ paro mokṣaḥ |
	

evaṃ manyate | mīnātīti māyā | mīñ hiṃsāyām | ātvaṃ | ṇapratyaya iti || 5 ||
	

	
	

prakārāntaram āha | yayetyādi | trailokyam iti | pariśuddhaṃ buddhakṣetraṃ | sacarācaraṃ pūrvavat | vyāptaṃ tadudbhūtatvāt | sā hi dharmakāyaḥ | tanniṣyandaś caitat sacarācaraṃ | evaṃ manyate | nirmimīta iti māyā | māṅ māne | ṇapratyaya iti | 
	
	
	

prakārāntaram apy āha | sā punar ityādinā | punar iti punar aparaṃ | aparaḥ prakāra ity arthaḥ | saiṣeti herukarūpā mahāmāyā | saṃharati trailokyam iti | dṛṣṭipātena maheśvarādīnāṃ duṣṭānāṃ bhasmīkaraṇāt | punaḥ paścāt sṛjate | tad eva nirdoṣīkṛtya mahādhvajaghaṇṭādhvanibhiḥ pratyujjīvanāt || 6 || 
	
	

	
	

tataḥ kim ity āha | guhyakānām ityādi | bāhyavajrakule devā īṣadguhyatvād guhyakāḥ | teṣām iyaṃ mātā | mātrīty arthaḥ | ataś ceyaṃ mahāmāyeti viśrutā khyātā | mīño māṅaś ca ṇapratyayaṃ kṛtvā pūrvavad iti bhāvaḥ ||

āstām iyaṃ svayam īdṛśī | sādhakānāṃ tu kiṃ karotīty ata āha | trailoketyādi | niruttarajñānasvabhāvatvāt vidyā | iyaṃ mahāmāyākhyā vidyā sādhakānāṃ trailokyaṃ sādhayati | sarvakāmāḥ sarvābhiprāyā vidyādharatvādayaḥ | tāṃś ca prakarṣeṇa dadātīti | 
	

idaṃ ślokārdham uddeśaḥ | asyaiva nirdeśo yayetyādir anena yogamāyāvidyenety etatparyantaḥ || 7 ||
	
	

	
	

	
	

yayā vidyayā | sādhakeśvarīti sādhakānām īśvaryā | vijñātamātreṇeti anuṣṭhitamātrayā | sādhakeṣu sidhyanta iti saṃbandhaḥ | 
	

ke siddhyanta ity āha | devetyādi | devāḥ śakrādayaḥ | dānavāḥ pretamaharddhikāḥ | gandharvā dhṛtarāṣṭraprabhṛtayaḥ | saha yakṣādibhir vartanta iti tathoktāḥ | sādhakeṣv iti sādhakānāṃ | siddhyanta iti vidheyā bhavanti || 8-9 ||

	
	

vaśam iti vidheyatvam | 
	

bhūtānīti sattvāḥ | jalajāni makarādīni | sthalajāni vyāghrādīni | evaṃ tāvad bāhyasya trailokasya sādhanīti nirdiṣṭaṃ | 
	
	
	
lokottarasyāpi trailokyasya sādhanīti nirdeṣṭum āha | prakṛtītyādi | 
	

atra ślokārddhena saṃkṣepato hetutantram uktaṃ | svayambhūpañcajñāninām ity anena ślokapādena phalatantraṃ | tṛtīyapañcamaṣaṣṭhaiḥ pādair upāyatantraṃ | 

prakṛtir nijaḥ svabhāvaḥ | tayā prabhāsvarāḥ prabhāsanaśīlāḥ | prakarṣeṇa nirmalā ity arthaḥ | ke punas ta ity āha | 

dharmā iti | svalakṣaṇadhāraṇād dharmāḥ sarvavastūnīty arthaḥ | 
	

tāni punaḥ sarvavastūni cittamātrāṇi | cittasyaiva tena tenākāreṇa pratibhāsanāt | 
	

tadyathā svapne bhavanodyānavāpīkarituragamanuṣyādirūpeṇa caitasikānām api cittaviśeṣatvāt | 
	

uktaṃ ca daśabhūmake—
	

cittamātram idaṃ | yaduta traidhātukam iti |  

āryalaṅkāvatāre 'py uktaṃ—

bāhyo na vidyate hy artho yathā bālair vikalpyate |
vāsanāluṭhitaṃ cittam arthābhāsaṃ prajāyata iti | 
	

vāsanāluṭhitam iti vāsanābhir upahataṃ viplutam ity arthaḥ |

bhavantu cittamātrāḥ sarvadharmās te tu kutaḥ prakṛtyaiva bhāsvarāḥ prabhāsvarāś cety ata āha— ādītyādi | ā āder ādiḥ | āditaḥ prabhṛtīty arthaḥ | sarvāvaraṇamalābhāvāc chuddhāḥ | malavāsanānām abhāvād anāvilāḥ | hiśabdo yasmādarthe | yasmād ādiśuddhās tasmāt prakṛtibhāsvarās [[[tasmāt prakṛtiprabhāsvarāḥ]]] |
	
	

nanu vidyamāneṣu maleṣu tadvāsanāsu ca kathaṃ śuddhā anāvilāś ca | atatsvabhāvānāṃ malānāṃ tatrāgantukatvāt | ākāśe tamastuhinadhūmābhrarajasām iva | tasmāt prakṛtiprabhāsvarāḥ | 
	

yady evaṃ na syāt tadā na kasyacid bodhiḥ syāt [,] cittasvabhāvānāṃ malānāṃ sati citte kṣayāyogāt|
	

tasmāt prakṛtiprabhāsvaratvaṃ cittasya bodher hetuḥ | bodhiḥ phalaṃ | 

sā punar bodhiḥ savāsanasarvāvaraṇakṣayād ātyantikī cittasantānasya viśuddhiḥ | saiva dharmakāyaḥ | saṃbhoganirmāṇakāyasaṃgṛhītānāṃ dharmāṇāṃ kāyo vāsa āśraya iti kṛtvā || 10 || 

	
	

tatra buddhā bhagavanto dharmakāyena svayaṃbhuvaḥ svayam eva bhavanti | sarvāvaraṇakṣayataḥ kaivalyāt | svarūpeṇaiva prakhyāntīti kṛtvā |
	

<tribhiḥ kāyaiḥ> pañcajñāninaḥ pañca jñānāny eṣāṃ | ādarśajñānaṃ | samatājñānaṃ | pratyavekṣaṇājñānaṃ | kṛtyānuṣṭhānajñānaṃ | suviśuddhadharmadhātujñānaṃ ceti |
	

tatrādarśajñānam acchādarśanirmalam anantaṃ śāśvataṃ ca madhyavartino jñānatrayasyāśrayaḥ | sarveṣāṃ jñānāntarāṇāṃ pratibimbodayasthānaṃ | yena pratibimbodayena tathāgatāḥ sarvākārasarvadharmadarśino bhavanti ||
	

samatājñānaṃ— sarvasattveṣv ātmanirviśeṣatājñānaṃ | sadā mahāmaitrīmahākaruṇāsaṃprayuktaṃ | 

pratyavekṣaṇājñānaṃ— sarvajñeyeṣv avyāhataṃ sarvasamādhidhāraṇīnāṃ nidhānaṃ parṣanmaṇḍaleṣu sarvāsāṃ svavibhūtīnāṃ nidarśakaṃ | mahādharmavṛṣṭīnāṃ pravarṣakaṃ ||
	
	

yena jñānena tathāgatāḥ sarvalokadhātuṣv anantaiḥ kāyavākcittanirmāṇaiḥ pratikṣaṇam anantānāṃ sattvānām arthaṃ kurvanti, tad eṣāṃ kṛtyānuṣṭhānajñānaṃ || 
	
	

yena jñānena tathāgatāḥ sarvadharmatathatāṃ suviśuddhāṃ paśyanti, tanmātradarśanāt | sarvanimittamalānām atyantam aprakhyānāt | tad eṣāṃ suviśuddhadharmadhātujñānaṃ |
	

ādyāni trīṇi jñānāni saṃbhogakāyaḥ | caturthaṃ nirmāṇakāyaḥ | pañcamaṃ dharmakāyaikadeśaḥ |
	

svayaṃbhuvaś ca te 

pañcajñāninaś ceti tathoktāḥ | tathāgatā ity arthaḥ ||

teṣāṃ phalabhūtānām upāyāḥ pragīyante prādhānyena deśyante | tatreti | tasmin hetau prakṛtiprabhāsvaratve sati | 
	

na hy etat phalaṃ saty api hetau vinopāyair bhavati | hetor anāditvena sarveṣām ādita eva bodheḥ prasaṅgāt | tasmād upāyāḥ pragīyante | 
	

ke te ity āha | yoginītyādi | yoginīti pañca ḍākinyaḥ | yogamāteti | mahāmāyā | traidhātukam aśeṣata iti | sarvaṃ traidhātukaṃ | tābhya eva saṃbhūtāḥ | kuleśāḥ kulāni ca | 
	

katham etāni traidhātukaṃ | triṣu vajradhātuṣu tathāgataguhyeṣv antarbhāvāt | katham ete upāyāḥ | hetoḥ sampattau bodhibhavyatālakṣaṇāyāṃ satyām eṣām eva bhāvanayā jāpādiparicchadayā bodher āśu prāpteḥ | prāptāyāṃ ca tasyām ebhir eva saṃbhoganirmāṇakāyasaṃgṛhītaiḥ pareṣāṃ yathābhavyaṃ bodhitrayaprāpaṇāt || 11 || 
	
	

	
	

siddhyatīti | mārgarūpeṇa phalarūpeṇa ca sādhakānāṃ niṣpadyate | yayā vijñātamātrayā | aśeṣaniḥśeṣam iti | etad eva yoginyādirāśitrayaṃ |
	
ekasyāpi rāśer aparityāgān niḥśeṣaṃ | ekasyā api devatāyā aparityāgād aśeṣaṃ | aśeṣaṃ ca tan niḥśeṣaṃ ca |
	
kasmāt siddhyati | yasmāt sarvaṃ māyāvikurvitaṃ | sarvam idaṃ mahāmāyādhīnaṃ mārgaphalāvasthayor ity arthaḥ |
	

trailokyasādhanīti yat pūrvam uddiṣṭaṃ tan nirdiṣṭaṃ | 

sarvakāmapradāyiketi yad uddiṣṭaṃ tad api nirdeṣṭum āha | harata ityādi | harate | ākarṣayati | svārthāya parārthāya vā | buddhānām iti buddhān | bhuṅkta iti ākṛṣyopabhuṅkte | surāgrāḥ suraśreṣṭhāḥ śakrādayaḥ || 12 ||

	
	

yogāḥ samādhayaḥ | teṣv aiśvaryaṃ vaśitvaṃ samāpattaye vyutthānāya ca | vidyā mahāmāyā | indrajālaṃ sainyādīnāṃ nirmāpanaṃ darśanam antardhāpanaṃ ca yathāyogaṃ sadasatāṃ |
	
	

mohanaṃ mūrcchāsaṃjananaṃ | stambhanaṃ niṣpandīkaraṇaṃ | māraṇaṃ ratnatrayāder atyantāpakāriṇāṃ | uccāṭanaṃ sthānatyājanaṃ | ādiśabdād aṅgabhaṅgajvaragraharākṣasagrahaṇādikaṃ || 13 ||
	

	
	

vaśaṃ gato vaśyaḥ | iha tu vaśyārthaṃ karma vaśyam uktaṃ | ākarṣaṇe dve | pādākarṣaṇaṃ piṇḍākarṣaṇaṃ ca | prathamaṃ bhūmyā | dvitīyam ākāśena | trīṇy etāni karmāṇi vaśyākarṣaṇakarmāṇi | parapure paraśarīre praveśaḥ parapuraśabdenoktaḥ | ādiśabdena vyutkrāntyādīni gṛhyante | tāny eva kāryāṇi parapurādikāryāṇi | antardhānānīti | adṛśyatvāni || 14 ||
	

	
	

	
	

anyonyam anuraktayor api vidveṣaṇaṃ vidveṣaḥ | jambhanaṃ mūkīkaraṇaṃ | pātālagamanaṃ bilapraveśaḥ | vidyādharāṇāṃ cakravartī yas teṣāṃ nigrahānugrahakṣamo mahāvidyādharaḥ | tadbhāvas tattvaṃ | ajaratvam amaratvaṃ nirvyādhitvam iti | trayam api sugamaṃ | cārtho gamyate | nirvyādhitvaṃ ceti | etan mohanādikaṃ sarvam anena yogamāyāvidyena kriyata iti śeṣaḥ | māyeti | mahāmāyā | vidyaiva vidyaḥ | sarvabuddhadharmāṇām ekarasatvaṃ yogaḥ | yogaś cāsau māyāvidyaś ca | 
	

vidyādharetyādi ślokārdhaṃ saptadaśākṣaraṃ | nirvyādhītyādikaṃ caturdaśākṣaraṃ | eteṣu ca sarvabuddhadharmaharaṇādikarmasu prayogāḥ kecid atraiva tantre vaktavyāḥ | kecid anyatantrānusāreṇa draṣṭavyāḥ | 
	

vijñānamātreṇety uktaṃ | anuṣṭhitamātrayety arthaḥ | ato 'nuṣṭhānaṃ darśayitum āha | akṣaretyādi | akṣaraṃ mantrākṣaraṃ | mantrarūpaṃ mantrajā mūrtiḥ | akṣaraṃ ca mantrarūpaṃ ca | tena tābhyām ity arthaḥ | vikurvanti | vikrīḍanti | karmādisiddhibhiḥ | yoginaḥ sādhakāḥ || 15-16 ||
	
	
	
	

	
	

akṣaraṃ katham anutiṣṭhantīty āha | paṭhanti cintayantīti | atra pāṭho vācā jāpaḥ | cintā cittena jāpaḥ |

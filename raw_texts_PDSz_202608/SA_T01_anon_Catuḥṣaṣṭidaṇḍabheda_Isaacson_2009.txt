Title: Catuḥṣaṣṭidaṇḍabheda
Author: anonymous 
Source: HSS 24, 204v2–204v4; transcribed in full in Isaacson 2009
Tibetan: D1210 

catuṣṣaṣṭidaṇḍasya lakṣaṇa (sic for lakṣaṇam) divārddhena prāṇasaṃkhyā pañcasahasra 540 || evaṃ saṃkhyā gaṇitena ahorātreṇa ṣaṭśatādhika (sic) ekaviṃśatisahasrañ ca yathāsaṃkhyena gaṇitvā jñātavya ghaṭi ekena prāṇa 360 | catuḥṣaṣṭidaṇḍasya bhedaḥ samāptaḥ ||

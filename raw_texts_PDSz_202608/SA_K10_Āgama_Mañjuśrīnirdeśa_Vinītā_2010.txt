Title: Mañjuśrīnirdeśa   
Source: Vinītā 2010, no. 15
Tibetan: D 177 
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó 

evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān śrāvastyāṃ viharati sma jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme mahatā bhikṣusaṃghena sārdham ardhatrayodaśabhir bhikṣuśataiḥ pañcamātrair bodhisattvasahasraiḥ | 

tena khalu punaḥ samayena bhagavān anekaśatasahasrayā pariṣadā parivṛtaḥ puraskṛto dharmaṃ ca deśayati sma | 

atha khalu mañjuśrīḥ kumārabhūto daśayojanapramāṇamātraṃ chattraṃ gṛhītvā bhagavataḥ pṛṣṭhān mūrdhni saṃdhārayati sma | 

atha susīmo nāma saṃtuṣitakāyiko devaputro 'vaivartiko 'nuttarāyāṃ samyaksaṃbodhau saparivāras tatraiva parṣadi saṃnipatito 'bhūt | saṃniṣaṇṇaś ca | utthāyāsanād yena mañjuśrīḥ kumārabhūtas tenopasaṃkrāntaḥ | upasaṃkramya mañjuśriyaṃ kumārabhūtam etad avocat |

adyāpi tvaṃ mañjuśrīr na tṛptim upayāsi bhagavataḥ pūjākarmaṇi | 

mañjuśrīr āha | tat kiṃ manyase devaputrāpi nu mahāsamudro vāripratīcchanatṛptim upayāti |

devaputra āha | na hi mañjuśrīḥ |

mañjuśrīr āha | evam eva devaputra mahāsamudropamaṃ gambhīraṃ duravagāham aprameyam asādhāraṇaṃ sarvajñajñānaṃ paryeṣitukāmena bodhisattvena mahāsattvena pūjātṛptim āpattavyaṃ tathāgatapūjāyām eva | 

devaputra āha | kimārambaṇena mañjuśrīs tathāgataḥ pūjayitavyaḥ |

mañjuśrīr āha | caturbhir devaputrārambaṇais tathāgataḥ pūjayitavyaḥ | katamaiś caturbhiḥ | tadyathā bodhicittārambaṇena sarvasattvapramokṣārambaṇena triratnavaṃśānupacchedārambaṇena sarvabuddhakṣetrapariśuddhyārambaṇena | ebhir devaputra caturbhir ārambaṇais tathāgataḥ pūjayitavyaḥ |

idam avocan mañjuśrīr āttamanāḥ susīmo devaputraḥ, te ca bhikṣavas te ca bodhisattvāḥ sā ca sarvāvatī parṣat sadevamānuṣāsuragandharvaś ca loko mañjuśriyaḥ kumārabhūtasya bhāṣitam abhyanandann iti |

mañjuśrīnirdeśaṃ nāma mahāyānasūtraṃ pañcadaśamaṃ samāptam |

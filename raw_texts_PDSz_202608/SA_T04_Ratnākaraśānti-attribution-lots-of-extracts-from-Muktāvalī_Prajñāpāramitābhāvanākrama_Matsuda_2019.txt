|| oṃ namo buddhāya || 

[1] niṣprapapañcam anālambam acintyam avikāriṇam | 
sarvadoṣavinirmuktaṃ vande sarvavidaṃ sadā || 

yatprasādakaradhvastamohadhvāntacayaṃ sphuṭam |
paśyanti jagatas tattvaṃ taṃ vande vadatāṃ varam || 

[2] iha khalu bodhisattvenānuttarāṃ samyaksambodhim abhisamboddhukāmena prajñāpāramitā bhāvayitavyā | tatra prathamataḥ kvacid api mano'nukūle rahasi sukhāsana upaviśya paryaṅkam ābhujya duḥkhaduḥkhatayā saṃskāraduḥkhatayā pariṇāmaduḥkhatayā ṣaḍgatikaṃ sattvam imaṃ duḥkhitam avalokya mahākaruṇāsamanvāgato bodhicittam utpādayet |

[3] tadanu bodhicittasvarūpam avadhārayituṃ dṛḍhīkartuṃ vṛddhiṃ virūḍhiṃ vipulatāṃ paripūriṃ netuṃ bhāvān evaṃ vicārayet | yad etan nīlapītādyākārajātaṃ na tad bāhyam āntaraṃ vā vastu | ekānekasvabhāvavirahāt | tathā hi | na tad ekaṃ bhāgabhedena pratibhāsanāt | nāpy anekaṃ paramāṇuśaḥ paramāṇor ayogāt | tathā hi yady asau sāṃśaḥ sa kathaṃ paramāṇuḥ | atha niraṃśaḥ tadā saṃyuktāḥ paramāṇavaḥ sarvātmanā saṃyogāt parasparam abhinnadeśāḥ syur iti sarvaḥ piṇḍaḥ paramāṇumātraḥ syāt | gajo 'pi girir api sāgaro 'pi | uktaṃ cāryalaṅkāvatāre | 

bāhyo na vidyate hy artho yathā bālair vikalpyate |
vāsanāluṭhitaṃ cittam arthābhāsaṃ pravartate ||

tasmān nāsti vijñānabāhyo grāhyo 'rthaḥ | tadabhāvād grāhakatvam api vijñānasya nāsti | sarvathā dvayaṃ nāsti grāhyaṃ grāhakaṃ ca | evaṃ vijñānavādī | upakalpitaṃ ca vijñānaṃ praty uktam || 

[4] tataḥ kevalam anādyavidyābhyāsavāsanābalaviplutānāṃ bālānāṃ buddhir asataiva tena tenākāreṇa pratibhāsate | tam eva cākāram asantaṃ nimittīkṛtya lokasya dvayakalpanā dvayagrāhaś ca sarvānarthanidānabhūtaḥ pravartate | yāvac cāyam ākāraḥ pratibhāsate tāvat paramārtho na dṛśyate | keśamaśakādidarśibhis taimirikaiḥ keśādiśūnyatāvat || 

[5] tataḥ sa evam upaparīkṣya sarvanāmāni sarvanimittāni parivarjayati | tatas tasya sarvadharmānālambane samādhau nirjalpe nirābhāse sthitasya pūrvaprayogavāsanābalād anābhogato 'nabhisaṃskārataḥ sarvaprapañcanimittānām astaṃgamād avikalpam anābhāsaṃ sarvadharmaśūnyatādarśanaṃ kevalavimalānantanabhonibhaṃ lokottarajñānaṃ jāyate |

[6] tad eva pāramārthikaṃ bodhicittam | saiva mukhyā prajñāpāramitā | sa eva ca sarvāvaraṇapratipakṣo mārgaḥ | tena ca pratipakṣeṇālayavijñānasaṃniviṣṭānāṃ sarveṣāṃ sāṃkleśikadharmabījānāṃ tadvāsanānāṃ ca parikṣayāt pratiṣṭhādehabhoganirbhāsānāṃ vijñānānāṃ nirodhāt tad ālayavijñānam ālayavijñānalakṣaṇaparityāgād anāsravadhātulakṣaṇaṃ parigṛhṇāti | 

[7] sa evānāsravo dhātuḥ | buddhānāṃ dharmakāyaḥ | bhūtakoṭiḥ | tathatā | paramārthaḥ | dharmadhātuḥ | bhūtāni tattvāni teṣāṃ koṭiḥ paryanta iti bhūtakoṭiḥ | nityaṃ tathaiva bhāvād iti tathatā | paramasya lokottarajñānasyārthaḥ | paramaś cāsāv arthaś ceti vā paramārthaḥ | dharmā āryadharmāḥ teṣāṃ dhātur ādhāra iti dharmadhātur iti | tatprāptau tadadhīnā sambhoganirmāṇakāyaprāptiḥ | sarvākārasakalajagadarthalakṣaṇeti ||

[8] tad imāṃ bhagavatīṃ prajñāpāramitāṃ bhāvayato 'vasthāḥ samupajāyante | yathoktam |

audāsīnyam ivāyāti svapatīva nirutsukam |
cittaṃ nairātmyam ālambya śrāntaṃ viśrāmyatīva ca ||
karoti stabdhatām akṣṇoḥ śirasaś cāvanamratām |
staimityaṃ cittacaittānāṃ śūnyatā śūnyatekṣiṇām ||
dṛṣṭatattvaḥ kṛtārtho 'ham iti liṅgekṣaṇāt punaḥ |
bhāvanāṃ notsṛjed vīraḥ parato 'py asti tat param ||

jñāne tu pariniṣpanne sarvam ekarasātmakam | 
nirvibhāgam anādyantaṃ nirākāraṃ nirāgraham || 
anutsedham anāyāmam adīrgham aparimaṇḍalam |
sūryanirbhinnatimiram ākāśam iva nirmalam ||
samatādharmanirvedhāt sarvaṃ samasamaṃ punaḥ |

vikalpaḥ samvṛtiḥ khyāto vikalpāntarakāraṇam |
sa eva paramārthas tu vikalpavinivartakaḥ ||

[9] bhāvayatā ca samādheḥ ṣac caurā veditavyāḥ | ye kuśaladharmavittaṃ muṣitvānarthe pātayanti | tadyathā styānamiddhalayauddhatyakaukṛtyavicikitsāḥ | tatra styānaṃ dadhyādibhakṣaṇajanitaḥ kāyagauravatādiviśeṣaḥ | middho nidraiva | layo līnatā cittasya | ālambane 'paṭupracāratā | auddhatyam uddhatatā cittasya vikṣepaḥ | kaukṛtyaṃ kutsitaṃ kṛtaṃ kukṛtam | tasya bhāvaḥ kaukṛtyam | paścāttāpaviśeṣaḥ | vicikitsā saṃśayaḥ kim evaṃ gurūpadiṣṭaṃ na vā || 

[10] tatra styānamiddhapraśamāya āhāre mātrajñatā | anityatāsmaraṇam | śaradāśatavyavalokanam | layanirākaraṇāya prajñāvīryabalam udyānavāpīśarajjyotsnādipramodakārivastusmaraṇaṃ vā | auddhatyapraśāntyā upaśamaḥ | kaukṛtyavicikitsāvināśāya samādhiguṇasmaraṇam | kalyānamitre ca tīvraśraddhā gauravapremaprasādatā ca | yadā ca doṣaṣaṭkavarjitaṃ svarasavāhi ca bhavati cittaṃ tadā samyaggatam upekṣet ||
